# Discovery crosswalk

A single mention of “LAMA2 therapy” could refer to an antibody bridge or to two complementary laminin linkers. Even a match on the two linkers can describe different delivery designs. These tables let readers trace the named topic, the component proposal, and later comparisons at their respective levels of detail. The identifiers are for lookup. Their order carries no priority.

| [Historical occurrences](topic-occurrences.csv) | 825 named entries | Use the original H number to distinguish a direct mention from a comparison mentioned in passing. |

## Reading the records

**Historical topics.** The October 28 coverage review reconciled the October 27 initial screen (357 entries), expanded screen (453), candidate reassessment (14), and the LAMA2 reference dossier (one). It indexed 668 distinct recorded names across the 825 entries. Explicit bundles can have several direct topics. The dated mapping has 873 direct and 18 contextual links. A topic can represent a therapeutic family, measurement, disease, comparator, or development approach. Mentions measure coverage. They carry no weight as independent studies.

We shortened the public topic labels while retaining membership in the original coverage review. For 139 topics the table also carries up to two public citations from an October 28 focused recheck (HFR numbers) or a singly mapped, directly cited historical entry (H numbers). A blank source field means the compact index does not assign a citation to that topic. Its mention-level provenance remains available through the occurrence table.

**Component proposals.** The October 28 screen compared proposed combinations and functional uses of existing molecular parts. The source coordinate identifies a search route, report, and row within that screen. Concept IDs group P009 with P027 (amphotericin and sterol) and P017 with P043 (ALK1 and BMPRII signaling in hereditary hemorrhagic telangiectasia). Related constructs keep their own records. For example, P031 and P050 both address the renal glycocalyx through syndecan-4 but use different preparations.

**Functional comparisons.** The follow-on search began October 28 and completed its recorded assessments on November 2. Seven repeated concept groups account for the ten within-screen merges. Three concepts also occur in the component screen. They concern LAMA2 linkers, a cartilage lubricant mimic with hyaluronan, and amphotericin-based airway transport. The tables preserve all contributing rows. Six additional concepts mentioned in the completion record lacked recoverable descriptions and sit outside the 67. The source coordinates in this table refer to the follow-on search.

**Broad screen.** The October 28 screen contains 12 biology records, 11 delivery and assembly records, ten material-access records, and one mechanism review. The original study compared some of these with earlier candidates. Their IDs preserve their role as fresh screening records.

**Source interpretation.** The last three tables carry up to two public links from the cited record. These may document a component, platform, assay, existing treatment or institutional development listing. They retain the date and scope of the original assessment. The proposed combination, therapeutic response in the named disease, and terms for using it each require their own evidence. Across tables, the units and repeated concepts differ. Use the [program atlas](../atlas/README.md) for curated evidence and the [research guide](../research/README.md) for the dated comparisons.
