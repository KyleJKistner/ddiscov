## ZG16 and intestinal bacterial exclusion

Recombinant ZG16 corrected abnormal bacterial movement in Zg16-null mucus. Human ZG16P can bind bacteria and mucin. The proposed attachment of human ZG16P to a mucin/PEG matrix depends on the matrix retaining active protein. In a separate mouse experiment neither tested formulation was detectable at two hours.

**Next comparison.** Start with soluble human ZG16P in already injured human tissue. Measure viable bacterial exclusion and epithelial permeability, then compare the tethered form only if soluble exposure limits function. Earlier disclosures cover rectal and gel formulations.

[Mucus rescue](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5137749/) · [Human ZG16P labeling](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10235990/) · [Human protein binding](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12167852/) · [Matrix study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12826806/)

## API5 protection of intestinal Paneth cells

Recombinant parent API5 protected Paneth cells and organoids in work that included four human lines homozygous for an ATG16L1 risk variant and four non-risk lines. Mouse treatment started with injury induction. NYU subsequently described development of minimal fragments.

**Next comparison.** Identify the fragment and property that NYU is developing. Compare it with parent protein in independent donors after injury has begun, and measure epithelial function along with cell survival. A collaborative study requires access to the current fragment.

[Organoid study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9720609/) · [NYU development listing](https://license.tov.med.nyu.edu/product/apoptosis-inhibitor-5-api5-based-therapeutic-for-the-treatment-of-inflammatory-bowel-diseases)

## DNase I and elafin for airway mucus transport

Bovine DNase I increased elastase activity in human cystic fibrosis sputum. When that sputum was instilled into mice it caused lung hemorrhage, which an elastase inhibitor suppressed. DNase worsened outcomes in a trial in non-CF bronchiectasis. Other sputum experiments showed that elastase inhibition can delay DNA-dependent mucus solubilization.

**Next comparison.** Compare DNase I, cleavage-resistant GG-elafin and the unlinked mixture in disease-specific sputum. Measure net transport and epithelial injury together. A fused construct would need a demonstrable advantage over the mixture and compatible rights to both components.

[Bronchiectasis trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9596315/) · [Sputum injury study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9476859/) · [Elastase and mucus processing](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3235130/)

## TAY-R1 readthrough for nonsense COL7A1 variants

The sponsor describes restoration of functional collagen VII in patient fibroblasts by its readthrough program for recessive dystrophic epidermolysis bullosa. The public description leaves compound identity, variant-specific effects and mechanical skin strength unresolved.

**Next comparison.** Use the sponsor's partnership route to establish the exact compound and data package. Compare skin strength across relevant genotypes with a readthrough control and assess what systemic treatment adds to available wound-directed therapies.

[Sponsor pipeline](https://taytherapeutics.com/pipeline/) · [Sponsor science](https://taytherapeutics.com/science/)

## Surface-applied SYM2081 for skin inflammation

SYM2081 altered mast-cell degranulation in human skin explants immersed in drug solution. Mouse studies used preventive or concurrent treatment. The compound acts at GluK1 as well as GluK2, which matters when attributing a skin response to one receptor.

**Next comparison.** Apply the formulation to the skin surface and measure dermal exposure and functional response across donors with established inflammation. Compare with the relevant topical treatment. Pitt lists technology 06272 for licensing.

[Skin and explant study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12087757/) · [Receptor pharmacology](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9580595/) · [Pitt technology](https://inventions.pitt.edu/technologies/novel-glutamate-receptor--06272)

## Retaining IL-1 receptor antagonist in injured cartilage

The cationic catIL-1RA fusion protected bovine cartilage and produced joint-associated immunoassay signal. A small conference study reported delayed analgesia in rats. Joint signal can include inactive fragments, and broader osteoarthritis trials of IL-1 blockers showed limited or absent clinical benefit.

**Next comparison.** Compare the authenticated fusion with free antagonist at matched active exposure after joint injury. Quantify intact drug alongside pain or joint function. The investigators' existing stabilization work sets the relevant comparator.

[Fusion and cartilage study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13266973/) · [Rat pain abstract](https://www.ors.org/wp-content/uploads/AM26/3.pdf)

## Local Hedgehog signaling for tissue repair

TI23 is a PTCH1 nanobody partial agonist. Experiments showed Hedgehog pathway activation in human cells and skin and tongue responses in mice. Experiments restoring diabetic skeletal repair used native Hedgehog proteins.

**Next comparison.** Select a specific local repair endpoint and compare TI23 with an existing ligand at measured exposure. Stanford lists the technology and prior disclosures include targeting formats. A defined therapeutic window would add evidence beyond pathway activation.

[TI23 study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7682405/) · [Stanford technology](https://techfinder.stanford.edu/technology/potent-patched1-ptch1-nanobody-ti23-activation-hedgehog-signaling-pathway) · [Diabetic niche study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5725192/)

## AKT inhibition for Proteus syndrome

Miransertib (MK-7075, ARQ092) rescued prenatal lethality in a mouse model of pathological AKT signaling. The phase 2 record inspected in October 2026 listed 38 participants across cohorts and had yet to post results. Prenatal survival and treatment of established overgrowth are separate questions.

**Next comparison.** With the clinical and compound custodians, identify a postnatal model in which an existing lesion and a relevant functional endpoint can be followed. Compare achievable exposure and response with the current regimen. Merck and NHGRI are separate material and data contacts.

[Prenatal mouse study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42788321/) · [NIH clinical registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04316546)
