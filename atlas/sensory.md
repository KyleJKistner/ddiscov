## NPHP5 replacement for inherited retinal degeneration

The sponsor reports that the clinical-intended ssAAV2/5-GRK1-hNPHP5-WPRE vector produced functional responses of differing durability in six affected dogs. A peer-reviewed study measured a cortical response to light in one midstage dog. Its overlap with the six is unresolved. Earlier vector designs also restored retinal electrical responses and visually guided behavior.

**Next comparison.** Penn lists co-development and licensing. A useful first decision is whether the consortium has a specific responsibility to transfer, along with the vector, data and rights needed to carry it out. The existing team already covers several manufacturing and regulatory milestones.

[Sponsor dossier](https://fnih.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/NPHP5-RPDD-Request_Redacted-MW_Redacted-Disclaimer.pdf) · [Canine cortical response](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10807495/) · [Penn technology](https://upenn.technologypublisher.com/technology/38439)

## Splice correction for USH1C retinal disease

An April 2026 conference abstract describes screening about 100 antisense oligonucleotides. Juvenile intravitreal treatment with Combo-29, which combines MOE-29 and MO-29, reportedly improved retinal electrical responses and full-length transcripts through 12 months. The abstract omits group sizes and effect estimates. Published human organoids carry different mutations.

**Next comparison.** Obtain the exact reagents and screen data from the participating groups. Compare the combination with its components in a human c.216G>A retinal model and relate splice correction to photoreceptor function.

[2026 research abstract](https://faculty.lsu.edu/sfnbr/sfnbtr_2026_program.pdf) · [Human retinal organoid study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00018-026-06321-y)

## PCDH15 replacement for Usher 1F retinal disease

Full-length dual-vector delivery improved retinal electrical responses in mice treated early. Treatment at postnatal day 30 produced no significant improvement. Primate expression was measured in three regions of one treated animal. Mouse hearing rescue with mini-PCDH15 and developmental zebrafish retinal rescue with mini-pcdh15b concern different constructs and functions.

**Next comparison.** Compare the chosen human retinal construct with the current full-length benchmark in an established disease model. Qualify the custodians and permitted uses for the exact vectors. Published research plasmids carry academic or nonprofit restrictions.

[Mouse retinal study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10727994/) · [Dual-vector study](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/177700) · [Zebrafish retinal preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.05.686814v1) · [Mini-PCDH15 hearing study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-38038-y) · [Research plasmid terms](https://www.addgene.org/199184/)

## Ocular halofantrine for inherited retinal degeneration

A 2026 preprint reported visual-tracking and retinal electrical responses after ocular injection of halofantrine in rodents. Organoids from one affected donor showed changes in staining and ciliary morphology. The dose used for efficacy contained four times the amount used for the exposure study. Eye drops remain a proposed route.

**Next comparison.** Measure retinal function and ocular and plasma exposure on the same regimen. The oral product has a known cardiac hazard. NIH lists a route to request development materials and data for a delivery comparison.

[Retinal preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13193134/) · [NIH development listing](https://www.techtransfer.nih.gov/news/therapeutic-to-treat-inherited-retinal-disorders-and-age-related-macular-degeneration) · [Oral safety label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2002/20250s7s8lbl.pdf)

## Local NT-3 and rolipram for cochlear synaptic injury

Local neurotrophin-3 and rolipram each have separate animal studies reporting cochlear repair. The proposed poloxamer P407 depot would release both agents. A biphasic response to cyclic AMP raises the possibility that one agent could interfere with the other.

**Next comparison.** Measure local exposure and compare auditory-nerve responses and hair-cell function against each optimized single treatment. Any transferable result would concern a specific dose and release schedule in a field with existing otic formulations.

[NT-3 study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4842978/) · [Rolipram study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cellular-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fncel.2024.1363219/full) · [Cyclic AMP dose response](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3277666/)
