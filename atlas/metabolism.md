## Mitochondrial nitrogen disposal in methylmalonic acidemia

Liver expression of SuperSIRT5 improved blood ammonia and body-weight measures in a small mouse study. Methylmalonic acid remained elevated. A separate TAT/mitochondrial-targeting construct delivered a different cargo, MCM, to mitochondrial fractions.

**Next comparison.** Test whether the targeting format delivers active SuperSIRT5 into the matrix of disease-relevant hepatic cells and improves nitrogen-to-urea flux. The enzyme and its prospective delivery platform require compatible material and license terms.

## Hepatic sulfide handling in ethylmalonic encephalopathy

AAV2/8-TBG-h.ETHE1-HA improved survival and function in Ethe1-null mice that received N-acetylcysteine around treatment. Durability varied. Human liver transplantation supplies a distinct observation of systemic benefit alongside persistent neurological injury.

**Next comparison.** Recover the exact research construct and model with the historical collaborators. Compare a proposed delivery improvement with that reference under matched supportive treatment, measuring both sustained function and sulfide handling.

## Thymidine phosphorylase replacement for MNGIE

One explanted patient liver provided paired treated and control lobes in a 2025 study. AAV-mediated expression improved perfusate nucleoside clearance. A separate mRNA–lipid nanoparticle preprint reported biochemical rescue and transient leukopenia.

**Next comparison.** Choose the exact AAV or mRNA reference, then connect sustained nucleoside control to disease-relevant function and tolerability. Explant access, vector materials and mRNA program rights have different custodians.

## Gut microbial trimethylamine production in trimethylaminuria

FCAR (AL0015) lowered trimethylamine in a slurry from one healthy donor. In mice reconstituted with human microbiota and lacking Fmo3, the carnitine-plus-FCAR group had a lower plotted mean than carnitine alone. The figure gives no significance test for that paired treatment comparison. The study also measured systemic exposure.

**Next comparison.** Compare authenticated FCAR across multiple donors and dietary precursors with matched substrate controls. Keep the FMC and IMC chemistries separate. Alfasigma is the published FCAR applicant.

## Sulfite scavenging in SUOX deficiency

In Suox-knockout mice, oxidized glutathione (GSSG) and glutathione trisulfide (GSSSG) extended lifespan by roughly two to three days. Urinary sulfite and thiosulfate remained unchanged with GSSG and increased with GSSSG. The added sulfur load may matter to the choice of scavenger.

**Next comparison.** A sulfur-free molecule is a proposed successor. Compare its reaction products, toxin exposure and neuronal function with the published compounds. The disclosed commercial connection identifies provenance, with exact material and transfer rights still to establish.

[Suox-knockout study](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/181299)

## Pantothenate kinase activation and coenzyme A metabolism

BBP-671, PZ-2891 and PZ-3022 are distinct molecules. A sponsor letter distinguished recruitment closure in propionic and methylmalonic acidemia from the exposure and safety window encountered in PKAN. NIH awards fund successor chemistry addressing variable human exposure to BBP-671.

## MAP3K15 and glucose regulation

People carrying protective MAP3K15 truncations have lower diabetes susceptibility. Kidney studies implicate ASK3 in salt handling and blood-pressure regulation. Lifelong truncation, removal of the protein and adult catalytic inhibition affect different features of the target.

**Next comparison.** Compare protective truncations, protein lowering and a kinase-dead reference in adult human islet and renal models. Pair insulin secretion with renal ion handling. Research reagents and the intended activity, compound and territory require their own permissions.

## ZnT8 and regulated insulin secretion

Human SLC30A8 loss-of-function genetics supports protection against diabetes. Perturbation of cell lines, anisomycin treatment in human islets and antibody protection of grafts provide different kinds of experimental evidence. An islet survival readout alone cannot resolve dynamic secretion.

**Next comparison.** Compare transport-inactive ZnT8, depletion and the chosen pharmacological reagent in mature human donor islets. Measure insulin release and shutoff as glucose changes, with viability in the same preparations. Antibody, RNA and small-molecule routes have separate materials and rights.
