## Surface complement regulation in PNH red cells

Reinserting native decay-accelerating factor (DAF/CD55) restored proximal complement regulation in patient red cells. Severely affected type III cells still lacked terminal protection. The proposed fusion joins monovalent IH4 to mature human DAF residues 1–249. IH4 also binds healthy red cells.

**Next comparison.** Compare survival of affected and normal cells under complement challenge, dilution and flow. Check mechanics and macrophage uptake at the same coverage. IH4 has granted fusion claims and a historical license whose present scope requires confirmation.

[Patient cell study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC397690/) · [Terminal complement limitation](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/113043) · [IH4 patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9879090B2/en)

## Soluble tissue factor and thrombotic activity

NIH names humanized antibodies 58B3 and 56E5 in its licensing notice. Genetic prevention and ex vivo depletion with polyclonal antibodies support a role for soluble tissue factor in thrombosis. Those interventions have different identities from either humanized clone.

**Next comparison.** Request exact-clone materials and data. Compare antithrombotic activity with preservation of normal hemostasis at matched exposure. NIH's co-development route provides a place to establish who would use the resulting comparison.

[NIH licensing notice](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-16440.pdf) · [Mechanism study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12589612/)

## ADAMTS7 selectivity in vascular remodeling

Human genetics implicates ADAMTS7 in coronary disease. BAY-9835 inhibits both ADAMTS7 and ADAMTS12, and double-knockout mice developed valve pathology. The effects of a selective intervention begun in adulthood require a separate comparison.

**Next comparison.** Compare selectivity and vascular function against BAY-9835 and the published compound 50 series while measuring valve effects. Research probe access and the rights to develop an analogue require separate arrangements.

[BAY-9835 chemistry](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10895658/) · [Valve study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11925103/) · [Human variant study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3591856/)

## TNAP inhibition for pseudoxanthoma elasticum

DS-1211 prevented ectopic mineralization in mouse studies. The 65-participant phase 2 study of DS-1211b examined safety, pharmacodynamics and exposure. The measured plasma analyte was DS-1211a. The inhibitor SBI-425 belongs to a separate chemical family. Daiichi's 2025 discontinuation disclosure cited timing and competitiveness.

**Next comparison.** A differentiated product would need a defined improvement in exposure, tissue distribution or selectivity. Compare mineralization and chronic bone effects at matched exposures. Daiichi and Sanford Burnham Prebys are routes to clarify materials and rights.
