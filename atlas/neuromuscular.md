## Splice correction and force retention in dysferlinopathy

DYSF exon-27 splice-correcting PMOs restored the reading frame and membrane resealing after laser injury in patient cells. DG9 improved delivery with a different PMO in another dystrophy. Dysferlin experiments show that resealing can improve while contraction-induced degeneration persists.

**Next comparison.** Test a DG9–DYSF2 conjugate in genotype-matched mature muscle and compare it with its separate components. Measure force retention after mechanical injury along with splice correction. DG9 has a licensing route, and DYSF antisense rights need separate review.

[DYSF splice study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11834094/) · [DG9 delivery](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-59494-8) · [Resealing and degeneration](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0038036)

## BAG3-mediated protein quality control in myotilinopathy

AAVrh74.tMCK.hBAG3 improved functional and histological measures in male TgT57I MYOT transgenic mice treated at four to six weeks, after early aggregate pathology. Adult established disease and patient-derived muscle represent different stages and systems.

**Next comparison.** Compare BAG3-mediated repair with mutant-protein suppression in a human MYOT model. Pair force with chronic protein quality-control measures, and establish vector, model and improvement rights for the exact study.

[MYOT mouse study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13148945/)

## Gigaxonin replacement for giant axonal neuropathy

Intrathecal scAAV9/JeT-GAN was tested in 14 children. One of four dose cohorts met the prespecified probability criterion for motor benefit without a correction for multiple cohort comparisons. Motor nerve electrophysiology did not recover. A later vagus-nerve study uses a different route.

**Next comparison.** Identify a specific delivery or peripheral-nerve coverage question with the current program. Taysha reported a rights reversion and initiation of material return to Hannah's Hope Fund. The exact transferred assets and separate platform terms require confirmation.

[Clinical trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11973737/) · [Intraneural vagus extension](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07543991) · [Rights disclosure](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1806310/000095017025027348/tsha-20241231.htm)

## A2A receptor modulation for ADCY5-associated movement disorder

Istradefylline improved motor and signaling phenotypes in an Adcy5 mouse model. The paper's randomized experiment in two people tested caffeine. An adult with R418W improved on istradefylline, while an adolescent with the same variant had no improvement after six months.

**Next comparison.** Define the compound and variant before comparing istradefylline with caffeine or theophylline on a prospective movement endpoint. Model, drug-supply and indication rights involve different parties.

[Variant mouse and caffeine study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12876317/) · [Adult case](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7533992/) · [Adolescent case](https://doi.org/10.1002/mdc3.70572)

## Antibody protection against gelsolin fragment aggregation

The 2024 Fabs reduced aggregation in vitro. A distinct 2014 bispecific nanobody reduced burden and improved selected muscle physiology with early treatment in mice. An ER-directed AAV nanobody is another design. A 2025 Fab20-derived IgG series already addresses biophysical optimization.

**Next comparison.** Authenticate the sequence, lot and format, including a reported Leu/Pro discrepancy. Compare tissue protection against the current IgG or Fab on a functional endpoint. The UGent/VUB and Orion/Turku materials have separate provenance and permission requirements.
