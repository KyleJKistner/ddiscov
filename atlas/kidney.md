# Kidney filtration and urinary solute handling

[Program atlas](README.md)

## Catalase and renal pressure regulation after ischemia

PEG-catalase restored pressure-dependent small-vessel responses in ischemically injured rat kidneys. Clone-62 anti-PECAM/catalase protected pig lung grafts when given before cardiac arrest. Human kidney targeting used a different antibody, carrier and cargo.

**Next comparison.** Compare the conjugate with free and PEG-catalase and a contemporary carrier in an already injured perfused kidney. Measure active enzyme in the relevant vascular compartment and sustained renal function. Prior antioxidant carriers make this a specific formulation and access question.

[Rat kidney function](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7988813/) · [Pig lung conjugate](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5505068/) · [Renal carrier comparator](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11751284/)

## Rebuilding the glomerular albumin barrier

MMP2/9 inhibition in diabetic mice restored glycocalyx measures and reduced albumin leakage. SDC4 was depleted in that model. SDC1 lipid particles restored function elsewhere, while a published SDC4 proteoliposome supported growth-factor signaling without directly fusing with cell membranes.

**Next comparison.** Test outward installation of full-length glycosylated SDC4 using the SDC1 lipid architecture. Compare albumin restriction and fluid filtration. TIMP3 protection is a separate follow-up variant. Relevant renal and proteoliposome rights depend on the chosen architecture.

[Diabetic kidney study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7184681/) · [SDC1 platform](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10643000/) · [SDC4 proteoliposomes](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3277125/)

## LCAT replacement and renal lipoprotein injury

MEDI6012 prevented renal lipoprotein X deposition and reduced albuminuria in small diet-provoked mouse experiments during injury induction. A single-person study with the related ACP-501 enzyme showed target engagement. The products differ in production process. Binding antibodies alone do not establish neutralization.

**Next comparison.** Secure reference enzyme, disease plasma and terms for the comparison. Test whether a specific stability or activity improvement reduces injury from lipoprotein X at a sustained exposure.

[MEDI6012 mouse study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6374542/) · [ACP-501 case](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4826469/) · [Antibody registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03578809)

## Cystine diamides for urinary crystal control

LH1753 prevented bladder stones in male Slc3a1-knockout mice, and sparse late urine samples contained an active metabolite. LH708 is a separate diamide. The identity of the current company lead cannot be inferred from the LH1753 paper.

**Next comparison.** Authenticate parent and metabolite, then compare their urinary exposure and crystal inhibition with LH708 across relevant human urine conditions. A historical option expired, leaving the current commercial and material terms to be established separately.

[LH1753 mouse and metabolite study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11247636/)

## SGLT5, 1,5-anhydroglucitol and neutrophil function

Human variants and cellular transport assays link SGLT5 to renal 1,5-anhydroglucitol handling. Remogliflozin, dapagliflozin and empagliflozin already inhibit the transporter in the published assay. GSD1b and G6PC3 deficiency provide neutrophil disease contexts.

**Next comparison.** Qualify the isoform and tag used in the transport assay. Compare a candidate selective inhibitor with these gliflozins on renal exposure, metabolite lowering and neutrophil function. New starting chemistry and associated rights require identification.

[Human transport study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10439028/) · [G6PC3 case study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35506446/)
