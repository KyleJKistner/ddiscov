## Mandate and source boundary

Maximize financial value retained from the marginal dollar across drug-development stages, while favoring credible success and survivable downside. The desired outcome is a high probability of recovering invested cash with a return. A useful experiment, an increased company valuation and cash distributed to the investor are different outcomes. No reviewed package currently establishes that recovery probability. Personal return targets, recovery deadline, reserve and affordable cumulative loss remain private inputs; this reconciliation does not invent them.

The workflow remains **screen broadly → compare explicit estimates → deepen a handful → narrow further → consider inquiries**. Missing offers widen uncertainty rather than preventing comparison. Budget flexibility expands the scenarios worth examining; it does not make additional deployment desirable by itself. The current action is desk research. Inquiries, purchases, clinical work and commitments remain later decisions.

## Bounded next outputs and stop rules

1. **Paired APR/erlotinib memo.** Compare exact article/population, recoverable records, rights and jurisdictions, developer overlap, plausible recruitable population, one residual bridge and why someone would pay for ddiscov's work. Separate existing data access from new human evidence. Finish with a retained-cash hypothesis, explicit low/base/high assumptions and the differences that could break the tie. Stop the public-source pass when a named nonpublic record or willingness-to-deal question is the remaining dependency; record it for later inquiry.
2. **CBD comparator.** Bound authentication, practical route, functional/exposure evidence, repeat-dose margin and relevant composition/field interest. Include the remaining development burden and a financing-unavailable outcome. Advance only if the exact package creates a plausible transferable benefit. Evidence that required article/rights or the practical route cannot fit a bounded package under defensible estimates moves it to reserve or a newly scoped comparison. Unverified access alone does not demote it; apply the same standard to the lead pair.
3. **Short insulin/AAT/verteporfin gap checks.** Identify the existing baseline, one unsolved customer/operator need, competing work and the smallest consequential deliverable. Compare with waiting for external evidence. Advance a candidate only when the proposed contribution is specific and its possible compensation is intelligible; otherwise retain a concise reopening condition instead of proposing generic formulation or replication work.

Every surviving package must show dated contributions and net investor receipts after upstream rights, operating costs, follow-on funding, sharing and any dilution. Start a self-funded new vehicle at 100% founder equity; model third-party obligations separately. Include negative, inconclusive, positive-without-financing and no-deal outcomes. Distinguish company proceeds, restricted grants and unspent capital from investor income. Accept the round when it identifies which differences change the research order and which unresolved facts control a later commitment—not when every dossier is longer.
