### Decision and evidence hygiene

- **New scientific linkage:** opposing C2β versus C3 genetic directions in *the same Mtm1-null disease context* changes the qualitative interpretation of an already known missing PIK3C3 curve. It **does not change a numeric potency or safety estimate**.
- **High-value falsifier:** a correctly measured and adequately engaged, C3-sparing BLU or independent C2β inhibitor fails to improve force in independent human tissue with a demonstrated delayed-rescue positive control. This lowers target priority. A BLU-specific failure with retained genetic rescue redirects chemistry instead.
- **Unavailable / not established:** physical BLU lot, transferred assay, human-muscle or vulnerable-tissue free PK, selectivity-reconciled C3 IC50, human efficacy, sponsor rights clearance and certified model readiness. Defined article, public synthesis, catalogue route, physical stock, model access, and commercial rights remain separate flags.
- **No-author-gate:** no correspondence or owner approval is required for this scientific screening decision; none was attempted.
- **Read precedence:** [current shortlist](../../../decisions/current-shortlist.md) > [selection policy](../../../decisions/selection-policy.md) > round-5 integrated reviews > historical reports. The [schema-5 cumulative register](../../portfolio-register.json) was read for coverage/shortlist context; **the canonical shortlist and register were not rewritten**, to avoid changing historical denominators or colliding with concurrent updates.
- **Scope:** this is original literature synthesis and proposed discriminating experiments, not newly obtained animal/human data, no purchase, no clinic action, and no experimental execution.

**Outcome:** useful new antitarget constraint and a verified status upgrade for a competitive program; **no defensible S-tier promotion or newly qualified replacement program** in this pass.
