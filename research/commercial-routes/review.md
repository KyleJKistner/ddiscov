# Independent review of the commercial-route comparison

Review date: 9 November 2026 UTC. Reviewer was not an author of the specialist route analyses. Scope: reasoning, traceability, current decision consistency and selected source checks; this is not an independent reanalysis of every clinical study, patent clearance, provider audit or transaction valuation.

## Review findings

The specialist analyses preserve the principal distinctions needed for this decision:

- A new founder-funded company starts with 100% founder equity. Ownership of an existing drug, license royalties and later financing dilution are separate questions.
- A sponsor's willingness to out-license supports an access prospect; it does not show that the sponsor would purchase ddiscov's work.
- Paid services, funded research, self-funded participation, a license and a prize have different cash directions and risks. A contract fee is not profit, grant funding is not automatically capital recovery, and a future milestone is not cash at signing.
- The earlier cost totals are analyst scenarios, not vendor-derived project prices. Neither geographic discounts nor a replacement all-in price is invented.
- Stronger human evidence does not establish an unoccupied commercial opportunity. Existing licensed assets and assigned research functions receive explicit attention.
- APR-246's limited human skin signal is retained. Its oncology failure and recent work on other candidates do not supply a new negative AEC result.

The comparison can support an order for resolving commercial uncertainties. It cannot yet support a quantitative investment ranking, a high probability of cash recovery, or an 80% failure estimate. Agreement among several reviewers is not empirical calibration.

## Selected independent source checks

- [Sol-Gel pipeline](https://www.sol-gel.com/pipeline/): confirms the SGT-210 platform, completed Phase I label and general out-licensing interest. It does not identify an SGT-210-specific offer, fee or open paid assignment.
- [NCI Start-Up 3.0](https://techtransfer.cancer.gov/partnering/licensing-agreements/startup-license): confirms the $5,000 execution royalty, two-year evaluation phase, patent-expense contribution, downstream royalties and applicant/technology conditions. The comparison correctly uses this as a structure example, not an offer for a reviewed candidate or the total cost of licensing.
- ReGen [priority rules](https://regendrugschallenge.org/challenge-priorities/), [submission details](https://regendrugschallenge.org/submission-details/) and [FAQ](https://regendrugschallenge.org/faqs/): confirm the new-formulation exclusion, costly-condition focus and need for clinical or systematically analyzed evidence. The wording does not justify treating every single-center clinical trial as categorically ineligible. None of the comparison's current reformulation packages becomes eligible simply because an oral generic exists.
