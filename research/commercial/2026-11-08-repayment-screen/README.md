# Commercial screen: development packages and investor cash recovery

**Review date:** 8 November 2026, America/Los_Angeles.

**Baseline:** [dc0cdcc](https://github.com/KyleJKistner/ddiscov/commit/dc0cdccfc6b9b4158094cb11d602ffb9cddf6028). Candidate evidence remains in its dated dossiers through Round 26. This pass verifies selected commercial/program records and prepares comparable transaction questions.

## Decision

**None of the five exact-use packages currently establishes a priced, executable transaction with a supportable high probability of returning investor principal plus a return.** The reviewed sources identify assets, counterparties and inquiry mechanisms. Actual payment obligations, complete costs, attainable interests, operator access and investor distribution terms remain unresolved.

The most specific first commercial-fit question is **topical erlotinib/KRT16 with Sol-Gel**: the company's October 2026 presentation expressly contemplates small feasibility studies in new indications. **AAT process work** supplies a second concrete route to test for commissioned work through relevant product/platform counterparties. These are priorities for obtaining consequential information, not financial investment rankings.

Prepare written scope and economic terms before committing to development. [Counterparty briefs](counterparty-briefs.md) are ready for review and remain unsent. The [structured comparison](opportunity-packages.json) contains five package records, one separate financing-sourcing route, 26 source records, access limitations and explicitly unknown commercial inputs.

**Sol-Gel:** Is there an unsatisfied KRT16-related development need, and would it support a defined contribution on commercial terms? Its public plans make this question specific. They establish neither interest in this indication nor willingness to pay. The December 17, 2025 Darier release is dated by the release body, not the later page chrome. [October presentation](https://solgel.gcs-web.com/static-files/37f518f0-a477-4a72-ae63-a6e36dbc1bbe), [Darier update](https://ir.sol-gel.com/node/9126).

**AAT:** Does a relevant product/platform party need a particular qualification result and want to commission it? The capable operator and clinical/regulatory sponsor must be identified on their actual roles. XVIVO's support route requires an institutional research agreement; CSL's global IIS terms restrict support to approved research. These facts support inquiries, not a margin or capital repayment. [XVIVO](https://www.xvivogroup.com/investigator-initiated-research/), [CSL commercial portal](https://csl.yet2.com/), [CSL IIS](https://www.csl.com/research-and-development/awards-grants-and-initiatives/investigator-initiated-studies).

**APR and enasidenib:** Resolve the actual existing-article/data contribution and the commercial reason to pay for it. Existing human evidence and reduced scientific uncertainty are useful inputs, but each still needs obtainable terms and a funded continuation. [Aprea filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1781983/000110465926122405/apre-20260930x10q.htm), [BMS research process](https://www.bms.com/research-and-development/independent-research.html).

**FD-2:** Establish a coherent joint-institution option/access proposition before costing a development commitment. The relevant next commercial outcome remains a contingent partner/license transaction.

## Additional sourcing route: revenue-supported development finance

Royalty and other contractual payment structures can fund drug development while tying investor proceeds to a defined revenue stream. [Royalty Pharma](https://www.royaltypharma.com/partners/types-of-partnerships/) describes existing and synthetic royalties and launch/development capital; [SWK's historical filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1089907/000162828025013989/swkh-20241231.htm) describes commercial-stage revenue financing. These establish mechanisms, not a particular accessible investment.

No appropriately sized, offered participation, exact product, seller, price or underwriting package was identified for ddiscov in this pass. Keep this as a **route-level sourcing question**, separate from the five exact-use proposals. An actual candidate would need verified receipts, senior claims, product concentration, competition, remaining economic life, obligor quality, enforceable rights, transferability, costs and investor distributions. Existing sales reduce some uncertainty while leaving credit, commercial, legal and liquidity risks to assess. [O01–O02 in the structured record](opportunity-packages.json).

## Quote-ready scope and decision gates

For each selected package, obtain a scope distinguishing:

1. The exact work and acceptance criteria, including an interpretable negative result.
2. Material/data access and all required background/foreground rights.
3. The qualified operator, sponsor responsibilities, third-party dependencies and schedule.
4. All-in costs, currency, overhead, payment timing, exclusions and plausible overruns.
5. Cancellation rights, liability, residual obligations and the genuine stopping point.
6. Customer payments versus restricted support, optional milestones and future financing.
7. The waterfall from project cash to investor cash, including later commitments and deductions.

Historical planning bands retain their original meaning. The APR band concerns an early article/evidence tranche; AAT's band concerns qualification and excludes later organ/transplant work. Neither supplies a current quote or a whole-program cost.

A commissioned contribution could fit a repayment-focused mandate if its collectible payments cover the cost and risk of delivery and leave investor value. This depends on an actual need, competent execution and workable terms. A self-funded option or scientific milestone earns its place through a separately underwritten continuation; a potential future license is contingent upside.

## Interpretation and next decision

The [selection policy](../../../decisions/selection-policy.md#apply-the-capital-recovery-requirement) defines the cash outcome. Personal budget and return inputs belong in the private commitment analysis. A high expected value, an approval rate, an industry transaction headline and the chance of one scientific win do not establish the probability of investor principal recovery.

The current output is a prepared comparison and a set of specific requests for missing terms. No offered investment, supplier quote, high-confidence repayment estimate, outreach, submission, experiment or financial commitment was created by this review.
