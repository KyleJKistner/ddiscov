# Ruxolitinib / HHD: the development proof worth funding next

**4 November 2026. Proposed research plan, not an executed study or prescribing protocol.** This plan integrates the [clinical audit](rux_hhd_clinical.md), [product/exposure assessment](rux_hhd_product.md) and independently reviewed source records. Its purpose is to turn encouraging human observations into a decision about a defined development program.

## Recommendation and intended claim

**Fund preparation and a bounded controlled clinical proof with the marketed 1.5% cream, if the project is intended to build a differentiated HHD clinical package.** The useful claim is control of painful established lesions and recurrent disease with acceptable treatment burden and exposure. A permanent drug-free cure is not required for that claim. A first experiment showing a calcium marker change is not an adequate substitute for the clinical question already supported by human cases.

The initial intended population is adults with confirmed HHD, persistent or recurrent assessable lesions despite documented conventional management, and a treatment area that can be measured and monitored. This population is a planning choice. The protocol must define diagnostic confirmation, infection handling, background therapy, age/severity bounds and exposure-related exclusions. Genetic status should be documented when available; histopathology/family history in a case report does not become individual sequence confirmation.

The project has two different funding decisions. The first is whether controlled evidence about this readily specified clinical article is worth creating: **yes, on scientific grounds**. The second is whether to finance a standalone proprietary product: **not yet supported by the present dossier**. Existing publications, a mature franchise and formulation claims make the second decision dependent on a real added product benefit and route to capture value.

## What is already sufficient to proceed

The clinical dossier distinguishes five exact-strength case reports with varying background treatments and follow-up. Some describe topical-only response or maintenance, while others involve dupilumab or oral upadacitinib. The nine-patient preprint remains abstract-only and is not a pooled response numerator. This is enough to justify a controlled question without pretending it already answers that question.

The exact marketed article is defined, and primary AD studies provide an extensive formulation/exposure reference. Clinical HHD exposure through eroded folds remains an uncertainty that can be measured during proof. There is no need to require independent synthesis, a new proprietary vehicle or a newly engineered organotypic model before asking whether the existing article provides useful control.

The most informative design warning comes from a different intervention, DERM-101/LX3305. Its primary HHD subgroup results report week-12 improvement of at least one severity grade in 5/10 active and 5/6 placebo participants, using fixed analysis denominators with unresolved visit-level missingness. This is neither a ruxolitinib failure nor a transferable estimate of placebo response. It demonstrates why uncontrolled improvement in a fluctuating disease can be highly misleading. [Clinical audit, DERM-101 section](rux_hhd_clinical.md)

## Stage A — make the comparison executable and interpretable

The funded output is a small, reviewable protocol and measurement package, together with article/accountability and exposure-assay specifications. Completion depends on deliverables, not on waiting for an author to declare the idea promising.

| Work package | Concrete output | Advance or redirect decision |
|---|---|---|
| Article and control | Marketed formulation/presentation/lot specification; storage and cream-mass accountability; either a qualified matched-vehicle route or an explicitly pragmatic control | If a matched vehicle is unavailable, use the prespecified whole-product add-on design. Do not label an unrelated moisturizer as matched. |
| Baseline and diagnosis | A consistent lesion map, diagnostic criteria, documented prior response and current therapies, infection assessment, and short repeated baseline observations compatible with care | If the lesion endpoint is unstable or unreliable, repair measurement or narrow the population before efficacy interpretation. No deliberate worsening is needed to qualify the assay. |
| Outcome measurement | Standardized photography with scale/site identity, blinded image review, reproducible erosion-area assessment, daily local pain/treatment burden and rescue recording | Establish inter-reader and repeat-measurement error; select a useful-effect threshold before treatment outcomes are unblinded. |
| Exposure and safety | A validated or fit-for-purpose plasma assay, sampling rationale, gram/BSA/erosion-state data and a label-informed clinical monitoring plan | Confirm that application and exposure can be linked. Failure to measure exposure is a protocol limitation, not proof of an unsafe product. |
| Regulatory and operational scope | Sponsor responsibilities, protocol/statistical analysis plan, applicable IND determination, ethics/consent and data handling, site capacity and itemized cost estimates | A new-indication development study must not assume the marketed-drug IND exemption. This report authorizes no clinical execution. |
| Product contribution | One intended benefit beyond unsystematic off-label use, plus a specific data/formulation/regulatory ownership plan | If the output cannot support the intended commercial objective, classify the study as clinical evidence generation and do not disguise it as a proprietary asset investment. |

A clinician/site team is an execution requirement for a clinical study. That fact is different from requiring an inventor's or owner's opinion to decide scientific priority. This sprint sends no messages or procurement requests; the public evidence already supports the finite scientific recommendation.

## Stage B — controlled established-lesion proof

### Preferred first design

Use a randomized **parallel patient-level comparison** of exact marketed cream plus standardized background care versus the same background care, with masked central assessment, unless a qualified matched vehicle supports full blinding. A planned 8–12-week controlled observation period is a design starting point, not a validated HHD endpoint duration or a regulatory requirement. The final duration should capture both healing and recurrent disease under the intended regimen.

A paired-lesion study can be efficient for an initial local-response feasibility question when comparable lesions exist. It needs simultaneous allocation, site-specific pain/area outcomes, handling of cream transfer and systemic exposure, and analysis that retains the patient as the independent unit. A whole-patient quality-of-life score cannot be independently assigned to each treated fold. Multiple lesions, photographs and visits do not multiply the randomized sample size.

A crossover design is less attractive until recurrence, carryover and exposure kinetics in HHD are understood. The long and variable apparent topical terminal half-life from AD is not a validated HHD washout rule. Do not deliberately withdraw a useful treatment solely to produce a “durable cure” narrative. A subsequent maintenance phase can measure recurrence and burden under an explicit ongoing or intermittent treatment policy.

### Endpoint hierarchy

Specify one primary clinical estimand. A practical exploratory choice is **blinded change in the area of a prespecified active erosive lesion**, with repeated measurements over the controlled period and baseline adjustment. It needs concordant evidence on pain, closure, rescue treatment and recurrence to support a useful product claim. If the intended claim concerns whole-patient recurrent burden, use a patient-level measure of days with painful active disease instead, after qualifying its reliability. These are alternatives to choose prospectively, not a menu from which to select the best result afterward.

Report actual lesion area and its uncertainty, standardized closure criteria, patient-reported local pain, rescue use and treatment burden. Keep treatment interruptions, infection treatment, background changes and withdrawals in the analysis. DLQI can characterize whole-patient impact, but one historical baseline score or a published case narrative is not a longitudinal treatment-effect estimate. Do not import an AD EASI/IGA response threshold or minimum important difference as if it were validated for HHD.

Supportive secondary outcomes should make the chosen primary benefit clinically credible; separate superiority on every measure is not required. Prespecify how rescue therapy, infection treatment, background changes and missing assessments enter the primary estimand, so healing after rescue is not silently attributed to the cream. Report all initially randomized participants and, if maintenance entry selects responders, the selected maintenance denominator and its restricted interpretation.

The comparison should retain routine measures to reduce sweat/friction and manage infection consistently across groups. If prior effective care is continued, record it and estimate the incremental effect. Specify permitted background care in the protocol. The historical dupilumab and oral-JAK combinations do not automatically become acceptable trial regimens: the current label does not recommend these combinations, and any proposed exception requires its own explicit protocol risk rationale and appropriate clinical oversight. If the future product claims to replace burdensome systemic therapy, that later comparison needs the relevant therapy and its risks in the design. A favorable vehicle comparison alone cannot establish superiority to all available care.

### Exposure and biological interpretation

Record grams applied, treated BSA, erosion/maceration state, anatomical location and actual regimen at every informative visit. Sample systemic exposure in a prespecified early/active-lesion state and after improvement, with timing relative to application retained. Preserve participant-level outliers rather than relying on cohort means. The 281 nM whole-blood laboratory reference is not an automatic exclusion, stopping or efficacy boundary.

The proof can support clinical anti-inflammatory control without demonstrating direct correction of ATP2C1. If clinical benefit is accompanied by uncertain molecular changes, advance the supported clinical claim. If inflammation improves but pain, erosions and burden do not, that is inadequate evidence for the proposed product benefit.

### Sample size and uncertainty

The five heterogeneous case reports do not provide a defensible response probability or endpoint variance for powering a definitive trial. The DERM-101 subgroup is too small and uses a different article and endpoint. No invented sample size, power or trial budget is presented as validated.

Stage A should produce a preliminary variance/reliability estimate and a prespecified minimum useful difference. Size Stage B around the precision required to distinguish useful benefit from a clinically trivial effect, accounting for patient-level clustering, expected withdrawals and analysis choice. If only a small exploratory study is feasible, state that it is for signal and variance estimation. An imprecise confidence interval crossing the useful threshold is inconclusive, not established futility; a positive case-style point estimate with wide uncertainty is not confirmatory success.

## Stage C — confirmation and product differentiation

An interpretable exploratory signal earns an independently conducted confirmatory comparison in the population and regimen supported by Stage B. Confirm duration of control, exposure through active versus healed skin, treatment burden and clinically relevant safety. Expand disease extent or age only with an appropriate exposure and risk rationale; an adult limited-area study does not establish a pediatric or extensive-disease product.

Develop a new formulation only if the existing article exposes a specific limitation—irritation, delivery, absorption, application burden or another meaningful deficiency—and the proposed change is likely to solve it. Preserve or bridge the benefit of the exact article before claiming a better product. A lower-cost aspiration is not a demonstrated manufacturing, reimbursement or rights advantage.

The business gate is explicit: identify the intended filing and ownership of the resulting data, the claim space and manufacturing route, and why the product would be used relative to branded off-label care, future competition and alternatives such as systemic therapy, procedures or gene-based development. A 505(b)(2) concept or possible limited regulatory exclusivity is not a completed commercial strategy.

## Optional mechanism module

A mechanism experiment earns funding when its result changes article selection, population, combination or exposure. A compact initial design can compare a qualified ATP2C1-heterozygous background with its control, with and without a relevant inflammatory challenge, then test measured ruxolitinib engagement alongside mechanical cohesion/permeability. One qualified background can support exploration; confirmation needs biological independence and an appropriate variant scope.

Use an authenticated cell identity and do not substitute Ker-CT for N/TERT without acknowledging the change. Include viability and healthy-control function. SPCA1 correction before development is a causal control, not proof of delayed mature-barrier repair. CDN1163 is not an established complete-barrier rescue control on the reviewed final-paper evidence. Failure in an immune-free assay cannot rule out an inflammation-mediated clinical effect.

## Advance, redirect and stop rules

**Advance** when the controlled result shows a reproducible useful clinical improvement, or comparable control with a meaningful burden/tolerability advantage, with interpretable exposure and acceptable observed safety. Early exploratory evidence can justify confirmation before a definitive estimate is available.

**Redirect** when delivery, measurement, population or regimen prevents interpretation, or when the existing formulation's limitation points to a specific better article. A paired-lesion feasibility failure does not automatically establish patient-level clinical failure. A model without the intended inflammatory context cannot reject that mechanism.

**Stop the proposed claim** when a sufficiently informative comparison rules out the prespecified meaningful benefit, when clinically necessary exposure reproducibly produces unacceptable harm, or when the effect is entirely explained by background/rescue treatment and cannot be demonstrated with the product. Retain narrower useful claims only when the evidence actually supports them.

**Stop or defer the standalone asset thesis** when no differentiated benefit or workable ownership/manufacturing/regulatory route can be articulated. This need not invalidate an academic or sponsor-led clinical research project. Keep the user's development objective visible when choosing which type of project deserves resources.

## Cost and execution boundary

The budgetable units are protocol/site preparation, diagnostic review, article/control supply and accountability, image assessment, clinical visits, plasma sampling/analysis, safety laboratories, data management/statistics and follow-up. A new vehicle or organotypic model adds separate CMC or assay-development cost and should be conditional. Multisite recruitment and rare-disease ascertainment may dominate elapsed time even though the marketed article removes a synthesis step.

No supplier quote, confirmed eligible patient pool, site commitment or approved budget was obtained. A precise dollar or completion-date claim would therefore be invented. Funding or executing it would require an actual sponsor and clinical infrastructure; none is represented as secured here.
