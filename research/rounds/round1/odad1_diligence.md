# ODAD1 and API5 diligence: what changes the investment decision

> **Historical review snapshot.** Later findings and rankings are reconciled in the [current shortlist](../../../decisions/current-shortlist.md) and [round 4 review](../round4/README.md). Statements about activity or access describe this review period.

Prepared for the ddiscov process audit, November 3, 2026 (US Pacific). Repository snapshot: `c241dc4c6cfa7cd5312a7237dda61a4d2af50c97`. This is an intermediate research memo. No owner, investigator, bank, or licensor has been contacted. Public sources establish evidence and counterparties; they do not establish current commercial access or exclusive rights.

## Decision

**ODAD1 is a scientifically credible research program, but the public evidence does not identify a funding-ready therapeutic asset.** The repository already flags the most important translation gaps: replacement before differentiation, rescue in nonfounder genotypes, local ciliary motion versus directional mucus transport, limited independent tissue, and delivery/IP dependencies. The main correction is therefore to enforce those dependencies in the ranking. An A-tier scientific hypothesis should not be presented as an A-tier executable asset.

**API5 has a more concrete owner-led transaction route, but the proposed optimization work substantially overlaps the owner's publicly described program.** NYU's live listing describes a synthetic peptide opportunity and ongoing work on a minimal functional fragment, efficacy in other IBD subtypes, and basic pharmacokinetics and toxicology. It does not disclose completion of that work or identify a publicly validated optimized lead. A credible proposal requires an option or license to a defined peptide, supporting data and know-how, followed by a milestone agreed with the owner. Generic fragment optimization plus PK/tox is not a demonstrated independent contribution.

## 1. What the repository already gets right

The current `decisions/README.md` explicitly says that ODAD1 replacement was performed before differentiation, describes approximately 6.5–7.5 Hz beating against healthy controls near 10 Hz, and calls the published clips local motion. Its proposed experiment concerns mature founder-variant and early-stop airways and directional mucus transport. It requires confirmation of tissue availability, another independent founder donor, delivery rights, and data terms, and acknowledges a prior inhaled RNA disclosure naming ODAD1.

`research/screen-index.md` identifies the organoid study as involving two other genotypes. `research/README.md` again identifies the published rescue as occurring in two nonfounder genotypes before differentiation. These are not newly discovered omissions. The diligence below adds specific competitive, nomenclature, ownership, and product-definition issues and explains why the existing cautions must become hard advancement gates.

The approximate beat-frequency numbers above are repository statements, not independently extracted numerical tables in this audit. They should not be repeated as independently verified measurements. Field-level or organoid-level sample sizes also must not be relabeled as independent patient counts.

## 2. ODAD1: the demonstrated biology and the proposed product differ

### 2.1 The 2026 rescue paper supports a mechanism

The primary study is *Loss-of-function variants in ODAD1 disrupt ODA docking and induce actin cytoskeletal remodeling in primary ciliary dyskinesia*, Cell Discovery 12:25, published March 31, 2026. The paper reports seven unrelated Han Chinese patients, including homozygous `c.705_706insGCAG` and `c.-41-2A>C` genotypes. Full-length wild-type ODAD1 delivered by lentivirus restored ciliary activity and outer dynein arm localization in patient-derived organoid models. [S1]

The decisive methods statement is that patient basal cells were transduced, assessed after 72 hours, and subsequently transitioned into apical-out organoid culture to differentiate. The result therefore demonstrates correction during formation of a differentiated airway model. It does not demonstrate that an administered product reaches established ciliated airway tissue in vivo, enters the relevant cells, achieves sufficient coverage, and restores directional mucus clearance. [S1]

This distinction changes what can be financed. The published molecular rescue can support target validity and an experiment. It cannot substitute for a defined delivery product. An investor would need to know whether the proposed asset is an inhaled transient mRNA, an integrating vector, an edited basal-cell product, or another construct; each brings different manufacturing, exposure, durability, safety and IP dependencies.

### 2.2 Avoid donor and endpoint inflation

Seven patients in the clinical cohort does not establish seven independent patient rescue experiments. The accessible figures include multiple measurements and fields; for one structural comparison there were two healthy donors, two insertion-genotype donors and two splice-genotype donors, with many field observations. Exact independent donor counts for every rescue result remain unresolved in the retrieved material. A final investment package should obtain the supplement and source data and identify each independent donor, genotype, assay, replicate and treatment arm. [S1]

Ciliary beat frequency and a local motion video are intermediate endpoints. Directional transport, transport persistence and effective coverage are closer to the intended clearance mechanism. A faster but uncoordinated patch can fail to produce a clinically useful transport result. This is a mechanistic inference, not a claim that the paper showed failure.

The study also reports that cytochalasin B partially improved multiciliogenesis without restoring ciliary motility. This is further evidence that increasing ciliated-cell formation and restoring the missing motor/docking function should not be conflated. [S1]

### 2.3 Founder genotype evidence is separate

The 2023 Nature structural study, *Axonemal structures reveal mechanoregulatory and disease mechanisms*, included one patient per studied genotype. Its ODAD1 cases included a homozygous splice-associated `c.853G>A` variant and a homozygous `c.448C>T`, `p.Arg150*` early stop. Those data establish structural and functional disease phenotypes; they are not therapeutic rescue data. [S2]

**Variant normalization is a practical search correction.** The Dutch founder variant historically described as `c.742G>A`, `p.Ala248Thr` is represented on the current MANE transcript as `NM_001364171.2:c.853G>A`, `p.Ala285Thr`. Searches restricted to the legacy or current notation can miss relevant models, patents and active projects. Store gene aliases (ODAD1/CCDC114), transcript accession and version, and both legacy/current variant names in the candidate record. [S3]

The splice-associated founder variant has been linked to abnormal splicing and premature termination; the structural study treats the relevant variants as demonstrated or predicted null alleles through frameshift/premature termination and nonsense-mediated decay. This does not justify assuming a pathogenic dominant-negative protein that must be suppressed. A failure of replacement cannot by itself establish that hypothesis: delivery, expression, localization and corrected-cell coverage must first be demonstrated. The repository's contingent question about additional suppression should remain a question, not the basis of a differentiated therapeutic claim. [S2, S3]

## 3. Material new competitive evidence: UCL is already pursuing the founder variant

UCL publicly advertises *Stem cell models to evaluate novel therapeutics for airway ciliopathies*, supervised by Hannah Mitchison. It explicitly proposes adenine base editing for the recurrent `CCDC114/ODAD1 c.742G>A` variant and says affected-patient airway cultures have already been characterized. Its plan includes model development, editing and off-target assessment, followed by assessment of delivery across cell stages and treatment conditions. The project is linked to the UCL Living Airway Biobank and clinical network. [S4]

This is directly relevant to the repository's founder-variant opportunity. The founder genotype and the research question are not unattended. It creates two possible interpretations that need to be resolved with the institution: a partner with relevant models and an adjacent therapeutic program, or a competing owner of necessary starting material and know-how.

The listing is a project plan, not evidence of a successful editing product, a clinical program, a granted patent, or exclusivity. It also does not establish that the tissue is unavailable. The justified correction is narrower: **downgrade any whitespace assumption and move UCL's program and rights relationship into the critical path.** [S4]

## 4. Availability and freedom to operate require separate verification

### Tissue and data

UCL's Living Airway Biobank is a real institutional resource with an external-researcher access pathway and an NHS network. Public ethics records establish a governed tissue collection. They do not give a current ODAD1-specific inventory, the number of independent founder donors, willingness to replenish cultures, commercial-use consent, commercial MTA terms, or rights to resulting data and inventions. [S5]

The immediately useful availability request would identify the exact variant and transcript aliases, viable basal-cell aliquots and passage history, donor independence, commercial use, repeat supply, and rights to publish, use data in financing and regulatory packages, and own or license resulting inventions. Without those responses, the appropriate field is “public resource identified; access unconfirmed,” not “available.” No such request has been sent.

### Patents

Ethris's published family lead `WO2020165352A1`, *Treatment of ciliopathies*, names ODAD1 in a disclosure concerning protein replacement using RNA in respiratory disease. A gene appearing in a patent description does not establish an enforceable claim covering every gene-replacement strategy. Conversely, the displayed ceased status of a WO application does not establish that all national family claims are dead or that a product has freedom to operate. [S6]

A useful legal work product is a jurisdiction-specific claim chart against the actual proposed construct, RNA chemistry/vector, formulation, delivery route and indication. A target-level patent flag is only a diligence lead. This audit has not completed a national-family legal-status or claim-scope opinion.

### Competition

ReCode's public pipeline identifies RCT1100 as an inhaled DNAI1 mRNA program for PCD, with additional PCD work naming CCDC39, CCDC40 and DNAH5. This is relevant evidence that airway delivery and PCD are active competitive categories. It is not an ODAD1 therapeutic, and the absence of ODAD1 from a public pipeline cannot establish either whitespace or availability. [S7]

## 5. API5: a transaction hypothesis, not a generic optimization opportunity

### 5.1 What the current listing actually establishes

NYU listing `CAD01-05` identifies an API5-derived synthetic peptide opportunity for IBD and an active internal Therapeutics Alliances development program. Its stated work covers fragment refinement, extension to other IBD contexts, and early PK/toxicology. It also describes patent applications for recombinant API5 fragments in multiple jurisdictions. **The source describes work in progress; it does not establish that an optimized peptide, adequate exposure or a safety package is complete.** [S8]

The independent project cannot count those same activities as a novel gap without seeing the owner's current workplan. A sensible first financing use would be a staged option or collaboration that grants access to the exact lead, relevant full-length-versus-fragment comparisons, supporting records, and a defined right to commercialize. The incremental milestone must be agreed after assessing what NYU has already done and is already funding.

### 5.2 Published full-length evidence is not fragment validation

The primary Nature paper, *The γδ IEL effector API5 masks genetic susceptibility to Paneth cell death*, provides mechanistic and preclinical evidence that recombinant API5 can protect Paneth cells in genetically susceptible mouse and human organoid settings. The publication should be distinguished from the later synthetic-fragment development opportunity. A result with recombinant parent protein does not establish that an undisclosed peptide retains the same potency, selectivity, stability or exposure-response relationship. [S9]

The repository's proposed study of donor-specific epithelial recovery after injury could be useful if it tests a clinically relevant treatment timing and the actual licensed lead. Its novelty and economic value remain conditional: NYU may already have done it, may plan it, or may own the only relevant lead. The proposed experiment should therefore be a negotiated development milestone, not a substitute for establishing control of an asset.

### 5.3 What would upgrade API5

An executable API5 investment case would require, at minimum:

1. A disclosed lead sequence/construct and reproducible activity data for that same material, with clarity about parent protein versus peptide.
2. Written status of the exact background IP and material package, existing licenses, available fields/territories, exclusivity scope, and option/license economics.
3. Owner-approved allocation of the next experiments and resulting IP/data rights, so the new company funds a distinct value-creating step.
4. A plausible administered product and evidence of exposure at the relevant epithelial site.
5. A pre-specified therapeutic-timing test that distinguishes prevention/protection from restoration after established injury, across appropriate independent human donors.
6. A budget based on actual material, assay and vendor access rather than a generic planning estimate.

A live listing satisfies none of these completely. It does make a specific counterparty and negotiation route visible, which is more actionable than a target with no identifiable owner or access path.

## 6. Ranking and process changes justified by this audit

The process should keep three separate judgments: strength of biological evidence, feasibility of executing the next study, and control of a financeable asset. Do not combine them into an undifferentiated A-tier label. An unknown access or IP gate remains unpassed even when the mechanistic paper is strong.

For each high-priority candidate, define the asset as: **molecule/construct, formulation and route, patient population, intended treatment timing, endpoint, owner, and rights package.** Separate material access, commercial development rights, data rights, freedom to operate, and competitive differentiation. They answer different questions.

Recommended current status:

| Candidate | Scientific position | Executability/ownership position | Appropriate next decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| ODAD1 founder-airway replacement | Credible target; published rescue is in different genotypes before differentiation | Exact mature-airway product, independent tissue, delivery rights and UCL program relationship unresolved | Keep as partner-dependent research priority; do not call funding-ready |
| API5 peptide | Parent-protein evidence and identifiable owner-led development | Exact peptide and data package undisclosed; optimization overlaps owner; availability unconfirmed | Prioritize defined option/license diligence if owner terms and incremental milestone are attractive |

Neither can honestly be called “obviously fundable” on the current public record. Research can identify a credible conditional investment thesis; only owner responses, contractual terms and the relevant experiments can close the decisive remaining uncertainties.
