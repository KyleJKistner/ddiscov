## Decision
Keep HJ-02 in a short, gated licensing/replication queue. The evidence justifies a conversation with OHSU and a costed attempt to reproduce the functional signal. It does not justify describing a fundable company as already found. Relative to an occupied commercial program, HJ-02 has a clear owner and an explicit licensing invitation. Relative to a development-ready drug, it lacks a public transferable package demonstrating durable injured-control functional benefit, exposure, safety and an agreed route to exclusive commercial control.

Full molecular-target identification is not a prerequisite to financing a phenotypic small molecule. Its absence does, however, increase the cost/risk of making analogues, interpreting safety, choosing exposure biomarkers and distinguishing useful repair from a pleiotropic electrophile effect. Do not convert this concern into an automatic veto.

## Verified scientific facts, keeping experiments separate
### Sepe et al., JACC Basic to Translational Science 2022
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36317132/ (full primary paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9617125/; https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jacbts.2022.04.009).
HJ-01, HJ-02 and ISP were administered on days 3–10 after ischemia/reperfusion. Day-14 reinnervation and isoproterenol-provoked arrhythmias were assessed in five mice per group. Cardiac output and ejection fraction were assessed on day 15, five days after treatment ended. Figure 8 has n=5/group, sham n=4, and uses sham as the Dunnett reference for the two pump-function panels. Infarcts were smaller with HJ-01/HJ-02. At least two observers were blinded for cardiac-function/scar quantification. The authors describe the functional assessment as prompted by the immune-cell finding. The study is a small exploratory signal, with useful blinded measurements, rather than a prospectively confirmed treatment-vs-injured-control durability result. The patent and dissertation describe overlapping research; they should not be counted as independent replication.

### Blake et al., ACS Chemical Neuroscience 2022
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35156811/; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9112862/; https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acschemneuro.1c00854.
HJ-01/HJ-02 are acrylamide-containing small molecules with a published four-step synthesis from commercial starting material. Both restored neurite outgrowth at 100 nM. They do not inhibit PTPσ catalytic activity at biologically relevant concentrations. Cellular experiments support modulation of PTPσ–Trk interactions, but the authors explicitly report no direct-binding data and allow an unidentified binding protein. The inactive analogue HJ-03 lacks the reactive acrylamide. PTPσ catalytic-inhibition assays therefore cannot serve as the sole potency assay for manufacture or optimization of HJ-02.

### Additional primary negative/unfinished evidence in the author's dissertation
Source: Matthew R. Blake, OHSU 2022 dissertation, https://digitalcollections.ohsu.edu/record/9690/files/Blake.Matthew.2022.pdf (search-indexed text of the target-identification discussion and Appendix A/Figure 8.1).
The author describes extensive target-identification attempts. An approximately 60-kDa membrane-associated band was inconsistently enriched; Src was not validated. Many variations of the experiment failed to produce an identified target. This adds concrete context to the published direct-binding caveat. It is unfinished work from the original group, not independent replication or a current claim that the target remains undiscovered in unpublished work. Ask the inventors what has changed since 2022.

### Blake et al., Physiological Reports 2023: related intervention only
Source: https://physoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.14814/phy2.15702 (also PMID 37226390; PMC10209516).
This study used ISP, not HJ-02. At day 40, vehicle and ISP groups had similar innervation, pump-function measures and infarct size. Functional comparisons had five animals/group. Provoked PVC and severity comparisons were nonsignificant (p=.8 and .9). Vehicle animals had spontaneously reinnervated as scar CSPG sulfation declined. No continuous conscious-mouse long-term arrhythmia data were obtained. This weakens the argument that accelerated reinnervation alone establishes durable repair; it does not show that HJ-02 lacks a compound-specific lasting benefit.

## Why the current A-tier interpretation is too strong
1. The most persuasive headline conflates two statistical questions. “Treated animals are not significantly different from healthy sham” does not establish improvement versus injured vehicle, and is not an equivalence test. Obtain raw animal-level data and estimate the actual HJ-02–vehicle contrast and uncertainty. The scar comparison is a positive signal in its own right; do not erase it.
2. The time window is short. Five days after cessation is evidence of persistence beyond dosing, but it is not a six-to-eight-week remodeling outcome. A durable-benefit thesis cannot borrow the 40-day result of another molecule.
3. Provoked arrhythmia susceptibility is not spontaneous arrhythmia burden, sudden death, rehospitalization or long-term function. A commercial target-product profile must name which benefit is sought and which preclinical readout can credibly predict it.
4. Late scar size needs initial-injury balance. An endpoint scar difference is more persuasive with a pre-dose injury measure, prespecified exclusion rules and concealed randomization. The published records inspected do not establish that complete design.
5. Target uncertainty complicates medicinal chemistry and systemic safety. The relevant question is whether the functional effect can be reproduced with an exposure margin and a tractable structure–activity relationship, not whether the target label sounds mechanistically clean.
6. The invitation to license has not yet become a controllable asset. That transition is a short business-development diligence task; more generic literature searches will not complete it.
7. A post-MI product must add to existing care and has a demanding clinical development path. Preclinical package value and financing a full indication program are different milestones. Market size alone does not establish a reachable value inflection.

## A bounded development decision
### Before commissioning substantive biology
Request from OHSU a non-confidential package answering: current worldwide/territory-specific license and option status; any cardiac-field restrictions; granted claims and pending family members; exact active compounds and reference lots; complete animal-level efficacy data and exclusion/mortality logs; any post-2022 PK, safety, target-identification or larger-animal work; current inventor participation; any sponsored-research overlap. Request an option structure or other path that preserves the commercial value of a successful externally funded result. These are proposed questions, not messages sent.

### First experimental tranche
Authenticate an independently prepared lot and reproduce the functional neuronal response with exposure measurement and an inactive structural comparator. Obtain basic stability/solubility and exposure information sufficient to interpret the next study. A confirmed phenotypic response with manageable exposure is a reason to continue despite an incomplete target mechanism. Failure at authenticated concentrations, a strong nonspecific liability or an unavailable commercially useful license is a reason to stop.

### Decisive replication
Use a qualified independent operator. Prespecify HJ-02 versus injured vehicle as the primary comparison; use HJ-01 as a useful chemical-family comparator if affordable. Match or adjust groups using pre-dose injury measurements. Assess durable pump function/remodeling after dosing has ended, with rhythm monitoring that can detect a tradeoff. Include both sexes and report every randomized animal and exclusion. Choose power from raw effect/variance data, conservative shrinkage and a predeclared meaningful effect; five animals per arm should not become the default merely because the original paper used five.

### Commercial pass criteria
An asset option or equivalent attainable rights; reproducible exposure-linked effect; direct functional benefit over injured controls with credible uncertainty bounds; a clean enough safety window to justify lead optimization; an agreed next development milestone and an operator quote. A promising endpoint without control of the asset can support a scientific collaboration but does not automatically create investable enterprise value for the user.

## Planning budget and timeline
No vendor or owner quote was obtained. Illustrative planning allowances, not market-price claims: roughly $15k–50k for material identity/functional transfer/basic exposure feasibility, then approximately $100k–250k for a substantial independent rodent functional/durability package with telemetry, histology, bioanalysis and analysis. A broader contingency allowance may bring the staged research decision into the $150k–350k range, excluding license consideration, company operating costs, GMP and IND-enabling work. The lower end requires an existing competent assay/model; method development, telemetry failures or a repeated study can exceed this. Allow several months after contracting, not a near-term assumption of a complete partner-ready package. Root may omit numbers because no quotes support them.

## Search scope and remaining limitations
Inspected primary chemical and cardiac studies, the long-term ISP study, OHSU licensing page, patent family publication, official September 2026 grant index, and indexed original-group dissertation. Searches for HJ-02 independent replication, pharmacokinetics, material suppliers and licensing did not identify a public modern development package. This is an unresolved public-data gap, not proof that none exists privately. HJ-02 is worth one well-structured owner inquiry and a conditional study plan; repeated broad searches are unlikely to resolve the deal status.
