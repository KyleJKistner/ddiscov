## 2. A transport-positive assay is not automatically a useful-function assay

The 2026 JCI Insight model introduces heterozygous R94Q into **MShef11 hESCs** and reports four edited subclones from two editing rounds. Its patient-derived R94Q corroborative model comes from the earlier Van Lent study; that reuse is not another independent patient. At differentiation days **34–36**, patch-clamp studies found no overt mutant-versus-control difference; the reported total was **33 cells**. Mitochondrial transport was impaired, with figures using **six wild-type and twelve variant neurons from three differentiations**. ACY-738 supplies a transport-response example, not P2 efficacy or a human neuromuscular-force result. [S03](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/200106)

The 2021 Brain study provides a different functional anchor: one MFN2-R94Q patient background showed increased network-burst rate, and a corrected isogenic line normalized the reported phenotype. Figure 2's electrophysiological observations are **9–16 wells from two differentiations**, not 9–16 patients. Editing occurred before differentiation. The separately tested DLK inhibitor was introduced during differentiation, and its reported mitochondrial effects do not establish delayed reversal of a mature burst or motor-unit deficit. [S04](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8418338/)

These studies need not disagree. Single-cell electrophysiology and collective network activity ask different questions; developmental context and genetic background also differ. A culture may have an interpretable transport deficit while having no deficit in a particular electrical measurement. Failure to improve that normal measurement would be inconclusive for the proposed therapeutic claim. Conversely, suppressing abnormal spontaneous bursting would be insufficient if the apparent normalization also reduced useful evoked output.

The practical funding distinction is between finding an interpretable first human response and establishing a broader product claim. One qualified disease/control background can support an exploratory decision. Independent backgrounds and another allele become important for confirmation and generality; they are not compulsory preconditions for every initial qualification dollar. Isogenic controls strengthen causal attribution, but an explicitly nonisogenic comparison can still inform a bounded exploratory question if its limitations are retained. These are applications of the [current selection policy](../../../decisions/selection-policy.md), not a new experimental protocol.

## 4. Allele selection must retain correction and contrary evidence

The 2020 correction withdrew figures and conclusions after cells purported to express human R364W were found to express **R364T**. It also withdrew the extrapolated R364W pathogenicity and pro-fusion-harm propositions. That is a specific correction, not retraction of the entire original article, and it is not evidence that every MFN2 allele will benefit from P2. Current direct PMC access was intermittent and the publisher page was restricted; the substantive correction text was recovered from the indexed primary PMC document. [S08](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7332790/)

The 2022 fibroblast study reports mitochondrial responses to **Chimera** in four CMT2A backgrounds, including R364W, under a metabolic-stress condition. Its donor sources and investigator lineage overlap the earlier work; do not count repeated culture observations as independent P2 validation. The appropriate update is to retain R364W as a candidate challenge background while measuring its actual response, not to declare it either a contraindication or a proven responder. [S13](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8947610/)

Lagos and colleagues' 2026 study reports reduced fusion frequency in both L248H and M376V fibroblast contexts despite opposite mitochondrial morphologies, with a fission defect prominent in L248H. It does not test P2. The indexed primary text supports this bounded distinction; full figure-level reanalysis was not attempted. Morphology alone therefore cannot specify whether useful function improves with P2. An elongated organelle is not itself evidence of adequate fusion, safe quality control, or a reason to exclude a patient. [S12](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41419-026-08838-3)

## 5. Consequences for the next marginal commitment

The current information supports a finite option rather than unrestricted continuation. The next deliverable must say whether it buys article/model qualification, a first useful human-functional observation, or confirmation of a broader claim. Those deliverables have different continuation values. A cheaper ready imaging route may still be worth considering, but rank six and the original illustrative envelope cannot be carried over merely by keeping the original price label.

The model question should be matched to the product claim. A repair claim needs a deficit that exists before the intervention; a strong negative inference about reversal also requires evidence that the chosen system can reveal post-deficit improvement. Those conditions should not become a universal requirement to reverse every deficit before considering useful ongoing control or preservation. A convincing positive can justify independent confirmation before every mechanistic uncertainty is resolved.

A useful direct muscle contribution is not automatically a failure. It changes the attribution and possibly the development claim. Likewise, a credible response confined to T105M can support a narrower option rather than an all-CMT2A label. Broader allele coverage, a usable systemic exposure/tolerability window and a defensible commercial contribution remain separate later uncertainties. None is supplied by mitochondrial motion, catalog availability or a model grant alone.

Close this human/model source pass. Reopen only for a specific exact-P2 human functional or exposure dataset, a newly demonstrated disease-dependent motor-unit assay, an authenticated relevant model specification that changes feasible scope, a material correction, or a concrete access/cost change that alters the next decision. Repeating generic searches for the same missing bridge has diminishing information value. Waiting remains an available decision; no scientific selection requires an owner reply.
