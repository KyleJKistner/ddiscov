### Exact article and biological units

The parent 8015 mixture, pure 8015-P2, less-active P1, MiM111 and Chimera C are distinct articles. Historical retrieval aliases do not make them interchangeable. The 2024 single-patient T105M human-cell findings concern the mixture; the earlier four-patient work concerns Chimera C. Neither establishes a response to exact P2 in human motor units. The mouse and rat P2 studies support an established-disease hypothesis, but use the same T105M allele and substantially overlapping research lineage. Different species and multiple correlated endpoints are not independent human or allele replications. [2024 paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11493442/); [2026 rat paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13513834/).

The rat audit makes the therapeutic cohort's male-only scope and within-animal measurement structure explicit. Repeated grip readings, selected CMAP recordings, two limbs and image fields do not increase the animal denominator. Randomization and blinded rat assessments are reported. Four rats per dose and seven WT references do not turn failure to differ from WT into an equivalence result. The high-dose group stopped after apparent normalization; the paper's minimum-effective-dose label and improvement at a lower dose must remain endpoint-specific observations, not human dose thresholds. Mouse functional figure counts not independently recovered remain unknown rather than borrowed from histology.

The rat regimen was Monday–Friday, despite shorthand daily-dose descriptions. An acute high-dose mitochondrial-motility observation cannot establish exposure or target coverage during lower-dose chronic efficacy. A 2024 activated-charcoal dose differs between Methods and Results; both values and their locators remain recorded without silently repairing the paper. The prior off-target correction also stands: MAO-A 59% is single-concentration radioligand displacement; hERG binding is not a functional current measurement. These limits do not erase animal benefit or establish toxicity. They prevent an unsupported human therapeutic-window claim.

## What the next dollar would have to buy

The marginal comparison concerns the improvement in the best available continuation after a bounded action, net of that action's costs and the remaining costs needed to use its information. A hypothetical complete-drug upside cannot be divided by a small desktop-review bill to manufacture EV. Public facts do not support numerical probabilities, an NPV, a return-per-dollar estimate or a replacement allocation.

| Possible commitment | Decision-useful output | Limit on inherited priority |
|---|---|---|
| Exact-article and model feasibility only | Establish whether an authenticated P2 and a disease-dependent functional system can support an interpretable next question | A cheaper or same-price mechanistic/qualification deliverable is not the original independent human-function bridge. Compare its information and follow-on cost separately. |
| Bounded independent useful human function | Determine whether exact P2 improves a relevant functional deficit at interpretable exposure, with attribution sufficient for the intended claim | The original rank and envelope remain conditional assumptions, not evidence that this scope is feasible or adequately priced. |
| Confirmation and broader allele scope | Test whether a promising first result generalizes enough for the intended product population | Further backgrounds, replication, exposure and safety work have additional costs; no automatic release follows the first result. |
| A commercially retained contribution | Establish a lawful, usable data/product contribution with acceptable rights and development economics | Data ownership, regulatory use/reference, supply and development licenses are distinct. Scientific value alone does not prove private financial capture. |
| Wait or stop | Preserve capital when the available action is uninformative or has unattractive remaining costs and retained value | Public-source closure does not automatically demote P2; repeated review without a named new artifact is not a new informative tranche. |

The original US$75–250k envelope cannot be treated as an inclusive quote for model creation, chemistry scale-up, chronic systemic safety, pediatric development or clinical manufacture. A long model-build or rights dependency may materially change cost and timing. Conversely, a public article and a plausible model route can justify considering a small feasibility commitment before human efficacy exists. The correct response is to price and compare the actual deliverable, not require the final positive result before considering the experiment that could produce it.

## How future results would change the decision

These are decision branches, not an executable protocol. No doses, sample-size rules, new quantitative advance thresholds or operational work orders were created.

1. **Identity, exposure or model qualification is unresolved.** Narrow the uncertainty and compare a finite feasibility deliverable. An inadequate article or an assay without the relevant disease deficit cannot establish drug or target failure. If the useful functional question requires a major new platform, recompare the cost and time rather than retaining the original rank by label.
2. **Exact P2 produces a credible useful functional positive in one qualified background.** Consider independent confirmation and relevant safety/exposure follow-up. Preserve the genotype and claim. Complete multi-allele validation is not an entry requirement for valuing that first result.
3. **Only mitochondrial morphology or movement improves.** Retain mechanism information, but the original human motor-unit question remains unanswered. Any next mechanistic tranche needs its own information-value case.
4. **Only the mixture or a related molecule works.** The exact-P2 branch has not earned the result. A different article would be a separately defined program and new marginal decision, not a substitution inside this review.
5. **P2 fails in an interpretable functional comparison.** Adequate article/exposure, assay relevance and precision determine the strength of the negative. A strong post-deficit target-reversal rejection also needs evidence that the deficit was modifiable in that system. Those stronger negative-inference requirements are not a universal prerequisite to recognizing a credible positive or useful ongoing control.
6. **Benefit is genotype-limited, direct-muscle mediated, symptomatic or exposure-dependent.** Narrow the product claim and assess usefulness. A real muscle contribution can matter; it cannot be described as axonal regrowth without evidence. Useful ongoing control need not be a permanent cure. Nonspecific hyperexcitability, injury or a clinically trivial change is not useful recovery.
7. **Required exposure impairs relevant healthy function, or product/rights economics are unattractive.** Stop the affected article/claim or financial route when the evidence is adequate. A successor, different delivery project or purely scientific dataset would need a separately stated purpose and fresh comparison. Waiting remains available.

The current advance condition is therefore an informative, feasible next commitment followed by reproducible useful exact-P2 function appropriate to its claimed scope, coherent exposure/tolerability and a credible route to retain value. It is not broad human efficacy already proved, unrestricted access already secured, or a full-program investment recommendation.
