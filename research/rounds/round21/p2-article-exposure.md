It does not establish that an independent P2 program already has a useful human motor-unit response, a human systemic exposure window, or broad allele coverage. The chemical anchor remains the slower-eluting chiral fraction, with the later trans-(1R,2R) assignment. Pure P2, the parent mixture, P1, MiM111, CPR1-B, Chimera C and generic piperine must retain separate evidence records. The existing human-cell observation is still parent 8015 in one T105M background. Cells, mitochondria and repeated culture experiments are useful measures of reproducibility at their own level. They cannot establish independent patient replication. The unresolved bridge is not whether the compound family can move a mitochondrial readout; it is whether the authenticated P2 article can improve the useful function selected for the intended human product claim.

The mouse and rat studies also share a substantial investigator and material lineage. Different species and different outcome methods reduce some uncertainties, but they do not eliminate shared execution, analysis or selection risks. An independent result would therefore buy information that merely repeating the same public-source interpretation cannot provide. This does not require dismissing the original investigators' findings or assigning an invented probability of bias.

One appropriately qualified disease/control background can support an exploratory continuation decision. Broader donor and allele confirmation belongs to the broader claim and later commitment. It would be circular to require the result of the proposed human-function work before permitting any bounded qualification effort. Conversely, availability of cells or a transport assay does not mean a useful motor-unit question has already been made experimentally answerable. The [current selection policy](../../../decisions/selection-policy.md) controls these stage distinctions.

## Animal function: meaningful signal with bounded claims

The existing late-treatment mouse evidence includes motor, sensory and electrophysiological improvement; its histological corroboration is based on animal means, not a large collection of independent axons. The rat paper adds an explicitly randomized and blinded comparison and dose/time observations. The detailed ledger retains each regimen and cohort separately, including the shorter highest-dose dose-response arm. [Mouse methods and Figures 5–6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11493442/) · [Rat sections 2.4–2.5 and 4.1–4.10](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13513834/)

Several distinctions matter to marginal value:

First, improvement against a pretreatment deficit or vehicle is not the same hypothesis as equivalence to healthy reference function. A result that fails to differ significantly from WT at small n can remain compatible with a consequential residual deficit. It is also compatible with a substantial benefit. The responsible interpretation preserves both possibilities instead of replacing them with either complete cure or no useful effect.

Second, stopping a cohort once it reaches a favorable observed criterion makes subsequent exposure and observation unequal. That does not invalidate an earlier result. It does prevent treating the stopped arm as a full-duration cohort or a demonstrated post-withdrawal follow-up. Off-treatment durability is a separate claim; useful ongoing control can retain value without being a permanent repair.

Third, an integrated endpoint can improve through more than one tissue. A muscle contribution is potentially useful, even if the initial narrative emphasizes nerves. Force or CMAP improvement alone cannot identify exclusively axonal regeneration. Conversely, failure to establish that exclusive mechanism cannot erase a useful integrated functional benefit. Claims about nerve repair, sensory restoration, muscle function and duration should be evaluated on the outcomes that actually support them.

Fourth, the M376V comparison in the mouse paper was not a negative drug-response study in an established-deficit model. A normal baseline in that particular model cannot reject the corresponding clinical genotype or establish drug resistance. A T105M-positive result also cannot establish all-CMT2A efficacy. Narrowing the intended population can preserve an option while changing its reach and economics.

### Measurement and reporting precision

The rat methods identify within-animal aggregation and selection rules for grip, CMAP, sensation and MRI. Those rules should remain attached to any claimed reproducibility. A selected best trace or maximal pull is a defined measurement, not automatically a biased one; the question is whether that definition is consistent, blinded, repeatable and useful for the intended comparison. No additional biological sample size is created.

The readable rat methods support blinding and randomization. Equivalent reporting was not recovered from the mouse main text. This is a limit of verified reporting, not proof that the safeguards were absent. Complete animal flow, exclusions and image-only functional denominators were not reconstructed. No effect-size calculation or reanalysis was performed.

Two exact inconsistencies remain: the mouse EHR Methods state **2 g/kg** charcoal while Results state **2 mg/kg**; the rat results describe **front-paw** sensitivity while Methods describe **hind paws**. Their locators are recorded in E12 and E09. Neither inconsistency is silently repaired, nor does either by itself establish efficacy failure.

## Exposure and safety: what remains unqualified

Three distinct quantities are still being conflated too easily: potency in a cellular morphology assay, an acute mitochondrial transport response, and sustained free drug at the relevant tissue during useful functional improvement. The published record supports each only within its own article, model and measurement. A high acute dose with a favorable transport readout cannot establish low-dose steady-state coverage. Motility is also a downstream pharmacodynamic readout, not direct occupancy.

The parent-mixture binding and partition values remain attached to the mixture. Exact-P2 plasma PK and mouse EHR observations are retained, with their small-group and formulation limits. They do not establish human free nerve, muscle, cardiac or CNS exposure. Reading the published PK modeling did not involve rerunning it or producing a new simulation. [2024 Table 1, Figure 3 and PK methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11493442/)

This distinction has an economic consequence. A very potent compound may still require costly work to establish a usable human regimen, while a modestly potent compound can be useful if a practical exposure is safe and maintainable. A nominal potency ratio should not replace the cost and information value of resolving the actual exposure question.

The rat active preparation uses a DMSO stock, while its stated vehicle is cyclodextrin. The readable description does not explicitly establish matched final DMSO. This is a transfer uncertainty, not demonstrated solvent-induced benefit or toxicity. Powder purity and preparation instructions likewise do not constitute a qualified clinical formulation or stability package. No synthesis or new administration protocol is proposed here.

The [Round 5 off-target audit](../round5/p2-off-target-audit.md) remains controlling: its 87 rows comprise 75 binding and 12 enzyme/cell assays at one nominal concentration. MAO-A and hERG entries are binding measurements; the separate MAO-B enzyme result cannot fill a missing MAO-A functional curve. The current supplement route was unread, so this pass carries forward the previously verified transcription rather than claiming a fresh complete primary-supplement audit.

A screening interaction can justify closer exposure-linked interpretation without proving a clinical adverse event. A negative binding point can be useful preliminary evidence without proving absence of functional channel inhibition. These symmetric limits preserve the panel's actual information while rejecting a universal therapeutic-index claim.

## Implications for the next marginal commitment

The highest-value unresolved scientific question remains exact-P2 useful human function with interpretable exposure. Initial article/model qualification is defensible when it is a finite way to determine whether that question can be answered. It should be described and valued as qualification, with the remaining cost and uncertainty still visible. A polished public dossier is not a substitute for the missing function result.

A credible positive in an initial qualified background can justify independent confirmation. The next claim must match what improved: a neuronal signal, useful motor-unit performance, direct muscle contribution, preservation, ongoing control, or a stronger repair/durability claim. Requiring broad genotype coverage or a complete systemic development package before the first useful observation would obscure the value of staged learning.

A negative is more demanding to interpret. Failure in an unqualified model, inadequate exposure, an insensitive readout or an uncertain article can stop that execution route without rejecting P2 or mitofusin biology. A strong negative established-reversal conclusion needs a model capable of registering meaningful recovery after the relevant deficit. That requirement is not a universal barrier to studying preservation or ongoing control.

An informative result could also support a scientific contribution without supporting an independently owned product. The separate rights/capture review therefore remains necessary for the intended marginal-dollar objective. This article lane does not establish acceptable rights, a feasible budget, retained financial value or an alternative program's superiority.
