# Exact 8015-P2: product, rights and retained value

**Round 21 · 4 November 2026, America/Los_Angeles · public retrieval 5 November 2026 UTC.** Existing program `R3-NM-01`, based on commit `8aa186ce010b4e173fe63ee4217075efa11c7e81` and tree `fa67db6c5b03bf2905193592a185f154d430eab1`. [Structured evidence](p2-product-capture.json).

## Decision

Retain the exact-P2 program as a conditional option for an informative human-function decision. This capture review supplies no basis for raising the inherited conditional development rank of six. It makes the public rights position more specific without establishing an available commercial asset, a price for access, or a numerical financial EV.

The most consequential addition is a **2026 stereoisomer-focused application, WO2026044165A1**, which connects the slower-eluting P2 fraction to the active cyclopropane configuration and seeks protection around enriched stereoisomer compositions. Its disclosure strengthens the link between the research article and an active patent strategy. It is not an issued patent or an independent therapeutic replication. [PC21-S08]

The older granted compound family, its actual continuation, and the separately granted CMT2A rat-model patent must remain distinct. Historical Round 5 already correctly separated the model right; this review does not rewrite it as an earlier error. Source exhaustion for APR, RM, BLU, NDC or mIGF has no effect on their development ranks.

The next marginal commitment would have to buy a useful change in the continuation decision: an interpretable exact-P2 human functional result, a more concrete product/exposure proposition, or information sufficient to stop. Scientific information, a transferable development package and financial value retained by ddiscov are separate outputs. Neither a positive biological result nor an inventor's public commercialization intent establishes that the resulting private value would accrue to ddiscov.

## 1. Identity is sufficiently specific to frame qualification

The 2024 article defines P2 operationally as the slower-eluting chiral SFC fraction, distinct from parent 8015. Its abbreviation for undifferentiated 8015 must not be copied as an absolute P2 assignment. The 2026 rat article identifies the active trans-(1R,2R) cyclopropyl enantiomer; the later worm article prints the corresponding full chemical name. [PC21-S01–S03]

The exact article used here is **(1r,4r)-4-hydroxy-N-[(1R,2R)-2-(4-phenylbutyl)cyclopropyl]cyclohexane-1-carboxamide**. MiM111, Chimera C, generic piperine and the parent mixture remain related search terms or comparators, not interchangeable articles. Public identity supports a bounded qualification question; it does not certify any future lot.

The research supply history is also specific. The mouse paper records an MTA. The rat paper reports company-gifted powder of greater than 99% purity. The worm paper reports WuXi manufacture, greater than 95% purity and company provision under an MTA. These are separate study statements, not evidence that the batches or purity specifications are interchangeable. [PC21-S01–S03]

A named manufacturer reduces one kind of uncertainty: there is a documented production history. It does not establish an independently accessible current order, an authenticated reference lot, a transferable analytical package, permitted downstream uses, price, or lead time. No catalog, quote or lot was treated as available, and no supplier was contacted. A future quotation would still need to distinguish a defined article from an adequate delivered product; an inexpensive label match would not answer the scientific question.

### Issued composition claims, rather than the earlier descriptive breadth

US12396968B2 was published on 26 August 2025. Its 19 issued claims are composition claims. Claim 1 uses G=CH and X=NR¹Cyc; claims 4 and 6 specify cyclopropyl and hydroxycyclohexyl features, while claim 9 fixes a cyclopropyl/four-carbon-linker branch. The claims include stereoisomer language. This is a concrete textual relationship to P2's published structure, not a complete claim chart. [PC21-S06]

The retrieved US20240050386A1 rendering contains 27 numbered positions, with 13, 14 and 25–27 marked cancelled. Broad method language remains in descriptive clauses. The continuation's rendering contains 21 numbered positions, begins with a G=N branch, and has a blank position 19; its methods 20 and 21 depend on different composition claims. These textual limits are preserved rather than silently filling missing diagrams or importing wording between versions. [PC21-S05, PC21-S07]

The practical inference is limited but useful: existing composition rights warrant specific product-to-claim review before a material commercial commitment. This pass did not inspect every original chemical drawing or the complete prosecution record. It therefore makes no coverage, exclusion, validity, infringement or freedom-to-operate determination. The presence of a different claim branch in a continuation does not by itself create clearance.

### A newer exact-stereoisomer disclosure

WO2026044165A1 was published on 26 February 2026. Paragraphs 0105–0107 connect the slower P2 peak with the (R,R) configuration using reported stereospecific resynthesis and elution comparison. Its 28 published claims include enrichment ratios; phenyl-specific, substantially single-isomer and treatment provisions are separately identified. It also states that the government has certain rights arising from NIH support. [PC21-S08]

That disclosure is newly recovered for this program's current decision. It should make an assumption of freely capturable exact-P2 ownership less comfortable. It does not prove a new independent efficacy dataset: its overlapping potency and article lineage are not counted as another successful replication. Its broad formulation and stability possibilities likewise do not establish a validated clinical formulation, measured shelf life or delivered cost. Patent claims and contemplated uses do not supply observed patient benefit.

The distinction between scientific readiness and commercial readiness remains important. An exact starting article can be sufficient to justify exploratory qualification before all downstream product questions are settled. Conversely, an executable scientific question does not justify assuming a free license or treating a future positive dataset as an exclusive drug asset.

### The rat-model right is separate

The official USPTO Gazette identifies US12721327B2, issued 1 September 2026, as a rat-model patent assigned to CMTA and the University of Pennsylvania. Its representative claim names R364W or H361Y knock-in rats with specified degeneration, and its continuity is to US11800859. [PC21-S09]

Only the representative claim and bibliographic entry were checked here. This does not establish that every other model is unencumbered, nor that a particular service is currently available on acceptable terms. A compound permission, model permission, physical supply route and data-use agreement can each leave different unresolved facts. The capture decision must account for whichever of these a selected route actually requires, without making every possible model a compulsory dependency.

## 3. Regulatory and development language does not identify a human P2 program

The FDA record dated 6 October 2025 remains for a generic **small molecule mitofusin activator**, sponsored by Mitochondria in Motion Discovery, Inc. It is designated for CMT and explicitly not approved for the orphan indication. The record does not name exact 8015-P2 or establish its IND status. [PC21-S10]

The historical SBIR record is also easy to overread. The 2021 Phase II STTR award names trans-MiM111 and pre-IND goals; the 2022 award distinguishes MiM111 and CPR1-B. Those funding-phase labels are not completed human clinical phases, and the named molecules cannot transfer a development status to P2. [PC21-S11, PC21-S12]

Targeted exact-P2, company-name, IND and registry-index searches recovered the known publications and generic designation, but no verifiable exact-P2 human trial identifier, administered-human result or public IND authorization. This is the outcome of a bounded search, not proof that no confidential submission or unindexed program exists. The 2026 worm paper itself describes potential future human development. [PC21-S02]

These observations keep current-program attribution precise. An identified sponsor creates an incumbent-development context, but it does not establish clinical de-risking, a finished product, or a license available to a new developer. No proposed human dose, clinical protocol, approval timetable or regulatory incentive valuation was created.

## 4. What different marginal commitments could buy

The earlier independent review assigned P2 **conditional rank six** and an illustrative **US$75–250k / 4–8 months** for the exact article and an independent human functional bridge. Chronic toxicology and clinical supply were expressly excluded; the general exclusions also included rights, licensing, GLP, GMP, trials and commercialization. These are analyst assumptions, not a new quote, feasibility finding or full-program budget. The original report input and its SHA256 are pinned in the structured record.

| Route | Potential useful output | Condition controlling retained value |
|---|---|---|
| Preliminary article/model qualification | Establish whether an interpretable P2 question can actually be asked. | A narrower output requires fresh comparison of information yield, remaining costs and waiting; it cannot inherit the full bridge's rank or EV. |
| Independent exact-P2 functional package | Reduce a central translation uncertainty with usable provenance and exposure context. | Publication, data custody, regulatory reliance and commercial rights must be distinguished; scientific value alone does not price retained financial value. |
| Authorized development or license route | Combine informative data with a practicable product contribution. | No availability, terms, exclusivity, obligations or counterparty commitment is verified. |
| Separate product or successor branch | Potentially address a specific article or delivery limitation. | A different molecule or major product redesign would need its own budget and comparison; it is not selected or screened here. |
| Wait, narrow or stop | Preserve capital when another search or narrower deliverable changes no consequential decision. | Reopen for a concrete source or fact that alters identity, function, exposure, cost or retained capture. |

All five routes are **unexecuted**. No development execution is authorized by this review.

The informative scope matters more than the inherited ordinal number. Paying the same amount for article authentication and an organelle-motility readout would buy less than the original human motor-unit question. Paying substantially more to build a new platform would change both the maximum loss and the comparison with waiting. Neither substitution should pass merely because its label still says “P2 qualification.”

There is no requirement that an exploratory study already possess the result it is meant to discover. A qualified first background can be informative before broad allele replication. A useful direct-muscle contribution or ongoing-control product need not be dismissed simply because it does not prove axonal regeneration or permanent cure. Those distinctions affect the value of the chosen product claim; they do not remove the need for a usable exposure and safety proposition.

A credible positive could support a narrower next step even while some later development work remains. An inadequate model or unresolved article does not establish biological failure. At the other extreme, repeatedly generating mechanism-only information after the important decision is clear can consume capital without improving the best available continuation. This pass closes at that boundary.

## 5. Source stop and retained limits

The bounded review covered the exact article's production provenance, specified drug and model patent records, the public orphan record and program-specific development evidence. It did not screen broader opportunities or reopen the five completed source passes.

USPTO Patent Center returned no readable dossier; the assignment site failed; the newer PatentScope page returned 403. No access control was bypassed. The complete current chain of title, maintenance, claim construction, global family coverage, license terms and permitted data-use terms remain unverified. Patent formula images and original claim pages were not comprehensively audited.

Reopening requires a concrete decision-changing artifact: an authenticated exact-P2 human functional/exposure result, a readable material-to-claim or title event, actual permitted product/data rights and cost information, or an exact-identity clinical development record. Repeating the same generic patent or trial query is not a new milestone.

**No outreach, purchases, recruitment, experiments, simulations or material receipt occurred.** No scientific or financial model utilities were executed, no manufacturing or experimental procedure was proposed, and no financial commitment was made.
