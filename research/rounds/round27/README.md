## Decision

APR and erlotinib remain a matched pair; CBD3063 is the leading early-development challenger in the same broader desk tier. The relative financial order of those three is unresolved because controlled clinical evidence and an early functional package may command different proceeds. These are priorities for further analysis, not a precise expected-return ranking or a recommendation to fund four studies.

### Nifedipine/lidocaine: an existing-product route

The opportunity is an option on a defined territory's product dossier, supply and regulatory reference, followed by a limited bridge that a subsequent specialist developer could use. The 2002 chronic-fissure study and existing Antrolin product supply a concrete starting article. The Italian official manufacturing notice supports current product-holder/manufacturing context; it does not offer a license. The Malta listing is marked expired and is not used as proof of current authorization. See human sources B04–B09 in [human-assets.json](human-assets.json).

The 2026 trial compared nifedipine plus **2% lidocaine** with diltiazem plus 2% lidocaine in a mixed acute/chronic population. It reported similar healing, not superiority, and is not automatically a bridge to the historical **1.5% lidocaine** article. The diltiazem VEN307 program supplies consequential contrary evidence: its second pivotal trial failed the primary pain comparison, and a later issuer filing described regulatory uncertainty and license termination. A new pivotal or component-contribution requirement is therefore plausible; this does not establish nifedipine failure. Sources: [2026 trial](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/wjs.70361), [VEN307 result](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1426800/000114420414007779/v368123_ex99-1.htm), [subsequent filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1426800/000114420414045571/v385024_8-k.htm).

**Desk question:** Which territory has an unsatisfied product opportunity, a usable dossier and a financeable bridge? Model both a limited transfer/bridge and a substantial new efficacy requirement. Existing manufacture does not establish exclusivity, attainable terms or a cheap U.S. approval path. Downgrade if the modest territorial interest cannot retain enough value after the buyer's remaining development cost.

### APR and erlotinib: preserve a matched comparison

The APR report includes **two children**, with a nonrandomized, unblinded contralateral vehicle comparison on one child's palms and uncontrolled scalp treatment in the other. Calling both treatments entirely uncontrolled loses a relevant design detail. The erlotinib report includes **two related patients**, with randomized blinded opposite-foot phases and later uncontrolled extensions; formulation, keratolytic co-treatment and unequal responses matter. Neither record establishes a reproducible population effect. Sources: [APR primary report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6965199/), [erlotinib primary letter](https://www.pachyonychia.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/LeeG2022Topical-Erlotinib.-pdf.pdf), human H02/B33/B34.

Use the same initial cost and probability assumptions for **qualified article plus controlled useful clinical benefit**. The tie is a fair placeholder, not an empirical equivalence claim. A recognizable commercial platform for erlotinib changes access and participation questions; it does not establish superior investment value. APR's discontinued oncology development likewise does not establish failure of its AEC use or free commercial rights. Current issuer/platform records are H01/H04–H06.

**Desk question:** Which exact article can be transferred, which relevant patients can be recruited, and which controlled endpoint could establish a useful benefit within the bounded package? Then compare treatable population, realistic product economics and retained interests. A material article, recruitment or capture difference can break the tie. The [parallel APR ranking audit](https://github.com/KyleJKistner/ddiscov/blob/cc34bf24119f398b1061baca9c7d7a35cc897a1e/research/phase2-program/apr-ranking-audit.md) supplies prior evidence reconciliation; that separate PR is not incorporated or merged by this round.

### CBD3063: early development with a concrete product challenge

The original racemate has behavioral pain evidence across mouse/rat models and sexes, active comparisons and an institutional composition-rights lead. The proposed package tests independent useful analgesia, exposure, repeated dosing and a practical intended route. Channel modulation alone is not success. Sources: [original primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10666126/), [NYU listing](https://license.tov.med.nyu.edu/product/peptidomimetic-modulator-of-cav22-n-type-calcium-channel-for-treatment-of-chronic-pain), early E01–E04 in [early-assets.json](early-assets.json).

The evidence does not establish chronic human safety or a practical chronic oral product. Individual enantiomer findings are not interchangeable with the racemate. If useful exposure requires a new analogue or extensive optimization, it becomes a different, larger proposal. Pending co-owned IP is a rights lead, not established validity, available exclusivity or an offer.

**Desk question:** Can this exact article reach useful analgesic exposure by a credible clinical route with an adequate repeat-dose margin, and what option/licensing interest would retain value from that result? Its broader use and possible composition interest support keeping it alongside human-characterized opportunities despite greater biological distance.

## Keep the inexpensive product challengers close

Three packages remain immediate challengers. Their lower price can be attractive, but the proposed incremental contribution is less specifically defined than the core four. Apply the same desk test to all three: **name the existing baseline, a specific unsolved performance or cost problem, and why the proposed improvement could earn retained payment**. This is a product-definition comparison, not an offer requirement.

| Challenger | Cost low / base / high | Base months / useful-milestone p | What would move it into the four |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| AAT donor-lung preparation/perfusion process | $85k / $165k / $310k | 5 / 55% | An operator-relevant advantage that can be established before an expensive donor-organ/recipient program and can support meaningful process economics. |
| Topical insulin for refractory corneal epithelial defects | $100k / $180k / $330k | 7 / 45% | A practical container, handling, delivery or supply advantage beyond already-stable preparations, with useful activity preserved and defensible capture. |
| Local verteporfin for scar prevention | $135k / $260k / $455k | 9 / 45% | A useful product/data contribution beyond recruiting SCARFREE-001, with better marginal value than waiting for external human evidence. |

The AAT evidence concerns physiology in discarded donor lungs, not demonstrated recipient benefit or the proposed process improvement. Insulin stability has already been reported for specified formulations; another generic stability demonstration may add little. Verteporfin has large-animal/human-xenograft evidence and an active human study. Source records P01, P05–P08 and P30–P38 in [product-process-assets.json](product-process-assets.json) preserve those distinctions.

**Exact 8015-P2, PEPITEM and rectal tacrolimus** are the nearest scientific/development reserves. P2 has meaningful post-onset animal function, but a useful human functional assay may itself need development and participation in an existing owner can leave less distributable cash. PEPITEM has functional bone evidence but chronic product/comparator and licensing-chain challenges. Tacrolimus has human evidence but active-comparator and systemic-exposure concerns. These are comparisons of whole packages, not assertions that their science lacks value.

## Financial comparison under uncertainty

The central calculation asks how much **net investor cash on the paying branch** would be required for nominal expected break-even. With all-in cost C, useful-milestone probability p and conditional investor-cash probability q, that hurdle is `C / (p × q)`. The five-year horizon is an analyst comparison assumption. Cash amounts must be after sharing, dilution, senior claims and required uses of proceeds. This model assumes no further investor outlays on any branch; later funding is supplied by another party or the project stops.

| Package | Base p | Base q: investor cash given useful milestone | Required paid-branch net cash |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Nifedipine/lidocaine | 55% | 25% | $2.36m |
| APR | 40% | 25% | $4.00m |
| Erlotinib | 40% | 25% | $4.00m |
| CBD3063 | 45% | 24.5% | $3.04m |
| AAT process challenger | 55% | 30% | $1.00m |
| Insulin product challenger | 45% | 30% | $1.33m |
| Verteporfin product challenger | 45% | 25% | $2.31m |
| P2 reserve | 40% | 15% | $5.58m |

These are **required amounts, not valuations, offers or probabilities of recovering principal**. A cheaper milestone can have a low hurdle while creating too little saleable value to command that payment. A process contribution and a controlled clinical result may have very different value.

The early lane originally estimated a company transaction event. This round adds an explicit distribution factor before comparing it with investor cash: CBD3063 uses 35% company-transaction probability × 70% conditional realization = 24.5%; P2 uses 30% × 50% = 15%. All are very-low-confidence analyst scenarios. Company receipts and a financing valuation cannot silently become investor distributions.

The [calculation output](financial-sensitivity.json) also evaluates the **same illustrative $0.5m, $1m, $3m and $10m net paid-branch amounts** across candidates. These are stress-test inputs, not estimated sale-price ranges. For the core four, central expected net cash is:

Rounded values illustrate sensitivity; they are not predictions. The current preference does not claim all four have positive expected returns at a known transaction price. Next, model gross commercial value and investor retention separately using patient/use counts, product economics, buyer-funded work and plausible ownership/distribution structures. This can use public evidence and estimates before inquiries.

## What the broader search changed

- **Added a mature product/dossier route and an early pain challenger.** Nifedipine and CBD3063 now compete directly with the rare-skin incumbents.
- **Corrected the APR comparison.** The contralateral-palm detail is preserved, and APR/erlotinib have matched scope and assumptions.
- **Made known competition affect the package.** SKNV's July 2026 exclusive sublicense and existing compounded Kefunova mean calcipotriol/5-FU must be a nonduplicative contribution within that licensed chain. Compounding is not FDA approval. See P39 and the [company announcement](https://sknv.com/fluorouracil-calcipotriene-patent-license/).
- **Excluded specific weak theses.** The deferoxamine radiation-patch listing depends on a retracted central paper; topical naltrexone dry-eye posted results and phenytoin ulcer controlled studies do not support prioritizing those efficacy theses. Separate uses remain separate questions. Sources and limits are recorded with each product candidate.
- **Preserved contrary evidence.** The [longlist](longlist.md) includes negative human studies, advanced incumbents, larger-capital benchmarks and exact-article liabilities, with reasons to reopen them.
