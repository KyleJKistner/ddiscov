# Broad longlist and disposition

**Reviewed 9 November 2026.** These 49 packages cover 48 distinct asset/use combinations. The four cross-lane finalists are in the [decision](README.md); lane ranks below are local comparisons, not a global funding order.

All costs are **analyst low/base/high estimates in thousands of USD** for the stated bounded milestone, including an entry/access allowance. They are not quotes, contractual caps, complete approval budgets or an allocation recommendation. Milestone probability ranges are subjective; a useful result and investor capital recovery are different events. Details, contrary evidence and primary source records are linked in each lane.

Calcipotriol/5-FU appears twice because a product-only contribution and a larger clinical/registration bridge have different scopes. Neither the two packages nor the related hyaluronan indications provide independent diversification.

## How to reopen a lower-priority package

Reopen when a specific new observation changes useful benefit, product need, cost, access, capture or downstream burden. A missing quote is not a rejection reason. A known negative result, duplicate forthcoming experiment or presently weak contribution is a reason to spend the next desk hour elsewhere. Each lane records its own reversal conditions.
