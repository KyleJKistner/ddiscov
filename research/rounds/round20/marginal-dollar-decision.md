## Use the clinical comparator precisely

The July 2026 insulin series reports **45 eyes from 43 patients**, with complete closure in **44 eyes** and a median time of **29 days**. It used a specified Humalog-derived 1 IU/mL ophthalmic formulation four times daily, with supportive treatments continuing. It is a retrospective cohort without a concurrent control, with selected eligibility, heterogeneous etiologies and limited follow-up. Its observed closure proportion is not an unbiased causal response probability and cannot be compared directly with rat closure percentages.

The earlier comparative PED study used **61 insulin patients and 23 autologous-serum historical controls**. It reports closure in **51/61 versus 11/23**. Different enrollment periods, selection and confounding limit that comparison. Its recurrence proportions also have materially unequal follow-up, so they do not establish a controlled durability advantage. These limitations do not erase the comparative clinical evidence; they define what it can and cannot say. The [comparator ledger](migf-comparator-update.json) preserves the exact products, access levels and denominators.

The acute postoperative diabetic studies address a different clinical setting. Their units per drop must not be silently substituted for the units per milliliter used in persistent-defect formulations. Evidence from a particular insulin molecule, vehicle, container and storage condition is not evidence that every compounded insulin preparation is interchangeable or ready for clinical use. No price, access route, supply or formulation was qualified through purchase or receipt.

This is why the gel's active comparison must represent a feasible clinical alternative. It is also why the actual comparator should not be caricatured as either a universally curative cheap drop or an inert treatment lacking nerve effects. The present evidence supports a meaningful benchmark and remaining uncertainty.

## Define a useful product advantage

There are two plausible value routes: better useful repair or comparable useful repair with a meaningful practical advantage. Faster closure is not the only possible benefit. Durable epithelial integrity, a relevant reduction in recurrence, tolerability, optical performance, administration burden and handling can matter, provided the claimed advantage is actually demonstrated for the intended use.

The reported once-daily rat gel result versus three-times-daily IGF-1 is a rationale for investigating a practical advantage. Similar means in small groups do not demonstrate equivalence or noninferiority. They also do not establish a human dosing schedule, the amount of active species reaching epithelium, or the separate contribution of residence time, amount delivered and receptor activity.

For a product decision, each comparator may be used at its own feasible regimen. Equal molarity or equal measured bioactivity is not a universal requirement across different molecules. Such matching can help isolate a mechanistic explanation; useful outcomes, tolerability and total burden govern the practical comparison. Conversely, fewer instillations alone can lose their value if offset by blur, instability, difficult dispensing, cold-chain or handling demands, cost, or inadequate repair. No such net advantage is established here.

The claim must also match the evidence. A steroid-suppressed acute wound is not a human chronic denervation disorder. An epithelial repair claim does not automatically include nerve restoration. A sensory measurement is more informative than staining alone, but a small short study does not establish durable clinical restoration. Likewise, barrier-related markers and surface closure do not establish durable adhesion or resistance to recurrent breakdown.

Useful treatment need not produce a permanent cure. The relevant duration and recurrence standard depends on the claimed benefit; off-treatment persistence is not a universal gate for every useful product. A new indication or nerve claim requires its own evidence, comparator and value assessment rather than inheriting a successful epithelial result. This package specifies decision requirements, not a new experimental design, dose, sample size or numeric threshold.

## Keep the next commitment bounded

The inherited **$40–150k / 3–6-month** envelope is retained only as provenance for a potentially informative local-product and active-comparator decision. It has not been shown executable at either end. Suitable model access, a qualified article, interpretable exposure and relevant product behavior must fit the actual scope before that range can be treated as a work order.

**A preliminary qualification deliverable does not inherit the original rank or EV rationale.** If the work answers only identity, assembly, exposure or model readiness, it buys less than the original comparator/function question. Recompare that actual information yield and the remaining cost to an informative answer, even if the initial price remains unchanged. A qualification result can still be valuable if it cheaply stops an unproductive route; it is not the same information as successful comparative repair.

The range excludes rights and license payments, major new model construction, a new chemistry or broad formulation campaign, a complete sterile ophthalmic development and validation package, GLP toxicology, GMP clinical manufacture, patient trials and commercialization. The paper and patent do not establish these costs or their timing. A physical gel-state observation does not validate shelf life, sterility, in-use protection or retained functional potency.

The retained commercial value also remains unpriced. Public disclosure may make a research article identifiable while leaving the investable contribution dependent on rights, product evidence, manufacturing know-how or a distinct improvement. Chinese application and grant records do not establish worldwide exclusivity or freedom to operate. The old grant's Arg/Ala discrepancy is preserved as a real source limitation and is not silently corrected into a legal conclusion.
