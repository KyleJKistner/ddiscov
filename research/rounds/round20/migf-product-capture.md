## Product readiness is a separate question

The earlier patent reports that refrigerated gels maintained their gel state without precipitation for at least one month. That is a physical-state observation. It is not a demonstrated shelf life for a sterile, packaged ophthalmic product, a retained potency result, or an in-use contamination assessment. Patent statements that preparation is simple or low cost likewise supply no executable price or validated commercial process. [S3, S4]

For a product developed as a U.S. ophthalmic drug, FDA's CGMP discussion identifies sterility through shelf life, container protection and multidose in-use contamination control as substantive product considerations. Separately, FDA's December 2023 topical-ophthalmic-quality page remains labeled **draft, nonbinding and not for implementation**. Its listed topics include impurities, particulates, container delivery, stability and optional release/dissolution controls for relevant dosage forms. The draft is not presented as binding regulation or a fixed checklist for mIGF. This audit does not settle the product's regulatory classification or approval route. [S7, S8]

The financial implication is that a reproducible chemical sequence is only part of the proposed asset. The useful unit is the **specified material, assembled formulation, delivery presentation and clinical claim together**. Assembly and rheology can affect retention and delivery; processing or packaging can change what reaches the eye. Accordingly, identity, useful exposure, practical handling, optical performance and retained bioactivity must remain linked in the costed product question. This is decision reasoning, not a manufacturing recipe, a new assay protocol or a claim that all later development requirements belong in the first tranche.

No validated sterile supply route, commercial presentation, complete shelf/in-use stability package, regulatory agreement or actual development quote was established by the sources reviewed here. Neither a clear sequence nor a positive rat result fills those gaps. Absence from this bounded review is not proof that such information exists nowhere.

## Incremental value and capture

The original investment premise was a bounded comparison with a practical topical-insulin product, asking whether mIGF yields better useful repair or meaningfully lowers the burden of obtaining comparable repair. The clinical comparator lane supplies the current human evidence and its population/formulation limits. This capture lane does not equate insulin with free IGF-1, import one regimen's outcome into another, or assume a universally negligible insulin compounding/handling cost. [L1, L2]

Equivalent useful repair could still be valuable if a verified reduction in treatment burden, reliable handling or another practical benefit is large enough to justify the product's price and development cost. Conversely, a difference in animal dosing frequency does not establish human adherence, convenience, willingness to pay or an incremental clinical effect. Those remain hypotheses. Matching a cheap, manageable comparator without a useful additional advantage would weaken the independent financial route even if mIGF is biologically active.

The share of value ddiscov could retain is also unresolved. A useful formulation/data contribution may support a commercial option, but publication does not allocate that option to ddiscov. A potentially valuable indication, a patent application, a research supply and a licensable commercial package are different assets. Rights payments, royalties, partner participation, remaining product costs and dilution could consume an apparent benefit. No values for those inputs were estimated here, and there is no new NPV or probability model.

## Keep the assumed tranche finite

The prior independent review placed mIGF fifth with a **US$40,000–150,000 / 3–6-month** judgmental envelope for a local-product/active-comparator question, conditional on a suitable model. The preserved report input remains the provenance. The envelope is not a quote, a full development budget, or evidence that either end is feasible. [L2]

A preliminary article/exposure/model-readiness deliverable that cannot answer the original useful-function/comparator question **does not inherit the fifth-place rank or EV rationale**, even if its price is unchanged. Compare what it actually resolves, the remaining commitment needed to learn the useful answer, other eligible opportunities, and waiting. A qualification failure can rationally stop investment without proving that the biological mechanism failed.

Major new model construction, medicinal-chemistry changes, a broad formulation campaign, rights acquisition, a full sterile ophthalmic development package, clinical supply, toxicology, a patient trial and commercialization are not silently included in the inherited envelope. Their relevance and costs were not resolved. Expansion of cost or time, or reduction of information yield, requires a new comparison. No portion of the envelope is released by this source review.
