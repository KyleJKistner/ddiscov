## Material changes

The gel's rat repair and small sensory signals remain. The audit distinguishes acute injury, steroid-suppressed healing and treatment after an already persistent deficit; it retains the existing material controls. Tracer fluorescence, rabbit aqueous-humor PK and nominal gel concentration do not establish intact active exposure at the epithelial target. Similar small-group means and reduced rat dosing frequency do not demonstrate noninferiority or human convenience.

Insulin is a substantive clinical comparator. A multicenter persistent-defect series and a historical-control comparison add context beyond acute postoperative studies and isolated cases. Their populations, exact formulations, background care and follow-up differ. Observed healing proportions are not causal rates, unequal follow-up cannot establish recurrence superiority, and units per drop cannot be substituted for units per milliliter. Mixed sensory observations prevent assuming that insulin lacks nerve-related potential. Trial plans remain separate from results.

The older Nankai grant explicitly describes the Arg-ending mIGF sequence, while its sequence list ends in Ala and granted claim 1 refers to that list. The inconsistency is present in the original document and remains unresolved. The newer corneal application is corroborated by Nankai's institutional record. Neither record establishes settled exact claim coverage, current title, a license, global freedom to operate or a ddiscov-owned asset. Refrigerated physical gel-state retention is not a validated sterile, potent ophthalmic shelf life.

The inherited **$40–150k / 3–6-month** assumption concerns a useful local-product/active-comparator decision. A preliminary qualification scope that answers less does not inherit rank five or its EV rationale, even at the same price. Compare actual information, remaining costs, practical benefit, retained value and waiting. Useful comparable repair with a meaningful reduction in total burden could have value, but fewer instillations alone do not establish that advantage.
