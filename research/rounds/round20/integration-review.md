## Review and evidence limits

Three independent cross-reviews cover nine authored files: the article and comparator pairs; the capture pair; and the root decision pair plus preserved original ranking input. Reviewers do not review their own authored lane. Every review binds its inputs by path, byte count and SHA-256 and distinguishes publication blockers from explicitly retained uncertainties.

The main-text audit preserves the gel's functional evidence and its existing material controls. The acute and delayed models, sample counts, biological-unit uncertainty, treatment timing and sensory method gaps remain separate. The unread supplement is an access limit, not proof of absent data. Tracer fluorescence and rabbit aqueous PK are not substituted for intact epithelial target exposure. A practical product comparison does not universally require equal molarity between distinct molecules.

The comparator update distinguishes acute postoperative diabetic defects from persistent epithelial defects and neurotrophic disease. Historical controls and uncontrolled series do not establish unbiased causal effects. The historical insulin/serum groups have unequal follow-up, so their recurrence proportions cannot establish controlled durability superiority. Product identities, vehicles, unit conventions and storage observations are source-specific. Sensory findings are mixed; current comparator programs are not completed results.

The patent audit distinguishes an exact disclosed sequence from settled granted coverage. The Arg/Ala inconsistency was checked in the original grant, not silently harmonized. The older application's wording is not carried into the grant unchanged; the newer corneal record is an application. Institutional attribution, official current title, license availability, validity and freedom to operate remain separate. Physical gel-state stability does not establish sterile product shelf life or retained potency.

These limits remain material to later development. Passing an audit means they are accurately recorded and the decision is appropriately conditional; it does not mean the missing scientific, legal or financial facts have been established.

## Marginal value and claim interpretation

The prior $40–150k / 3–6-month envelope is an unverified analyst assumption. A qualification-only deliverable that cannot answer the original practical comparator question cannot inherit its rank or EV rationale, even at unchanged price. Recompare actual information yield and the remaining costs of obtaining a useful answer. Rights, major new model construction, a broad formulation campaign, complete sterile ophthalmic development, clinical manufacture, toxicology, trials and commercialization are not silently included.

A useful practical advantage can include comparable repair with less total treatment burden. A smaller number of instillations alone is insufficient if offset by poor optical performance, handling, tolerability or repair. Likewise, an epithelial repair claim, a durable recurrence benefit and nerve restoration require different evidence. Useful therapy need not provide permanent off-treatment cure. No new experiment, numeric threshold or dosing recommendation is generated.
