## Selected uncertainty

**Enasidenib for germline IDH2-R140Q D2HGA2: what incremental clinical or product contribution remains beyond the existing pediatric sprinkle, and through which approval, exclusivity or contractual route could a developer capture value?** The immediate choice is between an indication-development package using an adequate existing article, a genuinely improved presentation, investigator-led evidence, and stopping or redirecting the next dollar.

The question precedes any assumption that a new infant formulation deserves funding. It is also narrower than asking whether enasidenib works: the existing exact-disease clinical observations justify continued clinical-development consideration, while the current task asks which next expenditure can change an investment decision. The [evidence audit](enasidenib-product-capture.md) and [funding decision](funding-decision.md) complete that question within the permitted document-only scope.

## Decision rule

Choose the smallest feasible information tranche whose plausible answers change the best subsequent action after accounting for future costs, time, competition and capturable value. Compare it with other tranches and retaining the money. Historical spending and the current allocation are not evidence of a marginal return. Patient benefit, scientific information and financial capture are valuable but different objectives; do not silently substitute one for another.

Conceptually, information value is the expected value of the best continuation available after the answer, including stopping, minus the value of the best continuation now and the cost of obtaining the answer. Apply the same remaining-cost and time basis on both sides. No probability distribution, revenue forecast, numerical NPV, simulation or claimed dollar optimum is supplied here: the repository does not establish the required costs, commercial terms, reachable population, uptake or approval timing. A qualitative choice under those limits is more defensible than fabricated precision.

The [Round 8 allocation](../round8/portfolio-allocation.md) explicitly reflected the earlier instruction to concentrate effort on BLU. Its 55/15/10 leading points measure planning effort, not money or success probability. Round 15 preserves that historical record but supersedes it as a rule for choosing a financial or evidence marginal dollar. The chosen sprint does not assert that enasidenib has the highest total asset NPV.

## Completed information gain

The sprint separates three previously conflated possible expenditures. Existing-article indication development remains plausible; independent formulation invention has no demonstrated incremental need in the reviewed public record; useful investigator-led evidence does not automatically become an exclusive drug asset. Current U.S. rules exclude an enasidenib rare pediatric disease voucher on the already-approved-active-moiety condition, while leaving conditional orphan and new-clinical-investigation exclusivity mechanisms. A new formulation is not required merely to pursue a first D2HGA2 indication.

The primary-source delta is regulatory and decisional. The sprint adds **no new patient outcome, validated infant delivery need, dose, formulation performance result or exact-use screen**. The previously known sprinkle, patent and adult formulation study are inputs, not rediscoveries. No prior valuation is claimed to have counted voucher revenue.

## Stop and reopening rule

This public-source pass is complete. Do not start another generic formulation, CNS, patent or prevalence search to fill an unpriced business model. Reopen only for a specific new document that can change the action: actual incumbent administration limitations in the intended population; an adequate existing-article route and usable product information; a credible rights/supply or regulatory-use position; a substantive exact-disease clinical/program update; or a rule change affecting the contemplated route. A rights fact can be necessary for commercial execution without making an owner reply a prerequisite for scientific selection.

No outreach, purchases, recruitment, experiments, simulations or material receipt occurred. No development spend, contact, trial or material order is authorized by the recommendation.
