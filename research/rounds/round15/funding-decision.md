# Round 15 funding decision

Evidence cut: 4 November 2026. Decision unit: the next marginal evidence/development dollar for the existing enasidenib/D2HGA2 opportunity. This is a decision recommendation; current authority remains document review, deterministic integrity checks and repository publication.

## Decision

**Retain enasidenib's affirmative clinical-development recommendation. Hold automatic funding of a new pediatric presentation. Prioritize a defined first-D2HGA2-indication contribution using a suitable existing article when evaluating the next development commitment.** If an independent presentation is proposed, first establish the residual administration need and meaningful benefit over that article. Do not make formulation novelty a prerequisite for all value creation.

The [selection memo](marginal-dollar-selection.md) explains why this uncertainty received the sprint. The [evidence audit](enasidenib-product-capture.md) and [structured decision](funding-decision.json) control the distinctions below.

## Minimal evidence for a later development commitment

The next commitment must name the proposed article and indication, the decision-changing deliverable, the best available comparator, and the action taken on success, failure or an inconclusive result. For an existing-article route, identify usable supply/product information and the development or regulatory-data contribution. For a new presentation, additionally identify the actual administration problem and the meaningful improvement. Commercial permission and rights must be sufficient for the proposed transaction or execution, not assumed from published science or a potential regulatory pathway.

Evaluate remaining development costs, time to useful output, credible clinical/evidentiary success, reachable users, current/future competition and the share of value the proposed developer could retain. These inputs remain unknown here. Do not replace them with voucher proceeds, an orphan-label monopoly assumption, the published case count or arbitrary probability weights. An unanswered commercial term can block a particular investment without making an owner reply the gate for scientific selection.

This list specifies what makes a proposed commitment intelligible; it is not an instruction to run another unlimited diligence pass. Within the present restrictions, the selected public-source audit is complete. A later source-specific desk task should have a named new artifact and a plausible decision consequence. Otherwise retain the option to wait or redirect.

## Portfolio and closure

The old 100-point queue remains available as a historical planning record; it no longer determines the next marginal-dollar choice. No replacement allocation or numerical asset ranking is invented. BLU retains its staged scientific qualification package, and the other candidates retain their current evidence-based dispositions. Round 14's HHD interpretation remains intact.

Reopen this enasidenib contribution audit only for substantive primary clinical/product/program evidence, documentary incumbent administration limitations, a materially different rights/supply/regulatory-use position, or a relevant rule change. Preserve all existing closed-search boundaries. There is no contact request, procurement, recruitment, experiment, simulation, material receipt or automated monitoring action in this decision.
