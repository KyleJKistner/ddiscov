Evidence cut: 4 November 2026. Existing candidate `R5_ENASIDENIB_IDH2_R140_D2HGA2`. See the [structured evidence and source ledger](enasidenib-product-capture.json) for source identifiers, dates, locations and access limits.

## Finding

**A first D2HGA2 indication-development package using an adequate existing article remains a live opportunity. A new pediatric formulation has not yet demonstrated an incremental need.** Current law excludes rare pediatric disease priority-review-voucher revenue for the already-approved enasidenib moiety. It does not categorically exclude orphan exclusivity for a new D2HGA2 indication. The selected product, clinical evidence, rights, supply and eventual commercial capture remain separate decisions.

This changes what the next expenditure must buy. It supports examining a defined indication-development contribution before assuming formulation invention. It does not reject the therapy, establish a financeable standalone asset, or require a new animal experiment before clinical development.

## Current U.S. regulatory findings

### Rare pediatric disease priority review voucher: exclude for this moiety

Current 21 U.S.C. §360ff(a)(4)(B)(i) excludes a small-molecule product containing an active moiety previously approved under the listed NDA/ANDA routes. Enasidenib was approved in 2017. A new pediatric indication, dosage form or salt of the same moiety does not satisfy this requirement. Assign **zero voucher revenue** to the exact enasidenib opportunity; this is a legal-condition exclusion, not an estimated low probability. The current award sunset is September 30, 2029; it does not cure the exclusion. [L1, L2, P3]

Do not reject 505(b)(2) categorically on the separate statutory reference to applications submitted under 505(b)(1). FDA explains that 505(b)(2) NDAs are submitted under that subsection; the RPD draft's footnote 9 expressly preserves this interpretation. The approved-moiety condition independently decides the enasidenib case. Old dates in the 2019 draft do not override the current statute. [L3, L4]

This new audit does not correct an alleged earlier voucher valuation: none was established in the prior package. It also gives no automatic voucher credit to an unapproved alternative such as BLU or P2. All applicable disease, evidence, application, timing and ownership conditions would still need support.

### Orphan exclusivity: possible for a new indication, not established

Current §360cc provides seven-year protection against specified same-drug approvals for the same approved use or indication, subject to its conditions and exceptions. Its clinical-superiority provision concerns the same use or indication as an already approved drug. Prior AML approval alone therefore does not automatically require clinical superiority for a first D2HGA2 indication. Designation, approval, exact indication and applicant entitlement remain unestablished here. [L5]

The February 2026 amendment explicitly addresses the approved use or indication. Do not use an obsolete disease-wide reading or describe molecule-wide exclusivity. The inspected FDA enasidenib record concerns AML; its August 2024 exclusivity expiry says nothing by itself about patent expiry or a future D2HGA2 award. This inspection is not an exhaustive orphan-designation census. [L5, L6]

### Three-year exclusivity: clinical contribution matters

21 CFR 314.108 requires qualifying new clinical investigations, other than bioavailability studies, essential to approval and conducted or sponsored by the applicant. A BA/PK-only bridge does not earn this protection merely because a presentation is new. Protection concerns the relevant approved conditions or change. [L7]

FDA's March 2026 **draft, nonbinding, not-for-implementation** guidance distinguishes a study with an essential safety/effectiveness purpose from PK-only work with routine safety monitoring. PK measurements do not disqualify a genuinely qualifying mixed-purpose investigation. Whether a proposed package meets the requirements is application-specific; no award is inferred from independent published case reports or a proposed formulation budget. [L8]

### 505(b)(2): potential evidence route, separate from permission and capture

The route can support qualifying modifications, including a new indication or dosage form, using an appropriate evidence package and scientifically justified reliance. It may permit reliance on information without an applicant-owned reference right to every underlying study. It does not eliminate CMC evidence, relevant clinical evidence, bridging or applicable patent certifications/statements. No project-specific FDA agreement on an application strategy or approval was established in this audit. [L3, L9]

A right to rely on regulatory findings is not a manufacturing license, access to a proprietary product dossier, a supply agreement or patent clearance. Existing patent findings remain historical inputs; this sprint does not reopen a patent landscape or deliver a freedom-to-operate opinion.

| Opportunity | Potential useful contribution | Capture route that remains conditional |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Existing-article D2HGA2 indication development** | A defined R140Q population, usable pediatric exposure/PD framework, prospective organ benefit and safety, and a more reliable labeled treatment pathway | A sponsor-aligned or licensed development arrangement; a qualifying first-indication approval; useful regulatory data or a transaction. No novel presentation is required merely to make this route coherent. |
| **B. Improved pediatric presentation** | A measured administration, dose delivery, stability, burden or tolerability benefit in a documented subgroup | An executable article, supporting clinical/CMC evidence and relevant rights. A new presentation alone confers neither orphan exclusivity nor three-year exclusivity. |
| **C. Investigator-led evidence** | Better longitudinal clinical knowledge, evidence supporting care, or trial readiness | Potential dataset/partnership value if regulatory usability and relevant rights are established; owning data does not confer ownership of enasidenib or guaranteed sales. |

The alternatives can overlap, but their budgets and claimed benefits must not be conflated. Data custody, publication rights, regulatory use/reference rights, manufacturing permission and commercialization rights are distinct. A commercially useful dataset need not own the molecule; conversely, scientifically useful data need not create a privately capturable drug franchise.

FDA recognizes medically appropriate prescribing of approved drugs for unapproved uses. Together with the indication-specific exclusivity scope, this supports retaining incumbent/off-label competition in the economic case. It does not quantify uptake or deny value from a pediatric label, qualified presentation, reliable access or a partner transaction. [L5, L10; economic inference]

## What determines the next expenditure

An adequate existing article can avoid inventing a formulation, conditional on an executable supply and usable-information route. A new article adds formulation identity, content/delivery uniformity, solid form, dissolution, impurities, excipients, stability, packaging and administration work. Both routes retain exposure, outcome ascertainment and safety requirements from the clinical dossier. These are cost drivers, not quotes or measured timelines. No market size, reachable patient count, recruitment rate, revenue, discount rate, royalty or success probability is estimated.

The [funding decision](funding-decision.md) closes the desk pass: preserve affirmative clinical development, hold automatic formulation spending, and specify the documentary facts that could change that decision. Closed CNS, BLU, zinc, SLC6A6 and HHD retrieval remain closed. No outreach, purchases, recruitment, experiments, simulations or material receipt occurred.
