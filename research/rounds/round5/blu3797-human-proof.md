# BLU3797: a decision-ready human-function proof

**Assessment: 4 November 2026.** This is a proposed research comparison, not an executed experiment. BLU3797 remains the first staged human-function priority. The additional work makes that decision more concrete; it does not establish a clinical development candidate or S-tier asset.

The central question is now narrow: **can independently authenticated BLU3797 improve force and fatigue in human MTM1-deficient muscle after a reproducible functional deficit exists, at exposures that spare relevant healthy and disease-vulnerable tissues?** A repeat improvement in differentiation markers would not answer it. The first claim is post-deficit human functional benefit. A mature-muscle reversal claim additionally requires prespecified physiological and structural maturity criteria; an observable deficit alone does not establish an adult-like state.

### A useful correction to model interpretation

The manufacturer's isogenic DMD and DM1 products do not imply an isogenic XLMTM pair. Likewise, the commercial p.L463S model and the academic p.E48* model are distinct. The latter cannot supply a matched control for the former.

Inspection of the public Curi graphs also shows that healthy-control force changes substantially over the displayed maturation period. The size of the disease/control gap depends on time and plate format. These are manufacturer characterization graphs, with no donor/lot/experimental hierarchy displayed in the image. Their reported force difference therefore motivates a qualification experiment; it does not establish a fixed assay window or independent replication. Do not select a favorable measurement day after seeing the drug result. [Model page and linked figures](https://www.curibio.com/xlmtm-model).

There is a practical route even if an academic line is not distributed: use appropriately licensed public starting materials or a parental line and construct the required controls independently. The scientific decision does not require an author reply. Availability of an exact model, permission for a proposed use, identity of a received lot and useful-function qualification remain separate statuses.

## 2. What the BLU3797 source data actually support

The [cell-context audit](blu3797-cell-context-audit.json), generated by [the read-only extraction script](../../../methods/blu3797_cell_context_audit.py), preserves the published workbook values and their cell addresses. A second parser, openpyxl, matched all **887 populated cells** across the eleven examined sheets. This verifies extraction, not experimental validity.

The source study selected one MTM1-null C2C12 clone after observing clone-to-clone heterogeneity, including impaired myogenic identity in nontargeting controls. BLU3797 was added after 72 hours of differentiation and remained for48 hours. That is a real delayed addition within differentiation; it is not a demonstration that a mature, force-deficient human tissue recovered. Figure 3 describes representative observations from repeated experiments, with imaging fields and n=10. The released sheets lack a complete experiment/well/field hierarchy, so ten source values cannot be recast as ten independent cultures or biological donors. [Shearer et al., Results, Figure 3 and Methods](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/198568).

Numeric strings with asterisks remain included; no source exclusion key was recovered. The audit also provides unmarked-only sensitivity descriptions, without adopting an exclusion. Increased nuclei per structure with fewer structures is compatible with enhanced fusion, but neither quantity establishes force restoration. No new p-values or potency fit are calculated.

Two context boundaries prevent accidental evidence inflation:

* Supplemental Figure 2 uses **PIK3C2B-depleted MTM1-null cells**, not healthy WT muscle. Its baseline is not a healthy normalization denominator for Figure 3.
* Figure 5A-D concerns genetic PIK3C2B inactivation in MTM1-null mouse muscle, whereas Figure 5E-F intentionally studies **PIK3C3/VPS34-KO C2C12 cells**. The workbook and article are consistent. The latter lipidomics result cannot be relabeled direct lipidomic normalization of MTM1-deficient human muscle by BLU3797. Its 21 lipid classes contain reported nominal significance flags for 7 KO comparisons and 1 WT comparison; no adjusted q-value column is present in that sheet. [Primary article and source workbook](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/198568).

The [round4 audit](../round4/blu3797-audit.md) remains applicable: wire hanging has a 60-second ceiling, the small late-start experiment has an unresolved dosing-label conflict, and incomplete baseline records and missing animal identifiers limit exact reconstruction. These limitations narrow the transferable claim; they do not erase the reported survival and motor response.

## 3. Stage A: qualify the question before interpreting the drug

The minimum model package should contain an MTM1 disease background with a matched gene-corrected control, plus an independent disease genotype/background. A healthy-parent/engineered-MTM1-loss pair is a useful orthogonal control, but should not be counted as a second patient. Multiple clones from one donor assess editing and clone effects; they do not increase the donor count.

Qualification must establish all of the following:

1. **A measurable functional deficit.** Show the disease/control difference in absolute twitch force, an appropriate force-frequency measure and fatigue under a prespecified stimulation challenge. Record tissue dimensions, viable muscle content and specific force as complementary measures. Normalization must not conceal an absolute loss of useful force.
2. **An interpretable treatment window.** Establish the baseline trajectory before allocation. A stable or predictably changing deficit is acceptable if contemporaneous controls and baseline adjustment can distinguish recovery from maturation or spontaneous drift. A transient peak in healthy controls is not an adequate denominator.
3. **MTM1 dependence and post-deficit modifiability.** Prior genetic correction establishes that MTM1 causes the deficit; it may prevent disease without showing that an already-deficient tissue can recover. Separately, qualify a functional positive intervention applied after the same deficit, ideally temporally controlled MTM1 restoration. If prior correction works but no delayed positive intervention does, the post-deficit rescue question remains unresolved in that assay. If a delayed positive control works and appropriately verified delayed PIK3C2B perturbation fails, the target's post-deficit hypothesis is challenged. If target perturbation works but BLU fails, demote the molecule rather than the whole target.
4. **Reliable biological units.** Randomize at the tissue level within independently repeated differentiations, retain donor/clone/batch/tissue identifiers, and mask functional and image analysis. Treat time points and repeated stimulations as repeated measurements. Use a qualification pilot to estimate between-batch and between-donor variability before choosing the confirmatory sample size.
5. **Reproducible material.** Authenticate exact BLU3797 stereochemical identity and analytical purity. Document the vehicle, measured dissolved concentration and stability in the actual assay medium. A supplier page or joined literature route does not replace received-lot characterization.

Failure at this stage stops interpretation of a therapeutic effect in that model. It is a model/material failure, not automatically a biological rejection of PIK3C2B.

## 4. Stage B: the discriminating comparison

| Comparison | Decision it resolves |
|---|---|
| Disease vehicle versus disease BLU3797, started after the qualified deficit | Whether the exact compound recovers useful function after disease expression |
| Matched corrected vehicle and corrected BLU3797 | Size of the recoverable disease gap; generic muscle stimulation, growth or toxicity |
| Delayed, titrated PIK3C2B genetic perturbation | Whether target reduction after the qualified tissue deficit shares the pharmacological effect and how the target-response relation behaves |
| An independently authenticated second chemical scaffold, only after cellular target/selectivity qualification | Whether the phenotype is a shared target effect rather than BLU-specific pharmacology |
| A disease-relevant functional comparator such as the independently reported mTOR-pathway intervention, at measured tolerated exposure | Whether BLU offers a useful differentiated effect; no cross-study superiority is assumed |
| Continuous exposure, a prespecified pulse and measured washout | Whether maintenance depends on continued exposure and whether a short pulse creates lasting function |

BLU2720 is a less potent related compound, not an inherently inactive control. Its different cellular engagement has to be measured in the same system. A nominally identical dose does not make the comparison exposure-matched. Newly disclosed PIK3C2B scaffolds are candidates for orthogonal qualification; a patent enzyme value alone does not qualify them as human-cell probes. See the companion pharmacology review for exact entities and limitations.

The primary estimand should be the difference in **change from baseline** between allocated disease-treatment and disease-vehicle tissues, analyzed with the donor/clone/batch structure preserved. Report the raw force outcome alongside the fraction of the contemporaneous genetically rescuable gap recovered. Do not report a rescue fraction when that denominator is absent, unstable or too small to interpret. Fatigue should be reported with absolute force retained across the challenge, so a low starting force cannot look favorable merely because its relative decline is small.

Before unblinding the confirmatory comparison, lock an assay-repeatability floor and a distinct minimum useful effect informed by the correction-control gap, the active comparator and the intended product benefit. Statistical precision alone cannot determine usefulness. Lock the exposure range and unacceptable healthy-tissue effect as well. Advance only when the lower confidence bound for the prespecified functional estimand exceeds the larger of the two effect thresholds. An interval entirely below the useful threshold supports futility; an interval crossing it is inconclusive. Preserve donor/batch dependence when estimating that uncertainty. There is no justified clinical minimum or power calculation in the present public data, so this document does not invent one.

Mechanistic measurements should accompany function: target engagement, calcium handling, relevant endosomal lipid/signaling readouts and muscle structure. Because PIK3C2B affects integrin trafficking, preserve passive tension/geometry or an equivalent check on force transmission and attachment. Improved mechanical transmission may be useful biology, but need not establish normalized intrinsic excitation–contraction coupling. These measurements help distinguish explanations and cannot overrule a negative functional result. The published withdrawal experiment, in which P62 remained improved while function declined, is a specific reason to avoid making a single molecular marker the success criterion. [Primary BLU3797 study](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/198568); [primary integrin study](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2202236119).

## 5. Exposure and tissue window

Pair function with measured drug concentrations in medium and tissue, including the unbound fraction where interpretable, and with cellular target engagement. Preserve the difference among biochemical IC50, NanoBRET engagement, corrected graphical thresholds and functional exposure. The released mouse plasma observations do not themselves establish a free human-muscle concentration or a complete concentration-time profile.

The safety comparison needs to test the exposures that actually produce muscle benefit. Healthy neuronal-network function is relevant because human PIK3C2B loss-of-function evidence raises a seizure-susceptibility hypothesis; genetic loss does not demonstrate that adult BLU3797 dosing will cause seizures. MTM1-related hepatobiliary vulnerability makes disease-context canalicular function relevant as well. Such models require their own qualification. These are discriminating measurements, not a presumption that the drug is toxic. The companion biology review separates genetic, disease, drug and tissue contexts.

Equal-free-concentration comparisons across culture systems characterize intrinsic tissue sensitivity; they do not establish the systemic window. Connect the muscle-effective exposure to vulnerable-tissue exposure using measured or suitably bounded unbound muscle/plasma and brain/plasma relationships, with relevant metabolites considered separately. No favorable brain exclusion or tissue distribution is established for BLU3797 here. A concerning in-vitro overlap with unknown distribution holds the window unresolved; a measured overlap at the exposures needed in vivo supports a stop. Low brain exposure would still leave disease-liver exposure to resolve. A reduction in a molecular marker cannot override adverse function in a tissue actually exposed to its harmful range.

## 6. Decisions after the proof

| Observed result | Portfolio action |
|---|---|
| Substantial delayed force and fatigue recovery across qualified backgrounds, with orthogonal target support and a tissue window | Advance exact BLU3797 or an explicitly identified development analogue into a larger translational package; then assess differentiation, chronic exposure and product viability. |
| Strong target-genetic rescue but BLU fails at adequate engagement or has no usable window | Retain the target hypothesis; demote the exact molecule and evaluate a different scaffold or tissue-exposure strategy. Do not transfer BLU efficacy to the replacement. |
| Morphology, fusion or markers improve without useful force | Do not promote the therapeutic claim. Consider a narrower developmental/model use only if it has independent value. |
| Benefit exists during ongoing maturation after a measured deficit, but not in a later qualified state | Retain a narrower post-deficit developmental-compensation hypothesis if useful function is substantial. It does not establish mature chronic reversal. Pure pretreatment remains prevention. |
| One genotype responds and another qualified genotype does not | Reassess a genotype-defined program; do not average away nonresponse or claim all XLMTM. |
| Force improves only by generalized stimulation while fatigue or healthy tissue worsens | Stop the current functional-rescue thesis at that exposure. |
| Prior correction prevents the deficit, but no delayed positive intervention changes it | The assay establishes disease dependence but has not established post-deficit modifiability. Requalify the rescue question before declaring target failure. |
| A matched correction control does not improve the supposed disease deficit | Rebuild or retire the assay before using it to decide disease-specific efficacy. |

The most valuable next evidence is a controlled human-function result that separates these branches. More repeated mouse markers, more catalog searches or a composite ranking score would leave the main uncertainty intact.

## 7. What would make this fundable

A credible initial funding proposition is a bounded milestone: **independent human muscle-function rescue with a measured target and tissue window**. The package should show which result advances the exact article, which result supports the target but rejects the article, and which result ends the biological thesis. It should also name the current comparator and the differentiated product claim the successful result could support.

This is earlier than an investable clinical asset. No budget, timeline, licensing outcome, clinical efficacy probability or investor commitment has been established. Public synthesis, scientific selection, commercial claims and material access remain distinct. Additional literature can sharpen the experiment and uncover fatal facts; it cannot substitute for the missing human functional result.
