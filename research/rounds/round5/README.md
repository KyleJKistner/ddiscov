# Round 5: BLU3797 development proof and broader challenge

**Evidence cut: 4 November 2026.** This round gives BLU3797 additional target, pharmacology and human-model attention while screening other exact intervention/use pairs. It updates the [current shortlist](../../../decisions/current-shortlist.md), [selection policy](../../../decisions/selection-policy.md) and schema-5 [cumulative register](../../portfolio-register.json).

## Outcome

**BLU3797 remains the leading staged human-function proof. No S-tier asset is declared.** The target rationale remains substantial, the public material and comparator routes are more specific, and the missing human-function/exposure evidence is now attached to a discriminating development plan. Extra attention sharpened the test rather than insulating the candidate from unfavorable evidence.

The round contains **12 new exact intervention/use screens** and material updates to **seven existing candidates**: four core programs, RM4404, APR-246/AEC and the existing dantrolene/Darier comparator. Four new conditional reserves have meaningful human evidence. These counts are not counts of independent molecules, validated drugs or successful experiments.

### BLU3797: evidence and the next decision

| Question | Current assessment | Next evidence that would change the decision |
|---|---|---|
| Is PIK3C2B a credible disease target? | Protein-retaining catalytic genetics and inducible post-onset intervention support the hypothesis in mice. Allele dose, tissue and developmental context remain important. | Delayed catalytic perturbation restores useful function in qualified human MTM1 tissue. |
| Is exact BLU3797 an active article? | Defined S structure, cellular engagement and mouse pharmacology support activity. | Authenticated independent material, transferred engagement/selectivity and direct human function. |
| Is a public experimental route concrete? | Joined upstream/final chemistry and human model precedents are identified. No lot was received or reproduced. | Article identity, model qualification and delayed functional modifiability. |
| Does the released efficacy data show human muscle recovery? | No. New source extraction concerns differentiation/fusion and separate lipidomic context; the animal motor assay has a ceiling. | Useful force/fatigue recovery after an established human deficit, with independent biological backgrounds. |
| Is a selective systemic exposure window established? | No. Released mouse plasma observations are terminal snapshots without sufficient timing, free fraction or tissue distribution. | Effective muscle exposure connected to brain/liver exposure and functional adverse-effect ranges. |
| Is it a fundable finished asset? | Not established. A bounded human-function milestone is a more defensible proposition. | Independent function, exposure, comparator value and a defined product/value-capture plan. |

The [human proof](blu3797-human-proof.md) incorporates an [independent challenge](round5_blu_proof_challenge.md). It separates target failure, molecule failure and assay failure. It requires a delayed positive functional control, preserves predictable maturation rather than demanding flat force, and distinguishes a useful-effect threshold from technical precision. Equal culture concentrations are not assumed to establish a systemic window.

## Findings that materially changed interpretation

**BLU source and pharmacology:** the [additional cell audit](blu3797-cell-context-audit.json) retains marked values and panel context. A second parser matched 887 populated cells across eleven sheets. Figure 5E–F intentionally concerns PIK3C3/VPS34-knockout cells; this is not a labeling error. The [pharmacology review](round5_blu_pharmacology.md) separately records an unresolved finite VPS34 IC50 versus all-zero released responses, the enzyme-concentration qualification of the 28 nM potency, terminal plasma samples, and exact comparator compounds 47/91. These findings do not establish a new toxic off-target or a failed efficacy experiment.

**P2:** an operational transport assay can lack the electrical deficit required for a repair claim. The [leader update](round5_existing_leader_updates.md) identifies independent isogenic and patient models, a second-allele rat route, and the solvent-transfer question. The [recovered off-target panel](p2-off-target-audit.md) contains 75 binding and 12 enzyme/cell rows. MAO-A's 59% is binding displacement, and hERG binding is not a current assay. A screening-dose/EC50 ratio is not a clinical safety margin.

**Darier:** the [dantrolene update](round5_ndc_comparator_update.md) closes the previously unread CTIS result gap. Sixteen adults started; ten completed and entered the paired analysis. The manuscript flow diagram assigns six early discontinuations to insufficient response. The positive completer PGA/DLQI signal is qualified by no control, changing denominators, retinoid withdrawal/restart and no direct barrier result. The uploaded reports are final-submitted but internally draft-labelled. Dantrolene becomes a concrete adult clinical benchmark; it supplies no topical NDC exposure or efficacy by analogy.

**PPCS:** the [clinical/function challenge](ppcs-clinical-function-challenge.md) verifies a separate severe infant case while retaining spontaneous prior recoveries and concurrent-care confounding in the larger series. Engineered cardiac patches stabilized without a significant force increase. The [chemistry challenge](round5_pantethine_chemistry_challenge.md) confirms a name/catalog inconsistency and reducing culture conditions, without asserting a wrong historical vial or invalid efficacy. Historical indirect plasma nondetection and newer relative LC-MS are compatible; neither establishes the missing quantitative oral-to-heart precursor bridge.

**Local challengers:** RM4404's published salt differs from an alternative supplier's free base. APR-246's cell timing, incomplete cornification, cream-dose wording and exact issued-claim predicate remain attached to its human observations. Both stay live candidates for independent qualification. NDC retains no automatic preference over them.

**New clinical challengers:** the LQT2 clinical product is Orkambi, while pure lumacaftor supplies the cell experiment. Independent G604S data show that improved trafficking can worsen electrical function. Enasidenib offers unusually direct causal-metabolite logic with human cardiac observations, but an exact-disease hepatic adverse report and pediatric delivery prevent an execution-ready label.

## Reproducibility and scope

The unchanged BLU workbook remains in [source data](../../source-data/README.md). Round 4 checked 815 populated cells in four sheets; round 5 checked 887 in eleven different sheets, giving 1,702 distinct checked cells. This is extraction verification, not independent biological replication. The new [reader](../../../methods/blu3797_cell_context_audit.py) reports descriptive values without fitting efficacy curves, imputing missing data or constructing animal links.

P2's original ZIP and the English CTIS result PDF were retrieved through normal public publisher/regulator routes. Their stable retrieval URLs, sizes and hashes are recorded. The P2 archive contains no animal-level efficacy table. The CTIS report provides aggregate estimates, not participant-level data suitable for a new efficacy calculation. No new p-values, clinical responder rates, received material or wet-lab results are represented.

Future research should use the current integrated decisions, retain negative follow-up and exact product identities, and seek evidence that changes one of the open gates. The research archive must not be used to resurrect superseded optimistic interpretations.
