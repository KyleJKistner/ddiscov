# Round 5 additional screen: five exact intervention/use pairs

**Evidence cut-off: 2026-11-04. Scope:** public scientific and programme evidence; no outreach, purchases, experiments, or remote changes. These are research decisions, not treatment recommendations.

## Decision

This pass found a substantial human electrophysiology challenger and a compelling genotype-specific metabolic rescue signal. It did **not** establish an S-tier development asset. The most useful discoveries were the boundaries that an abstract-led screen would miss: the clinical LQT2 product contains **lumacaftor and ivacaftor**, one apparently rescued hERG variant becomes electrophysiologically worse, a PFIC repurposing opportunity is already approved in Japan, and prospective progestin initiation failed in the CCHS patients who were newly treated.

| Exact intervention/use pair | Current disposition | Reason to retain or stop |
|---|---|---|
| Lumacaftor/ivacaftor (Orkambi), selected trafficking-defective **KCNH2/LQT2** | Retain as a serious **scientific challenger; development hold** | Human QTc signal with cellular concordance; variant-specific functional harm, short uncontrolled follow-up, and product/component attribution remain material. |
| Enasidenib, **germline IDH2-R140 D-2-hydroxyglutaric aciduria type II**, especially established cardiomyopathy | Retain as a **genotype-specific human rescue lead; formulation/programme hold** | Human cardiac improvement accompanies suppression of the causal metabolite. Chronic paediatric exposure, hepatic tolerability, and a differentiated development proposition remain unresolved. |
| Sodium 4-phenylbutyrate, **ABCB11/BSEP-trafficking PFIC2** | **Occupied, approved benchmark** | Genuine transport and human disease evidence; Japan approved the PFIC1/2 expansion in August 2026. A new project must add measurable value beyond the existing programme. |
| Desogestrel, **PHOX2B-PARM CCHS** ventilatory rescue | **Stop broad development claim** | Historical selected responders are not prospective replication. The newly treated patients failed; a separate combination-product trial also stopped after nonresponse. |
| Tebapivat (AG-946), **PIEZO1 hereditary xerocytosis** mature-red-cell rescue | **Stop high-priority rescue claim** | Metabolic activation did not generally correct the prespecified physical defect. The small KCNN4 subgroup is a separate hypothesis. |

These decisions do not automatically change the BLU3797 shortlist. They provide outside benchmarks for how much evidence a high rank should require.

## 1. Lumacaftor/ivacaftor for selected LQT2

### The intervention is resolved at the clinical-product level

The 2026 paper calls the intervention “lumacaftor,” but MAST2's primary registry and the investigators' university announcement identify **Orkambi**: 200 mg lumacaftor/125 mg ivacaftor per tablet, two tablets twice daily, up to seven days. The registry preserves background beta-blocker dose, has no placebo, and discontinues study drug at discharge. A pure-lumacaftor programme therefore cannot inherit combination-product clinical efficacy without a component bridge. [L2–L3]

The new publication reports 16 adults, mean per-patient QTc shortening of 31 ± 21 ms, and trafficking correction in three of five patient-derived cellular variants. In the baseline-QTc ≥500 ms subgroup, 75% reached a reduction of at least 40 ms. Seven days without observed ventricular arrhythmias establishes neither arrhythmia prevention nor chronic safety. [L1]

### The primary negative changes patient selection

An independent 2020 study tested three patient-derived cardiomyocyte lines. Lumacaftor restored trafficking in all three, but **G604S showed longer APD90**, whereas N633S and R685P improved. Restored membrane abundance is consequently an inadequate inclusion rule: a dysfunctional channel may reach the membrane without improving repolarization. [L5]

The accessible 2026 supplement uses **5 µM lumacaftor for seven days** in cells, at least three differentiations per line. W568C did not show trafficking rescue. Its QTc time-course plot has 14 patients at baseline and days 2–5, 12 at day 6 and 10 at day 7; later days were excluded from the paired omnibus analysis because observations were missing. Differentiations are not independent patient genotypes. [L4]

The whole 2026 article was access-limited in this pass; the abstract, public supplement, registry and investigator announcement were inspected. Exact QT correction, blinded adjudication, exposure results and missingness reasons must remain **unresolved**, not silently assumed favourable. Shortening associated with higher starting QTc also requires repeated baseline/control data to distinguish effect modification from regression toward the mean.

### Exposure and comparator

The current Orkambi label describes approximately 99% plasma protein binding for both components, a treatment-associated heart-rate reduction, and CYP3A induction. Thus 5 µM nominal culture concentration is not a validated unbound cardiac exposure bridge; heart-rate correction and background-drug exposure are decision-relevant. The label identifies a marketed CF product, not an LQT2 approval or a cleared commercial route for this project. [L6]

Mexiletine is a concrete comparator: a small retrospective potassium-channel LQTS series reported QTc responses in a substantial subset, including patients already taking beta-blockers. It was neither randomized nor a head-to-head comparison with Orkambi. Cross-study effect sizes cannot establish superiority. [L7]

**Decisive next proof:** an independent, variant-matched comparison of lumacaftor, ivacaftor and their combination after a reproducible electrophysiological deficit is established, with paced APD, IKr/gating and triggered-activity readouts at measured free exposures. Include G604S as a disconfirming control and a practical mexiletine comparator. A favourable trafficking blot with worse repolarization stops that variant. A later clinical claim requires controlled, heart-rate-robust, patient-level benefit that persists beyond initiation; the paper's 40 ms response threshold is a useful candidate benchmark, not an automatic safety or funding rule.

**Availability conclusion:** exact marketed combination and a completed investigator-led programme are public. Supply for a particular study, pure-component clinical equivalence, development rights and a differentiated business are not established. No contact is needed to recognize those distinct facts.

## 2. Enasidenib for IDH2-R140 D2HGA2

The 2023 primary report describes two children receiving reported daily doses of **50 and 60 mg**, normalized D-2-HG, and improved cardiac function in the child with severe dilated cardiomyopathy. Those are article-reported doses, not a proposed paediatric regimen or a verified salt/base conversion. The accessible abstract supports established-disease improvement; it does not supply an audited numerical ejection-fraction trajectory or controlled effect estimate. [E1]

A separate 2024 case provides both replication and a limitation: treatment began at age 3 years 11 months with 50 mg daily; cardiac function improved, but ALT reached 118 IU/L. Reducing dosing to three times weekly maintained metabolite suppression and improved laboratory abnormalities; further reduction to twice weekly allowed trough metabolite reappearance. The text describes biochemical improvement, although ALT/CK after reduction should not be represented as both unequivocally normal against the article's printed reference ranges. [E2]

The target logic is unusually direct: inhibit the germline enzyme's neomorphic production of a toxic metabolite, then test organ recovery. However, urinary metabolite normalization is target/pathway engagement, not proof that neurologic or cardiac injury is fully reversible. Natural maturation, background treatment and selection remain confounders in uncontrolled reports. Restrict the lead to the demonstrated mutant-IDH2 disease; do not transfer it to D2HGDH-deficient type I, L-2-HGA or combined disease merely because their names contain hydroxyglutarate.

The official IDHIFA label identifies **enasidenib mesylate tablets containing 50 or 100 mg enasidenib**, adult IDH2-mutant relapsed/refractory AML use, and instructions to swallow tablets whole. It does not establish a licensed small-child formulation. Its commercial attribution is BMS/Celgene with licensing from Servier. [E3]

**Decisive next proof:** a development case must solve precise paediatric delivery and demonstrate durable causal-metabolite control at tolerable exposure, with standardized cardiac function and developmental trajectories. An independent patient-derived cardiac model treated after contractile dysfunction could separate biomarker normalization from functional rescue, but would supplement the clinical evidence rather than replace it. Stop an exposure/formulation candidate if metabolite control requires sustained hepatic injury, or if adequate target engagement repeatedly fails to improve the selected reversible functional deficit.

**Disposition:** this is a serious clinical repurposing signal and potentially a focused formulation/programme problem. It is not an unowned new molecule or yet an obviously scalable company. Small published case counts must not be mistaken for an audited market census. A 2026 infant-report bibliographic identity was verified in publisher-deposited metadata, but its primary results were not retrieved and are not counted as additional efficacy evidence. [E4]

## 3. Sodium phenylbutyrate for BSEP-trafficking PFIC2

The 2015 prospective series treated four patients with at least one G982R, R1128C or T1210P allele. Sodium phenylbutyrate (Ammonaps) started around 150–250 mg/kg/day in two doses, with some escalation to 500 mg/kg/day or 20 g/day; UDCA and rifampicin continued unchanged. The report links improved cholestasis to canalicular BSEP appearance and increased bile-acid concentration in bile. This goes beyond serum-marker normalization, although it is not a direct randomized estimate of native-liver survival. [P1]

The earlier homozygous T1210P case connected a polarized hepatocellular model to patient biopsy and biliary findings. It should not be added to the later four-patient series as an independent replication without resolving participant overlap. Engineered Can10 localization is also not a patient-derived functional hepatocyte assay. [P2]

**The programme landscape changed:** an August 24, 2026 sponsor announcement reports Japan's approval of Buphenyl for PFIC1/2 under OrphanPacific's exclusive Immedica licence. PMDA independently documents the indication expansion and a six-patient uncontrolled phase 2 programme, with three of six meeting the week-24 histology criterion. This is a recognized development path, not a fresh unclaimed lead. [P3–P4]

A different Chinese study, NCT04531878, was withdrawn for lack of funding with no efficacy results. It is not a failed clinical efficacy trial. [P5]

**Decisive next proof for a new entrant:** show a predeclared improvement over existing sodium phenylbutyrate/UDCA-based care and relevant IBAT inhibition—whether better sustained transport, tolerability or native-liver outcomes—using a specified genotype and formulation. BSEP-null or intrinsically transport-dead variants cannot be assumed to respond to trafficking correction. Glycerol phenylbutyrate is a distinct product and does not inherit this evidence automatically. Without a concrete differentiated product, stop the new-asset branch and retain this as an external benchmark for repurposing success.

## 4. Desogestrel for PHOX2B-PARM CCHS

The 2024 full report is much weaker than its “two of five responders” summary implies. Three newly treated women received **desogestrel 75 µg/day for three weeks to three months** and retained near-absent hypercapnic responsiveness. The two responders had already taken contraception for years: one desogestrel, the other levonorgestrel/ethinylestradiol. One was a previously published responder, and responses persisted after washout. [D1]

That design cannot prospectively validate baseline heart-rate variability as a treatment-selection biomarker. A signal from existing long-term responders, assessed after treatment and persistent through withdrawal, cannot be pooled with new initiation as five independent drug challenges. Nor does restored awake chemosensitivity establish adequate spontaneous breathing during sleep.

The primary record for **NCT01500473** supplies a separate negative: one participant, termination for nonresponse, no positive hypoxic response, and a reported sleep off-ventilation duration of 89 seconds. This trial used **desogestrel 0.15 mg plus ethinylestradiol 0.03 mg**, a different product from 75 µg desogestrel alone; do not treat its result as an exact monotherapy replication or call 89 seconds an improvement without a baseline. [D2]

**Disposition:** stop a broad fundable-rescue claim. Reopening requires an independent prospective responder group, a prespecified selection rule and sustained improvement in the actual sleep-ventilation deficit on a defined product. Reusing the historical responders or switching progestins without a new causal bridge does not reopen it. Known contraceptive formulations remove chemical-identity ambiguity; they do not solve efficacy, sex/age applicability or commercial differentiation.

## 5. Tebapivat for PIEZO1 hereditary xerocytosis

The 2025 primary report studied RBCs from 12 PIEZO1-HX patients, three KCNN4-HX patients and ten controls. Tebapivat increased PK activity by more than 40%, but generally failed to reverse dehydration in PIEZO1 cells. KCNN4 cells showed an O_hyper increase exceeding 10 mOsm/kg. These are different genotype strata, not interchangeable proof. Exact incubation concentration and duration were not recoverable from the inspected primary abstract; they remain missing fields. [T1]

This is a useful negative because the proposed metabolic mechanism was engaged while the physical defect remained. It lowers the probability of useful rescue of already abnormal PIEZO1 RBCs. It does **not** establish that all PK activators fail, that effects during erythropoiesis are identical to mature-cell treatment, or that haemolysis and survival cannot improve by another mechanism.

**Disposition:** stop the high-priority mature-PIEZO1 rescue claim. The KCNN4 observation is a small exploratory sub-hypothesis, not a sixth candidate discovery; senicapoc/KCNN4 already exists in the portfolio and is a relevant mechanistic comparator. Reopen only on replicated, variant-defined improvement in hydration/deformability or survival, at documented exposure, with a meaningful benefit beyond enzyme activation. Do not substitute mitapivat data, compound identity or dosing for tebapivat. The current primary registry confirms an Agios phase 2 SCD programme and the AG-946 alias. That establishes an existing clinical asset, without establishing study supply, rights or efficacy for HX. [T2]

## Deduplication and evidence discipline

Exact-pair and alias searches were performed against `research/portfolio-register.json` (schema 4) and the supplied round-5 exclusion list. None of these five exact pairs was present. Phenylbutyrate in other diseases and senicapoc/KCNN4 remain related existing entries, not new evidence for these pairs. Already-covered 4-aminopyridine/KCNA2 was rejected before deep review. The 2026 LQT2 conference abstract and final article are one trial family; repeated differentiations, repeated ECGs, case follow-up and regulatory reuse of clinical data are not independent replication.

The accompanying JSON records the source status and missing fields. Public product identity, relevant evidence, study-material access and development rights remain separate attributes. No catalog hit or approval elsewhere was treated as a licence to commercialize; no unreturned email was treated as a scientific failure.
