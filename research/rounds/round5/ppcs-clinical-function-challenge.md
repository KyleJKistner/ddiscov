# PPCS deficiency: independent clinical and functional challenge

**Evidence cut: 4 November 2026.** This extends the new pantethine/PPCS screen with a separate clinical report and a closer examination of the functional evidence. It is research diligence, not a treatment recommendation or a new experiment.

## Decision

**Retain CoA-precursor treatment for PPCS-deficiency cardiomyopathy as a focused validation reserve.** A separate infant report strengthens the reason to investigate. It does not resolve the identity of the cell-treatment reagent, the oral-to-heart exposure bridge, causal attribution of clinical recovery, or differentiation of a commercial product. Do not promote oral pantethine to S-tier from the combined reports.

This is one intervention/use family under review. The additional case is not a new candidate, and follow-up of previously reported patients is not an independent replication cohort.

## 1. The separate infant case adds meaningful evidence

Goetz et al. describe an infant with homozygous PPCS c.727G>C, p.Ala243Pro and prolonged severe cardiac dysfunction despite intensive support. Pantethine was introduced at seven months. The authors report rapid normalization of cardiac measures, followed by withdrawal of most cardiovascular drugs; ACE inhibition continued. Normal cardiac measurements persisted two years later during continued treatment. The variant was classified as likely pathogenic, while functional validation was still in progress. [Primary report, DOI 10.1002/ehf2.15283](https://academic.oup.com/eschf/article/12/4/3195/8488057); [PMC copy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12287790/).

This observation is more informative than a generic statement that a treated patient was doing well: disease was established, the temporal change was large, and the preceding course was severe. It still has no randomized comparator, withdrawal/rechallenge or direct measurement of the proposed myocardial CoA mechanism. Continued ACE inhibition and the changing clinical course limit attribution. The report's dosing schedule cannot establish a superior dosing strategy from a cross-patient comparison.

The infant is distinguishable from the twelve cases tabulated by Zhang et al.: p.Ala243Pro is absent from that table's variants, and the age/course and clinical group differ. The four treated participants in Zhang include two patients already reported in2018. Count the infant separately from those four, but do not treat a count of published cases as an estimate of disease prevalence or a clinical success rate. [Zhang et al., Table 1 and Methods](https://iris.unibs.it/retrieve/73dc3bf9-28fd-45b1-bcd0-5305f9fa5050/43856_2025_Article_1017.pdf).

The correct inference is an encouraging clinical association with heterogeneous contexts. It is not four or five independently demonstrated reversals caused by a defined drug. The infant report raises priority for a discriminating experiment; the pre-treatment recovery history lowers confidence in a simple before/after causal interpretation of the larger series.

## 3. The engineered-heart result is useful, but narrower than restoration

Zhang et al. use two patient iPSC backgrounds and two control backgrounds, with one clone per line. The cardiac controls are not described as isogenic corrections. The WT-PPCS complementation experiment is in fibroblasts and cannot be imported as a matched cardiac-force rescue control. [Methods and Results](https://iris.unibs.it/retrieve/73dc3bf9-28fd-45b1-bcd0-5305f9fa5050/43856_2025_Article_1017.pdf).

The force experiment begins treatment when recellularized patches enter continuously stimulated chambers, at day7 of3D culture, and follows them through day21. The source reports **stabilization of contractile force without a significant increase over time**. Healthy patches gain force during maturation. The force-frequency response and effective refractory period improve only partially. Reduced abnormal calcium events add functional support, but are not equivalent to recovery of a normal ventricular output. [Figure 3 and corresponding Results](https://iris.unibs.it/retrieve/73dc3bf9-28fd-45b1-bcd0-5305f9fa5050/43856_2025_Article_1017.pdf).

Figure 3C's reported force denominators are patches: controls8, patient95595 untreated15/treated13, and patient103596 untreated9/treated8. The affected donor count remains two backgrounds, and the two control lines should not be assumed interchangeable merely because they were pooled. The caption's enumerated comparisons are against healthy controls; the treated-minus-untreated force effect was not independently reconstructed here. The authors' stabilization description is not recast as a new causal effect estimate. No new p-values, equivalence test or raw-data reanalysis is asserted. The paper links source Data6–10; that disclosure is separate from our inspection of the figure and published summaries.

This does not make stabilization worthless. Preventing a progressive decline could be clinically valuable. It means that the proposed product claim should distinguish preservation, developmental compensation and reversal of established contractile loss. The current patch experiment does not establish complete recovery after a stable mature deficit.

## 4. Identity and exposure are the immediate article-level questions

The Methods call the reagent “pantethine” while identifying Sigma16702. The manufacturer's product is **(R)-pantetheine**, the reduced monomer; pantethine is the disulfide. This discrepancy could be a naming/catalog error or could affect what was actually delivered. It does not prove which bottle or chemical species the investigators used. The chemistry challenge therefore needs to distinguish the nominal reagent, authenticated input, extracellular redox products, intracellular precursor and orally administered product. [Primary Methods](https://iris.unibs.it/retrieve/73dc3bf9-28fd-45b1-bcd0-5305f9fa5050/43856_2025_Article_1017.pdf); [manufacturer16702](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sigma/16702).

The cellular exposures are 100 µM in the reported 2D functional assays and 500 µM for the 3D patches. The methods use heat-inactivated serum and describe reducing medium components. A nominal bath concentration cannot be assigned to an oral dose or to circulating intact pantethine. Conversely, metabolism of the administered molecule does not automatically invalidate an observed clinical effect: an active metabolite or salvage route could matter. The companion chemistry review evaluates that possibility without treating all CoA precursors as interchangeable.

The same paper tests4′-phosphopantetheine in fibroblasts and reports a smaller CoA response under its conditions. It does not test that molecule in the cardiac-force experiment. It is an alternative to compare under qualified exposure conditions, not a superior heart-repair candidate established by the publication.

## 5. The next proof should discriminate among explanations

The most valuable near-term result would connect **authenticated chemical species → intracellular CoA restoration → useful cardiac function**, while showing that the effect persists under a biologically credible extracellular environment.

Use the reported patient variants or independently constructed isogenic variants in a parental line suitable for the intended use. Qualify a reproducible disease-dependent contractile or electrophysiological deficit and a matched correction control. Establish whether the chosen endpoint is modifiable after the same deficit, rather than relying only on correction before differentiation. Registry listings for the original lines are separate from received materials and permitted commercial use; scientific selection need not wait for a reply from their owners.

Compare exact pantethine, exact pantetheine, the separately defined4′-phosphopantetheine and appropriate precursor/metabolite controls. Measure chemical conversion and intracellular metabolic response rather than assuming which component reaches the cell. Compare relevant serum conditions only in a way that maintains matched culture health; a serum manipulation that independently destroys the model would be uninterpretable. Resolve the functional contribution with force, rate adaptation and arrhythmia-related outputs, alongside cell number and tissue integrity.

For a restoration claim, begin after the functional deficit is present and distinguish a rising-force recovery from a reduced rate of decline. For a preservation claim, predeclare the decline that the intervention must prevent and compare against the best practical standard-care or mechanistic control in that system. Lock a minimum useful effect and its uncertainty rule before unblinding. A marginal biochemical change should not rescue a negative functional result.

### Advance, narrow or stop

| Outcome | Decision |
|---|---|
| A defined species improves relevant established cardiac function across qualified backgrounds under credible exposure conditions | Advance that exact intervention and formulation hypothesis. Keep clinical causal and development questions explicit. |
| Benefit is reproducible only as stabilization | Consider a preservation program if the magnitude is useful; remove a full-reversal claim. |
| Benefit requires a high, chemically artificial bath that cannot be connected to a realistic delivery route | Do not advance the oral product thesis on those data. A new delivery article would need its own evidence. |
| Only a distinct precursor works | Reassign the evidence and candidate identity; do not retain the original compound name for convenience. |
| Function remains negative despite adequate metabolic restoration in a modifiable model | Demote the heart-function thesis or identify the specific irreversible stage; do not promote solely on CoA levels. |
| The model lacks a reproducible genetically dependent deficit | Requalify the model before treating a negative drug result as target failure. |

## Commercial interpretation

The clinical signal and a public experimental route could justify a bounded translational study. They do not yet establish a differentiated product. Widely obtainable supplementation, rare published cases, uncertain active-species exposure and new precursor/formulation possibilities create different scientific and value-capture questions. A broader heart-failure indication cannot be inferred from the monogenic cases. No freedom-to-operate, market-size, budget or funding-success assertion is made.

The reserve should move upward if independent species-resolved cardiac function supports the oral or another clearly defined product. It should move downward if the apparent rescue depends on unrepresentative chemistry or if the clinically relevant exposure cannot produce useful function. More uncontrolled case summaries alone will not settle that distinction.

## Source-reading limits

The full 17-page Zhang report was read through its public institutional PDF, including Methods, Table 1, case narratives and Figure 3 captions. The separate Goetz report was read in publisher/PMC primary text. A direct local PDF download failed; this is not treated as absent published evidence. Supplementary numerical files were identified but not reanalyzed in this memo. The completed [chemistry and exposure challenge](round5_pantethine_chemistry_challenge.md) reconciles historical indirect plasma nondetection with newer relative LC-MS evidence, distinguishes pantethine from pantetheine and phosphorylated precursors, and preserves the unresolved oral-to-heart exposure bridge. No author contact or private patient information was used.
