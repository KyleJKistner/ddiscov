## Scientific decision

BLU3797 retains the leading finite qualification recommendation. The public precursor junction is better established, while the source enzyme identifier and reaction conditions require an independently specified assay. The selected p.L463S human force route, absolute-force estimand, exposure/energy-function plan, exact47 comparator and quantitative decision rules make the next task concrete. They do not constitute a received lot, executed study, human efficacy result or systemic safety window.

Enasidenib and ruxolitinib retain affirmative clinical-development proof recommendations. Vorasidenib's direct R140Q activity sharpens the CNS comparison without displacing enasidenib's exact-disease cardiac evidence. Ruxolitinib's next proof must measure useful control and burden against defined contemporary care, retaining rescue and maintenance denominators.

Four new exact uses, including one existing-concept expansion, and two existing-use updates are recorded. Lisdexamfetamine/KCNMA1 is the strongest new clinical-research reserve, with selected-responder bias explicit. W7 is deprioritized as a systemic article; selective thin-filament desensitization remains a discovery question. Epalrestat/PMM2 is held on verified futility. No S-tier or proprietary-asset promotion follows this sprint.
