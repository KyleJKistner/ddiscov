# Round 8 independent challenge — exact W7 versus the thin-filament thesis

**Reviewed 4 November 2026. Existing-screen candidate, not a newly discovered lead.** Baseline alias `screen-index:accessible-cellular-01:511` already retained the TNNT2-R94C thin-filament relaxation/force comparison. This review adds an independent electrophysiology challenge and makes the distinction between a target opportunity and an exact development article more decisive. No material, model, experiment or commercial right was obtained.

## Decision

**Retain selective thin-filament calcium desensitization as a discovery reserve; deprioritize exact W7 as a systemic development candidate.** The human genotype-specific functional result is useful. It does not close a direct cardiac ion-channel liability at the same nominal micromolar order as older sarcomere effects. W7 can remain a mechanistic tool when its off-targets are explicitly controlled. It should not displace BLU3797 or the mature clinical dossiers as a near-development asset.

This is stronger than a generic warning that W7 inhibits calmodulin: a separate primary whole-cell study measured direct **hERG blockade at IC50 3.5µM**, plus **Kv1.5 6.5 µM** and **Kir2.1 13.4 µM**. These are exact-W7 hydrochloride electrophysiology results. They do not establish a clinical arrhythmia rate, but they materially worsen the starting assumption for chronic systemic treatment of a cardiac disease. [W7-S02]

## Why the favorable human-model comparison is not enough

The R94C isogenic experiment is unusually useful for causality within one background, and direct force plus relaxation is substantially more informative than a calcium-fluorescence change alone. However, a greater diastolic benefit per systolic loss is a **tradeoff**, not absence of negative inotropy. Normalize kinetics to beat period carefully: an apparent relaxation improvement can reflect a changed rate, conduction, activation sequence or viable force-producing population. It therefore needs paced confirmation, absolute systolic output and beat-to-beat electrophysiology alongside the relative tradeoff.

The 2010 channel result matters particularly because W7's direct effects can influence the very calcium/force behavior used to infer selective thin-filament action. A preserved bulk calcium-transient amplitude does not exclude ion-channel blockade, altered repolarization or arrhythmogenic susceptibility. The 2016 authors' calcium-independent contraction result supports a myofilament contribution under their conditions; it does not cancel direct electrophysiology measured independently. These are compatible observations.

Chronic treatment of children raises a duration/product requirement that neither an acute myocyte exposure nor an isolated-heart perfusion addresses. This statement is a translation requirement, not a prediction of pediatric toxicity. No feasible human dose, systemic free concentration, therapeutic index, tissue-selective delivery system or chronic safety package was recovered in this review.

## What would merit further allocation

The next valuable question is **whether a more selective article can reproduce the R94C relaxation–force advantage while preserving excitation and useful systolic work**. The project should be framed as target/chemistry discovery, with W7 an imperfect reference.

| Workstream | Advance condition | Stop or redirect condition |
|---|---|---|
| Article-specific reference | Identify actual W7 form/content and measure medium free exposure in a reproducible assay. Retain dose, duration, pacing and tissue geometry with each result. | Unknown article or unmeasured exposure does not support a numerical window. Do not repeat nominal W7 assays indefinitely. |
| Functional differentiation | Compare concentration-response curves at **matched diastolic improvement**, recording absolute systolic force/work, relaxation and viable tissue—not equal nominal concentrations or a preferred single dose. | A relaxation change requiring unacceptable loss of useful systolic output redirects chemistry. Loss of function in corrected controls remains visible. |
| Mechanistic attribution | Show thin-filament calcium-response change in an appropriate permeabilized/reconstituted or target-linked assay, with orthogonal selective chemistry or a mechanistic perturbation; preserve matching genotype. | W7-only agreement does not establish selectivity. A new analog inherits a hypothesis, not the W7 paper's efficacy record. |
| Electrophysiology | Establish exposure-matched action-potential/conduction/arrhythmia effects in the same functional system, with relevant ion-current qualification and measured exposure. Test the known hERG/Kv/Kir liabilities directly rather than substituting a calmodulin panel. | Benefit cannot be separated from a consequential electrophysiologic liability under the intended exposure/duration: stop the exact article's systemic thesis. This need not kill the thin-filament target. |
| Generality and product | Replicate in an independently qualified disease background, preferably another thin-filament variant with isogenic control, and define a specific clinical population and advantage over relevant options. | OneR94C background does not establish all RCM, all TNNT2 or HCM; do not build a population valuation from one donor. |
| Value capture | A defensible new selective chemistry, delivery or exclusive data/rights position that can produce differentiated benefit. | A purchasable, long-published research reagent plus a single-model repurposing observation is not itself an exclusive, fundable asset. Patentability/FTO and rights remain unassessed. |

**Comparator logic:** mavacamten remains a useful mechanistic comparator in the exact patient model. Its clinical availability or efficacy in obstructive HCM does not establish efficacy in restrictive cardiomyopathy. A valid new study should reproduce the complete relaxation–systolic tradeoff under matched conditions and report actual concentrations and formulation. EGCG or another literature calcium-desensitizer is not automatically a clean orthogonal substitute; its own promiscuity and exposure would need qualification.

No universal numerical hERG margin or allowed systolic-force decrement is assigned from the present cross-study data. Those prospective limits require a defined product exposure, relevant assay performance and clinically meaningful function. The present allocation decision does not wait for a supplier email: public evidence already justifies **lowering exact-W7 development priority** while preserving the selective-target research question.
