# Round 8: cross-portfolio ranking and resource allocation

**4 November 2026.** Rank the next decision-changing development action, while keeping the strength of human evidence, scientific tractability and standalone product value separate. These recommendations allocate **100 planning-effort points**, not dollars, expected financial returns, clinical-success probabilities or authorized experimental spending.

## Decision

**BLU3797 remains first, enasidenib second and topical ruxolitinib third.** BLU now has a bounded qualification work order rather than another open-ended research memo. Enasidenib retains the stronger exact-disease human organ-function anchor, and ruxolitinib has a concrete controlled clinical question. The ordering reflects the value of the next development decision and the user's request to concentrate on BLU; it does not claim BLU already has better human evidence or easier commercial rights.

No candidate meets the complete S-tier standard. That is an evidentiary result, not a quota failure. No new screen has earned a credible claim of a readily financeable proprietary drug asset. The completed work still supports an affirmative initial BLU qualification recommendation and finite clinical-development preparation for the two clinical leaders. ## 2. Why the leading decisions changed

### BLU: greater operational tractability, more explicit pharmacology risk

The public CN precursor route and original drawings now close the structural junction to the JCI synthesis. They do not resolve all yield arithmetic or authenticate a lot. The original enzyme catalog entry and stated lipid units cannot be carried into a protocol uncritically. A declared construct, side-by-side BLU/47 assay and orthogonal readout replace the vague instruction to reproduce a headline IC50. Native protein PRM resources provide an engagement-method starting point, while the missing NanoBRET tracer remains a real limitation.

The p.L463S manufacturer force/fatigue data support one specific first model, with a nonisogenic reference and a narrower claim. The fixed useful-force threshold, exposure-linked energetic check, conditional second background and distinct advance/inconclusive/redirect/futility branches make the work finite. The new vascular kinase-inactive evidence also prevents using old normal histology as blanket target-safety clearance. [Article dossier](blu_article_exposure.md) · [Model dossier](blu_model_function.md)

### Enasidenib: preserve the cardiac lead, sharpen the CNS comparison

Vorasidenib's primary discovery and FDA records include IDH2-R140Q activity. It therefore belongs in a CNS-directed comparison, although tumor:plasma distribution, adult glioma pharmacology and a pediatric age12+ label do not establish normal infant-brain exposure or D2HGA2 rescue. Wild-type activity and high-exposure animal toxicity also prevent calling it a presumptively safer replacement.

The enasidenib project should first state whether it seeks established cardiac benefit, neurological improvement or neonatal prevention. Those outcomes cannot inherit each other's evidence. Existing pediatric sprinkle experience means a new formulation project must solve a demonstrated administration or exposure problem, rather than merely advertise a pediatric presentation. [Clinical leader challenge](clinical_challenge.md) · [Round-7 article/claims dossier](../round7/enasidenib_product.md)

### Ruxolitinib: a meaningful treatment-burden claim, with a real counterfactual

Concurrent care is essential in a fluctuating disease. Botulinum-toxin evidence adds an active sweat-control benchmark, but its very small randomized study and survivor-enriched pilot follow-up are not a definitive standard against which every case can be scored. The pilot's11 relapses were among26 HHD participants; mixing in4 Darier participants changes the denominator and must not dilute the HHD relapse fraction.

A useful cream may earn a role through sustained control, less painful treatment, lower rescue burden or a tolerability advantage. It need not repair ATP2C1 to be valuable. Conversely, a vehicle-controlled induction response cannot alone establish superiority to a procedure-based care pathway or a six-month maintenance claim. Existing formulation claims and the branded franchise still limit standalone value capture. ## 4. Explicit reallocation rules

- **BLU material or model failure:** use the single documented requalification/alternative-route allowance. If the route still cannot yield an interpretable test within the defined resources, suspend that tranche and reallocate its unused effort; do not call it a target-negative result.
- **BLU qualified useful force benefit:** prioritize independent first-background confirmation and the exposure/causal comparison. Add another background when it changes a generality or product decision. Preserve the distinction between exact-article confirmation and a broad target claim.
- **BLU target benefit but molecule liability:** redirect chemistry only when another qualified selective perturbation preserves benefit. A new scaffold must earn its own pharmacology and function; it does not inherit BLU's mouse record.
- **Clinical leader lacks incremental product value:** retain a separately labeled clinical-research project if justified, while removing unsupported standalone-asset claims. Apply the same rule if BLU's only defensible output is target validation under someone else's product claims.
- **A challenger earns controlled useful function and a credible product contribution:** move it above an incumbent when that changes the next decision's value. Do not protect prior rankings because a candidate received more pages of diligence.
- **A later negative supersedes an earlier positive:** update the current register and preserve the old source/decision in history. A terminated trial is not erased by a favorable initial biomarker or case report.

## 5. The independent-product question remains open for all three leaders

The commercially relevant deliverable must be specified before a larger investment: a reproducible and differentiated molecule, a genuinely improved formulation/delivery route, a clinically meaningful new use with a credible permitted development path, or another defensible product advantage. Data may have research or transactional value, but ownership of a dataset is not automatically exclusivity over a therapeutic.

Public patent/application information is evidence about claims and current activity, not a legal clearance opinion. No owner silence, catalog presence, pipeline omission or absence of a trial proves open rights. Conversely, unanswered commercial terms are not a reason to defer the scientific qualification decision. The public package now makes the scientific next step reviewable; actual materials, execution resources and any later commercial transaction remain distinct facts.
