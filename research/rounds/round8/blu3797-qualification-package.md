# BLU3797: qualification work order and quantitative decisions

**Round 8 · 4 November 2026 · public-evidence planning package.** This is the controlling operational extension of the [round-6 funding memo](../round6/round6_blu_milestone.md). It specifies a finite qualification project and the conditional experiment it would enable. No material has been received, money committed, person contacted or experiment conducted. A written work order is ready; a received lot, transferred assay and executable laboratory SOP are not represented as already qualified.

**Recommendation: retain BLU3797 as the leading qualification project.** Commit the first block to authenticating the exact S article, establishing independently specified pharmacology and qualifying one post-deficit human force assay. Release the exploratory function block only when those inputs are interpretable. The present evidence does not justify a clinical program, a broad genotype claim or an exclusive-asset valuation.

## 1. What this project must decide

The immediate question is whether authenticated BLU3797 improves established contractile dysfunction in **MTM1 c.1388T>C, p.L463S human engineered skeletal muscle**, at measured exposure that can be related to C2β engagement and useful-function tolerability. The intended first claim is on-treatment benefit in one background. Preservation during maturation remains useful but must be distinguished from restoration of mature, previously lost function.

Three hypotheses must remain separate:

1. Selective catalytic C2β inhibition can benefit MTM1 disease.
2. Exact BLU3797 is a suitable molecule for producing that benefit.
3. An independent development project can create a differentiated, commercially usable product or data package.

Failure of a lot or model does not test hypothesis 1. A useful result from another inhibitor does not establish hypothesis 2. Positive biology alone does not establish hypothesis 3. Blueprint-origin chemistry, subsequent corporate ownership and independent target/compound patent families apply to BLU just as existing programs and formulations apply to enasidenib and ruxolitinib. A new analogue inherits a hypothesis, not BLU's efficacy record. ### Public method uncertainties now converted into qualification tasks

The original biochemical method reports 60nM enzyme, 1mM ATP and 150nM phosphatidylinositol. At face value that is 2.5 substrate molecules per enzyme and at most 0.015% ATP conversion. The published 28nM IC50 is also close to half the nominal enzyme concentration. These observations prevent an unqualified Ki inference or a requirement that an independent assay reproduce 28nM within an arbitrary fold range. They do not establish which printed parameter is wrong.

The journal's reagent catalog entry conflicts with the manufacturer's mapping: PV5374 is C2β kinase, whereas PV3574 identifies dilution buffer in the official protocol. Preserve the exact source location in the article dossier. This is a documentation mismatch; it is not evidence that the investigators substituted buffer for enzyme. An independently specified low-active-enzyme, initial-rate, interference-controlled assay is the appropriate deliverable. The manufacturer's alternative protocol uses different ATP/lipid conditions and cannot be presented as the recovered original method. [JCI article](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/198568) · [Official C2β protocol](https://documents.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/PIK3C2B_%28PI3K-C2beta%29_Adapta.pdf)

The public route now has an identified upstream chiral precursor. Patent yield arithmetic and the journal final-step yield remain unresolved. Structural reconstruction is more credible; lot quality, stereopurity and actual reproducibility still require measurement. The published cell-engagement method remains incomplete, and the source-table/fit conflict for VPS34 is retained. Neither a vendor purity line nor a broad kinome score closes those gaps.

## 3. Finite work blocks, outputs and release decisions

### Q0 — Article, exposure and pharmacology qualification

Deliver one release record for the exact lot, one independently specified C2β biochemical method, a fit-for-purpose medium/tissue bioanalysis method and a usable target-engagement interpretation. Measure the lot's dissolved recovery, binding and stability in the intended culture medium, including plastic/matrix loss. Establish both nominal and measured total concentrations; estimate unbound medium exposure only with a validated separation/recovery method. Intracellular total concentration is not intracellular free concentration.

Use the following **prospective operating specifications**, not alleged published BLU batch measurements:

- HPLC purity at least98%; chiral ee at least98%; qNMR active content95–105% of the declared anhydrous/salt/solvate-adjusted basis. Structural inconsistency or wrong stereochemistry rejects the lot regardless of a purity percentage.
- Initial research LC–MS accuracy80–120% and precisionCV≤20% across the relevant matrix/range, with an LLOQ below the lowest interpreted exposure. Measure extraction recovery, matrix effects and nonspecific binding; recovery need not itself be80–120% if reproducible and adequately corrected. Dissolved-material recovery and free-fraction mass balance are separate measurements. Label these fit-for-purpose research criteria. ICH M10's regulatory validation framework is a reference, not a validation claim for this proposed assay.
- Biochemical kinetics must be linear in time and active enzyme over the fitted range, with low substrate conversion and a detection window independently demonstrated. Product-based orthogonal confirmation and reporter interference controls are required before treating an apparent potency shift as biology.
- A useful positive functional result is not required to reach80% C2β occupancy. Where a calibrated occupancy assay exists, approximately80% engagement is a **high-engagement characterization target for a negative challenge**, not a proven efficacy threshold. When the native method is non-occupancy CETSA, record an engagement response and its limits; never manufacture a percentage.
- At the eventual minimum useful exposure, characterize C2α and VPS34. A proposed objective is≤20% inhibition/engagement in qualified comparable assays, but numerical percentages from different methods must not be directly equated. Failure of this objective triggers an article/selectivity interpretation, not automatic proof of human toxicity.

**Release Q1:** identity is correct, dosing is analytically interpretable and there is a credible measurement route to C2β activity/engagement. If direct native engagement remains technically unresolved, a limited function feasibility study can still be considered with biochemical, exposure and cellular-PD triangulation; it cannot produce a qualified target-negative conclusion. Stop repeating the published assay verbatim if its unresolved units prevent a measurable reaction. Re-specify the independent assay once and document the change.

### Q1 — Qualify the functional window in one background

Proposed first calibration block: **3 independently initiated casting/differentiation batches ×2 lines ×4 tissues =24 tissues**, observed longitudinally around days13,16,20,23 and27. This is a resource specification, not a power calculation or a statement that three batches guarantee qualification. Independently initiated batches must not merely be three plates cast from one preparation.

Day20 after locally defined tissue casting is the proposed pre-dose time, and day27 the proposed primary endpoint. The public supplier figure does not disclose its day origin; those proposed local days cannot be assumed identical to the figure. Freeze or change those times using **blinded, drug-free qualification data** before treatment outcomes exist. The required disease deficit must precede dosing. A predictable maturation trajectory with contemporaneous controls is acceptable; a flat plateau is not an automatic prerequisite.

Lock direct electrical stimulation, rest interval, force calibration, acquisition settings and the number of evoked twitches to aggregate. Use a sub-fatiguing primary recording; conduct fatigue measurements separately so the measurement does not create the putative treatment effect. Collect repeated readings sufficient to characterize force repeatability without counting them as additional tissues.

Qualification passes only if there is a reproducible positive reference–disease force gap, sufficient viable/attached tissue yield and precision to distinguish the intended useful effect. As a proposed operating minimum, require at least3 of4 tissues per line/batch evaluable under predeclared technical criteria, and positive gap direction in all3 initial batches. These are early process limits, not estimates of expected yield. Record every failed tissue. No treatment-related detachment can be discarded as a technical exclusion after randomization.

If the gap or repeatability is inadequate, allow one documented protocol/window correction and one requalification block; then redirect the model or stop this route. Do not create an unlimited optimization project. The p.E48*/corrected academic pair, CRICKi008-A/p.Tyr198* resource and public fibroblast-to-muscle construction routes are conditional alternatives with distinct availability and force-validation requirements. They are not already qualified replacement assays. [Model choices and source limits](blu_model_function.md)

### F1 — Exploratory post-deficit force comparison

Proposed core: **3 new independent batches ×2 lines ×4 arms ×4 independently randomized tissues =96 tissues**. Arms are vehicle plus three analytically selected BLU exposure levels. This gives12 tissue exposure units per line/arm distributed across3 batches, but still **one disease donor and one nonisogenic reference donor**. The block is96 tissues across batches, not a claim that one physical plate supplies three independent batches. Separate terminal sampling and engagement wells are additional resources and must be itemized.

Nominate one primary exposure level from Q0 exposure/engagement/selectivity results before viewing force outcomes. Use the low and high levels to characterize the response and exposure limitations. An exploratory signal selected from the other doses requires independent confirmation; it is not a multiplicity-controlled confirmatory discovery. Do not force exposure by escalating beyond solubility or demonstrable tolerability simply to reach a nominal concentration from another assay.

Measure force and exposure on the same treatment schedule: after initial dosing, immediately before a specified medium change, after the final refresh and at the endpoint, with elapsed times and parallel appropriately matched tissues recorded. A sampled pre-change concentration is not asserted to be the true minimum without a suitable time course. Record medium changes and drug replacement. Continuous exposure is the first useful-product hypothesis. Pulse/washout follows only if an exposure-sparing or off-drug claim becomes valuable after on-treatment benefit; it is not an initial gate.

A comparator47/genetic module is conditional on its readiness. It is especially valuable for distinguishing target benefit from BLU-specific effects. Do not stall every exploratory positive while developing all controls. Conversely, a negative that lacks an adequate target-specific or delayed-modifiability control cannot reject the reversal hypothesis.

### F2 — Independent confirmation and the relevant window

Select exposure and lock the absolute-force threshold using qualification information, then acquire independent confirmatory data in the first background. A confirmed narrow result can justify adding a second disease background, preferably a null genotype with an isogenic causal comparison, to test generality; the second background is not required to acknowledge the first-background result. Do not relabel additional p.L463S clones or tissues as additional donors. Each background needs its own baseline deficit and exposure interpretation.

Confirm useful function, not only p62, PI3P, fusion or tissue appearance. Compare exact47 and selective genetic support where qualified. A result confined to a hypomorphic allele can still support a narrower product hypothesis. Test exposure–benefit and unwanted function over a bounded range around the minimum useful concentration. A3-fold free-benefit exposure is a proposed characterization margin, not a validated therapeutic index.

Only after a useful muscle signal, extend to the focused cardiac/neuronal/disease-liver questions selected by evidence and distribution. Respiratory claims require a separate respiratory-muscle/physiology bridge; limb force and resting breathing do not establish respiratory reserve. A larger product investment also requires a specific population, comparator advantage and value-capture route. No automatic S-tier promotion follows a single positive force experiment.

## 4. Estimand, denominators and quantitative advance/stop rules

### Primary outcome and locked useful-effect threshold

The primary outcome is **absolute evoked peak twitch force in µN at the locked endpoint**, adjusted by the pre-dose force contrast. Within each independent batch:

`delta_b = (mean treated endpoint − mean treated baseline) − (mean vehicle endpoint − mean vehicle baseline)`.

The same tissues contribute baseline and endpoint; repeated twitches are first aggregated within tissue. Estimate the batch-blocked treatment contrast and its uncertainty. A conservative initial summary uses the mean of the independent batch contrasts with a small-sample t interval; show each batch. A tissue-level blocked analysis can use independent randomized tissue units, but it must not turn one donor into a population claim or treat an unstable random-effects estimate from3 batches as reliable generality. Any alternative analysis and handling of small-sample uncertainty must be frozen before treatment decoding.

Let `Gcal` be the positive reference–disease vehicle force gap estimated in the blinded qualification block in the same format/time window. Define the prospective investment target as:

`M = max(0.25 × Gcal, delta_repeat)` µN.

The **25% gap fraction is an analyst-selected minimum useful preclinical effect**, not a clinical MCID, a published BLU force effect, a natural-history threshold or a Bayesian success probability. `delta_repeat` is a conservative repeatability floor in µN, established under the locked recording protocol; one transparent candidate is `1.96 × SD(paired repeat-reading differences)` at the same controlled physiological state and rest interval, with its assumptions and uncertainty reported. Differences across maturation days must not be used as measurement-repeatability errors. This is a measurement floor, not the standard error of the treatment-arm mean. Qualification must show that the chosen threshold remains discriminable. If repeatability is poor enough to dominate the disease gap, redirect the assay instead of declaring the target ineffective.

Lock `M`, its units and derivation before each independent treatment block. Report the contemporaneous reference gap as a diagnostic and the gap-normalized effect as a secondary description. Do not silently alter the success threshold with a post-treatment denominator. With a nonisogenic reference, the gap is a **reference gap**, not a demonstrated genetically rescuable fraction. A second background gets an independently calibrated absolute threshold before its drug outcomes are seen.

| Result | Quantitative operating rule | Decision and allowed claim |
|---|---|---|
| Exploratory benefit | Point estimate≥M and two-sided95% interval lower bound>0, with qualified material/model, interpretable exposure and no confirmed unacceptable harm | Advance to independent confirmation in the tested line. Nominal exploratory inference; incomplete direct engagement limits the target-mechanism claim but need not erase the function signal. |
| Strong but imprecise trend | Point estimate≥M but interval includes0, or interval spans both no effect and useful effect | Inconclusive. Consider one prospectively sized independent confirmation only if precision/resource assessment remains favorable; no efficacy promotion. |
| Confirmed useful effect | In independent confirmation, lower95% bound>M at the locked primary exposure, with appropriate engagement/causal support and useful-function tolerability | Advance that article/background claim. A broad genotype claim additionally requires the independent-background result; no clinical or systemic-safety inference. |
| Qualified functional futility in this model | Upper95% bound<M, adequate exposure/time and interpretable high target perturbation, qualified force assay, and demonstrated post-deficit modifiability for a reversal claim | Stop this article/use under the tested conditions. Downgrade the target only when the failure also survives orthogonal selective perturbation and an independently qualified disease background. |
| Underexposure or assay failure | Wrong article, unquantifiable exposure, absent/uninterpretable engagement, unreliable deficit or missing measurement integrity | Redirect article/measurement/model. This does not constitute target-negative evidence. |
| Benefit and an unacceptable functional liability at required exposure | Replicated useful-force loss or mitochondrial functional impairment in healthy/appropriate disease tissue at the minimum beneficial exposure, with exposure and assay interference resolved | Hold BLU expansion; compare47/genetic perturbation and exposure distribution. Molecule-specific liability redirects chemistry. Selective target liability narrows or downgrades the target. |
| No differentiated product route | Scientific result is positive but the exact product offers no defensible incremental benefit, delivery/burden advantage or usable rights/data route | Stop the standalone asset thesis; retain separately labeled research value. Apply to BLU, enasidenib and ruxolitinib alike. |

Confidence limits must include the actual analysis unit, baseline handling, exclusions and missingness. An interval crossing the useful threshold is not proof of futility. Precision can be poor even when many repeated twitches look consistent. No probability of clinical success or financing success is assigned without a defensible model and data.

### Force, fatigue and missingness safeguards

Report absolute active force and tissue cross-sectional area, attachment, passive tension and dimensions alongside specific force (active force divided by a qualified cross-sectional-area estimate). More tissue mass or improved force transmission may be useful, but it differs from greater intrinsic myofiber specific force. Do not normalize away cell loss or a treatment-induced geometric change. Fatigue reporting includes absolute maintained force and force–time integral, not just percent remaining from a weak starting twitch. Relaxation, passive tension, calcium dynamics and morphology are secondary mechanistic/functional descriptions.

Randomization occurs at the independently treated tissue, blocked by batch/position and baseline force. Several independently treated tissues from one donor can be exposure units; repeated frames/twitches are not. Report donor, line, clone, differentiation, casting batch, plate, tissue, time and readout counts separately. Record all randomized tissues and planned samples, not only survivors. Post-randomization detachment or death contributes to the treatment-failure record. Prespecify sensitivity analyses for missing force; do not replace missing values with a favorable survivor mean. The exact imputation/model choice is locked during qualification, before drug decoding. [NC3Rs experimental-unit guidance](https://eda.nc3rs.org.uk/experimental-design-unit)

## 5. Exposure, target engagement and mitochondrial safety

The released mouse concentrations are terminal/final-dose **total plasma** observations with incomplete sampling-time and free-fraction information. They are not Cmax, AUC, muscle free concentration or a human dose justification. The published NanoBRET68.2nM and biochemical28nM belong to different assays. Plot useful force against measured medium free exposure and observed cellular target response; do not manufacture one common potency axis by dividing unrelated nominal values.

Native target protein can potentially be followed by public PRM peptide assays, but their original non-muscle validation does not validate muscle engagement. Qualify dynamic range, antibody/peptide specificity, thermal behavior and live-cell compatibility as appropriate. PI3P, mTORC1 and p62 are linked pathway observations; they cannot uniquely identify C2β occupancy or replace direct force. PIK3C2B abundance and perturbation method remain recorded because protein loss and catalytic inhibition may differ.

At minimum beneficial exposure and the same exposure schedule, pair muscle force with viability, ATP-linked respiration and respiratory reserve, interpreted with viable-cell/protein/mass denominators and unnormalized totals. If respirometry cannot be qualified in the actual 3D geometry, use an independently qualified compatible energy-function measurement and record its limits; an untransferred 2D platform must not become a hidden initial gate. Use morphology as a prompt for functional checking. A provisional **20% reproducible decrement** in healthy-tissue force or ATP-linked respiration, present in at least2 independent batches and beyond assay repeatability, is a **review/hold trigger**, not an established toxicological boundary. Confirm concentration, compound interference, tissue mass and cytotoxicity before assigning mechanism. A smaller consistent critical functional liability may still matter; the trigger is not permission to ignore it.

The October2026 combined cardiac C2α/C2β knockout and related transformed-cell depletion findings motivate this focused check. They do not show that selective BLU causes cardiotoxicity. Conversely, long-term reported histology in C2β kinase-inactive mice does not establish complete safety: a separate kinase-inactive endothelial study reports altered vascular growth/mTORC1. The direction of pathway effects can differ by tissue. Do not require a full vascular-development battery before the first muscle signal; carry it as a later developmental exposure question. [Safety evidence and denominators](blu_model_function.md)

Equal free concentrations in different culture models compare intrinsic sensitivity. A systemic window requires measured or bounded tissue distribution, metabolites and relevant exposure duration. Culture overlap is unresolved without that bridge; it is not automatically fatal or automatically safe. If47 or selective catalytic genetics retains benefit while BLU has the liability, change article. If several selective catalytic interventions reproduce both benefit and liability, test whether population, delivery or exposure schedule can separate them; absent a defensible separation, lower the target's development priority.

## 7. Numerical provenance and remaining public limits

Published values are restricted to their source article/construct/model. Proposed lot limits,20% research-bioanalysis precision,80% high-engagement characterization,20% anti-target objective,3-fold headroom,24/96 tissue layouts,day20/day27 timing,25% reference-gap target and20% functional-harm trigger are **prospective planning choices**. They must be recorded as such and refined with blinded qualification before confirmatory treatment outcomes. Unknown force variance, native occupancy calibration and actual exposure are not replaced by invented values.

The Assay Guidance Manual's automated HTS validation recommendations assume biological relevance has already been established; a generic Z′ threshold cannot establish relevance of this3D disease model. ICH M10 supplies a formal regulatory bioanalysis framework, not a claim that the proposed early assay is already validated. [Assay Guidance Manual](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK83783/?report=classic) · [ICH M10 final guidance](https://www.fda.gov/media/162903/download)

Public evidence now supports a much more concrete initial work order. It still cannot settle received-lot behavior, exact original NanoBRET components, native muscle target occupancy, reproducible human-force efficacy, systemic exposure separation, commercial freedom to operate or the ultimate value of an independent product. Each remaining uncertainty has a named output or decision branch; none is converted into an owner-email prerequisite.
