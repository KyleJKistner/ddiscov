# BLU3797: human functional qualification and target-related safety package

**Round 8 | 4 November 2026 | Evidence cut: public material reviewed in this sprint.**

This is a proposed, staged research package. No material was purchased, no experiment was performed, and no supplier or investigator was contacted. All tissue counts, timings, numerical decision examples and assay choices below are prospective operating assumptions. They are not observed BLU results, clinical meaningful-change thresholds, quoted prices for a study, or a statistically powered protocol.

## Decision

**Start with the cataloged Curi CB-SKM-XLMTM p.L463S human muscle model and a separately cataloged healthy male reference. Qualify an established twitch-force deficit, then test authenticated BLU3797 after that deficit exists.** Use within-patient-line randomized drug comparisons for the first functional signal. The healthy line estimates a contemporaneous reference gap and checks nonspecific drug effects; it does not establish that every patient/reference difference is caused by MTM1.

The smallest useful first package is a **24-tissue untreated qualification grid followed, if qualified, by a 96-tissue exposure-response grid**, with separate destructive engagement/energy samples. It can deliver an interpretable positive signal without first completing a corrected-line program, second-genotype validation, a chronic animal package or a full safety battery. A negative result needs stronger qualification, especially evidence that established deficits can change in the chosen model/window. The root Round-8 decision architecture governs statistical advance/stop rules; this document fixes the biological meaning and operational units.

**Public evidence supports a fundable qualification question, not an already qualified article or an S-tier development asset.** The central question is whether exact BLU produces useful muscle output at measured exposure while preserving energetic function. Mouse genetics strengthens that question; it does not answer it in this human line.

### Why the selected force window is provisional

The Curi first graphic has a markedly changing healthy trajectory across culture age. Its separately labeled 96-well graphic shows a more persistent deficit and the site describes approximately 2.5–5-fold lower twitch output with repeatable day-27 traces. The images do not identify donor or tissue counts, SD versus SEM, independent experiments, exact stimulation conditions, or precisely what “day” is relative to thawing/differentiation/casting. **No published CV, intraclass correlation, repeatability coefficient or usable sample-size input was recovered.** Reading plot error bars or treating contractions as independent observations would not solve this. [M02]

Accordingly, use **day 20 to day 27 after the locally documented tissue-casting origin** as a candidate post-deficit treatment interval, with qualification readings nominally at days 13, 16, 20, 23 and 27. These are proposed assay days, not a claim that the manufacturer used this exact schedule. Freeze the day origin, dosing start and endpoint after blinded untreated qualification and before drug results. If that interval does not retain a reproducible gap and adequate dynamic range, change it once using the untreated trajectory, or redirect the model. Do not select a favorable age after viewing BLU outcomes.

The public JoVE platform paper supports longitudinal direct twitch/tetanic-force measurement and describes a **24-well** protocol using commercially obtained primary healthy myoblasts. It is a platform-method reference, not validation of p.L463S, the current 96-well geometry or a transferable variance estimate. [M09]

### Additional human functional precedent found this sprint

Conference abstract **067**, Lionello/Tedesco et al., April 2026, reports human 3D XLMTM models built from immortalized myoblasts and iPSCs with different MTM1 variants. Contractile impairment and calcium abnormalities are reported; the stated CRISPR-correction result is improved **fiber size**. The abstract supplies neither exact genotypes, donor counts, quantitative force effects nor a delayed rescue protocol. It strengthens the plausibility of human functional models but does not establish corrected-force rescue, identify an orderable model, or provide independent BLU evidence. A bounded search recovered no fuller exact XLMTM report that resolves those details. [M04]

Kora's model also has explicit limits: the article reports lysosomal/mTOR abnormalities but not the desmin, mitochondrial-morphology and integrin-signaling abnormalities sought in that system. Forced MyoD may bypass early developmental effects. Those observations argue against assuming every MTM1 model expresses every disease mechanism. [M03]

## 2. What the first assay measures

**Primary endpoint:** absolute electrically evoked peak twitch force, in µN, at the locked endpoint, estimated with adjustment for pre-dose force and batch. Use a fixed, sub-fatiguing stimulation/readout protocol and a predefined average of a short set of acceptable stable twitches. Qualification determines the field strength, pulse width, stimulation cadence, recovery interval, accepted waveform rules and force-calibration range. Those instrument parameters are not publicly resolved for this exact product and must not be invented as a replicated manufacturer's method.

This endpoint directly asks whether the same patient background produces more useful output after treatment. It does not, by itself, establish intrinsic myofibrillar restoration, NMJ efficacy, diaphragm rescue or respiratory benefit. Record direct muscle stimulation first; adding motor neurons would add a second mechanism and a new maturation problem to the initial test.

| Measurement | Role and denominator discipline |
|---|---|
| **Absolute twitch force** | Primary output. Preserve µN values, baseline and endpoint, all randomized tissues and the contemporaneous healthy/reference gap. A weak starting value must not create a large fold-change success by itself. |
| **Specific force / stress** | Secondary explanatory measure: force divided by a documented contractile cross-sectional-area estimate. Record how the tissue and contractile fraction were measured. Whole hydrogel area is not necessarily myofiber area. Do not replace a failed absolute-force endpoint with a favorable denominator-driven ratio. |
| **Tissue size, viable muscle area and attachment** | Image tissue geometry, post attachment, passive tension and viable contractile area. Growth or better force transmission can be useful, but distinguish them from increased force per contractile area. A drug that improves attachment is not automatically a false positive; it changes the mechanism and the portability of the result. |
| **Fatigue / sustained output** | A prespecified secondary endpoint after the primary twitch reading: absolute force-time integral or impulse over a fixed challenge, absolute late-train force, and relative force retention. Report both absolute and normalized traces. A lower initial force can yield an apparently favorable retention percentage despite less total output. |
| **Relaxation and calcium** | Relaxation time, contraction/relaxation waveform quality and calcium-transient amplitude/decay are mechanistic follow-ups. Increased peak force with materially impaired relaxation or unsustainable calcium loading is not an uncomplicated rescue. |
| **PI3P compartment, pS6, p62, DNM2, integrin localization, morphology** | PD/mechanism or explanatory endpoints. None can replace useful function. Prior BLU withdrawal and genotype-dependent DNM2 findings specifically caution against such substitution. [M10–M12] |

The phenotype-reference gap and repeatability floor are separate quantities. The former estimates a target worth recovering in this particular assay; the latter determines whether an apparent gain can be distinguished from measurement/culture noise. Because the reference is nonisogenic, “25% of the blinded calibration gap” is a prospective **program target relative to these two backgrounds**, not 25% correction of a genetically proven disease deficit.

## 3. A finite first run grid

### A. Untreated qualification: 24 longitudinal tissues

Use **three independently initiated tissue-casting/culture batches × two backgrounds × four tissues = 24 tissues**, measured repeatedly over the nominal day-13–27 window. Prefer independent thaws/casts and retain lot identifiers. If all cells originate from one manufacturing/differentiation lot, say so; three casts do not become three independent manufactured lots or three donors.

Within each qualification batch, obtain repeat low-fatigue recordings in a controlled session with a prespecified recovery interval. This estimates readout repeatability. Changes between days also contain maturation and biological drift and must not be labeled instrument error. Include physical post/instrument calibration, an electrical recruitment check, and a recoverable contractility-inhibition control in a small dedicated control module if needed. The JoVE platform's myosin-inhibitor example is evidence that an inhibitory assay control is feasible; it does not establish a disease-reversal positive control. [M09]

Lock the acquisition, waveform extraction, technical-exclusion rules, functional endpoint, time window and baseline-adjustment analysis before adding BLU. Qualification passes only if there is an observable patient/reference gap beyond the calibrated noise floor, neither background is at an instrumental ceiling/floor, and the chosen window permits an interpretable comparison. A stable healthy trajectory is helpful; predictable contemporaneous drift can be modeled. Rapid or poorly reproducible collapse is a reason to redirect, not to normalize it away.

### B. Dose/exposure exploration: 96 longitudinal tissues

Use **three new independently initiated batches × two backgrounds × four arms × four tissues = 96 tissues**. The four arms are vehicle and three BLU exposure levels chosen from the separately qualified chemistry/engagement range. This yields **12 independently randomized exposure units per background/arm distributed across three batches**, with one patient donor and one reference donor. It is an operating budget, not a declaration of adequate power. Place every arm in every batch; balance plate positions and handle both backgrounds in parallel.

Randomize eligible tissues after the pre-dose reading using a documented allocation procedure, with baseline force used for balancing rather than retrospective exclusion. Treatment labels and analysis remain masked. Record the full denominator from tissues cast, through pre-dose eligibility, randomized tissues and endpoint availability. Tissue detachment, cell death or silence **after dosing** may be drug outcomes and must not disappear as quality-control exclusions. Repeated contractions, microscope fields, technical mass-spectrometry injections and serial time points are nested observations, not additional randomized tissues.

Primary readout is day-27 force adjusted for day-20 baseline; short early recordings can distinguish immediate inotropy from a delayed biological change. Challenge fatigue only after the primary reading, or use separately assigned siblings if the challenge changes later function. Continuous stimulation used for maturation must be identical across arms and documented because it is itself an intervention.

Non-destructive medium sampling for measured exposure can be linked to the functional tissue ID. Destructive engagement, lipid, ATP and morphology samples require a separate sibling-tissue manifest; do not count those samples again in the longitudinal-force denominator. The article/exposure workstream defines actual sampling times, free-fraction methods and native-engagement feasibility. Cell-associated total drug is not free intracellular concentration, and a CETSA shift is not automatically a percentage occupancy.

### C. Independent confirmation and a second background are conditional

Select a candidate exposure range using exploration, then confirm in new batches with a locked endpoint, exposure and analysis. Confirmation sample size must use the observed qualified variance, batch structure, missingness and the root decision threshold. It is not fixed by the exploratory 96-tissue budget.

If confirmation is positive, add a different truncating MTM1 background and a same-background correction or independent causal perturbation. The p.E48* pair is a particularly useful published precedent if an executable material route can be documented. CRICKi008-A p.Tyr198* and the GM06813 reconstruction are distinct alternatives, not extra independent responders already demonstrated. A healthy-background MTM1 KO with isogenic parent can sharpen causality, but by itself is not a second patient genotype or proof of chronic congenital-disease reversal.

## 4. Controls must answer different questions

| Control | What success would establish | What it would not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle in each background/batch | Time, handling and solvent comparison for the exact drug test | Disease causality |
| Healthy reference ± BLU | Reference output, nonspecific strengthening and healthy-muscle liability at matched exposure | Isogenic normalization or all-genotype benefit |
| Same-background MTM1 correction established before myogenesis | MTM1 contribution to the phenotype and a developmentally normalized comparator | Reversibility after the day-20 deficit |
| **Delayed inducible WT-MTM1 expression** activated after documented deficit | A candidate test of post-deficit modifiability in the exact tissue/window, if it actually improves force | Automatic physiological replacement or a ready validated control today |
| Delayed PIK3C2B reduction | Directional target-mechanism test after deficit; useful concordance if it improves force | Equivalence of protein depletion to catalytic inhibition, or independent proof when the same pathway has no effect |
| Qualified chemically distinct C2β inhibitor, provisionally exact compound 47 | Independent chemical support if native exposure/engagement and selectivity are established | An automatically matched potency scale, inactive control, or proven XLMTM efficacy |
| AZD8055 | Candidate cross-pathway biological comparator, supported by mouse disease/force evidence | A validated human positive control, C2β-specificity or a safely repurposable systemic medicine |

Delayed MTM1 expression should be near the contemporaneous physiological reference range, with measured abundance/localization and vector/induction controls. A construct introduced before differentiation but induced only after deficit may separate delivery failure from timing; its construction and validation are real work. Large overexpression is not equivalent to endogenous correction. Published high-expression MTM1 gene-delivery findings include context-dependent pathology, so “more MTM1” cannot be assumed an inert control. [M13]

**Useful positive drug results do not have to wait for a successful delayed correction.** A randomized, exposure-linked force improvement can justify confirmation even if delayed-control engineering is unfinished. Conversely, drug inactivity in a model with no demonstrated post-deficit response cannot eliminate the reversal hypothesis. A healthy tissue that contracts strongly only proves the apparatus and reference can work; it does not prove the patient tissue is still modifiable. Mouse juvenile delayed genetics and local adult-onset deletion provide complementary biology, not a substitute for qualifying the actual human interval. [M10–M12]

AZD8055 should be a separately budgeted comparator arm only after a tolerable cellular exposure and mechanism readout are established. The previously audited delayed mouse force result is useful, but its unblinded/unrandomized design and cohort/dose-label issues remain. Do not expand the first BLU grid into a speculative combination study. BLU2720 is a weaker active related article, not an inactive analog. [M12]

## 5. Mitochondrial evidence: what was and was not resolved

| Evidence lineage | Exact perturbation and endpoint | Bound on interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Aki et al., Science, 8 October 2026** | Public abstract: PI(3,4)P2-bearing Golgi vesicles at fusion sites; cardiomyocyte-specific **combined C2α/C2β deletion** causes fragmentation/heart failure. [M14] | Main/supplement access failed; current Europe PMC metadata lists no PMCID/open full text. No cardiac single-KO denominator or β-only catalytic threshold was recovered. This is not BLU toxicity. |
| **Segawa thesis, same research lineage** | Figures 4-10/15/16: single α, single β and combined depletion fragment mitochondria in HeLa/COS7/A549; at least 30 cells/condition/experiment, three experiments. Figure 4-14: paired add-backs under **dual depletion**, at least 15 cells/condition, three experiments; α-D1268A/β-D1213A. [M15] | Paired WT add-back rescues; the design does not isolate a β-only catalytic safety window. Cells are nested measurements. Morphology in transformed cells is not energetic failure in human muscle. Thesis and paper are not independent replications. |
| **Alliouachene 2015** | Protein-retaining C2β-D1212A mice reportedly normal for viability/growth/fertility and histology across 38 tissues through 20 months. [M16] | Supplement Table S1 lists tissues, without an animal denominator/functional reserve assessment. Lifelong adaptation and healthy tissue limit extrapolation. |
| **Islam 2020** | Individual α or β loss did not alter measured blood pressure; combined loss did. Figure-3 groups: α control/KO 10/10; β 10/9; combined comparison 13/10. Aortic rings n=3/group. [M17] | Tissue-specific redundancy is plausible. Vascular smooth muscle does not prove cardiac mitochondrial tolerance. |
| **Newly integrated: Kobialka 2023** | C2β kinase-inactive mice have enlarged vessels from endothelial enlargement with increased mTORC1 during angiogenic growth; rapamycin normalizes morphogenesis. [M18] | Primary institutional abstracts verified; full PDF failed, so age-specific/figure denominators are unresolved here. This qualifies broad “normal histology means no phenotype” reassurance; it is neither an observed BLU adverse event nor evidence of inevitable clinical vascular harm. |

The thesis explicitly discusses limited dynamic range in peripheral-region mitochondrial measurements: double depletion did not appear quantitatively worse than single depletion. That prevents an inference of equivalent biological severity. No source supplies a drug-concentration, inhibition-percentage or duration threshold at which selective C2β inhibition becomes harmful.

C2α sparing remains a worthwhile selectivity target, but does not erase the single-C2β morphology signal. Conversely, deletion of two proteins cannot predict the consequence of reversible partial catalytic inhibition. The favorable disease catalytic-knock-in genetics remains a distinct counterweight. [M10, M14–M18]

## 6. Exposure-linked safety modules, in order

The first module should answer the most immediate contradiction: does a force gain cost the tissue its energetic reserve? Later modules address the systemic product hypothesis. Their exposure ranges should be measured in each culture matrix; equal nominal doses do not establish equal free concentrations or engagement.

| Stage | System and observations | Interpretation / action |
|---|---|---|
| **With initial human force proof** | Patient and healthy muscle at the same effective exposures: viability/integrity, absolute fatigue output, relaxation; sibling tissues for mitochondrial organization plus ATP-linked respiration/energy reserve or a qualified orthogonal energy measure. Acute and end-of-exposure observations distinguish transient adaptation from accumulated injury. | Morphology alone is a mechanistic alert. Reproducible energetic/function loss overlapping beneficial exposure requires causal challenge or molecule redirect; apparently stronger twitch with collapsed sustained output does not pass as uncomplicated rescue. |
| **After credible muscle signal, before systemic expansion** | Human cardiac microtissues at measured beneficial muscle free-exposure range and a feasible higher characterization exposure: paced force/relaxation, rhythm/conduction readout, viability and mitochondrial/energetic function, with an established assay toxicant and vehicle. A repeat-exposure interval longer than the initial muscle proof is needed for accumulated effects. | Use matched C2β/C2α engagement where feasible. A threefold free-exposure challenge is a proposed characterization step, **not a proven chronic therapeutic index**. No in-vivo cardiac margin exists until tissue distribution is measured. |
| **Conditional neuronal module** | Mature, functionally qualified human neuronal networks; spontaneous/evoked activity, burst behavior, cell integrity and recovery after exposure. Compare matched exposure and actual neuronal target/antitarget effects. | Existing PIK3C2B genetics is a susceptibility signal with incomplete penetrance, not exact-BLU clinical neurotoxicity. An unfavorable CNS window can motivate peripheral restriction if muscle exposure is preserved. [M19] |
| **Conditional hepatobiliary module** | Qualified hepatocyte/canalicular system: transport and architecture, bile-acid handling and injury at relevant exposure. Add an MTM1-deficient/corrected context when a reproducible disease phenotype is established. | Healthy-cell viability cannot waive MTM1-related hepatobiliary vulnerability. Failure to establish a disease liver model remains an explicit uncertainty; it is not proof that BLU injures or rescues liver. [M20] |
| **Later developmental/vascular watch** | Endothelial size/mTOR signaling and appropriate functional vascular phenotypes if the product implies prolonged exposure during growth. | The Kobialka result supports a targeted later question, not a mandatory complete vascular-development program before the first muscle signal. [M18] |

The proposed energy measures must be qualified for 3D tissue geometry and normalized to viable tissue/cell mass alongside raw values. Increased oxygen consumption can reflect uncoupling rather than improved reserve; lower respiration can reflect reduced viable mass or altered work. Read energy, force, ATP availability and integrity together. Drug-sensitive dye signals alone cannot settle mitochondrial function.

To attribute a liability, compare exact BLU with a qualified chemical comparator and isoform-specific genetic perturbations. A selective β intervention reproducing benefit but not BLU injury supports a molecule problem; convergent β-associated functional injury at the required inhibition/exposure supports a narrower target window. Complete knockout and transient catalytic inhibition remain separate evidence units. Loss of pS6 in muscle is not a whole-body safety marker: neuronal and endothelial PI(3,4)P2/mTOR effects can move differently. [M18–M19]

## 7. Advance, redirect, inconclusive and stop

These categories attach to claims, not to enthusiasm for a target.

| Result | Decision |
|---|---|
| Qualified patient tissue, measured exposure/appropriate native engagement, a useful absolute-force gain with compatible sustained output and no overlapping energetic/integrity signal | **Advance to independent confirmation.** If a delayed rescue control is unresolved, state that limitation; it does not nullify the observed positive comparison. |
| Strong function gain but the native C2β mechanism or selectivity is unresolved | **Advance only as an exact-article functional lead with mechanism work outstanding.** Do not promote a C2β-specific product claim. |
| Morphology/PD improves without useful functional gain | **Do not advance on a function claim.** Reassess endpoint maturity, exposure and post-deficit modifiability within the bounded plan. |
| No adequate disease/reference gap, unstable maturation, assay floor, failed exposure or inadequate target detection | **Inconclusive for drug efficacy; redirect assay/exposure once.** It may be a valid stop for the current execution tranche, not for the target. |
| BLU inactivity with adequate engagement and precision, preserved tissue health and a successful delayed functional positive control | **Qualified negative for this exact article/background/window.** Stop or redirect the article. General target rejection requires convergent orthogonal evidence and relevant background coverage. |
| BLU injury overlaps benefit, whereas a qualified distinct β intervention retains benefit without the injury | **Redirect molecule/chemistry.** |
| Independent β interventions repeatedly lose useful function at the inhibition needed for benefit, and no exposure/distribution separation is credible | **Stop the current systemic product thesis or narrow it explicitly.** |

**Numerical assumptions for harmonization with the root decision record:** day-20 start/day-27 endpoint; 24 qualification and 96 exploration tissues; a default useful-force target of 25% of the blinded calibration reference gap, frozen as an absolute µN threshold together with the separate repeatability floor before treatment; contemporaneous gaps remain descriptive diagnostics; exploratory positive-effect uncertainty requirement followed by stricter independent confirmation. All are prospective. No arbitrary viability percentage, fibrosis score or mitochondrial-morphology threshold is asserted to be clinically meaningful. Safety effect limits and positive-control ranges must be locked using qualified assay performance before reviewing BLU results.
