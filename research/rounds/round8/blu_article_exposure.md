## 1. Freeze the authentic article before interpreting biology

The named article is **(S)-N-(5-(2-(1-cyclopropylethyl)-7-(cyclopropylmethoxy)-1-oxoisoindolin-5-yl)-4-methylthiazol-2-yl)acetamide**. The journal structure and named synthesis agree with Probechem's PC-26519 drawing. The vendor lists C23H27N3O3S and 425.55 g/mol; these also follow from the neutral structure. The journal reports nominal `[M+H]+ = 426` and a proton-NMR list, but no numerical final BLU3797 purity or enantiomeric purity. **BLU2720's 93.5% purity is unrelated to a BLU3797 batch.** The vendor's >98% HPLC statement is catalog evidence, not a receipt certificate. [AE-S01, AE-S02]

The calculated monoisotopic neutral mass is **425.1773129 Da**, with **calculated `[M+H]+ 426.1845894`**. These are proposed analytical reference values, not a published HRMS measurement. The listed proton integrations sum to 26; the named structure has 27 hydrogens including an exchangeable amide NH. That count alone is not a contradiction. The unusual integrated 4H envelope at 1.39 ppm also should not be silently rewritten into a preferred peak assignment. Preserve the original peak list and acquire an independently assigned spectrum.

### Proposed receipt and reference-material specification

**Every numerical criterion in this table is a prospective research-engineering limit, not a published BLU release standard, a regulatory release specification or an achieved result.** A qualified analytical lead may revise a limit before unblinding biological results, with a recorded rationale. No supplier promise substitutes for the measurements.

| Attribute and purpose | Required readout | Proposed acceptance / redirect rule |
|---|---|---|
| Constitution and formula | HRMS with isotope envelope; assigned 1H/13C NMR and 2D correlations; compare with the original structure and named intermediates | Correct connectivity and formula; protonated molecular ion within **5 ppm** of calculated 426.1845894 where that ion is observed. A matching nominal 426 ion alone is insufficient. A structural mismatch stops use of the lot. |
| Absolute stereochemistry | A validated resolving chiral method, plus a traceable S precursor/reference or another justified absolute-configuration assignment | **ee ≥98%**, corresponding to ≥99:1 S:R for a two-enantiomer mixture, and assignment consistent with S. Enantiomeric enrichment without knowing which enantiomer is present does not pass. Optical rotation alone does not establish identity. |
| Chemical purity | Orthogonal chromatographic/LC-MS assessment, chromatograms and peak integrations retained | **≥98% chromatographic purity** under the prespecified method; investigate unresolved coelution and significant unknown peaks. Area purity is not weight purity. Failure redirects purification and reanalysis before biological attribution. |
| Absolute active content and material form | qNMR with an appropriate reference, water/residual solvent assessment, counterion/solvate declaration and reconciliation | Measured content **95–105% of the declared content on its declared basis**; report actual neutral-BLU mass fraction. An explained hydrate/solvate is not automatically rejected for having less than 95% neutral drug by mass. Unexplained mass balance beyond **5%** requires resolution. All dosing calculations use measured content and actual form. |
| Route-related contaminants | Residual-metal screen appropriate to Pd chemistry; residual solvents/reagents and plausible close analogs; analytical limits stated | Report quantified contaminants and their maximum concentration in biological wells. No undocumented clinical metal limit is imported. If an impurity could explain a functional/mitochondrial effect, repurify or use a matched impurity control before attributing the effect to BLU. |
| Stock integrity and exposure material | Concentration by analytical assay, visual/analytical precipitation assessment, stability through actual handling and assay interval | Working-stock recovery **90–110%** of the content-adjusted target and no unresolved precipitation; **≤10%** loss or major new degradant through the validated stock-use interval. Failure changes preparation/storage and triggers requalification, not a target kill. |
| Lot traceability | Lot ID, origin, full analytics, reference spectrum, stock preparation history and retained reference aliquot | A unique chain from solid article to well assignment is required. A second independent lot or synthesis is used to confirm a decision-driving phenotype before advancement beyond initial proof. |

Do not label a newly made material “authentic BLU3797 reference standard” solely because it follows the published route. It is **independently authenticated material matching the published BLU structure** until comparison with an original reference becomes possible. That distinction does not prevent a useful independent qualification project.

## 2. Route reconciliation: what public evidence closes and what remains

### Preferred junction and alternatives

The usable junction is **(S)-5-bromo-2-(1-cyclopropylethyl)-7-fluoroisoindolin-1-one**, neutral formula C13H13BrFNO, average MW approximately 298.1554. It is JCI's starting intermediate for the reported cyclopropylmethoxy substitution and thiazole coupling. [AE-S01]

The original CN119490502A scheme, physical PDF p.28 / printed description p.19, and text on p.29 / description p.20 were checked. Esterification, benzylic bromination and cyclization using **(S)-1-cyclopropylethan-1-amine hydrochloride** furnish **A-1-1c**. The S label, drawn stereochemistry and fluorinated isoindolinone connectivity are consistent with the JCI junction. CN's subsequent hydroxy/difluoromethoxy chemistry leads to another series and must be excluded from the BLU route. The public route establishes a realistic chemistry starting point, not manufacturing capacity, authenticated inventory or commercial rights. [AE-S03]

The University of Michigan US20240226104A1 route also reaches the same named S junction through an amide/organolithium/formylation-reduction sequence. Its original PDF p.122 / printed p.47, paragraphs [0303]–[0304] and associated scheme were visually checked. Nearby examples contain copied headings for other substituents, so the exact drawing and reagents must accompany any extraction. [AE-S04]

### Quantity audit

| Record | Published quantity | Arithmetic result and interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| JCI BLU final step | 21.7 mg product; 0.114 mmol precursor; 38% yield | With neutral MW 425.55, mass-based yield is **44.73%**. A 38% yield corresponds to **18.43 mg** neutral product. Unreported content/form or transcription could explain this, but none is demonstrated. Do not assert an 85% purity merely because it would reconcile the numbers. |
| US [0303], starting acid | 6 g 4-bromo-2-fluorobenzoic acid; 18.4 mmol | Formula-derived amount is **27.40 mmol**. The 33 mmol/1.2-equivalent amine and 82.2 mmol/3-equivalent base align approximately with 27.4 mmol, while DMF's stated 37 mL/0.5 M aligns with about 18.5 mmol. No single editorial correction reconciles the whole record. |
| US [0304], amide and reductant | 6 g amide; 20.4 mmol; Et3SiH 6.8 mmol called one equivalent | Named amide MW implies **20.97 mmol**. The stated 6.8 mmol reductant cannot be one equivalent of 20.4 mmol. These values are visible in the original PDF, not only machine extraction errors. |
| CN A-1-1c inputs | 13.05 g bromomethyl ester / 40.04 mmol; 4.87 g S-amine HCl / 40.04 mmol | Formula-derived amounts **40.036 and 40.047 mmol**, respectively, agree with the stated salt and amounts. This resolves a material salt/free-amine distinction. |
| CN A-1-1c output | 3.17 g; reported three-step 26.3%; initial acid 10 g /42.91 mmol | Junction product is **10.632 mmol**; simple overall yield is **24.78%**. Step-2 crude is 13.5 g but 13.05 g proceeds, so a complete mass balance is unavailable. Adjusting only for that aliquot gives **25.63%**, not 26.3%. Do not convert the reported overall yield into a validated manufacturing estimate. |

**Operational choice:** use the CN pathway as the first independent route-development basis because its exact cyclization inputs and junction identity are well specified; retain the US route as an alternative. Before release, reconcile all planned quantities from structures and document actual intermediates, yields and stereochemical retention. The project can proceed from public chemistry without claiming that either publication is already a complete, corrected operator protocol. An owner response is not the gate. The article-release evidence is.

## 3. Biochemical activity: replace unresolved replication with a defined comparison

The JCI v2 PDF **page14**, Methods subsection **“PIK3C2B biochemical assay”**, identifies 120 nM human PIK3C2B as **“PV3574l, Thermo Fisher Scientific”**, then reports final 60 nM enzyme, 1 mM ATP and 150 nM diC8 PI. The incubation before substrate addition is 10 minutes; the kinase-reaction duration is not given in this subsection. [AE-S01]

Thermo's primary Adapta protocol lists **PIK3C2B PV5374** and separately **TR-FRET Dilution Buffer PV3574**. Its product page specifies PV5374 as human residues **155–1383**, GST-tagged, insect-cell expressed, 167.4 kDa. The journal code therefore does not identify a reconstructible enzyme article as written. This is a **documentation mismatch**, not evidence that the investigators used dilution buffer instead of enzyme. Nor does the public catalog establish which construct their experiment actually used. [AE-S05, AE-S06]

The previously identified 28 nM IC50 lies near half the nominal 60 nM enzyme concentration. The newly explicit lipid arithmetic adds another qualification: 150 nM substrate/60 nM enzyme is 2.5, and 150 nM maximum product from 1 mM ATP is 0.015%. These observations do not prove an invalid experiment; they prevent treating 28 nM as a transferable free Ki. Preserve the nM units and assess the reaction/detection range rather than silently changing them. NCATS guidance recommends checking enzyme-concentration dependence and initial-rate conditions for this reason. [AE-S07]

### Independent catalytic qualification work order

1. **Declare the enzyme article.** Record sequence boundaries, tags, mutations, source/lot and active-enzyme estimate. PV5374 is a public starting construct, not a confirmed reconstruction of JCI's reagent. An additional full-length or independently prepared human C2B construct provides a useful later construct bridge if the initial activity is decision-driving.
2. **Establish an interpretable reaction.** Determine ATP/lipid response and linear time/enzyme range. Record actual substrate units and preparation. Aim for **<20% substrate conversion** in the initial-rate experiment, a benchmark supported by the public Thermo protocol; demonstrate acceptable signal at the lower enzyme concentration instead of presuming the published 60 nM condition is portable.
3. **Compare BLU and exact 47 side by side.** Include vehicle, no-enzyme/no-substrate controls and a declared positive catalytic inhibitor. Measure at matched ATP conditions, including 1 mM ATP where technically validated, and at a locally determined ATP-Km condition. Do not compare isolated IC50 headlines across dissimilar constructs/ATP.
4. **Use an orthogonal readout.** Pair ADP generation with a validated lipid-product or direct phosphate-transfer assay; include a fixed-ADP detection counterscreen to detect compound effects on the coupled luminescence chemistry. A consistent concentration-response across distinct readouts supports catalytic inhibition; disagreement triggers assay/artifact resolution.
5. **Predefine data quality.** Proposed research criteria: control-window Z′ **≥0.5**, control CV **≤20%**, concentration-response captured within soluble exposure, and reproducible direction on **three independent assay days**. Inspect enzyme dependence and slopes; do not impose a Hill slope of one or automatically reject a steep tight-binding curve. No ±fold acceptance band around published 28 nM is justified until a genuinely comparable assay exists.

For antitarget profiling, public exact articles include C2α **PV5586**, GST-tagged residues **142–1412**, and VPS34 **PV5126**, full-length GST-tagged protein. They are tools to construct declared assays, not evidence of BLU's native-cell selectivity. Record any differences in ATP, lipid or active-enzyme fraction; an apparent numerical “selectivity ratio” without those conditions is not a portable safety margin. [AE-S08, AE-S09]

## 4. Cellular engagement without depending on a private tracer

The reported NanoBRET C2B IC50 is **68.2 nM**, a nominal concentration from an engineered fusion assay. JCI describes custom in-house tracers for the C2B/C2G/PIK3CG work without disclosing their chemical identity, concentration or affinity. Host-cell and medium details do not establish native-muscle occupancy. Addgene **238834** supplies a documented NanoLuc–PIK3C2B fusion-vector path; it does not supply the missing tracer calibration. Publicly searched Promega material did not resolve that exact mapping; its selection page is dynamically populated, so text-search absence is not evidence that no private or catalog assay exists. [AE-S01, AE-S10, AE-S11]

The source-data audit's other limitations remain: C2α and class-Iβ were omitted from the journal cellular table; PIK3CA's 20,000 nM entry is at the tested ceiling; the finite VPS34 14,366 nM entry is unreconciled with released all-zero dose-response cells. Preserve the published number and the conflict side by side. Do not fit a new number to zeros or treat the conflict as demonstrated VPS34 toxicity.

### Concrete public detection bridge

NCI's portal provides two **Direct PRM** assays for human C2B: **non-CPTAC-5672, ISDVFLC[+57.021464]R**, and **non-CPTAC-5673, LIESSLGSVATK**, developed in **SUM159 tryptic digest** with isotope standards and Q-Exactive instrumentation. The linked Washington University PRM SOP is downloadable. This resolves a useful assay-development component: two independently measurable peptides can support native-protein quantification without first obtaining a custom C2B antibody or tracer. It does not demonstrate detection in differentiated muscle, a drug-dependent thermal shift or occupancy. [AE-S12]

**Proposed sequence and interpretation:**

| Stage | Readout and prospective quality gate | Meaning of pass / failure |
|---|---|---|
| Native C2B detectability | Transfer both peptide assays to the actual muscle matrix; isotope-standard coelution/fragment-ion agreement, usable concentration range and CV ≤20%; verify protein specificity by an appropriate genetic control | Passing establishes measurable native protein. Failure is an assay-readiness failure; it is not absence of target or lack of drug binding. |
| PRM-CETSA feasibility | Compare vehicle and BLU in intact cells and matched lysate using a thermal-response series; unheated total-protein controls distinguish degradation/expression loss from thermal solubility. Include exact47 once it qualifies. | A reproducible concentration-dependent protein-specific shift supports engagement in that context. CETSA is an established method class, but C2B thermal responsiveness must be discovered, not assumed. [AE-S13] |
| Engagement interpretation | Replicate across three biological preparations; compare with measured extracellular and cell-associated exposure and catalytic/genetic PD direction | A thermal-shift EC50 is **not automatically fractional occupancy**. No shift does not prove no binding. Native thermal evidence plus function and independent target perturbation is stronger than a fusion assay alone. |
| Quantitative occupancy, if needed | Independently develop/calibrate a tracer assay, including tracer saturation, concentration at or below apparent affinity, adequate assay window, expression effects and viability/donor-signal controls; test the relevant cell context | Only a qualified quantitative method permits an 80% occupancy statement. An off-the-shelf K-3/K-10 tracer must not be declared compatible with C2B without data. |

Cap this bridge at **one assay-development stage plus one documented technical-redesign cycle** before choosing a different measurable engagement strategy. This is a planning cap, not an elapsed-time promise. A development project must not consume repeated force studies while article exposure and target modulation remain uninterpretable.

**Important decision distinction:** the proposed **80% C2B engagement** level is a high-engagement characterization/challenge target if a quantitative method is available. It is **not** a required minimum for a useful positive response. Benefit at lower engagement can be genuine. Conversely, a negative at unmeasured coverage cannot invalidate the target; even a well-covered negative is scoped to its exposure, duration, genotype, model maturity and demonstrated modifiability. Global target rejection requires orthogonal catalytic/genetic evidence across appropriate backgrounds.

## 5. Exposure qualification must accompany the force experiment

The public mouse workbook contains terminal or after-final-dose plasma observations without the recorded elapsed post-dose times or free fractions needed to reconstruct a 24-hour trough. It supplies no independently auditable free-muscle/free-brain/free-liver time course. An FBS-corrected figure line is not a measured plasma-unbound threshold. These are missing translational measurements, not evidence of poor tissue exposure. The published 68.2 nM engagement value and the 188 nM peak myotube-nuclear response are starting calibration anchors, not human dosing targets or muscle-force EC50s. [AE-S01; prior round-5 workbook audit]

### Exposure measurements and integration

| Question | Required data | Proposed interpretation / action |
|---|---|---|
| What actually entered the well? | Content-adjusted stock, measured medium concentration and solubility after preparation; batch/plate/medium lot | Nominal concentration is retained as an assignment, while measured concentration drives exposure-response interpretation. |
| What remained accessible through the functional interval? | Matched medium total and unbound concentration, with matrix-specific binding/recovery/stability controls; samples after first addition, before the next medium change, after the final refresh and at the terminal readout | Define sampling relative to actual medium changes; sampled post-feed and pre-change values are not assumed true Cmax or Ctrough. Greater than20% unexplained exposure drift redirects stability/feed/formulation work before a negative is interpreted. |
| Did compound associate with muscle cells/tissues? | Washed tissue/cell-associated total, blank carryover controls, tissue amount/cell-volume normalization and extraction recovery | Cell-pellet mass demonstrates association, not free cytosolic concentration. Unbound intracellular concentration requires a separately justified method. Never divide total tissue concentration by a plasma IC50 and call it target coverage. |
| Are engagement and function contemporaneous? | Native target/PD sampling from matched sister tissues at early exposure and terminal day27; retain baseline day20 and exact feeding/treatment timing | Destructive samples have their own denominators and never inflate the longitudinal force N. Align with the model workstream's p.L463S and nonisogenic reference protocol. |
| Is the analytical method adequate? | Matrix-matched calibration and low/mid/high QC; selectivity, recovery, matrix effects, dilution integrity and handling stability | Proposed exploratory accuracy **80–120%** and CV **≤20%** at decision-relevant concentrations. This is a research-fit starting gate, not a claim of ICH M10 validation; formal regulated work generally requires tighter criteria. If the expected separation is smaller than analytical uncertainty, tighten the method before deciding. |

A **10 nM–3 µM nominal exploratory bracket** is a prospective assay-development suggestion, conditional on solubility and viability. It is not a published BLU range proven to cover human muscle. Use the resulting measured exposure/engagement curves to choose the three functional exposure levels; do not fix them by applying the HEK/fusion 68.2 nM value to native muscle. Protein binding and uptake may differ sharply by serum/medium and differentiation state.

Define the minimum repeatable beneficial exposure as the lowest tested **measured free-medium exposure profile** associated with prespecified functional improvement, with uncertainty retained. The profile includes duration and pre-change exposure; a single concentration is an incomplete summary. Quantitative native engagement can be added if available, while a thermal shift remains a distinct measurement. The **threefold higher free-exposure challenge** is a planned characterization point for isoform and mitochondrial readouts, conditional on solubility. It is not a validated chronic safety margin or a mandatory development-wide therapeutic index.

At a functional-benefit exposure, **≤20% C2α and VPS34 inhibition/engagement** is a prospective clean-interpretation goal where validated comparable assays can quantify it. A >20% signal is an investigation/chemistry-redirect trigger, not proof of clinical toxicity. Do not translate biochemical residual activity directly into native-cell occupancy. Preservation of mitochondrial useful function must be evaluated in the same qualified muscle system; α sparing cannot exclude an on-target β liability. The model/translation workstream owns the energetic, force/fatigue and morphology criteria.

### Later distribution bridge, conditional on an informative positive

Before a systemic product claim, connect repeated-dose unbound muscle exposure to the beneficial profile and assess brain, heart and liver exposure against relevant tissue-specific functional findings. Record total and unbound plasma/tissue, sample times, blood contamination corrections, binding-method recovery and metabolites. Compute tissue/plasma ratios only for matched times or justified steady-state conditions. Tissue homogenate binding is a model for intracellular free exposure, not direct native occupancy. No brain exclusion, liver safety, oral bioavailability or human dose is inferred from the current mouse terminal data. Distribution can either create or remove a useful window that equal-concentration culture comparisons cannot settle.

## 6. Exact comparator work order: 47 first, 91 conditional

Its C2B enzyme is **Δ1–297, DPK1248–1250→KKT**, at 40 µM ATP and 200 µM PI; those conditions differ from both JCI and PV5374. Published 56/48 nM values for 47/91 therefore cannot rank them against BLU28 nM. Their HEK293-lysate kinobead result is not evidence of cell entry. If BLU and47 both give exposure/target-linked force benefit, confidence in target causality increases, but shared binding motifs and residual off-targets still matter. If BLU harms function while47 retains benefit at matched β modulation, redirect the molecule before rejecting the target. If47 does not enter cells or reach target, its functional negative is uninterpretable. If both qualified catalytic articles reproduce a harmful functional tradeoff at the benefit-required modulation, challenge the systemic target/product thesis using the model workstream's genetic and mitochondrial distinctions.

## 7. Advance / hold / stop record

| Gate | Advance | Hold or redirect | Stop scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| Material | Exact structure/stereo and content pass, stable soluble exposure | Repurify, correct declared form/concentration, or independently resynthesize | Stop the failed lot; do not call it target failure. |
| Catalysis | Reproducible inhibition with declared construct and orthogonal readout | Resolve enzyme/substrate/reporter artifact or construct dependence | Stop a nonmatching/misleading article attribution if activity disappears in qualified assays. |
| Native target modulation | Qualified engagement-compatible evidence plus exposure and mechanistically concordant PD | Method development or an alternative target-detection route; phenotype alone does not measure occupancy | Stop claiming direct native occupancy if no quantitative method exists; an informative phenotype may still justify bounded follow-up. |
| Human muscle function | Reproducible prespecified benefit at measured exposure with preserved useful mitochondrial/contractile function | Extend exposure/time characterization or qualify comparator47 | A qualified exact-article negative reduces BLU priority; broad target rejection requires stronger orthogonal/model evidence. |
| Product path | Useful muscle exposure separates from relevant liabilities and a credible value-capture route exists | Different chemistry/distribution, patient subgroup or indication thesis | Stop the current systemic commercial thesis if no useful functional window or defensible development/value-capture route remains. |

The chemistry and assay improvements make the next project more reviewable. They do not settle ownership, freedom to operate, scalable manufacture, formal toxicology or clinical efficacy. The matched intermediate in a patent is not permission to commercialize BLU, and public availability of a synthetic route is not a proprietary asset. Those distinctions remain separate decision fields rather than reasons to defer all scientific progress to outreach.
