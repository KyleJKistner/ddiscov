## The text cross-check and its limit

The EP and translated CN records expose the same **two principal structural branches**: claim 1 is directed to a Formula 1 compound for the stated medical use; claim 10 is directed to Formula 2 compounds. Their later target, disease, composition and in-vitro claims refer back to structurally defined compounds. Those dependencies matter: a mention of myotubular myopathy or C2β does not by itself turn the publication into a claim to every molecule that inhibits the target. Conversely, compound 47 is a disclosed member of this program; its usefulness as a scientific comparator does not create an independent proprietary position.

The narrow chemistry result must also retain the specification's broad substituted-aryl definition. Whether that definition expands an atom position without a drawn substituent handle is a **named claim-construction ambiguity**. The companion audit can report an unsatisfied element under the explicit-variable reading without deciding every legally possible construction. This ambiguity applies before any question about territorial status or enforcement.

No byte-for-byte identity of entire national claim sets is claimed. The web text omits or poorly represents chemical drawings, and the Chinese translation contains obvious formatting artifacts. A shared claim count or wording excerpt cannot substitute for original drawings or the controlling amended claims.
