## What needs careful interpretation

**Clone evidence:** A second engineered clone can address a clone-specific alternative explanation for the phenotype it actually reproduces. It does not create an independent donor background, demonstrate patient-variant coverage, or replicate every intervention. Exact edited alleles are not interchangeable with the broad monoallelic/biallelic labels. Ledger M03 identifies the remaining primary-source gap.

**Replication:** Cultures, clones, cells, images and patients answer different independence questions. The change in the statistical description should be reported as a version change, without assuming newly collected data or claiming a complete audit of the published analysis. A global culture statement does not itself map each plotted cell to its culture or establish the unit used in every test. See M04.

**Timing and comparator scope:** Rescue before an endpoint challenge can support causality while leaving treatment of an established mature barrier unresolved. Likewise, an imperfect attachment effect cannot be promoted to complete barrier recovery. The Discussion's characterization of the attachment result is an author report; the unrecovered S4 panels cannot support an independently extracted magnitude, denominator or analysis. Presence of a drug in Methods alone would not establish efficacy. See M05–M07.

**Identity and baricitinib:** Preserve the line-name inconsistency and historical exposure exactly as source provenance. Do not infer persistent drug exposure, residual JAK inhibition or a demonstrated confound. An unresolved washout history is an unknown, not positive evidence of carryover. See M02.
