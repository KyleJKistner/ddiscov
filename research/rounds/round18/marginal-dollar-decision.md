## What would reverse the next-dollar choice

1. **A tractable, informative existing route:** preserves the conditional qualification opportunity. Actual cost and access facts are still necessary for a later concrete commitment.
2. **Major scope expansion:** requires comparison of incremental cost, delay, learning and retained economics with eligible alternatives and waiting. Do not convert an unfunded model-development project into an assumed cheap validation step.
3. **A credible functional positive:** supports independent confirmation; it does not establish clinical benefit or ownership.
4. **An uninterpretable negative:** does not establish target failure. A qualified, model-specific negative can narrow the corresponding article/window claim; broader target conclusions require additional selective and independent support.
5. **Insufficient product contribution or retained value:** can defer asset development even if the science remains valuable. A scientific or public-benefit project needs its own stated objective.

No recovered source meets a reversal condition that clearly favors another candidate for this completed sprint. That is a bounded ordinal judgment under incomplete financial inputs, not proof that BLU has the highest absolute financial value.
