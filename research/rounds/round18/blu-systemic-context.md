## Human liver evidence does not collapse into a muscle readout

Inoue et al.'s primary abstract describes **two patients with liver involvement**, with reduced respiratory-chain enzyme activity in **two muscle specimens** and normal activity in **one liver specimen**. The full paper was not recovered. Exact variants, quantitative enzyme values, complex-specific results and specimen-to-person linkage remain unverified. [L18-S1]

The inference is limited: a muscle mitochondrial readout does not establish liver mitochondrial function, and a normal hepatic enzyme assay does not establish normal canalicular transport, reserve, all mitochondrial functions or drug tolerance. One hepatic specimen cannot establish a universal mechanism or a safe subgroup. This result adds a concrete example to the existing organ-specific interpretation rule; it does not add a universal mitochondrial gate or show that BLU harms or rescues liver.

The relevant earlier evidence remains separate:

The existing conditional hepatobiliary question remains appropriate when it changes a commitment. A complete de novo liver-model program is not automatically required before an informative muscle feasibility result; a reassuring muscle result likewise does not settle a chronic systemic product claim.

## EXCEL is a named future evidence source

The **January 2025** Astellas notice describes EXCEL, **NCT06581146**, as a prospective observational liver study lasting about one year, with approximately **50 males planned**, age below 18, requiring some ventilatory support. Liver/gallbladder assessments were planned roughly every six weeks, and concurrent participation in an XLMTM interventional treatment study was excluded. [L18-S2]

These are planned characteristics in a dated notice. Actual enrollment, present recruitment/completion status, outcome values and data access were not verified. The live registry page was a shell and its API inaccessible. Elapsed planned dates do not establish completion; planned participants are not observed patients. The notice identifies a specific future source of longitudinal information. It does not provide a qualified assay, samples, access terms or a task to initiate a duplicate study.

## The gene-therapy comparator is changing

### New program detail, with no new clinical efficacy estimate

Astellas's **27 April 2026** presentation names the **MyoAAV3.8** muscle-targeted capsid for ASP2957 and reports high muscle specificity with reduced liver targeting in nonclinical work. The slide and accompanying earnings transcript describe **clinical-study initiation at approximately 100-fold lower dose than AT132**. [C2–C3]

This is a sponsor-reported starting-dose/program comparison. The exact historical AT132 comparison dose and a common normalization for an absolute dose calculation were not established in the inspected passage. No absolute ASP2957 dose is inferred. The source does not demonstrate a 100-fold therapeutic window, equal efficacy, a final recommended dose, or a numerical reduction in patient risk. The slide and transcript describe the same sponsor event, not independent biological corroboration; the original nonclinical dataset and its denominators were not audited.

An official-domain indexed sponsor listing also identifies **NCT07876804 / 2957-CL-4001**, long-term follow-up for males treated with ASP2957, with status **Will Be Recruiting**. The detailed record was not recovered; actual enrollment, follow-up duration, cohort linkage and outcomes remain null. This is a stated follow-up plan, not demonstrated durability or an independently treated efficacy cohort. [C4]

**Decision inference:** a permanent market of patients unable to receive gene therapy cannot be assumed from AT132's history. The next program is explicitly pursuing different targeting and dose burden. Its clinical success remains unknown, and neither its eligibility exclusions nor its plans establish BLU adequacy in excluded patients.

### Existing clinical benchmark and population limits

The current readable VALOR sponsor record continues to describe an open-label phase 1/2 safety/tolerability/dose-finding study, with nine planned participants, in males projected to be no older than 36 months at dosing, requiring at least 20 hours/day invasive ventilation and a tracheostomy. Liver-related eligibility limits remain. This describes a selected trial population, not an eventual label, actual dosed denominator or a measured residual market. No interpretable ASP2957 clinical result was recovered. [C1]

The earlier ASPIRO trial supplies human respiratory/motor evidence for **AT132**, alongside four treated deaths with cholestatic liver failure in its exploratory as-treated analysis. Those benefits and harms remain attached to AT132. They are not assigned to ASP2957 or BLU. Existing INCEPTUS and biopsy records remain prior evidence rather than newly counted studies. [C5; prior repository continuation]

A BLU product could have value in a specified population or through a useful repeatable-control or complementary role, if those claims are demonstrated. Dish force is an informative early endpoint; it is not hours off ventilation or a validated clinical benefit threshold. An oral route does not establish a cheaper or safer treatment, and the entire XLMTM population cannot be converted into a revenue forecast.

## Cost, contribution and stopping

The independent review separates **$60–180k / 3–6 months** of article/exposure/model qualification from **an additional conditional $100–300k / 4–8 months** of functional work. These are analyst assumptions. The first block may end before useful human function is tested; the second is not an automatic release. No verified combined budget, guaranteed timeline or development total is calculated. Rights/licensing, GLP, GMP clinical manufacture, trials and commercialization remain outside those figures. [I1]

The next commitment must be tied to an informative output and actual scope. If an existing route requires substantial new chemistry, model construction, systemic work or rights acquisition, compare the changed incremental cost, time, information and retained value with alternatives and waiting. Publication existence, a catalog listing or future observational data cannot validate a quote or execution capacity.

All three update branches are closed. Reopen for substantive human outcomes, exact-product hepatic exposure/function, readable methods that resolve a named model input, or concrete costs/access/terms that change a finite commitment. No generic ADME, claim-mapping, catalog, licensing or inaccessible endothelial search was reopened. The APR and RM passes remain closed.
