# Round 19 — NDC-9009/Darier article and exposure audit

**Candidate:** R3-NL-01. **Base:** `d506a607ad01669ff4add03988e80d6bc0d9c633`; tree `caef05c1765c0d6872da9d7feb11960d4e43740d`. **Evidence cut:** 4 November 2026, America/Los_Angeles. **Retrieval:** 5 November 2026 UTC.

## Decision

The public evidence supports an NDC-9009 effect on **functional keratinocyte cohesion during junction formation**. It does not establish reversal of established Darier lesions, useful continuing control or prevention, persistence after withdrawal, or an executable topical product. The nominal culture concentration and the reported PBS solubility leave a material exposure question. They do not establish that the published experiment had no dissolved drug or that topical delivery is impossible. The finite public-source article/exposure pass is closed with those limits; there is no basis for advancing NDC into a clinical-efficacy or purchase-ready tier. [1,2]

The next marginal dollar has value only if a bounded qualification package can resolve a consequential uncertainty about the exact article, relevant exposure, a qualified functional disease phenotype, and a defined product claim. This is a decision criterion, not an instruction to perform an experiment. The current sprint undertook public-source review only. The inherited $40–150k and 3–6 month envelope is an illustrative portfolio assumption that included a functional answer. An article/exposure/model-readiness tranche is narrower: it does not buy that answer and cannot inherit the earlier rank-four rationale without a new marginal comparison. No expenditure, numerical financial EV, probability estimate, scientific model, simulation, or experiment was undertaken.

## What the primary Darier paper establishes

The main text distinguishes two SERCA activators. The **one-hour desmoplakin localization rescue is attributed to CDN1163**, not NDC-9009. NDC-9009 appears in the four-hour dispase fragmentation experiment, which tests monolayer cohesion directly. Consequently, “only a marker rescue” understates the NDC evidence, while attributing the one-hour CDN result to NDC overstates it. The functional observation remains tied to the junction-formation treatment window. [1]

The inaccessible supplement prevents confirming any further donor-by-treatment allocation, technical replicate structure, batch detail or missing exposure information. In particular, the three independent fragmentation experiments must not be silently recast as three independent patient-level efficacy observations. [1]

Untreated Darier cells reportedly approach control desmoplakin localization by four hours. That does not erase the separately reported four-hour cohesion defect: marker localization and resistance to fragmentation are different endpoints. Likewise, cells having been confluent for one to three days does not by itself show that treatment began after a stable, mature functional disease defect was established. The published calcium-switch context supports an assembly-window interpretation; no delayed-treatment established-lesion reversal result was verified. [1]

Two reporting details remain visible rather than being harmonized by assumption. The Figure 2 caption specifies six-well plates, while the general dispase methods specify twelve-well plates. DD3/DD4 required Y27632 during maintenance, and the comparison cultures were matched for that culture condition; the methods state that Y27632 was absent during experiments. Its absence during testing does not remove culture history. Neither issue alone proves that the result is invalid. They limit confidence in a fully specified, transferable qualification package. [1]

DD5–DD7 biopsy immunohistochemistry is tissue context, not three further NDC-rescued donors. Menadione stress findings and protein-extraction behavior also must not be converted into clinical NDC efficacy or formulation evidence. RIPA, beta-mercaptoethanol and urea/SDS extractability concern proteins, not the solubility of NDC-9009. [1]

## Exact article and exposure

The chemistry article's reported synthesis supports the inferred systematic name **4-(dimethylamino)-N-(2-methylquinolin-8-yl)benzamide** for NDC-9009; the full name is an interpretation of that synthesis, not an explicit name transcribed from the paper. The paper reports LCMS [M+H]+ of 306. Its Table 1 lists molecular weight 305, **17.1 ± 1.0% SERCA activation at 2 µM**, and **0.23 µM solubility in PBS at pH 7.4**. The separate product/capture audit crosswalks this inferred connectivity to the later patent's explicitly named C18 example. The assay concentration of 2 µM is not an EC50. The reviewed source portion does not establish a kinetic-versus-equilibrium or free-unbound interpretation of the solubility value. [2]

The later Darier experiment used nominal 5 µM NDC-9009. A nominal amount added to a culture and a solubility measurement in PBS are not interchangeable exposure measurements. Culture composition, cosolvent, protein binding, transient supersaturation and other matrix effects can change the relation between added amount and available drug. This audit did not recover a measured dissolved or free concentration for that Darier treatment condition. It therefore records an **exposure credibility gap**, not a numerical exposure correction or a failed-efficacy verdict. [1,2]

No verified primary artifact from the bounded search supplies an NDC topical vehicle, viable-epidermal concentration, skin residence, dermal pharmacokinetics, local exposure-response relationship, healthy-skin margin, or withdrawal outcome. The chemistry paper's noncutaneous mouse administration context does not validate a topical vehicle. A small molecular weight and the possibility of local administration also do not establish a useful cutaneous therapeutic window. These are unestablished requirements for a specified product; the search cannot prove that such information exists nowhere. [1,2]

The actual article remains separate from CDN1163 and from unnamed or differently named Neurodon development compounds. The 2026 gift attribution connects the published material to Neurodon but is not authentication of any future batch, a certificate of analysis, permission to obtain material, a license, or proof of current commercial availability. No material was requested, purchased or received. [1]

## Claim-specific qualification and negative-result logic

**Repair of an established lesion.** The observed assembly-window result does not establish reversal of a mature cohesion defect. For a claim whose intended value is repairing existing disease, the missing treatment-window and direct-function evidence is decisive. A negative from a qualified established-defect context would bear on that claim, subject to relevant article identity and exposure being established.

**Prevention or ongoing control.** The lack of established-lesion reversal does not logically eliminate these different product claims. They remain unsupported by the assembly experiment alone. A meaningful prevention/control claim needs its own evidence that the proposed benefit is useful and achievable under its intended treatment conditions; it cannot borrow a repair result or be inferred from a junction marker. This is a correction to the decision logic, not a new prevention/control result.

**Persistence after withdrawal.** Withdrawal durability belongs to an off-treatment durability claim. A continuously used product can have value without persistence after stopping, but the present sources do not establish such a product's benefit, burden or tolerability. Requiring durability for every possible product would prematurely collapse distinct options; waiving useful benefit or exposure evidence would be equally unwarranted.

**Interpreting a negative.** A meaningful molecule/claim negative presupposes an authenticated relevant article, adequate relevant exposure, a qualified disease-associated functional deficit, and a treatment window applicable to the claim. If actual exposure is unknown, the phenotype is absent, or the endpoint measures a different process, a negative can be uninformative. A model-negative and a clinical/product-negative are distinct. Conversely, failure to qualify a model or exposure package can still justify stopping investment in that route because the purchased information would not answer the intended question. No new protocol, numerical threshold or experiment is specified here.

These distinctions preserve the earlier stop rule's purpose while limiting it to what evidence can actually falsify. The result is neither an efficacy promotion nor a global dismissal of NDC. The burden stays on a finite, executable, rights-compatible package whose information yield merits its cost.
