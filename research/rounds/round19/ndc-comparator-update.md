## 1. A controlled topical Darier program already failed to separate from vehicle

Sol-Gel's **17 December 2025** announcement says its vehicle-controlled Phase 1b SGT-210 Darier study did not demonstrate efficacy differentiation, and the company stopped advancement in that indication. This is newly incorporated evidence, not an event that happened after Round 18. The September 2025 sponsor filing described the ongoing program as double-blind, using **erlotinib ointment 5%**; the final result announcement does not repeat the strength or provide a final product/batch reconciliation. [C19-S01, C19-S03]

The 2026 annual filing repeats the Darier discontinuation. Its separate descriptions of a favorable maximal-use PK study and an Olmsted compassionate-use case cannot be reassigned to the Darier efficacy result. They concern other observations. No final Darier arm sizes, effect estimates, confidence intervals, endpoint hierarchy, exposure data or participant-level results were recovered. [C19-S02]

**Decision inference:** this supplies a relevant warning against financing a topical Darier program on mechanism, formulation feasibility or uncontrolled improvement alone. It does **not** demonstrate equivalence to vehicle, prove why the trial failed, establish a quantitative vehicle effect, or show that SERCA activation/NDC-9009 will fail. SGT-210 is an EGFR-directed comparator program, not NDC-9009. The next NDC commitment must be able to distinguish an article contribution from vehicle and background care; this does not mandate adding an unavailable SGT-210 product to a future experiment.

## 2. Keep the PDE4 observations separate by product, route and treatment sequence

| Evidence | Exact observation and denominator | Limit for an NDC decision |
|---|---|---|
| Bowling/Oberlin peer-reviewed case | One 15-year-old; once-daily roflumilast **0.3% cream**; four-week treated-area BSA about 20% to 0%, itch NRS 8 to 0; residual sideburn involvement where application was limited. | Already in Round 8. Exact ATP2A2 substitution, brand and batch are unreported. No controlled comparison or direct barrier-function result. [C19-S04] |

Roflumilast **0.3% cream** was added to residual symptomatic plaques; later oral apremilast 30 mg twice daily was used, with three-month improvement reported. | This is a co-treated, sequential case, not replication of roflumilast monotherapy or its three-month durability. The original PDF returned 404; figures and complete layout were not inspected. [C19-S05] |
| Bruno conference-host summary, September 2026 | One 34-year-old; approximately 36% baseline BSA; **oral roflumilast 500 micrograms every other day**; qualitative lesion improvement. | The primary summary supplies no quantified post-treatment BSA, follow-up duration, withdrawal result, exact genotype, brand or full safety account. Original PDF was unavailable. Oral dosing is not evidence for 0.3% cream or NDC topical delivery. [C19-S06] |

The age, authors and treatment descriptions distinguish these reports, but they are not pooled into a response rate or a larger monotherapy series. The Stegura reference in the Bowling/Oberlin discussion is a citation to that same poster, not another patient. The newly reviewed oral report remains a conference-level, uncontrolled observation.

The peer-reviewed cream case reports no adverse effects during its short follow-up. Its broader suggestion of treatment without systemic risks is not adopted: a single observation does not establish an exposure or safety margin. [C19-S04]

**Decision inference:** exact 0.3% cream remains a relevant same-disease clinical comparator. Its symptomatic signal can be valuable even without proof of SERCA correction. A future NDC product could differentiate on meaningful lesion control, flare/rescue burden, tolerability, durability or functional repair, provided its chosen claim is actually demonstrated. Conversely, a mechanistic adhesion assay cannot establish superiority to an immune-directed treatment in a system that cannot express the latter's mechanism. These reports neither supply a controlled NDC comparison nor establish an independently capturable roflumilast asset; the separate rights analysis controls that question.

Its primary, immortalized and ATP2A2-knockout model descriptions do not establish that every model received every reported intervention. The abstract does not supply a new clinical cohort, matched NDC comparison, topical delivery result or delayed mature-skin repair dataset. The publisher full-text request returned 403, so exact model-specific denominators and rescue timing remain unresolved. [C19-S07]

## 5. Consequences for marginal-dollar EV

The material update is the SGT-210 controlled negative, plus clarification of how thin and heterogeneous the PDE4 observations remain. Together they increase the value of an early discriminating qualification decision and reduce the case for an automatic full program commitment. They do not establish new NDC failure, a cheap validated alternative program, a recalibrated probability of success, or a change in the inherited APR/RM/BLU/NDC conditional order.

For NDC, retain the staged question: can the exact article reach a useful local exposure and produce interpretable benefit for the intended claim, with a plausible product contribution? If the intended claim is repair of established dysfunction, an assembly or marker result is insufficient. If the intended claim changes to ongoing control or prevention, evaluate that claim's useful benefit and comparative burden separately; it is not automatically worthless because it is not reversal. No new protocol, dose, sample size, statistical threshold or simulation is introduced here.

No fresh result in this lane clearly favors diverting the selected source sprint to a different candidate. Source closure does not demote APR, RM or BLU. No standalone valuation is calculated, and all action branches remain conditional on future authorization and the rest of the qualification/capture record.
