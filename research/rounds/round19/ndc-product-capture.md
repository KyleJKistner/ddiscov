### Publication, claims, ownership and permission are different questions

A published description makes information public; an issued claim defines an exclusionary right subject to its actual limitations and legal status. The USPTO explains that a patent does not itself authorize practicing an invention. Assignment concerns ownership; a license can be limited by field, territory, time and obligations. Even a recorded assignment is not an Office determination of its legal validity or effect. [S11–S12]

The paper's Neurodon assignment statement and patent bibliographic records establish a credible rights question. They do not supply an audited chain of title, current maintenance/enforceability status, all relevant jurisdictions, encumbrances, license availability, a skin-use carve-out, usable know-how, manufacturing support, or commercial terms. Google Patents' status and assignee labels expressly disclaim legal conclusions. Official Patent Center and Assignment Center retrieval did not return a substantive current record in this pass. The audit therefore makes **no current freedom-to-operate, exclusivity, unencumbered-title or license-availability assertion**. [S1,S3–S6; retrieval notes]

The absence of an explicit Darier word in the reviewed grant text does not resolve broader composition coverage, another use claim, another patent family or an unpublished application. Conversely, the existence of a SERCA patent family does not prove that every NDC skin product is covered. Neither conclusion can be substituted for an exact product-and-jurisdiction claim analysis.

## Roflumilast is incumbent context, not automatically a portfolio asset

Arcutis' 2025 Form 10-K, filed 25 February 2026, describes a worldwide topical-dermatology license under AstraZeneca-controlled patents, know-how and regulatory documentation, together with Arcutis' own topical-product IP and regional licensing arrangements. Its current collaborator page corroborates the topical dermatology relationship. These are issuer descriptions of contractual and product positions, not an independent validity determination for every patent. [S9–S10]

For this decision, the consequence is practical: evidence that a roflumilast cream can help Darier patients is relevant comparator information, while additional Darier evidence may principally benefit an incumbent product. Neither an off-label case nor the age of the active moiety creates a ddiscov-owned commercial right. A distinct indication-data contribution, improved presentation, or collaboration could have value only if its usefulness and route to retained economics are established. No such deal is established here. The existing roflumilast case remains a clinical-control comparator, not direct proof of restored SERCA function or an independent exclusive asset. [L1; S9–S10]

NDC should earn a meaningful advantage in outcomes patients value: useful control or repair, tolerability, treatment burden, durability, or a clearly served subgroup. A unique mechanism or lower nominal assay concentration is insufficient. Practical comparators remain those appropriate to the intended claim, including the previously recovered oral dantrolene context and current topical care; a model must be able to represent the comparator's relevant effect before an apparent win is interpretable. This is a decision criterion, not a new protocol. [L1]

## Amend the inherited prevention-only stop rule

**The generic “only assembly/prophylaxis improves” stop is too broad if applied to the entire product.** Round 3 already distinguished a possible prevention thesis from lesion repair. The independent ranking and current shortlist compress that nuance into wording that could reject a useful ongoing-control product solely because it does not reverse established lesions. Round 16 and Round 18 provide the appropriate claim-specific precedent. [L1–L3]

Recommended current wording:

> Deprioritize the established-lesion restoration claim if benefit is limited to assembly or prevention in an adequately qualified, interpretable comparison. Preserve a separate prevention or ongoing-control thesis only when it has credible useful functional or patient benefit, feasible exposure and tolerability, and a meaningful comparator and capture advantage. Assembly or biomarker improvement alone does not establish that alternative product. Model or delivery failure is inconclusive for the target. Stop or redirect commercial spending when there is no practical window, no meaningful product contribution, or no credible retained economic route.

This change weakens no existing article, exposure, healthy-tissue, comparator or functional-evidence requirement. It makes the negative inference fit the tested claim. A credible positive can justify focused confirmation before every ancillary uncertainty is resolved, while an interpretable negative requires an adequate article, exposure, disease deficit and precision; a claim of **reversal** additionally requires that the established deficit was modifiable in the tested window. A narrow negative is not a universal verdict on SERCA activation or every ATP2A2 genotype. [L1,L3]
