## What question deserved this information dollar?

Can an exact NDC-9009 topical product deliver useful Darier benefit at tolerable epidermal exposure, with a defensible incremental contribution and a plausible share of the resulting value? A patient-cell adhesion result makes that question specific enough to investigate. It does not price the answer or prove a commercial product. The highest-value public work is therefore to distinguish an accessible article from an owned asset, an assembly assay from an intended clinical claim, and improvement from an incremental treatment effect.

The previous independent review ranked this conditional opportunity fourth and supplied an illustrative **$40–150k / 3–6-month** envelope for a topical-exposure and established-barrier question. These are historical analyst assumptions, not quotes, approval probabilities or the cost of a complete program. The [preserved review input](independent-review-input.json) identifies the prior review and its Round 15 repository basis. The historical four effort points assigned to NDC plus its comparator do not determine the next dollar.

## Amend the claim-specific stop rule

The old blanket stop for assembly/prevention-only activity is too broad if interpreted as a rejection of every possible NDC product. It remains appropriate to reject a claim of established-lesion repair when the only demonstrated effect is concurrent junction assembly. But repair, ongoing control and prevention ask different questions:

| Intended claim | Evidence it would need | What would not establish it |
|---|---|---|
| Repair of established lesions or a persistent functional deficit | Improvement after the deficit exists, attributable to treatment and useful in the intended disease context. | Starting treatment during assembly, spontaneous recovery, a marker shift, or an unqualified negative assay. |
| Ongoing disease control | Useful benefit during treatment, with tolerability, treatment burden and a relevant comparator defining the product's value. | Calling transient calcium-switch rescue clinical disease control; requiring permanent cure as a universal prerequisite. |
| Prevention or preservation | A meaningful reduction in relevant functional loss, flares or disease burden under a qualified challenge or observation window. | Assuming that faster junction assembly alone proves fewer clinical flares or infections. |

Withdrawal durability is required for a claimed durable off-treatment effect; it is not a universal entry condition for a useful on-treatment product. Conversely, changing the label from repair to prevention cannot rescue an ineffective, intolerable or commercially redundant article. A scope change requires a separate feasible claim, evidence path, comparator and retained-value judgment. No preventive efficacy is newly established here.

This amendment is a decision inference, consistent with the existing portfolio rule that the model and endpoint must test the intended claim. It does not introduce a new protocol, dose, sample size, success threshold or experiment. The [2026 Darier consensus](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13555-026-01838-3) supplies clinical context for useful chronic disease control; it is not evidence that NDC provides it.

## Keep the first commitment finite

The retained $40–150k / 3–6-month assumption describes a bounded decision about topical exposure and useful skin function. It is not independently validated in this pass. A suitable existing model, material route, analytical work and an interpretable comparison must actually fit that scope before the envelope can be treated as executable. No portion of that range is automatically released.

Do not quietly expand the same tranche to cover major new model construction, a new medicinal-chemistry campaign, a full formulation-development program, licensed rights, GLP toxicology, clinical GMP manufacture, a patient trial or commercialization. Their costs and timing were not established. If useful function cannot be evaluated within the assumed scope, make the initial qualification deliverable explicit and compare the revised total information cost with another eligible opportunity and waiting. An initial exposure or model qualification result can justify stopping before efficacy is tested.

**Reduced information yield also requires re-comparison, even if the price does not change.** If a proposed qualification-only deliverable no longer answers the original established-barrier/function question, it does not automatically inherit the review's fourth-place rank or its EV rationale at the same $40–150k. The retained ordinal rank is conditional on the original informative scope; neither a cheaper preliminary check nor a renamed tranche establishes that rank for itself.

| Finding at a future authorized decision point | Resulting action |
|---|---|
| Authenticated article, an interpretable exposure/function route and plausible commercial contribution fit a bounded scope. | Consider only that qualified next tranche. A favorable source review does not authorize the whole program. |
| Positive function is reproducible and useful, with tolerable local exposure and a meaningful comparator advantage. | Consider confirmation and the next product question; no automatic human-efficacy or commercial-value conclusion. |

Patient benefit and an independently investable asset are distinct. |
| Chemistry, model, rights or comparator requirements expand cost/time, or the deliverable answers less than the original useful-function question. | Re-rank the actual revised commitment and its information yield, including retaining the money. Do not preserve NDC's ordinal place merely because it was selected for this sprint. |

## Why no switch or promotion?

The first three programs retain their published decisions and closed source boundaries. Exhaustion of their desktop searches does not reduce their underlying development EV. The next alternatives in the prior independent review, including mIGF-Gel, remain conditional options; this sprint does not claim a new full primary-source valuation of them.

The NDC update is mixed. Better provenance reduces an identification ambiguity, while sponsor overlap and patent distinctions make independent capture more concrete and still unresolved. The controlled erlotinib failure is a useful warning against overvaluing uncontrolled changes, but its different mechanism prevents direct transfer to NDC. Comparator reports do not supply a clear, superior and independently capturable replacement asset. These findings justify a more disciplined NDC option, not a new ordinal promotion or a precise numerical decrement.
