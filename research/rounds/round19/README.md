## What changed

The article-derived NDC chemical identity can now be connected to the named **C18** example in a later Neurodon patent. The original application and later grant must not be conflated. This is a disclosure crosswalk, not complete claim coverage, a current legal-status finding, a license or authentication of a physical batch. Neurodon's public Darier program also makes the contribution/capture question concrete without identifying NDC as its other named program codes.

The comparator pass recovered the sponsor's **December 2025 SGT210 Darier non-differentiation from vehicle and indication stop**, reaffirmed in 2026. The final trial estimates and denominators remain unavailable. It is a different molecule and supplies no direct negative NDC result. Roflumilast reports remain separated into the previously reviewed 0.3% cream case, a heavily co-treated sequential poster, and a sparse oral conference summary. Neither pooling nor a new monotherapy replication is justified.

The old prevention-only stop is narrowed to its intended claim. Assembly does not prove established-lesion repair or clinical prevention/control. A qualified repair negative can still leave a distinct useful prevention or ongoing-control hypothesis, but that hypothesis requires its own evidence, tolerability, comparator and economic case. Off-treatment durability is required for a claimed durable off-treatment effect, not every useful product.

The independent review's **$40–150k / 3–6-month** envelope remains unvalidated. Its fourth-place rank assumed an informative topical-exposure and useful-function question. A qualification-only substitute that delivers less information **does not inherit that rank or EV rationale**, even at unchanged price. Major model/chemistry/formulation work, rights, clinical manufacture, toxicology, trials and commercialization are not quietly included. No portion is automatically released.
