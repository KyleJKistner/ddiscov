### GA002 and vaultAAV: clearer boundaries on consequential signals

The recovered **ChiCTR2500111157** record is retrospective, was last refreshed on 27 October 2025, and describes a single-arm study with **six planned participants**. The publisher abstract describes **three participants**. Neither provides a verified all-treated safety denominator. Institutional blinded-assessor wording may coexist with an open-label intervention; the registry's `blinding=None` field requires protocol-level clarification, not an allegation or a claim that the whole study was blinded. The full clinical letter, protocol and appendix remain unrecovered. [Registry](https://www.chictr.org.cn/showprojEN.html?proj=280541), [publisher abstract](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMc2606815).

The recovered vaultAAV main text reports neutralizing serum from immunized mice tested with cultured CHO-Lec2 cells. Both packaged and unpackaged mixtures can enhance baseline transduction; the neutralizing-serum comparison is the more specific shielding observation. This does not demonstrate human immune escape or successful in-vivo repeat dosing. VaultAAV remains a delivery reserve. [Primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1021/acssynbio.6c00512).
