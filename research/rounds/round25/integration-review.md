Dipyridamole's C9, engineered TARDBP and AD contexts do not become separate trials. GA002 institutional accounts, registry and abstract overlap. APR patent examples and later meeting abstracts do not create new clinical patients. These constraints remain in the decision-facing summaries.

## Value of the next information purchase

APR remains the most concrete inherited small-development option because the evidence links the intended disease, a historical article and an existing clinical/model context. The review makes the next question more specific without pretending that access, output rights, comparative benefit or prices have been secured. The [advancement memo](apr246-advancement.md) separates qualification from the later useful-function commitment. A clinical route may be preferable if it resolves the same decision more directly; a compulsory model reset is not imposed.

A36 ranks first only within this round's new-screen information questions. Its functional preclinical evidence is more developed than the other two leads, and an institutional filing supplies a concrete rights question. This does not establish that a licensable package exists or that its next dollar outranks APR. Dipyridamole needs a credible exposure and product/capture proposition; a new analog is a separate, larger asset. Atorvastatin retains a narrow mechanistic question with weak demonstrated capture. None has a current offered tranche, retained-value estimate or quote sufficient for a global numerical comparison.

GA002 remains the most consequential clinical-information question, but greater clinical significance does not imply an attainable investment. VaultAAV remains a reserve with translation and rights gaps. The latest Round24 decisions remain visible and are not silently demoted by a new paper. Closed source passes reopen only for a consequential new fact or an actual scoped opportunity.
