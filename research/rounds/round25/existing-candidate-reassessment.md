## 2. GA002 — information priority survives, with a narrower evidentiary claim

### What was directly verified

The NEJM publisher abstract describes three people with refractory epilepsy, a mean seizure-frequency reduction of 92.6%, and adverse events described as grade 1 or 2. The online date is 4 November 2026; the issue date is 5 November. The accessible page names a protocol and appendix but does not provide their bodies. Thus the exact efficacy window, participant-level outcomes, selection/attrition, concomitant-treatment changes and complete adverse-event table were not independently inspected in the clinical letter. [R25-E01](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMc2606815).

CIBR's 5 November institutional account describes three people with unilateral hippocampal-sclerosis mesial temporal-lobe epilepsy, reports a large early seizure reduction, and says two remained seizure free during follow-up of eight to twelve months. It describes blinded assessors and a temporary seizure recurrence associated with interrupted activating medication. These are institutional claims associated with the investigators, not an independent replication or a controlled withdrawal experiment. That anecdote does not establish causality, and stopping the activating medication does not reverse the persistent gene exposure. [R25-E02](https://cibr.ac.cn/detail/researchAchievementpaper/7e141d834d99406db7c00600ce933837).

The funder's 6 November account describes a commercial transfer and Chinese/US clinical permissions. Those are funder/institutional statements; this workstream did not inspect the corresponding regulator records. Permission to conduct a study is neither a benefit finding nor commercial approval, and an existing sponsored program does not establish that ddiscov can obtain an attractive economic interest. [R25-E03](https://www.nsfc.gov.cn/p1/3381/2825/145361.html).

### The registry changes the interpretation, not the patient count

The primary record **ChiCTR2500111157** was registered and last refreshed on **27 October 2025** and explicitly identifies retrospective registration. It describes a single-arm interventional study, **planned sample size six**, and fields showing no randomization, no blinding and no data-and-safety-monitoring committee. Safety is the primary outcome category. The registered execution period is **8 January 2025 to 7 January 2030**, and the recruitment period is **9 January 2025 to 31 December 2025**. These are planned dates in a stale record, not confirmation of current recruitment, actual completion or final enrollment. Absence of a listed committee does not establish absence of all oversight. [R25-E05](https://www.chictr.org.cn/showprojEN.html?proj=280541).

The three relevant denominators must remain separate:

| Quantity | What the inspected source establishes |
|---|---|
| Published clinical cohort | Three people in the NEJM abstract. |
| Registry planned sample size | Six planned participants; not an actual-treated count. |
| All-treated safety cohort | Not established by the accessible abstract or registry. Broader institutional program totals must not be added to the published cohort or treated as a safety analysis. |

The registry's blinding field and CIBR's blinded-assessor description are a **reporting-level discrepancy requiring protocol clarification**. An open-label intervention can have blinded outcome adjudication, so the records are not necessarily mutually exclusive. Neither record warrants calling the whole study blinded. Retrospective registration also limits how confidently the currently visible endpoints can be treated as prospectively fixed. These are reasons to preserve uncertainty, not allegations about investigator conduct. [R25-E01](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMc2606815), [R25-E02](https://cibr.ac.cn/detail/researchAchievementpaper/7e141d834d99406db7c00600ce933837), [R25-E05](https://www.chictr.org.cn/showprojEN.html?proj=280541).

### Next decision

Retain the leading clinical-information position. A lawful full clinical report/protocol/appendix or a substantive updated registry/result record could materially change confidence in benefit, safety and the next development question. The author data-sharing statement gives a concrete future release condition—within six months after **trial completion**—but neither completion nor a released dataset was verified. No outreach, purchase or monitoring was initiated. [R25-E04](https://www.nejm.org/doi/suppl/10.1056/NEJMc2606815/suppl_file/nejmc2606815_data-sharing.pdf).

A financial promotion additionally requires an attainable interest and actual terms. Even a more convincing clinical result cannot by itself rank an unavailable financing or license above an accessible development option. There is no basis here for a numeric expected return or an assumption that three observed responses estimate a population response rate.

## 3. VaultAAV — full text clarifies the reserve boundary

The recovered ACS paper was published online on **13 August 2026**, in the **16 October 2026** issue. Its principal neutralization comparison uses cultured CHO-Lec2 cells and serum from immunized mice. It does not report a human-serum cohort, treated patients or an in-vivo repeat-dosing benefit. Vault-packaged AAV retained transduction in the neutralizing-serum comparison; an unpackaged vault/AAV mixture performed poorly there. In the absence of neutralizing serum, both the packaged preparation and the unpackaged mixture enhanced transduction, so baseline uptake improvement alone is not specific proof of encapsulation-mediated immune escape. [R25-E06](https://doi.org/10.1021/acssynbio.6c00512).

The paper presents proof of concept and future possibilities. Its discussion itself limits a universal immune-invisibility claim by noting vault-directed autoantibodies in another disease context. The inspected principal figure does not supply a clear independent-replicate or serum-donor denominator; none is reconstructed from error bars or statistical labels. The authors' expectation that the approach might eventually permit repeat dosing is not an observed repeat-dosing outcome. [R25-E06](https://doi.org/10.1021/acssynbio.6c00512).

**Next decision:** keep the platform evidence/rights reserve. Reopen on consequential product-relevant translation or a concrete economic opportunity, rather than another rendition of the existing cell assay. Current ownership, the reported acquisition and attainable rights remain inherited uncertainties; this lane did not freshly verify them. The full-text recovery increases precision about what has been shown, without promoting the candidate above the defined local-development queue. [Round 24 matrix](../round24/existing-candidate-matrix.json).

### Why APR remains first after the challenge

APR's position rests on a comparatively concrete starting article and a consequential, bounded question about useful ongoing control in the same disease. The coordinated model recovery narrows a possible evidence route, while the official rights records identify actual claim and prosecution questions. Neither finding eliminates the uncertainties that could make a small next commitment unattractive. [APR advancement](apr246-advancement.md).

The parent/advancement checks establish a published US claim set and a maintained **pending** European application. The US corneal predicate cannot be ignored merely because a product is applied to skin. The European register's examination-in-progress status and 2026 renewal record are not a European grant, an available license or a freedom-to-operate opinion. Amended claims listed in October 2025 remain unread. These facts strengthen the need to evaluate capture before a substantial commitment; they do not force rejection of every possible product route or establish legal clearance. [R25-A03](https://ppubs.uspto.gov/pubwebapp/external.html?q=12521392.pn.&db=USPAT), [R25-I04](https://register.epo.org/espacenet/application?number=EP19740548&lng=en&tab=main), [R25-I05](https://register.epo.org/application?number=EP19740548&lng=en&tab=doclist).

GA002 could have greater clinical significance while offering no currently attainable contribution. VaultAAV could address a large delivery problem while still requiring much more product-specific translation and a rights route. Neither fact establishes a better **next purchasable commitment**. RM4404 and the remaining defined options are preserved as comparators if APR's actual scope or terms deteriorate.

The inherited dollar/time ranges in the matrix are historical analyst planning assumptions. They are not offers, present quotes, comparable milestones or complete program budgets. BLU's qualification and subsequent function ranges remain separate; AAT's initial range excludes organ/transplant work.
