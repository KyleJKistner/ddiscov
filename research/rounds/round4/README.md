# Round 4: continued screening and deeper candidate vetting

**Evidence cut: 4 November 2026.** Three domain teams screened additional leads and deepened the leading programs. The integrating review added an islet-manufacturing candidate, inspected BLU3797 raw source data, and challenged the strongest new corneal lead. A second independent workstream checked that corneal interpretation before integration.

**Result: 22 new exact intervention-use screens, six existing candidates updated, one new independent-validation priority, and no S-tier promotion.** There are seven update records because BLU3797 received both a domain review and a source-data/chemistry audit. The new records are not 22 independent molecules or unrelated families. Benchmark programs and negative screens remain visible.

The [current shortlist](../../../decisions/current-shortlist.md) controls over historical grades. The [cumulative register](../../portfolio-register.json) preserves 622 original coverage rows, 30 round-2 proposals, 36 round-3 candidate/use screens and this round's additions. These denominators overlap and must not be summed into a count of distinct validated drugs.

## What changed the decision

1. **BLU3797 is more reconstructible, but its small late-start claim is narrower.** Two public disclosures supply matching upstream chemistry. Source-data extraction reveals a regimen discrepancy and incomplete cohort linkage. Strong wire-hang observations remain; normal force and equivalence do not follow from reaching the assay ceiling.
2. **8015-P2 has a more concrete first human assay route.** CMTA describes an operating R364W motor-neuron service. A T105M donor wishlist does not provide stock, and a planned organoid project does not provide a ready force assay. An author correction removes an obsolete allele-based harm extrapolation.
3. **NDC-9009 remains conditional and its local priority is open.** Residual SERCA function, mature barrier repair and measured epidermal exposure matter more than total protein or extra biopsy counts. APR-246 has stronger human/timing evidence and competes for the next local qualification budget.
4. **AAT's causal foundation strengthened.** Independent active/inactive protein and protease-knockout controls weaken a generic added-protein explanation. They sharpen the warm-perfusate potency/comparator gate without resolving the finished product.
5. **RM4404 becomes a new independent-validation priority.** Its delayed functional observations merit replication. Correct paired-eye analysis, masked sensory testing, matched formulation and an active comparator are essential. The late structural medians do not establish an advantage merely because significance versus healthy controls differs.
6. **Negative evidence now changes selection explicitly.** Tamoxifen's XLMTM safety halt, luteolin's failed Fanconi outcomes, genotype-specific Bis-T-23 failure and copanlisib's withdrawn US approval prevent attractive older claims from re-entering as fresh opportunities.

## Deeper vetting: what source inspection changed

### BLU3797

The advanced intermediate can be connected to two public upstream disclosures. This is a plausible route requiring chemical reconciliation and lot qualification, not demonstrated commercial supply. The raw workbook was downloaded unchanged from the publisher and archived under its stated CC BY 4.0 license. All 815 populated cells checked across four selected sheets matched an independent reader.

Extraction preserves source-cell addresses, asterisks and missing observations. It calculates descriptive summaries only. It does not impute missing animals, fit new significance tests, pair unlinked cohorts, or decide that marked values should be excluded. Several high-dose observations reach the 60-second ceiling. A late-start survival header describes a different P22 dosing course from the supplement caption. The functional baseline includes only two entries per group, while later observations include five. These limitations affect the replication brief.

See [audit and route map](blu3797-audit.md), [derived data](blu3797-source-audit.json), [unchanged workbook](../../source-data/blu3797-source-values.xlsx) and [reproducible reader](../../../methods/blu3797_source_audit.py).

### RM4404

Independent review corrected an initial overbroad statement about blinding: the main text describes blinded secondary imaging review. Primary quantifier and sensory-assessor masking remain unresolved. This correction is in the published memo and JSON.

The surgical model treats paired eyes as independent. Supplemental totals of 22 and 12 are observations across three arms, not per-arm animal counts. The chemical model uses one injured eye per animal. The shorter course has assessment four days after dosing ends; it cannot inherit day-30 follow-up from the longer surgical cohort or be called verified pharmacokinetic washout. At day 30, axonal-network medians of 357.7 for vehicle and 362.1 for RM4404 do not establish structural superiority from different comparisons with healthy controls.

The vehicle is described as similarly prepared, but dose-matched solvent fractions are not explicit. This is a qualification question, not demonstrated confounding. Direct delayed functional comparisons leave a worthwhile replication proposition; they do not establish independently verified durable regeneration. [Primary study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/jnr.70142)

### AAT and the local-treatment comparison

The independent AAT study used an inactive loop mutant and albumin. Combined with the protease-knockout arm, this supports a causal protease component. The next gate measures both NE and PR3 activity in the warm formulation, then asks whether useful organ benefit survives a matched physical control and a simpler inhibitor. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6078707/)

APR-246's chronic human observations deserve more weight than an earlier shortlist label. NDC's residual-SERCA hypothesis is testable, but its cell assembly result is not intrinsically superior to improvement in established AEC lesions. Resource allocation should compare feasible functional qualification, tissue exposure and potential product advantage. Each intervention retains its disease and mechanism boundaries. [AEC study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41419-020-2223-8) · [Darier study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13440392/)

## Process changes made concrete

**Classify what a source is.** A service description, donor wishlist, future grant aim, trial completion label, investigator safety letter and catalog answer different questions. Extracting a disease or compound name without that context created false availability and readiness.

**Keep exact articles attached to evidence.** P2 is not the parent mixture; TAT-fused P110 is not the bare peptide; tadalafil cannot inherit a different BEST1 compound's patient data; active AAT is not any warm-circuit formulation. Identity and potency must survive transfer.

**Count the biological units.** Donors, animals and independent families control replication. Extra eyes, recipients from one pancreatic isolation, fields, repeat cultures and biopsy-only samples do not automatically add efficacy evidence.

**Make later failures hard to lose.** Negative Fanconi results, trial safety halts, withdrawn extrapolations and regulatory changes remain beside the original attractive papers. A later search should update a decision rather than rediscover the favorable first report alone.

**Qualify before consuming scarce resources.** Authentication, exposure measurement and model qualification can precede donor organs or a large disease-model study. Missing finished-model access can create an explicit build task when feasible, rather than an email-dependent scientific hold.

## Decision boundary

This round supports concrete research investments: RM4404 replication, APR-246 tissue qualification and better-specified leader experiments. It does not demonstrate a finished, financeable asset. S-tier still requires meaningful function, credible reproduction, feasible exposure, differentiation, acceptable functional risk and plausible value capture together.

No physical sample receipt, wet-lab experiment or commercial clearance is reported. The next useful evidence is a decision-changing functional result; another favorable literature grade cannot substitute for it.
