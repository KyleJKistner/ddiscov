# Round 4: organ preservation and renal function

**Evidence cut: 4 November 2026.** Seven new exact candidate/use screens and two existing-lead updates. No S-tier promotion. The companion JSON contains the individual decisions, experimental units, source URLs, material routes and go/stop rules. This review uses public evidence; it does not report new experiments or acquired materials.

## Decisions that change the portfolio

**AAT retains its core priority with stronger mechanistic support.** An independent mouse transplant study substantially reduces the concern that its entire biological case is merely added protein or altered fluid handling. It does not resolve whether the proposed warm human-EVLP product has the correct formulation or a durable advantage. **Sivelestat belongs in the comparator set**, because a simpler small molecule can test how much useful benefit requires AAT’s broader protease profile.

**Bis-T-23 is the strongest new kidney research reserve in this workstream.** It has an explicit post-deficit functional experiment and a public chemical route. Its negative result in another genetic model makes genotype selection central. It does not yet displace the leading candidates.

**FXT-001 remains a valuable preservation benchmark.** Additional independent studies tighten mechanism and safety claims, while the exact human-organ methods gap remains unresolved. A public analytical-data deposit improves traceability without supplying missing donor-level function.

**Genistein, luteolin and QX77 expose a useful weakness in candidate selection:** an improved lysosomal marker or better receptor localization can coexist with unresolved or negative whole-tubule function. These findings should determine the priority, even when obtaining the compound is straightforward.

## AAT: causal support is better than the previous review showed

The 2018 independent study used active recombinant AAT, an inactive reactive-center variant and protease-deficient recipient mice. Active protein protected early transplanted-lung function; the inactive variant did not reproduce protection. The cold-storage experiment used 1 mg/mL, 18 hours of storage and four hours of recipient reperfusion. NE/PR3-deficient recipients were also protected. This is a distinct experiment from the 2026 human warm-EVLP work. [A1–A2]

The inference is specific: protease inhibition contributes to protection in that model. It does not prove that the effect in the human circuit is entirely protease mediated, that any AAT formulation is equivalent, or that no drug reaches the recipient. It also means the first product qualification should measure **both neutrophil elastase and proteinase-3 inhibition in the actual warm perfusate**. A product’s label potency against elastase cannot stand in for either the second protease or activity after circulation.

A useful next comparison has three separable questions. Does active AAT outperform a control matched for protein, final electrolytes and physical fluid properties? Does it improve useful organ function beyond an NE-selective small molecule at comparable target suppression? Does benefit persist when the organ encounters drug-free, recipient-like reperfusion? These questions can be staged so that formulation failures are resolved before committing scarce human lungs.

The desired product advantage is a reproducible activity window linked to retained gas transfer, compliance and fluid handling. If a simpler comparator delivers the same useful result with better handling or carryover, the development case should move to that intervention. A favorable answer requires no correspondence; it requires a discriminating experiment.

## FXT-001: new boundaries, not an automatic downgrade

The author-associated Mendeley deposit, DOI 10.17632/n7jbyznw8g.1, describes NMR and mass-spectrometry data. Only its metadata was inspected. It should not be represented as a recovered donor-level physiology dataset or a reconciliation of the exact human-study salt. The Cell paper’s complete organ methods were not recovered in this round, and the previously oversized PDF was not retried. [F1–F2]

Two independent contexts matter. UAMC-3203 reduced acetaminophen injury through effects involving mitochondrial Sab/pJNK even when substantial lipid peroxidation was absent. Thus a positive response to the compound does not, by itself, diagnose ferroptotic death. Separately, a randomized post-stroke diabetic-rat study reported mixed cognitive effects, including worse aversive learning. These findings do not demonstrate failure or toxicity of a brief ex vivo lung exposure. They do prevent a blanket “ferroptosis-specific and nontoxic” characterization. [F3–F4]

The scientific decision should remain function led: identify the exact article, measure tissue and recipient exposure, and use orthogonal evidence to interpret mechanism. A second mechanistic readout is useful when it can distinguish explanations; it should not displace the organ endpoint. FiriX still publicly identifies FXT-001 as its lead, so a separate venture needs demonstrable incremental value beyond the known preservation result. [F5–F7]

## Material and model readiness

The seven additions have more inspectable identities than a target-only proposal, but none is an authenticated ready-to-run program. Bis-T-23 has public supplier listings and a published synthesis-related patent route; QX77 has an exact CAS and catalog record; the RTR sequence is short and public. Sivelestat and emricasan have prior manufactured or clinical articles. Those facts establish possible research supply routes. They do not establish delivered lots, activity after formulation, local organ access or commercial rights. [B4–B6, Q3, R1, S1, E1–E3]

A recurring chemistry risk is alias compression: a brand name, salt, stereochemical form or analogue is treated as interchangeable with the material in the experiment. The next procurement specification should bind each named candidate to an analytical identity and potency assay. For Bis-T-23, supplier textual structure metadata also deserves cross-checking; a copied string is weaker evidence than a reconciled structure and measured lot identity.

For the renal candidates, a polarized functional human model is the missing bridge. The published QX77 editing strategy can be rebuilt, but an immortalized cell’s vesicle movement is not a validated patient tubular endpoint. For the graft candidates, whole-organ access is a separate operational fact. Bench formulation and exposure work should earn entry into scarce-organ experiments rather than allowing missing organ supply to distort the biological ranking.

## What earns the next dollar of proof

AAT offers the clearest immediate experiment because the newly recovered causal controls sharpen the comparison. Bis-T-23 offers a tractable new reserve if human barrier-function testing can establish a genotype-defined window. Sivelestat can make the AAT decision more informative without becoming an independent venture thesis. Emricasan and RTR need stronger functional and route evidence before they compete for that budget.

The screen should retain these negative decisions. Otherwise a later search will rediscover the original promising cellular paper, omit its follow-up, and count the same unresolved lead as progress. The companion JSON separates established facts, inference, proposals and unavailable measurements so that the next round can update a decision instead of restarting the narrative.
