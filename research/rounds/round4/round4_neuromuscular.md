# Round 4: neuromuscular screen and development-readiness review

**Evidence cut: 4 November 2026.** Six additional intervention–disease families were checked against the cumulative register. BLU3797 and exact 8015-P2 received further review. This is public-source research, not an experimental result or a statement that physical materials have been received. The structured evidence and source identifiers are in `round4_neuromuscular.json`.

## Decisions

**Retain BLU3797 and 8015-P2 as leading proof opportunities, with more specific gates. Add CLP290 as a functional reserve, and P110 as a human-cell reserve. No S-tier promotion.** The new searches produced two useful implementation findings and several reasons to reject superficially attractive development stories.

BLU needs a disease-specific hepatobiliary comparison alongside its muscle result. P2 has a publicly described, already-used human motor-neuron assay route; however, that assay measures organelle movement and morphology, not neuromuscular transmission or force. A separate advertised T105M resource turned out to be a donor-recruitment table, illustrating why the document around an indexed result must be read before labeling a material available.

## 1. BLU3797: make the liver gate specific to MTM1 deficiency

The TAM4MTM investigators' November 2024 letter states that the tamoxifen trial was halted after hepatic abnormalities in children with XLMTM; tests normalized after withdrawal. The registry's account of the investigator webinar describes two affected participants, including acute cholestasis. A trial database that labels the study “completed” is not an adequate substitute for the investigator safety explanation. These observations concern tamoxifen; they do not demonstrate BLU3797 toxicity. [R4NM-S01–03]

A primary zebrafish study independently makes the disease context experimentally relevant: loss of mtm1 disrupts canalicular transport, and hepatocyte-specific re-expression partially restores bile flux. DNM2 chemical inhibition also partially rescued that phenotype. The paper is not evidence that PIK3C2B inhibition corrects cholestasis. [R4NM-S04]

**Decision implication:** a generic healthy-hepatocyte viability panel is insufficient for the first serious BLU package. Pair the intended muscle exposure with an MTM1-deficient, genetically rescuable canalicular-transport model and an appropriate healthy comparator. Ask whether effective muscle exposure worsens, preserves or improves bile handling. A biomarker-only hepatic assay cannot establish this. Model qualification is an explicit deliverable; a ready qualified human XLMTM liver assay was not established in this review.

Target engagement should also remain compartment-aware. The 2025 Journal of Lipid Research study connects PIK3C2B depletion to correction of an EEA1-associated PI3P pool and Rab4-vesicle biogenesis in Mtm1-deficient muscle cells. The previously reviewed 2026 Nature Metabolism work connects lysosomal phosphoinositides to Rag–mTORC1 and differentiation. These support choosing mechanistic readouts relevant to trafficking; neither adds an exact-BLU human-force result. The two mechanistic papers share investigators and are not independent clinical validation. [R4NM-S05–06]

The ranking remains conditional on useful post-deficit human muscle function. The revised first proof should be able to separate a target failure, an unsuitable compound exposure profile, and an unqualified model. The root review separately audits the BLU source workbook and the publicly disclosed chemistry route.

## 2. 8015-P2: a more concrete human assay route, without changing the endpoint standard

### Publicly described service versus demonstrated development endpoint

In March 2026, CMTA described a CMT2A assay developed with BrainXell and ANANDA Devices and already used for screening. It uses R364W patient-derived motor neurons and a healthy line in a platform separating axons from somata. Reported disease differences include mobile-mitochondria fraction, cell area and cell number. This is a useful independent operational route for a mechanistic screen. It is one patient background versus a healthy comparator, with no published P2-response or NMJ-force result on that page. Access is through a collaborative service model; a present slot, delivered lot and recipient terms were not verified. [R4NM-S07–08]

The distinction matters because the newly listed KU Leuven project describes adding MFN2/GARS1 models and stimulated contractile measurements to an organoid platform as future work. It does not establish a completed CMT2A force assay. [R4NM-S09]

**Proposed order:** use an authenticated P2/parent/P1 comparison in a qualified human neuronal system as the cheaper identity, exposure and mechanistic gate. Spend on mature neuromuscular model development only with a predeclared functional qualification criterion. Continue to require nerve-evoked output, direct-muscle comparison and genetic rescuability before calling the final outcome a human development proof. A positive mitochondrial-image result alone cannot move P2 into S-tier.

### A supply-table false positive was resolved

Search results for the Summer 2024 CMTA report display T105M male/female rows beside other MFN2 variants. Reading the actual PDF, page 19, shows a table of **desired donors for new cell-line creation**, not a list of available cell lines. It does not close the exact-T105M supply gap identified in round 3. The public PDF was downloaded normally and its heading/table were checked; it is cited rather than redistributed here. [R4NM-S10]

This changes the process: each material claim must retain the document's purpose, exact line identifier, producer, recipient eligibility and date. A mutation name in a resource-related search result is inadequate evidence of inventory. A source can establish biological interest while supplying no usable material.

### Morphology and genotype should not be collapsed into a responder label

An independent 2026 study of L248H and M376V fibroblast backgrounds found opposing mitochondrial morphologies alongside impaired fusion in both. It did not test P2. This makes a simple rule such as “elongated mitochondria means a fusion activator is inappropriate” unreliable. [R4NM-S11]

There is a related citation trap: the authors of the earlier Drosophila R364W paper withdrew their extrapolations about human disease pathogenesis and possible harm from pro-fusion therapy. This was a correction of those conclusions, not retraction of the whole article. Separately, the 2022 human-fibroblast study found a response to Chimera in R364W cells. That supports preserving R364W as a test background, but it is not exact-P2 efficacy or independent confirmation of the later P2 studies. [R4NM-S12–13]

Measure fusion events, transport and useful neural output rather than selecting patients or declaring rescue from morphology alone. P2's free-exposure and healthy-function requirements from round 3 remain unresolved; no new human unbound-exposure result was found in this bounded review.

## 3. New functional reserve: CLP290 for chronic SCI spasticity

The 2018 Cell study reported stepping improvements after staggered SCI lesions; groups began at ten mice, with eight vehicle and ten treated at week nine. It also had genetic KCC2 experiments, but those cannot establish the precise drug's mechanism. Complete-transection locomotor rescue was absent in that study. [R4NM-S14]

The independent 2025 study strengthens the chronic-use rationale: treatment was tested four weeks after complete rat SCI, with seven animals entering and six contributing many endpoint analyses. It used a within-animal pre/post/washout design with a blinded handler. Crucially, treadmill testing included manual support and perineal stimulation. This is assisted stepping/reflex control, not recovered unassisted walking. Several responses returned toward pretreatment after washout, and no separate vehicle session was described. [R4NM-S15]

Mechanism is a serious development question. A primary independent study found CLP257, the active parent of CLP290, did not enhance KCC2 in its assays and instead potentiated GABA-A currents; other binding activities were also reported. Thus a useful functional drug effect and a validated KCC2 mechanism remain distinct propositions. [R4NM-S16]

CLP290 has a defined commercial research identity, including Tocris 6758 and CAS 1181083-81-7. That is better material definition than an undisclosed code. Existing use patents and an active KCC2 clinical field prevent an “unowned overlooked target” premise; claim-by-claim freedom to operate has not been assessed. Ovid's phase-one OV350 announcement is a benchmark for the field, not evidence about CLP290. [R4NM-S17–19]

The most useful next proof is a blinded, vehicle-controlled comparison with an established antispastic comparator, measuring preservation of force and useful movement at the same exposure that changes abnormal reflexes. Include orthogonal KCC2 perturbation and a direct GABA-A assessment to distinguish mechanisms. The transfer value would be evidence that symptom control preserves motor capacity better than a relevant comparator. It would not be a regeneration claim. Keep below the leading shortlist until that distinction and exposure feasibility are resolved.

## 4. P110: useful human-cell evidence, but specify the whole peptide

The 2022 Brain study demonstrated mitochondrial and axonal-degeneration readout improvements in SPG11/SPG48 neurons, with genetic disease correction and DRP1 knockdown providing mechanistic support. It did not establish restored human circuit function. [R4NM-S20]

The 2025 SPG15 study adds patient-derived neurons from two affected siblings and an unaffected sibling, not two independent disease families. It reports improved mitochondrial and degeneration/apoptosis readouts, and explicitly identifies Tocris 6897, a cell-penetrating TAT-fused P110 material. It shares investigators with the earlier HSP work. [R4NM-S21]

There are at least two materially different products sold under related P110 names: the approximately 2412-Da cell-penetrating construct and the approximately 757-Da bare heptapeptide. The latter is not an interchangeable supply substitute. The exact complete product, termini and purity must be attached to any experiment; a catalog listing does not establish batch equivalence or stock. [R4NM-S22–23]

Retain as a reserve for a mature human axonal-transmission or neuronal-network proof with delayed treatment and measured intracellular exposure. Require sustained useful activity and mitochondrial quality control, rather than longer mitochondria or fewer swelling markers alone. No claim of human CNS delivery or a chronic therapeutic window is supported by the cell results.

## 5. Why three other new screens do not advance

**CDRI-1709S:** This named AdipoR1 agonist has oral rat functional results, but the dexamethasone experiment co-administered injury and treatment. The denervation experiment began treatment 24 hours after surgery, with five or six animals per later arm. A toe-spread score in this design does not demonstrate restored nerve conduction after an excised, ligated nerve segment. Exact synthetic information is referenced in a large supplement; its full salt/stereochemical mapping was not independently reconstructed here. [R4NM-S24] A delayed established-atrophy study with direct force, exposure and an active comparator would be a different and more discriminating proposition. Hold.

**BCH-HSP-C01:** This is a publicly defined and cataloged molecule, not an unavailable code. The primary work includes neurons from one AP4M1 patient and one AP4B1 patient, with heterozygous parental controls. Redistribution of ATG9A/DAGLB at micromolar concentrations is valuable mechanistic evidence, but no mature circuit rescue or measured in-vivo CNS exposure was established. [R4NM-S25–26] Preserve as a trafficking tool and candidate for functional qualification, below P110 on current disease-relevant cell evidence.

**Copanlisib:** The 2026 GNE organoid paper's rescue is autophagy-marker recovery, including an I329T isogenic model; it is not recovered contractility. Its assertion of immediate availability as an FDA-approved drug is outdated: FDA lists the Aliqopa withdrawal on 18 March 2024. The withdrawn approval concerned follicular lymphoma and does not prove inefficacy in GNE disease. It nevertheless invalidates the proposed regulatory shortcut. [R4NM-S27–28] Deprioritize systemic repurposing; a future selective pathway strategy needs its own functional and exposure rationale.

**Tamoxifen** supplies a particularly instructive negative. Its mouse literature includes a late-start survival benefit, so it was not merely a prevention-only hypothesis. The disease-specific human safety halt outweighs convenient generic sourcing and positive animal evidence for the present development decision. [R4NM-S01–03, R4NM-S29] Do not substitute it for BLU as an immediately easier XLMTM asset.

## Process changes supported by this round

1. Record the semantic type of availability evidence: inventory, research service, publication material, donor recruitment, or planned platform development.
2. Separate the complete test article from an active fragment, prodrug, mixture, stereoisomer or same-target successor.
3. Require the actual movement or transport task to be stated: assisted stepping, reflex normalization, mitochondrial motion and muscle force are different endpoints.
4. Check sponsor/regulator decisions directly when a paper uses “approved,” “available,” or “clinical” as an argument for readiness.
5. Carry corrections and contradictory mechanism papers into the decision record. A newer paper or database label does not erase them.

These changes improve the next experiments without waiting for correspondence to establish scientific merit. None substitutes for material authentication, assay qualification or the eventual evidence needed to support a development investment.
