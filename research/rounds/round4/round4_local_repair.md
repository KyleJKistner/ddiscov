# Round 4: local repair screen and NDC-9009 challenge

**Evidence cutoff: 4 November 2026.** Eight new exact-article/use records across six mechanism families, plus two existing-lead updates. The companion JSON preserves the alias checks, decisions, source URLs and retrieval limits. A screen is not a validated product or a count of independent studies. No outreach, purchases or experiments were performed.

## Decision

**Add RM4404 as a serious independent-validation reserve and topical APR-246 as a translational reserve. Keep NDC-9009 conditional on mature epidermal repair at measured exposure. No candidate in this domain reaches S tier.**

RM4404 is useful because the published experiment already addresses a question missing from many earlier leads: delayed recovery of a direct function, with observations after treatment ends. Its analysis also exposes a substantial weakness: two eyes from the same mouse were treated as independent observations in one model. APR-246 has actual improvement of established human lesions, but only two patients and no convincing withdrawal or barrier-quality assessment. These limitations define experiments that could change the decision; they do not create a reason to wait for an investigator's opinion.

## 1. RM4404: a useful delayed-function result with a real unit-of-analysis problem

The June 2026 study defines RM4404 as the existing DKK1-pathway inhibitor WAY-262611 in Kolliphor HS-15 micelles. It reports delayed dosing, sensory follow-up to day 30, and a separate days 5–10 protocol with day-14 outcomes. Supplemental Figure S2 gives direct vehicle comparisons: corneal sensitivity medians of 4.5 versus 2.79 in the surgical model and approximately 4.6 versus 2.04 in the chemical model. Reported totals are 22 and 12 observations across three arms, respectively. [S01]

Randomization is stated in section 2.9. The surgical model used both eyes and treated each eye as independent; the chemical model injured one eye per mouse. Accordingly, the former total is **not 22 independent animals**. The main day-30 figure's comparisons with uninjured controls do not establish equivalence or provide a direct drug-versus-vehicle durability test. Section 2.9 reports blinded secondary review of image tracings and quantifications. Primary-quantifier and sensory-assessor blinding remain unresolved; the supplemental questionnaire was inaccessible. At day 30, axonal-network medians were 357.7 with vehicle and 362.1 with RM4404 (healthy 394.6); different significance results against healthy controls do not establish structural superiority. The day-30 observations belong to the surgical model, 16 days after the longer regimen ended. The shorter regimen was assessed four days off treatment, not after verified pharmacokinetic washout. The authors disclose a UConn provisional patent. [S01]

### What would make the next study valuable

The first replication should use the animal as the biological block and an evaluator who does not know treatment. Corneal sensation, structural reinnervation, epithelial barrier and optical clarity should be interpreted together. A blink threshold can change because remaining nerves become more excitable; it does not by itself demonstrate useful reinnervation. Persistent benefit after withdrawal, with normal protective sensation and no hypersensitivity, is the desired result.

An active comparator should be selected for the intended clinical use. Cenegermin is relevant to neurotrophic keratopathy; a post-surgical hypoesthesia product may require a different practical comparator and population. This is a product-definition decision, not permission to combine all corneal diseases. The same delayed model can compare RM4404 with the portfolio's mIGF/MG53 approaches only after each formulation has a qualified exposure and control.

The disclosed chemistry is a meaningful advantage: original Sigma identity and a separate Cayman listing give a public procurement route. Neither establishes present stock or a qualified ocular lot. [S01–02] The published stock arithmetic implies different DMSO fractions for the low and high formulations; the vehicle is described as similarly prepared, without an explicit solvent accounting. This is a qualification ambiguity, not proof that the original result was confounded. A new experiment should specify every excipient in the controls. Micelle appearance after storage cannot substitute for measured drug content, release, chemical stability or sterility.

**Advance** after independent delayed functional recovery at measured corneal exposure, with an active comparator and healthy-tissue window. **Stop or reformulate** if the result depends on treating paired eyes as independent, an uncontrolled vehicle component, acute reflex excitation, or a formulation that compromises normal tissue. This is a more concrete opportunity than a generic Wnt-regeneration hypothesis, but it is not yet an independently validated asset.

## 2. Topical APR-246: human lesion improvement deserves a barrier-quality follow-up

The 2020 AEC report treated two children with persistent erosions and TP63 SAM-domain variants I576T and I537T. One patient's opposite palm received vehicle. Re-epithelialization and reduced pain were reported, but the treated palm remained incompletely cornified at week 33. The formulation was specified as 0.6 mg/g APR-246 in Seraqua cream, with three-week refrigerated stability and compound quality checks. Patient keratinocyte differentiation improved without reducing p63 aggregates. [S03]

These observations support studying an established lesion, but neither a large randomized effect nor permanent repair. The inference that APR-246 directly restores mutant p63 must be separated from the observed phenotype. A tissue result can be valuable even when the original mechanism is incomplete; conversely, restored differentiation markers do not guarantee a mechanically competent barrier.

The next independent human-tissue study should test delayed repair after a stable deficit, quantify permeability and mechanical integrity, and assess cornification through withdrawal and repeated stress. Normal epidermis must remain capable of repair and differentiation. Because the proposed article is a bioactive anticancer compound applied chronically to damaged skin, local exposure, DNA-damage markers and viable stem-cell reserve matter more than invoking systemic oncology tolerability. That is a candidate-specific risk, not a general objection to repurposing.

Material definition is better than a name alone: the original study describes chemical quality and the actual formulation. The remaining access task is to authenticate an independent lot and qualify a usable disease model. No ready stock of those exact patient cultures was verified; rebuilding an engineered TP63 epidermis is possible research work, not an already-qualified patient model.

The January 2026 US grant is also more specific than its title suggests. Claim 1 of US12521392B2 includes AEC affecting at least one cornea, while dependent claims include cream and skin erosion. [S04] This bounded reading establishes that claim scope requires care; it establishes neither universal freedom to operate nor a blanket bar on every skin program. A targeted public claim/family map should accompany a positive experimental result.

**Advance** only if the product repairs useful tissue function at a credible local window. **Stop or narrow** if it produces coverage without durable barrier, relies on toxic cell selection, or has no advantage over suitable wound care. This reserve merits stronger consideration than another protein-aggregation assay.

### APR-246 versus NDC-9009: the ordering is provisional

APR-246 has stronger evidence on species and treatment timing: actual chronic human lesions improved after treatment began, including a within-patient comparison. NDC-9009 has not demonstrated that milestone. APR-246 also has a more concrete published formulation history. Those advantages should raise its priority; they cannot be discounted because NDC was shortlisted earlier.

The reserve designation reflects a different product-development question. The APR-246 report leaves incomplete cornification, durable barrier quality, withdrawal dependence and the chronic local benefit-to-harm window unresolved. Its mechanism in AEC is not established by the presumed p63-reactivation label. NDC offers a narrower residual-SERCA/redox hypothesis that can be challenged in a bounded exposure–function study, but it too lacks a topical window and mature-lesion rescue. The public record does **not** establish that NDC is the superior overall asset.

Accordingly, run small qualification work on both if model capability permits. APR-246 should move ahead of NDC if its independent tissue study establishes durable barrier and cornification with a usable local margin, or if NDC remains prevention-only. Its patent family requires precise mapping in parallel; it is not the reason to demote the human observations or wait for outreach. A reserve is a conditional project with an explicit promotion rule, not a negative efficacy judgment.

## 3. NDC-9009: deeper model vetting tightens the selection rule

The new extraction of the Darier paper adds three material qualifications. The two culture variants are in the SERCA2 actuator domain, and average patient-cell SERCA2 abundance was about 70% of control. Baseline calcium characterization in the retrieved section used DD2. The additional DD5–7 samples were biopsy immunohistochemistry, not additional NDC-treated culture donors. The relevant culture evidence remains three patients and two variants. [S16]

ROCK inhibitor Y27632 was required to maintain DD3–4 and was used for consistency in the other DD cultures and two controls. It was removed 24–36 hours before experiments. Thus it is **not a demonstrated co-treatment confound**. The paper's section titled “Solubility assay” concerns detergent extraction of cellular proteins; it does not measure dissolved NDC-9009. [S16]

These facts change how the first independent model should be selected. ATP2A2 heterozygosity and total protein alone are inadequate entry criteria. A suitable model must have demonstrable residual pump function, an established functional adhesion/barrier deficit, and a positive-control route back toward function. Residual calcium sequestration, tissue maturity and stress state should be measured explicitly. A full-null model is useful as a mechanistic boundary, but an activator of residual protein should not be selected or rejected solely on that model.

The same distinction applies to safety evidence. The 2023 healthy-neuron observation was a change in spine-neck morphology at 0.1 µM, with the cited morphology sampling from one culture batch; it is not observed skin toxicity. The 2025 intraperitoneal CNS study does not establish epidermal exposure or a healthy-skin margin. [S17–18] The previous formulation concern remains: nominal assay concentration is not delivered free concentration. It should be resolved by measurement, not by either assuming failure from PBS solubility or assuming that a culture response solves topical delivery.

The selected proof should combine delayed mechanical cohesion and barrier recovery with pharmacologically relevant exposure. A pump-linked readout such as ER refilling helps interpretation, but cannot replace function. NAC, the distinct SERCA activator CDN1163, and an appropriate clinical-route comparator should be evaluated on achievable exposure and tissue tolerability, rather than nominal micromolar-versus-millimolar potency alone.

**NDC-9009 stays on conditional proof priority.** This round does not establish mature-lesion reversal, genotype-independent benefit or a topical therapeutic window. It does make the next experiment's failure modes and model requirements more precise.

### Dantrolene update

The October 2026 primary abstract reports mitochondrial calcium/respiration and adhesion improvement, with part of the response attributed to MCU. Its listing of ATP2A2-knockout models does not specify that the exact adhesion result was rescued in knockout cells. Full denominators and dose were not recovered. [S19] The result strengthens its rationale as an alternative mechanism comparator without proving it superior to NDC-9009.

The official registry confirms that the oral Darier trial ended in September 2025. A registry mirror lists the September 2026 results document SUM-151495, but the actual result tables remain unread. [S20–21] Planned enrollment of 20 is not verified enrollment, and completion is neither efficacy nor failure. This unresolved public document should remain an explicit gap; it does not require an email before the NDC comparison can be scientifically specified.

## 4. The remaining screens prevent evidence transfer and weak promotions

### BEST1: physiological current rescue is real, but exposure and article identity remain decisive

The 2020 2-NOAA/4PBA work used patient RPE with R255Q recessive and K30R dominant backgrounds. Both compounds improved the R255Q chloride-current phenotype; the K30R increase was statistically significant for 4PBA, not 2-NOAA. Key assays used nominal 2.5 mM exposure. The donor backgrounds and patched-cell counts must stay separate. [S06] Public analogue chemistry exists. [S22]

Tadalafil is a separate candidate. At 25 µM it restored M325T currents in transfected HEK293T cells but did not rescue R141H or L234V. COPII/Sec24a binding is a docking-based hypothesis. [S07] Neither the patient RPE data from 2-NOAA nor the clinical familiarity of tadalafil closes its disease-model and unbound retinal-exposure gaps. A 100-fold difference in nominal assay concentration is not a demonstrated clinical potency advantage.

For either article, the meaningful next endpoint is polarized RPE function and photoreceptor support, with normal secretion/cargo handling retained. No visual recovery or clinical efficacy was established in this screen. Both remain below the main shortlist.

### RGN-259: an informative human benchmark whose primary result must stay visible

SEER-1 randomized 18 patients. Healing after four weeks occurred in 6/10 active versus 1/8 vehicle recipients; the prespecified Fisher comparison had P=0.0656. The later day-43 secondary endpoint was significant, but does not turn the primary analysis into a positive trial. [S08–09]

The sponsor's August 26, 2026 notice says SEER-2 procedures had finished, analysis was continuing, and topline was anticipated in mid-November. [S10–11] This is a concrete near-term evidence event, not an outcome already observed. A new portfolio program must define an advantage over an existing sponsored clinical product; a research thymosin-beta4 vial cannot inherit the clinical formulation's evidence.

### Lufepirsen: existing program, incomplete current primary-result retrieval

Glaukos's filing and the investigator-hosted IRB-approved brochure establish a clinical NEXAGON program. [S12–13] The current registry mirror reports an ongoing study, but the primary registry's dynamic payload was not recovered in this pass. [S14] No randomized numerical efficacy result was retrieved. The brochure's 2026 upload path includes a page carrying a 2024 version date, so it is not by itself proof of current recruitment.

Its connexin43 antisense article must remain separate from αCT1 and tonabersat. Planned enrollment figures from different registry versions should not be combined. Retain it as a program/comparator record, with no claim that it is an available neglected asset.

### Phosphosulindac: sensory modulation is not automatically regeneration

The 2026 rabbit study reports a rapid, reversible improvement of dry-eye corneal sensitivity, with roughly 26–27 hours of effect from the most durable hydrogel. Normal-eye sensitivity was suppressed. The assays report four eyes per time point; independent animal counts for each comparison were not fully resolved. [S15]

This supports sensory pharmacology, with a healthy-tissue effect that needs explanation. A rapid blink response cannot establish new axons or pain relief. Short acute comparison with chronic anti-inflammatory drugs also cannot establish clinical superiority. It remains a useful comparator or a distinct symptom-management hypothesis, rather than a corneal-regeneration promotion.

### diMe-Dox: reduced cardiotoxicity does not supply a skin-repair window

The 2025 anthracycline paper reports p63 disaggregation and transcriptional rescue in engineered cells and mouse keratinocytes. diMe-Dox retained those effects, but its cell-viability effects were comparable to the other anthracyclines tested. No human lesion or tissue-barrier rescue was established. [S05]

That makes it a mechanistic alternative to APR-246, whose reported clinical phenotype did not require p63 disaggregation. The analog can advance only if useful epithelial repair separates from cytotoxicity and DNA/chromatin effects. It should not outrank the human-lesion precedent on a cleaner aggregation image alone.
