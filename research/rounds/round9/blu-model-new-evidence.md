## Decision

**Retain BLU3797, with 55/100 planning-effort points, as the leading bounded qualification priority and retain the affirmative Round 8 recommendation for a bounded first functional proof.** The new evidence does not warrant a rank change or a blanket development hold. The specific human model, exposure conditions and ability to interpret an inactive result remain unqualified.

## 1. The new primary observation

**Observation — Myology 2024, poster P345, Lionello and colleagues:** transgene-free differentiation of XLMTM iPSCs showed no reported difference in skeletal myogenic potential versus controls. In additional immortalized-myoblast 3D models, muscle diameter was reduced; electrically stimulated calcium release was not significantly different despite abnormal ryanodine-receptor protein levels. The abstract omits exact genotypes, donor/batch/tissue counts, maturity day, numerical force effects, uncertainty and delayed-rescue results. It contains no BLU3797 test. [Official abstract book, printed page 455 / PDF page 454, P345](https://myology2024.org/images/client/1627/files/pdfviewer/pdfviewer_36692/abstract_book_final___myology_2024.pdf) [S01]

**Inference:** a negative calcium phenotype in this report is not evidence of negative pharmacology. Statistical nonsignificance without an effect estimate, interval and denominator cannot demonstrate physiological equivalence. Nor can reduced diameter alone establish a qualified force deficit or sufficient force dynamic range for a rescue test.

**Inference:** this earlier report and the 2026 Lionello/Tedesco functional abstract cannot be treated as a documented longitudinal progression of the same cells. Shared investigators do not establish shared donor, variant, clone, protocol, culture age, stimulus or comparator. The 2026 report remains an independent human-function precedent at abstract level, with the limitations already recorded in Round 8. [S02]

**Proposed documentary gate:** preserve the identity and timing of each observation before using it to nominate an endpoint or interpret inactivity. This is a refinement of existing Round 8 provenance and assay-qualification requirements, not a new broad gate in front of the first positive-signal question.

The new 2024 report supplies no numerical maturity criterion that resolves this. The exact culture window remains an empirical qualification question already specified in Round 8.

## 4. Native disease timing does not assign a culture day

**Carried-forward primary evidence:** muscle-restricted and adult-onset mouse MTM1-deletion studies support a continuing muscle-maintenance role after early development. They remain useful biological support for asking whether an established deficit can be improved. They do not establish the p.L463S line's residual activity, the origin of a manufacturer “day 27,” or the reversibility of the chosen human tissue window. [S09–S10]

**Inference:** native disease timing motivates the question; exact-line longitudinal force and a demonstrably modifiable post-deficit state determine whether a particular negative answer is interpretable. A corrected line made before myogenesis can establish genetic contribution to a phenotype without proving reversal once that phenotype exists. Conversely, an interpretable drug-induced force gain need not wait for every causal-control engineering program to finish.

No new native-disease timing result in this review changes the Round 8 window logic. Mouse maintenance biology, congenital human disease and weeks in an engineered culture are different time scales; no numerical conversion among them is supported here.

## 5. Preserve the denominator and the asymmetric interpretation rule

**Proposed design, unchanged:** Round 8's 24-tissue qualification grid means three independently initiated batches × two backgrounds × four tissues. Its conditional 96-tissue exploratory grid means three new batches × two backgrounds × four arms × four tissues, yielding 12 randomized tissues per background/arm across three batches. Neither count supplies a power estimate, and both still represent one patient donor and one nonisogenic reference donor. Repeated contractions and serial readings remain nested observations. These are prospective layouts; no such work was conducted. [Round 8 baseline]

All cast, eligible, randomized and endpoint-evaluable tissues require separate counts. Treatment-associated death, silence or detachment can be outcomes and cannot disappear through survivor-only analysis. A preclinical threshold expressed as a fraction of a nonisogenic reference gap remains an analyst-selected program target, not a clinical meaningful-change threshold or a genetic rescue fraction.

| Result class | Appropriate interpretation, unchanged from Round 8 |
|---|---|
| Credible positive | A masked, randomized within-patient-line force gain with interpretable material/exposure and acceptable function supports independent confirmation. Incomplete delayed-control development need not erase that signal. |
| Imprecise or inadequately exposed negative | Inconclusive about useful pharmacology; redirect the deficient measurement or exposure question. |
| Model without reproducible deficit or dynamic range | Model-readiness failure for that route, not target failure. |
| Precise negative with adequate exposure/perturbation and demonstrated post-deficit modifiability | Can support model/article/window-specific futility under the existing threshold. Broad target rejection still requires orthogonal selective support and an independently qualified disease background. |
| Discordance between chemical articles and selective biology | Preserve separate article and target conclusions; do not make BLU-specific failure equivalent to failure of PIK3C2B. |

The only additional emphasis supplied by the new abstract is **phenotype provenance**: record which endpoint exists in which exact line, protocol and maturity window before borrowing sensitivity claims. It does not change the existing numerical advance/stop rules.

## 6. Explicit public-source ceiling and bounded next action

The bounded review recovered one new primary abstract. It did **not** establish exact p.L463S residual function, donor clinical history, control identity in the force plots, force denominator/variance, adult maturity, delayed force rescue, a qualified alternative available for use, or a BLU3797 human-force result. The earlier 2024 and later 2026 reports cannot be resolved into matched stages of one model from available metadata.

Some routes were blocked rather than exhausted: the current Brain full-text open failed after publisher redirection; the UCL resource/PDF and hPSC registry routes did not yield a fresh complete read; the exact ClinVar path returned a verification barrier. The prior Round 8 extraction is retained as prior evidence where labeled. None of these failures licenses a claim that the underlying information does not exist. Repeating the same access attempts is not part of this round.

**Next action within the current authorization:** integrate this finite evidence delta and its source/claim boundaries into the Round 9 decision record, retain BLU3797's leading bounded qualification priority, and close this human-model search lane for the present round. Further model-specific desk review should be triggered by a newly public full report, methods supplement or exact-line dataset that can resolve one of the named gates. The present record is sufficiently concrete to preserve the proposed first proof without pretending its biological inputs are already qualified. No outreach, procurement or experimentation is implied.
