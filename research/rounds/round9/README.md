## New-use decisions

**Miglustat / SCARB2-AMRF** is a conditional clinical-research reserve. Reported useful function must be weighed against directly reported neurological worsening, gastrointestinal/weight-loss burden and renal exposure constraints. The reviewed individuals are not a complete treated-population denominator.

**GSK2193874 / TRPV4-related neuromuscular disease** is a strong preclinical benchmark with an independent-development hold pending a specific contribution. The rescued mouse genotype/stage, the separate human GSK2798745 article, the ABS-0872 cellular work and the clinical ABS-0871/STAR-0871 program remain distinct.

The accompanying [alias audit](candidate-alias-audit.json) found neither exact use in the recovered repository. Miglustat was already present for other diseases; two new exact uses do not mean two new molecules. Structural comparators, failed search branches and renamed programs are not added to the candidate count.
