# Lisdexamfetamine / KCNMA1: stronger biological support, unresolved human denominator

**Round 9 · 4 November 2026 · Existing record R8-NL-02 · Base: a7f10cd8b84bc2ffe6a4cf0160f29e9ab443dad3.**

## Decision

Retain this as the highest clinical-research reserve below the three leaders. A controlled, genotype-matched mouse result strengthens the causal rationale for symptomatic benefit. It does not identify the human response probability or establish a differentiated drug asset. Keep the six planning points; direct them toward the human denominator, independently adjudicated function, and coverage/tolerability.

This is an existing-candidate update. No additional patient, experiment, or newly discovered medicine is claimed. The mouse article was not cited in the recovered Round 8 tree; the accompanying literal alias audit records that bounded novelty check.

## Additional primary evidence

Park et al. tested **dextroamphetamine sulfate**, not oral lisdexamfetamine, in heterozygous **Kcnma1 N999S/WT** mice. Figure 5C used seven mutant saline controls and six mutant drug-treated mice; wild-type groups each numbered seven. Drug reduced stress-associated immobility (adjusted p=.0053). Manual and automated scoring concern the same experiment. Separate locomotor/grooming controls weaken generalized activation as the sole explanation. Acute heterologous assays showed no direct BK inhibition by either stimulant under the tested conditions. [L2]

The human series remains six **previously known responders**, selected retrospectively, with parent-estimated attacks. It supplies no all-treated denominator. The newer Asp789Glu report is one different-genotype patient; the earlier Asn536His report used dextroamphetamine. Neither can be added to the six N999S participants to calculate a genotype-specific response rate. N999S and Asn1053Ser remain transcript aliases. [L1, L3, L4]

## What changes in the inference

If case inclusion required an observed response, the response proportion in the published sample is fixed by design. Without selection probabilities or the complete treated population, it cannot identify the probability of benefit for a newly treated person. A Bayesian calculation using six ordinary Bernoulli successes would answer the wrong sampling model.

The mouse result updates a different uncertainty: whether a pharmacological effect on a genotype-related phenotype exists beyond selected caregiver reports. It makes a purely reporting-based explanation less satisfactory. It supplies no missing human nonresponders; overlapping investigators do not constitute independent laboratory replication. Its triggered immobility endpoint cannot establish developmental benefit, chronic clinical safety, or a direct channel mechanism. [L2]

## Finite next evidence package

Prepare a genotype-resolved ledger whose inclusion rule is eligibility rather than outcome. Keep exact formulation, cointerventions, treatment coverage, nonresponse and discontinuation visible. Count events per observed time and time lost from meaningful activity separately. Attention, school performance and mood cannot substitute for dyskinesia adjudication. Complete event capture with masked review is more informative than selected before/after videos.

Any later clinical design needs an appropriate counterfactual and preservation of established effective care. This document does not recommend withdrawal, recruitment or administration. The approved product label supplies safety and age constraints, not a validated PNKD regimen. [L5]

**Advance the clinical-research thesis** if useful event/function benefit generalizes beyond preselected responders with acceptable coverage and burden. **Narrow or stop the broader claim** if the apparent effect is confined to attention/arousal, fails independent event adjudication, or requires unacceptable harms. **Advance a separate product thesis** only after a concrete contribution such as improved coverage or tolerability and a credible development/rights route is established.

## Structural comparator

A September 2026 primary cryo-EM study locates paxilline/penitrem A inhibition in the human Slo1 pore and describes state-dependent binding. It is a structural resource, not an N999S-selective drug, therapeutic window, or explanation of stimulant benefit. Count it as a comparator resource, not another screened therapeutic. [L6]
