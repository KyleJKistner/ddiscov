## R9-NL-01 — Miglustat / SCARB2-AMRF

### Exact evidence and denominators

| Primary report | Genotype and units | Functional observation | Contrary evidence / boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Colucci et al., online November 2024, February 2025 issue [M1] | One woman; homozygous SCARB2 c.1087C>A, p.His363Asn | Regained independent walking and feeding; serial myoclonus assessments and video follow-up through 24 months | Concurrent symptomatic medication, no control, substantial first-year weight loss; stable renal markers are not proof of renal repair |
| Quraishi et al., 2021 [M2] | One woman; compound heterozygous c.431_432insAG (reported alias c.435_436insAG) and c.862C>T, p.Gln288Ter | Dysphagia resolution, improved coordination and reacquired tasks over three years | Seizures were already controlled; renal function later worsened with additional urological disease; no randomized counterfactual |
| Atasu et al., 2022 [M3] | Two related people with homozygous c.134delA, p.N45MfsX88 | Both discontinued after worsening action myoclonus/ataxia | Shared family/genotype, different treatment durations; these are observed failures of tolerability/function, not speculative risks |

These are **four individually described treated people in three retrieved reports**, not an exhaustive case census or a response-rate denominator. Earlier cases are referenced by these papers but were not independently reconstructed here. Two siblings do not provide two independent genetic backgrounds. A favorable title describing disease modification cannot establish that claim in an uncontrolled case.

The 2025 report also measured falling blood Lyso-Gb1 and increased GCase activity. Those systemic measurements do not identify brain target engagement or explain the functional response. Its approximately 10 kg first-year weight loss materially limits a simple benefit narrative. [M1]

### Article, exposure and practical route

Miglustat is an oral glucosylceramide-synthesis inhibitor. The clinical articles do not authenticate a common historical manufacturer, lot or formulation; a future specified capsule cannot retrospectively resolve that gap. The three reports concern different genotypes and backgrounds. They support a substrate-reduction hypothesis rather than repair of SCARB2 trafficking.

The current U.S. capsule label includes renal dose adjustment, does not recommend use in severe renal impairment, and identifies tremor, peripheral neuropathy, diarrhea and weight loss. These constraints are unusually relevant to a disease involving renal impairment and myoclonus. The Gaucher label does not define an AMRF CNS regimen; copying a published high-dose case without an individual exposure bridge is not a development plan. [M4]

The immediate research opportunity is to reconcile functional benefit, genotype, renal function, exposure and tolerability in a complete case ledger. A patient-derived biochemical model could test substrate handling but cannot replace a meaningful motor/swallowing outcome. No assay, cell line or compound was procured or qualified in this sprint.

### Next decision and stop conditions

**Advance a finite clinical-evidence project** only if a defined functional benefit can be distinguished from cointerventions and natural history, with all starts, failures and withdrawals retained. Prespecify myoclonus-related function and swallowing/feeding rather than selecting whichever biomarker improves. Renal and gastrointestinal tolerability must be examined alongside neurological benefit.

**Hold the independent drug-asset thesis** until a differentiated article, delivery solution or other concrete contribution is identified. Generic access does not create exclusivity. A benefit that requires intolerable exposure, reproducible neurological worsening, or a biomarker-only effect would stop expansion of the exact-article thesis. These rules do not establish a variant-specific responder classifier: the small, selected reports cannot separate genotype from stage, exposure or other differences.

## R9-NL-02 — GSK2193874 / TRPV4-related neuromuscular disease

### Keep the articles separate

| Name | What is established in the sources reviewed | What does not transfer |
|---|---|---|
| **GSK2193874**, abbreviated GSK219 | Symptomatic mouse rescue in the 2024 TRPV4 study [T1] | Human safety, oral clinical exposure or human efficacy |
| **GSK2798745** | Separate clinical TRPV4 inhibitor with early human PK/tolerability studies [T2, T3] | Exact-GSK219 efficacy or a CMT therapeutic window |
| **ABS-0872** | Research inhibitor used in a 2025 epithelial/endothelial barrier study [T5] | Identity with ABS-0871 or clinical proof |
| **ABS-0871 → STAR-0871** | Same clinical program under a sponsor-reported renaming; healthy-volunteer phase completed, patient study planned [T4] | A publicly demonstrated CMT efficacy result or rights available to this project |

### Functional evidence and the critical stage boundary

The 2024 study treated symptomatic **homozygous Trpv4 R269C/R269C** mice and reported dose-dependent survival/motor rescue and restored blood–spinal-cord barrier function. Treatment began with limited motor-neuron soma loss. Figure 6 motor tests used 10–15 mice per genotype/group for horizontal balance and 7–14 for rotarod; histology/barrier panels used three mice per cohort with multiple sections. Exact arm-specific survival counts were not recovered from the accessible caption. Sections are not additional animals. [T1]

This is substantive post-symptom functional evidence, while remaining distinct from regeneration after extensive neuron loss, heterozygous human disease, or restored human bulbar/respiratory function. The genotype and disease-stage differences are central to transferability. The proposed future question is whether the exact intervention adds useful function in a clinically relevant established-disease setting, rather than merely normalizing calcium or preserving a barrier marker.

### Contrary and competitive evidence

The early GSK2798745 study supplied short-term healthy-volunteer/heart-failure exposure and tolerability, not chronic pediatric safety. [T2] In a separate controlled lung challenge study, 47 participants were dosed and 45 completed; prespecified efficacy criteria were not met despite predicted relevant exposure. That negative lung result does not refute genetic TRPV4 neuropathy, but it prevents assuming that inhibition and modeled exposure automatically restore function. [T3]

Setara's **30 October 2026 company announcement**, issued via Business Wire and retrieved through a full syndicated copy, reports that STAR-0871 was formerly ABS-0871, that its healthy-volunteer portion is complete, and that a CMT2C Phase 1b study called THERON is planned for **2027**. It also reports a $90 million company financing. These are sponsor-reported program/financing facts, not independent efficacy results. The funds cover a company pipeline rather than a verified budget for this one program. [T4]

The 2025 ABS-0872 cell study found that later intervention was less effective in restoring an experimentally disrupted barrier than earlier treatment. It is an additional timing warning from a different article and simplified model; it cannot establish a human treatment deadline. [T5]

### Next decision and stop conditions

Retain this as a high-quality preclinical benchmark and a **conditional differentiation reserve**. A distinct product could still compete, but the current evidence does not identify an advantage over STAR-0871. Any proposal needs an exact article, a specific functional advantage, exposure justification and a credible development/rights route. Public papers and catalog access do not supply the last item.

Advance only for a defined contribution such as demonstrably useful later-stage function, better relevant exposure/tolerability, or another credible product improvement. Hold an undifferentiated restart of the published probe. Failure to improve relevant function at qualified exposure would stop that tested article/model claim; mouse rescue cannot be inherited by another TRPV4 inhibitor without its own bridge.

## Portfolio consequence

Neither record merits displacement of BLU3797, enasidenib or topical ruxolitinib. Miglustat/SCARB2 enters a bounded clinical-research reserve below lisdexamfetamine; TRPV4 becomes an explicit benchmark and differentiation hold. Their initial diligence is charged to the existing five-point discovery allocation, not funded by silently expanding the 100-point total.

An additional query for ambroxol/SCARB2 did not establish an exact-use functional clinical record in the sources retrieved; Gaucher/Parkinson chaperone results were not transferred to AMRF. It is not counted as a third screen. The useful retrieved clinical evidence was for miglustat.
