## Decision contribution

**Retain RM4404 as a conditional independent useful-function question.** The functional record does not clearly favor replacing the user-selected sprint with another candidate. Its next value lies in distinguishing an interpretable product benefit from a reflex, model or analysis artifact. The retrieved evidence does not establish a validated budget, a clinical product or an exclusive DKK1/corneal-Schwann-cell mechanism.

The new work goes beyond repeating the paired-eye concern. It recovers a preliminary source with explicit long-regimen treatment contrasts, audits the histology's true biological units and statistical wording, and separates strong and weak comparisons across regimens. ## New source: preliminary contrast reporting changes a narrow absence claim

The official **2025 UConn Clinician Scholar Symposium** booklet contains the complete abstract *Schwann Dance: Nerve Regeneration in Corneal Axonopathy*, by Li, Paramo, Tallo, Bargagna-Mohan and Mohan, on PDF page index 69. Its delayed surgical regimen matches the later program, and it explicitly reports vehicle–RM sensory comparisons at days 14 and 30. The stated p values are 0.0007 and 0.0002. [F2]

This is a useful correction to any blanket statement that no public direct contrast exists. It does not fill in the final paper's missing animal-level contrast. The abstract lists t-test/ANOVA and lacks sample size, effect magnitude, uncertainty, masking and clustering details; the final paper uses nonparametric methods and sex-specific healthy-reference comparisons. Those analyses cannot be silently substituted for one another. Matching investigators, model and regimen support conservative same-program deduplication, but public animal identifiers do not establish literal cohort identity. Count **zero new independently verified cohorts**.

## Biological units and comparison hierarchy

The original primary report is [Li et al., 2026](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/jnr.70142), DOI 10.1002/jnr.70142, also available in [PMC13266094](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13266094/). The following are source-specific observations from its methods/captions, not a reanalysis. [F1]

| Evidence | Correct interpretation |
|---|---|
| Surgical CMI uses both injured eyes; six sensory samplings are averaged per eye | Animal, eye and repeated touch are different units. The reported analysis treats eyes as independent; a valid clustered uncertainty estimate cannot be reconstructed without their linkage. |
| Figure 18 thickness uses **three mice/group**, several sections and three readings/section; totals are 123–129 measurements | Three-digit N is not a mouse denominator. Do not invent a cluster-adjusted p value or use this as functional barrier validation. |
| Delayed chemical-injury Figure 12 has direct RM–vehicle structural comparisons with one injured eye/mouse | Preserve that supportive result separately from the weak day 30 CMI healthy-reference structural inference. One model's limitation does not erase the other comparison. |
| Early chemical-injury Figure 14 retains a direct sensory contrast after seven days without dosing | It is a real source-reported off-dosing signal in the early regimen; it is neither delayed-course durability nor measured clearance. |

Figure 18's caption also mixes an ANOVA label with Kruskal–Wallis/Dunn and reports H(3,…) alongside three listed treatment groups. Its exact statistical specification remains ambiguous. No official correction was recovered and no graph-only group count was inferred. The ledger preserves the printed values and wording instead of repairing them. The small biological sample and unresolved analysis require interpreting the thickness findings as exploratory architectural support, not robust evidence of barrier restoration.

The final paper's Figure 10 day 14 comparison is to injured untreated tissue, while its day 30 comparison is to healthy tissue; neither should be converted into a direct structural RM–vehicle test. Some prose/caption comparator and significance wording conflicts are retained. Likewise, a nonsignificant difference from normal is not equivalence. The stronger unilateral chemical-model contrasts must remain visible when judging the program.

## What the sensory endpoint can decide

The assay is an evoked blink threshold with an upper instrument setting of 6.0. Healthy references often sit at that ceiling. This makes a return toward normal supportive of recovery, but it cannot quantify supra-normal sensitivity beyond the ceiling or independently identify pain, epithelial protection or visual performance. The conclusion that hyperalgesia is absent would require evidence the reported endpoint cannot supply by itself.

The current result is not automatically “sensitization only”: the chemical model includes concordant structural observations. Nor is it demonstrated clinically useful reinnervation. The distinction is consequential because a product that produces unpleasant sensitivity without adequate tissue benefit could have very different value from one that restores protective sensation.

Representative clarity images and histological thickness are relevant support. They do not establish a quantified optical-performance endpoint, functional permeability, a standardized pain outcome or an all-treated chronic ocular safety window. Those limits concern what can be claimed; they are not proof that RM4404 harms vision or causes pain.

## Timing and intended product claim

Figure 15 compares early dosing ending at day 7 with delayed dosing ending at day 14, with both assessed at day 14. Treatment phase and time since last dose therefore change together. Its reported advantage for delayed dosing cannot isolate a unique regenerative phase from recent exposure or different withdrawal time. Multitimepoint sensory testing occurred before that day’s dose, so the exact hours since the delayed group’s preceding dose are not inferred. Shared medians and counts also indicate a comparison of existing program data, not a newly independent replication.

The three regimens stay distinct: long delayed CMI, early chemical treatment, and modified delayed treatment. Calendar intervals after dosing are not pharmacokinetic washout. No unmeasured clearance or permanent correction is credited.

**Useful ongoing control or a useful finite-course benefit can be valuable.** Permanent drug-free regeneration is a stronger claim, not a universal requirement for any product. The later proof should support the intended claim at its actual stage, with meaningful protective function and acceptable tolerability, rather than demand every possible mechanistic or permanence result at the first qualification step. This audit does not authorize that proof, set a clinical regimen or design a new experiment.

## Masking, missingness and access limits

Randomization and blinded secondary review of imaging are reported. Primary sensory-assessor masking remains unverified. Corneal tissue damaged during processing was excluded, but arm-specific exclusions and a complete analysis flow were not recovered. Planned power/sample-size language does not establish actual denominators for every result. Differences in total N between time points do not by themselves establish a dropout count.

The publisher's Data S1/S2/S3 downloads returned 403. Canonical PMC links returned preparing-to-download HTML shells. **Data S3 is the Transparent Science Questionnaire for Authors; its content was not read.** HTML-rendered Figure S2/S3 captions were readable. The Figure 18 image request returned a text placeholder rather than image bytes, so no visual planimetry, graph-only group count or axis-unit verification is claimed. Numeric tissue dimensions are retained as reported values without an invented physical-unit conversion.

The exact-article/update search recovered this 2026 primary program and the earlier institutional abstract, but no new independent RM4404 replication, controlled human result or accessible correction resolving these issues. This is a bounded retrieval result, not proof of absence. No raw animal data were recovered and no p values were recalculated.
