## Why this was the next eligible uncertainty

The independent *ddiscov_marginal_capital_ranking.pdf* evaluates discounted financial value retained by ddiscov after remaining development/commercial costs, rights, royalties, partner sharing and dilution. It places RM ahead of BLU conditionally because a local, potentially finite treatment could produce an earlier decision with less capital at risk. It also identifies that pairwise ordering as uncertain if model, rights or sterile-product work erases the apparent cost advantage. [Independent review, pp. 2–3]

APR's public-source pass is closed. Repeating it without a substantive new artifact has little expected information value. RM offered a different, bounded question: **does the published evidence support a reproducible useful-function product test with an interpretable article and exposure, or does it support only a sensory surrogate or a much larger development project?** The answer can preserve, narrow or eliminate the next commitment before more capital is exposed.

Historical allocation and sunk diligence do not decide that question. This sprint did not revalue all candidates, invent probabilities, or run biological/financial simulations. The review's ordinal order is an analytical input, not a calculated EV-per-dollar output. Waiting and redirecting remain available options.

## What the source audit changes

### Preserve the strongest relevant evidence without borrowing between cohorts

The full report contains multiple injury models, schedules and endpoints. A weak late structural comparison in the surgical model cannot erase a direct treatment-versus-vehicle structural result in the unilateral chemical-injury model. Conversely, an early-treatment off-dose result cannot become evidence of delayed-treatment durability. The [function ledger](rm-function-evidence.json) now separates those comparisons, their biological units and source-specific statistics. [F1]

This matters for the next dollar: a real model-specific signal can justify a bounded independent check even when broader regeneration language is unsupported. It also means a first tranche should answer one consequential, clinically relevant question rather than automatically reproduce every model and schedule.

### Sensation, tissue condition and pain remain distinct

A capped blink-threshold assay cannot independently establish absence of hypersensitivity or useful protective sensation. Tissue thickness and representative clarity images are also not complete barrier, optical-quality or pain outcomes. These are limits on the product claim, not evidence that RM has caused hypersensitivity or failed clinically. [F1; analytical interpretation]

The audit also distinguishes the many section-level thickness readings from the **three mice per group** that supplied them, preserves inconsistent statistical labels instead of repairing them, and identifies unequal time since the last dose in the early-versus-delayed comparison. No cluster-adjusted reanalysis, new effect estimate or causal timing claim was manufactured. [F1]

### The preliminary direct comparison is useful context, not an independent replication

A newly recovered 2025 UConn abstract reports direct RM-versus-vehicle sensory contrasts. Its analysis description differs from the final report and does not provide the observations needed to reconcile them. Treat it as a preliminary, potentially overlapping report: it supplies no additional independent replication and its p-values do not fill missing final-paper contrasts. [F2]

### Article readiness remains the key practical qualification

The known salt, concentration-basis and solvent questions remain attached to the exact micellar product. The published preparation and supplier records provide a starting route; microscopy, storage time or a research catalog do not authenticate a clinically suitable lot. Carrier studies must stay attached to their own formulation and exposure conditions. Apparent disagreement across matrices does not establish an RM toxicity threshold. [Product audit]

The appropriate implication is a proportionate article/exposure/tolerance question. A complete sterile ophthalmic development package is outside the assumed small tranche; it is neither proved unnecessary later nor required in full before every initial informative qualification. A reformulation or major platform requirement changes the cost and therefore the ranking question.

### The comparator is active, but its endpoints and follow-up do not transfer automatically

The newer DEFENDO extension record supplies a longer observation period, but it follows the original cohort, permits additional standard care including further Oxervate, defines responder-specific endpoints and prespecifies observed-case analyses in its SAP. Numeric extension result tables were not recovered. It cannot be treated as a clean drug-free durability estimate or independent randomized replication. The new Orunea Phase 3 program is an epithelial-healing trial, not a completed post-surgical sensory comparison. [Comparator audit]

These findings constrain claims about an unmet sensory need. They do not establish that existing therapy restores no sensation, that RM is superior, or that an epithelial-healing competitor resolves the exact RM product question. Clinical population, benefit and comparator must be specified together. Post-injury hypoesthesia, neurotrophic keratitis and persistent epithelial defects are not interchangeable markets or model labels.

## Current product claim and finite routes

The intended claim is **useful recovery or sustained control of injury-related corneal hypoesthesia, with tissue integrity, clarity and symptom burden appropriate to the selected use**. A benefit can be valuable without perfect histology, exclusive proof of a Schwann-cell mechanism or permanent drug-free cure. A stronger off-treatment repair claim needs relevant follow-up and exposure interpretation. Similarity to healthy controls by nonsignificance is not equivalence.

| Route | Question it can answer | Scope boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Existing article and one appropriate existing injury/function route | Does a reproducible preparation support an interpretable useful-function signal at acceptable local exposure? | No automatic all-model program or complete commercial CMC package. Exact controls, biological units and masking still matter. |
| Product contribution for a defined clinical population | Is there useful benefit or meaningful burden/tolerability advantage relative to appropriate care? | A surgical sensory result does not automatically become an NK or PCED claim. No clinical execution in this sprint. |
| Major formulation/model/rights development | Does the revised opportunity still exceed alternatives after its actual costs and time? | Treat this as a scope change; do not silently extend the small tranche. |

The selected scientific question does not require an owner reply. A later financial commitment cannot assume free rights, ready data, available materials or a viable market. The known university provisional/disclosure remains a possible route, not verified claim scope or licensing availability. No private terms, royalty assumptions, market size or revenue forecast are assigned. [Product/capture audit]

## Marginal-dollar decision branches

The independent review's **US$50–175k and 3–6 months** cover an illustrative product/exposure and independent functional-corroboration tranche. They are analyst assumptions, not quotes or validated schedules. The review specifically excludes a complete sterile ophthalmic development package; its general exclusions also include rights/licensing, GLP toxicology, GMP clinical manufacture, trials and commercialization. Time to useful information is not time to revenue. [Independent review, p. 2]

| What becomes known | Decision |
|---|---|
| A defined article and suitable existing model can answer a consequential question at genuinely small scope | Retain RM's conditional qualification position. This audit does not establish execution readiness. |
| Only a blink-threshold change survives without adequate evidence for the intended benefit, or pain/ocular harm outweighs benefit | Do not promote a repair product on the surrogate; narrow or stop that claim. |
| Salt, solvent, degradation or exposure makes the result uninterpretable | Resolve the specific transfer issue within scope; do not infer target failure from an unqualified article. |
| Sterile-product, model or rights work materially enlarges the project | Recompare actual remaining cost, time, information and capture against BLU, other eligible opportunities and waiting. |
| Scientific benefit remains but product contribution or retained value is inadequate | Defer or redirect asset development; label any separately justified evidence/public-benefit route honestly. |

Current evidence supports the first branch as a **plausible, unvalidated premise**. It also strengthens the need to keep the other branches explicit. It does not demonstrate a new rank reversal, an S-tier asset or a full-program commitment.

## Completion and reopening

This bounded source pass is closed. The transparency questionnaire, underlying animal-level observations, quantitative RM exposure/stability and complete current claim scope were not recovered. Failed access is not evidence that data do not exist. Remaining product performance and private-access questions cannot be closed by repeating generic searches.

Reopen only for a substantive accessible questionnaire/data file or primary correction, independent exact-article functional result, relevant stability/exposure/tolerance document, decisive comparator result, identifiable claim publication, or defined product/transaction facts likely to change the next commitment. The APR, BLU, zinc, SLC6A6, HHD supplement, Round 14 HS and enasidenib closed passes remain closed.

No outreach, purchases, recruitment, experiments, simulations or material receipt occurred or are authorized. No candidate screen, human treatment, independent RM replication or new experimental result was added. No numerical allocation or S-tier promotion was made.
