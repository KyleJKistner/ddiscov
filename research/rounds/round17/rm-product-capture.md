## Decision

**Retain conditional existing-article qualification.** Public documents define a molecule and carrier route, but do not establish a transferable ophthalmic product, measured corneal exposure or retained financial value. No new product, comparator or rights fact recovered here clearly displaces RM from the selected source sprint. A major reformulation, sterile-product or rights project would change the assumed tranche and require a new comparison.

The first practical question is whether the exact article and matched control are sufficiently specified and tolerated to make a useful-function result interpretable. That is narrower than a finished commercial manufacturing package. It also does not assume that a new vehicle must be invented. Research catalogs and related carrier studies do not settle the exact product's performance.

## Article and exposure: what is old, what is new

The Round 4–5 findings remain: Calbiochem catalog 317700 is described as WAY-262611 hydrochloride, whereas Cayman 17704 is free base; the nominal strengths and stock arithmetic leave control-solvent accounting material; particle morphology does not establish active-drug stability or tissue exposure. These are retained questions, not fresh discoveries. Shared identity labels cannot authenticate the historical lot or prove equal loading, release or exposure. [P1–P4; prior rounds]

| Newly extracted detail | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| The method literally says 45-micron filtration and preparation 1–2 days before in-vivo use. [P1] | Preserve the wording. It does not establish sterile processing, a contamination event or a month-long usable product. A short-use research article is the more defensible transfer starting point. |

| The two cited carrier studies describe different particle/payload systems and tolerance observations. [P5–P6] | Assess the exact formulation. Neither carrier identity nor a concentration comparison supplies a universal RM safety limit. |

No source correction to filtration wording, quantitative RM concentration–time profile, released/free fraction, chemical stability series or measured washout was recovered. This is a bounded retrieval statement, not proof that such data do not exist. Nominal concentration and elapsed time after dosing cannot supply those measurements. The 2009 compound's oral pharmacology is not an ocular exposure bridge. [P1–P4]

### Carrier evidence must stay attached to its formulation

Leonardi's cited primary abstract concerns Dynasan solid-lipid nanoparticles and reports fully nonirritant HS15 up to 0.05% in that system. RM describes a 0.5% starting carrier suspension. This is a specific tolerance question, **not a demonstrated tenfold violation of a universal ocular safety threshold**. Complete Leonardi methods/results were unavailable. [P5]

Hou's HS15–myricetin system provides counterevidence against a blanket carrier rejection: its accessible primary passages report acute rabbit tolerance, myricetin-content/stability work and coumarin-6 tracer permeation observations. They do not validate RM, transfer the carrier/tracer permeation findings to WAY-262611, or establish repeated-course tolerance in damaged human corneas. The independent cross-review directly inspected the relevant PMC tolerance and tracer passages; the whole article and supplement were not continuously inspected in this pass. [P6]

An altered blink threshold could reflect useful recovery, irritation or changed excitability. The exact control and tissue condition therefore matter. Absence of complete commercial specifications does not erase a biological signal, and a carrier analogy does not establish a safe finished article. Initial qualification should resolve the uncertainties that could make the chosen functional result uninterpretable; it need not complete every later development requirement.

## Comparator update: endpoint and lineage boundaries

### Existing baseline

The current Oxervate label continues to establish a neurotrophic-keratitis indication and controlled epithelial healing; its mean corneal-sensitivity changes were not clinically significant. The already-known open-label stage 1 DEFENDO report nonetheless supplies a sensory signal, with visit-specific observations rather than a randomized effect. Neither “cenegermin restores no sensation” nor “RM is superior” follows. [C1–C2]

### Newly integrated longer follow-up

The official NCT05552261/NGF0122 record identifies **24 actual extension participants**, the original DEFENDO lineage, and month-24/month-30 follow-up. Standard care, including further Oxervate, was allowed. The sensory-maintenance endpoint is conditional on earlier improvement and explicitly excludes missing categorical responses; its reported missing counts are 4 and 3 at the two visits. The earlier non-improver stratum has a separate missing observation at month 30. These are different endpoint groups. [C3]

The March 2024 SAP prespecifies an analysis set requiring an extension visit, exploratory observed-case analyses and exclusion of missing categorical values from percentage denominators. **This is planned analysis documentation.** The posted numeric result tables, treatment histories and clinical study report were not recovered, so implementation and effect estimates are not asserted. [C4]

The extension is not an independent cohort or clean durability estimate after one untreated course. Its 24 participants must not become the original study's 37, and a responder-conditioned maintenance percentage would not be an all-treated response probability. The registry says results were posted; failure to retrieve the tables does not establish absence of results. [C2–C4]

### Newly identified active competitor program

Dompé's 9 June 2026 Orunea announcement identifies a randomized, double-masked Phase 3 PCED program, NCT07519902, with approximately 150 adults planned and complete epithelial healing as its target. That is the announced plan, not verified current enrollment or an efficacy result. [C5]

This narrows the commercial claim: a broad epithelial-healing product enters an active field. It does not establish a completed sensory comparison, clinical inferiority of RM or a legal/commercial block.

| Intended use | Consequence for interpretation |
|---|---|
| Persistent harmful hypoesthesia after injury or surgery | Define useful protective function and symptom burden. Do not borrow all postoperative sensory reductions into a commercial population. |
| NK with epithelial disease | Cenegermin is an active comparator. A useful contribution may concern function, safety, burden or cost; superiority on every endpoint is not mandatory. |
| PCED or broad epithelial healing | Orunea makes current development competition explicit. Healing and sensory benefit require separate evidence. |
| Painful dry-eye or neuropathic pain | Greater mechanosensitivity is not automatically beneficial. No RM clinical pain efficacy is established. |

These are product-definition judgments, not treatment recommendations or a study protocol. A small qualification question can be selected before the complete indication package is ready, while the eventual patient problem must still constrain claims about value.

A bounded exact-title/inventor/product search did not recover an identifiable new application number, published claims, grant, license offer or transaction terms. This does not prove that rights or an application do not exist. The title and press account do not establish claim coverage, freedom to operate, exclusivity, data access or willingness to license. Research supply does not settle commercial clearance, and publicity is not a second efficacy cohort.

No owner reply is required to choose a scientific proof. The financial continuation also cannot assume free rights, ready materials, low manufacturing cost or capture of all surgical/dry-eye/NK demand. Meaningful patient benefit, a differentiated product and value retained by ddiscov remain separate.

## Finite spending implication and stop rule

The independent review's **US$50–175k / 3–6 months** is illustrative. It excludes a complete sterile ophthalmic development package; general exclusions also include rights/licensing, GLP toxicology, GMP clinical manufacture, trials and commercialization. No quote, stock commitment, validated cost or schedule was obtained. Those exclusions do not waive basic article quality or tolerability for an eventual permitted activity.

If exact-article qualification can answer a consequential function question within genuinely small scope, RM retains its conditional position. If meaningful progress requires substantial new vehicle/model development or costly rights, compare the actual revised tranche with BLU and waiting before expanding it. This pass does not establish a numerical RM-versus-BLU financial ordering.

The source pass is closed. Reopen for actual RM composition/lot corrections, stability/exposure/tolerance data, accessible comparator outcomes with usable denominators and treatment history, substantive relevant claim publications, or defined product/transaction facts that could change the next commitment.
