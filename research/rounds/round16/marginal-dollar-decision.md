## Why this uncertainty earned the next sprint

The independent **ddiscov_marginal_capital_ranking.pdf**, dated 4 November 2026, evaluated financial value retained by ddiscov after remaining development/commercial costs, rights payments, royalties, partner sharing and dilution. Its leading order was **APR-246 → RM4404 → BLU-3797 → NDC-9009 → mIGF-Gel → exact 8015-P2 → AAT/EVLP**. This is an explicitly conditional analytical ordering, not calculated returns per dollar. All ten pages were recovered and read against its pinned Round 15 commit. The report itself made no repository changes.

The user selected APR unless fresh evidence clearly favored an alternative. This sprint therefore tested the premise most likely to change that decision: **does a two-child human lesion signal admit a small, interpretable proof using an existing article and available disease models, or does it conceal a much larger product/model/rights project?** A favorable answer preserves a relatively short route to useful information; an unfavorable answer stops scope expansion early and frees the next dollar for another candidate or waiting.

The comparison uses remaining effort and expected decision change, not sunk effort or the historical 55/15/10 allocation. Useful information includes evidence that justifies stopping. Patient benefit, scientific information and retained financial value remain separate. No numeric success probability, NPV, market size or synthetic EV score is assigned because actual access, costs, reach and terms are unknown. No financial or biological simulation was performed.

## What the sprint actually changed

### 1. The clinical denominator is still two

The original 2020 report remains the human evidence anchor. P1's treated palm was almost covered at week 33; P2's scalp was mostly covered at week 32. Cornification remained incomplete, and treatment was described as continuing. [C1]

Two 2021 primary abstracts were newly reconciled. O020 adds P1's treated right sole, an untreated left sole, a P1 pain score and P2 analgesic-stop timing. A second abstract repeats the original two-child program. They add source detail, **zero verified unique people and zero independent cohorts**. The later P2 variant annotation conflicts with the original and remains unresolved. A P2 baseline score and P1 follow-up cannot form a 6-to-2 response. [C2–C3; clinical ledger]

These observations support a useful ongoing-control question. They do not produce a causal effect size, response probability, measured barrier restoration, dermal exposure target or chronic safety margin. The clinical ledger separates treated sites, comparators, time points and laboratory repetitions from patients.

### 2. Nominal article ambiguity narrowed; delivered exposure did not

The patent's explicit composition example agrees with the paper's precursor and finished-cream concentrations. That is concrete support for starting with an existing article. Current molecule/vehicle documents identify a documentation route, not a clinical lot or committed supply. [A1–A6]

The new sole detail makes per-palm dosing assumptions less defensible. Actual amount per area, finished-product stability, delivered local/systemic exposure and acceptable continued-use performance remain unverified. No retrieved fact demonstrates that a new vehicle must be invented; neither does it validate effortless transfer. [A1–A9]

### 3. Existing AEC models offer specific functional leads

Two papers absent from the Round 15 package make an existing-model route more concrete. Their value is to challenge the assumption that a new platform must be built, while preserving the difference between a disease phenotype, a responsive assay and an informative topical product proof.

It remains a potential benchmark, not a ready, transferred assay, a requirement to reproduce gene editing or a newly ranked therapeutic candidate. Neither publication establishes obtainable materials, cost or delivery time. Sheet cohesion is not the entire epidermal barrier. No model, experiment or simulation was run in this sprint.

### 4. Current program inactivity does not resolve capture

Aprea's newly read Q3 2026 filing reports no ongoing company APR-246 trials alongside patent-maintenance, regulatory and closeout spending. It distinguishes the publicly known molecule from remaining use/formulation and other patent interests. This defeats an assumption that inactivity means abandoned rights or an available license. It does not establish a new commercial block, worldwide trial absence or any terms. [AR1]

The known US AEC claim's corneal patient predicate remains relevant; skin administration alone does not remove it. Official current European status was not recovered, so published application text is not promoted to a verified current grant. Exact rights, data access and reachable AEC economics remain open. Broader p63 or chronic-wound markets are not credited. [AR2–AR4]

## Current claim and historical-gate correction

The intended product claim is **clinically useful sustained control of persistent AEC erosions and treatment burden during the specified regimen, at acceptable local/systemic exposure**. It requires worthwhile benefit against appropriate care, with tissue integrity and safety adequate for that use. Coverage, pain and dressing burden should be interpreted together; a marker-only improvement is insufficient to establish the claim.

The Round 5 APR-specific requirement for benefit to persist after withdrawal is superseded for this ongoing-control claim. Perfect cornification and a particular mechanism or aggregate clearance are also not universal entry requirements. Functional measurements can reject cosmetic coverage and substantiate tissue quality without making complete histological normalization the sole definition of value.

**Full barrier restoration** and **durable off-treatment regeneration** remain separate, stronger claims. The former needs functional evidence and the latter needs evidence after treatment stops. Neither is established. Historical Round 4–5 files remain byte-identical; the current register, shortlist and policy explicitly record the override.

A new preclinical efficacy program is not a mandatory reset after human observations. Additional article, exposure or tissue work should answer a consequential uncertainty. An appropriate controlled clinical-development route may ultimately have greater marginal value, but its requirements and cost must be evaluated separately and it is outside this sprint and its illustrative qualification envelope.

## Bound the next commitment before extending it

The independent review's **US$25–100k and 2–5 months** are analyst assumptions for article/formulation and bounded epidermal-function qualification. They are not supplier quotes, validated schedules or this sprint's spending. The review expressly excludes patient trials, major new model construction, rights/licensing, GLP toxicology, GMP clinical manufacture and commercialization. Time to information is not time to revenue. Overlapping ranges do not prove the ranking.

| Condition learned later | Marginal-dollar decision |
|---|---|
| Existing article and an appropriate accessible model can answer a consequential question within genuinely small scope | Retain APR as first conditional qualification priority. This source audit supplies no execution authorization. |
| A properly governed clinical route is more informative than more model work | Evaluate its actual separate cost and requirements; do not insist on a new preclinical reset. |
| Adequate progress needs substantial new chemistry, vehicle invention, model construction or costly/inaccessible rights | End the assumed small tranche. Compare revised cost, information and capture with RM4404, BLU and waiting before extending scope. |
| Credible later evidence shows no worthwhile benefit, unacceptable exposure or inadequate function for the intended claim | Stop or narrow that claim. Do not infer failure of every possible symptom-control claim from one mechanistic or histological endpoint. |
| Patient benefit is useful but retained financial value is inadequate | Defer or redirect financial development; separately justify any evidence/public-benefit route instead of calling it an owned asset. |

The present result lies in the first branch **as a plausible, unvalidated premise**. It does not establish spend readiness. There is no new numerical allocation, candidate screen, treated person, experimental result or S-tier promotion.

## Stop rule and permitted continuation

This public-source pass is closed. Its remaining uncertainties concern performance, access and terms that repeated generic searching cannot establish. Reopen only for a substantive new clinical/dose/transcript correction, exact-article stability or exposure document, controlled result, usable model-fit/access document, accessible substantive official claim record, or defined product/transaction fact likely to alter the next commitment. A source becoming accessible is a real trigger; an empty search result is not evidence of absence.

No outreach, purchases, recruitment, experiments, simulations or material receipt occurred or are authorized by this package. Prior closed BLU, zinc, SLC6A6, HHD supplement, Round 14 HS, enasidenib CNS and Round 15 contribution searches remain closed. No automation was created.
