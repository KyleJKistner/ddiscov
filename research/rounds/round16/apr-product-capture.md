Evidence cut: **4 November 2026**, America/Los_Angeles. Existing candidate `R4-L02`; parent `7707f09703be11af8befdfda85b8a840f82dd8fd`. [Structured evidence](apr-product-capture.json) · [Clinical lineage](apr-clinical-evidence.md) · [Decision](marginal-dollar-decision.md).

## Decision

**The existing-article qualification premise remains credible, with unverified cost and duration.** The source trail identifies the molecule and vehicle and now supplies a consistent composition example. It does not establish a transferable clinical product, a chronic exposure margin or commercial control. No fresh product or rights fact recovered in this bounded pass clearly displaces APR-246 from the independent review's first marginal-dollar position.

The finite question is whether the existing article can be specified well enough for a consequential functional or clinical comparison. New chemistry, a new vehicle and a commercial manufacturing package are not automatic prerequisites. If adequate transfer, model access or rights require a materially larger project, the small-tranche premise must be reconsidered before commitment. No development or spending was executed.

### Applied amount remains unresolved

The reported 5 mg/day wording and 0.6 mg/g concentration already produced the Round 5 ambiguity. The later O020 abstract adds active treatment of **both the right palm and right sole**, with left-palm vehicle and an untreated left sole. It therefore prevents assigning the reported amount to one palm alone. A literal 8.33 g/day implication is preexisting arithmetic, not verified consumption, site allocation, area, adherence or delivered exposure. Patient 2's daily active amount remains unestablished. [A1, A9; prior Round 5]

Nominal cell-medium concentration is not viable-skin or systemic exposure. Current supplier solubility documentation to 100 mM is not an experimentally established saturation ceiling; it does not prove the higher historical precursor concentration impossible. It identifies a transfer condition to resolve. The separate Cayman PBS-specific statement concerns a different medium. [A3–A4, A7]

### Stability and active species need proportionate interpretation

The paper's short refrigerated stability statement is useful but lacks the quantitative public data needed for independent transfer. Generic supplier solution-storage advice and base storage conditions neither validate nor disprove matrix-specific finished-cream stability. A bounded search did not recover a separate quantitative APR-246/Seraqua stability or dermal-permeation dataset; this does not prove no such data exist. [A1, A4, A6–A7]

Primary cancer/cell-free work demonstrates reversible MQ–glutathione conjugation and an intracellular reservoir. It supports distinguishing parent content from active-product retention when interpreting an article. It supplies no AEC skin kinetics, dermal safe threshold, MRP1-combination rationale for this project or obligation to build a complete metabolomics program. The redox/thiol concern itself was already in Round 5. [A8]

The original short mouse tolerance statement has no displayed data. The conference's descriptive human tolerability statement does not supply structured adverse-event ascertainment or measured exposure. Systemic oncology experience cannot by itself settle a chronic dermal benefit–harm window. [A1, A9]

## Fresh company evidence: inactivity does not establish available rights

Aprea's **Q3 2026 Form 10-Q**, filed **4 November 2026**, accession **0001104659-26-122405**, reports no ongoing company APR-246 clinical trials. Its APR-246 expense explanation identifies patent maintenance, regulatory work and program closeout. It disclaims ownership/license of compound-composition patents on the publicly known structure while reporting use/formulation claims and other dosing, manufacture, crystal-form and combination applications. [AR1]

This narrows one uncertainty: inactive trials and maintained rights coexist. It does not establish worldwide trial absence, abandoned rights, a willing licensor, transferable manufacturing or regulatory data, or cheap access. Company maintenance expenditure is not a price benchmark for the proposed topical qualification. No applicable private transaction terms were recovered. [AR1; interpretation]

## Capture conditions and completion

Financial value retained by ddiscov depends on the exact product's useful benefit, reachable AEC demand, rights and development costs, then any royalties, sharing or dilution. The academic AEC use family, Aprea-reported interests, product know-how, data use/reference and future approval incentives are separate interests. None is made available by this audit. No voucher or orphan entitlement, pricing, market size or revenue is assigned. Broader p63 disease and chronic-wound markets cannot be borrowed for a narrow AEC product.

The independent review's **$25–100k / 2–5 months** is an illustrative qualification envelope, not a verified budget or timeline. It excludes clinical trials, major new model construction, licensing, GLP toxicology, GMP manufacture and commercialization. Those exclusions matter: discovering such a requirement changes the proposed tranche and requires a fresh marginal comparison. An owner reply is not required to select the scientific question, while a spending commitment cannot assume free rights.

Useful sustained control during continued treatment is a legitimate target. Normalization of every cornification marker and drug-free cure are stronger claims, not universal entry requirements. Meaningful patient benefit and acceptable tissue integrity/safety still need to be demonstrated; visible coverage alone does not establish mature barrier restoration.

This source pass is complete. Reopen for an actual article/stability/exposure document, a source correction, an accessible substantive official claim record, or defined transaction/product facts likely to change the next decision. No repeated generic search for private terms is warranted. No outreach, purchases, recruitment, experiments, simulations or material receipt occurred.
