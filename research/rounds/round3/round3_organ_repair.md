# ddiscov round 3 — organ conditioning, repair and reperfusion

> **Historical review snapshot.** Later findings and rankings are reconciled in the [current shortlist](../../../decisions/current-shortlist.md) and [round 4 review](../round4/README.md). Statements about activity or access describe this review period.

Assessment: 4 November 2026. Repository baseline: `c241dc4c6cfa7cd5312a7237dda61a4d2af50c97`. This memo extends the portfolio register and round 2 blood/ex vivo review. It does not supersede other domain teams’ rankings.

## Decisions

**FXT-001/UAMC-3203 is the strongest newly identified organ candidate.** It has direct declined-human-lung evidence and a public chemical/material route. It merits a high-priority methods audit and a bounded comparison with the existing AAT lead. It is not yet an S-tier asset: exact human dose, donor-level magnitude, and full control details were not recoverable from the accessible primary text; independent post-transplant benefit is not established; a university spinout is already developing the exact lead.

**Retain AAT as a leading human-organ proof investment, but tighten its product definition.** The useful question is whether a potency-qualified, composition-matched ex vivo regimen creates persistent graft benefit. “Add AAT to perfusate” is already published, patented and adjacent to active clinical development. This round identifies a concrete formulation confound and a way to resolve it without waiting for correspondence.

**Do not promote CYM5442 merely because a new human-lung paper exists.** Its exact experiment is unusually inspectable, and its barrier result is useful, but oxygenation/compliance were not significantly better. Receptor loss creates a durability question. The defined biased agonist SAR247799 is a rational successor hypothesis, with a cheap discriminating proof before whole-organ work; its human microvascular negative result and documented sponsor discontinuation prevent promotion by narrative alone.

Ten new exact candidate records were screened, representing nine mechanistic families because CYM5442 and SAR247799 share S1PR1. “New” means absent from the reviewed repository/proposal inventory, not novel to science or patentable. The AAT update is an existing-lead refinement and is not included in the ten. No candidate is assigned S-tier in this review.

## 1. AAT: turn a promising literature result into a specific product proof

### What is actually demonstrated

The 2026 human study reports eight declined donor lung blocks, each split into a randomized treated/control pair, followed for 12 hours. AAT improved oxygenation/compliance and reduced fluid-loss/edema measures. That is eight biological blocks, not sixteen independent donors. The abstract does not provide exact dose or numerical effect sizes. The earlier eight-donor conference report is likely the same dataset; it should not be counted as replication. [A1, A2]

The public CSL patent describes a **different, five-donor pilot** using 10 mg/mL AAT versus saline. It does not establish protein/oncotic matching. One reported cytokine analysis was uncorrected for perfusate dilution. It includes future-tense clinical examples, which are not completed trials. Do not substitute this pilot’s dose for the unretrieved 2026 methods or add five and eight donors together without participant provenance. [A3]

The prior pig EVLP study used 3 mg/mL Zemaira with six animals per group; a separate recipient-IV study used five per group. Recipient treatment cannot establish the durability of an ex vivo-only product. The existence of a 2018 survival-model abstract was rechecked, but its full dataset and follow-up were not sufficiently accessible to upgrade the claim. [A4, A5, A6]

**Treatment-window classification:** these are injured-donor-organ reconditioning and peri-reperfusion protection experiments. They do not yet demonstrate durable reversal of established recipient graft failure. Even an improved endpoint relative to a deteriorating control need not mean restoration from the treated organ’s own baseline.

### New formulation finding

Zemaira’s label provides approximately 50 mg/mL A1PI after reconstitution and specifies functional elastase-neutralizing potency. Its reconstituted formulation contains mannitol 121–168 mM, phosphate 15–20 mM, sodium 73–89 mM and chloride 30–39 mM. The label directs use within three hours after reconstitution. It does not validate activity during a 12-hour warm recirculating EVLP run. [A7]

**Calculation, not a reconstruction of the published study:** achieving 10 mg/mL final AAT from a 50 mg/mL stock would make that stock 20% of final circuit volume. It would contribute approximately **24–34 mM mannitol and 3–4 mM phosphate**, before accounting for displaced perfusate. At 3 mg/mL, the corresponding fraction is 6%. Powder dissolved directly into a different fluid would create another formulation; the exact recipe matters.

This makes a saline control an incomplete causal test. Added protein, altered colloid osmotic pressure, ionic composition, mannitol and dilution can affect lung water and measured physiology. This is a plausible competing explanation, not proof the AAT result is spurious. It also creates a useful development opportunity: identify the minimum active AAT exposure that adds benefit after the physical formulation has been matched.

### Concrete product and proof route

Proposed product: a **defined, potency-qualified AAT graft-conditioning additive**, with stated starting product, reconstitution method, final perfusate composition, activity throughout circulation, recipient carryover and handling specification. A ready manufactured product is a starting route; it is not automatically an EVLP-compatible finished product.

The first stage can be resolved without scarce human lungs: compare delivered active AAT with antigen concentration, monitor circuit/tubing losses, and assess retained function across the intended warm exposure. Formulation controls should reproduce final electrolytes, osmolality, volume and measured oncotic pressure. Protein matching should use a clinically relevant albumin comparator and acknowledge albumin’s own biological effects.

The next stage should ask one causal question per independent donor pair. Separate pair blocks can compare AAT with a fully matched base perfusate and, once justified, compare it directly with FXT-001. Pairing must preserve the donor as the statistical unit; repeated biopsies and timepoints do not increase the independent sample count. Endpoints should require useful postwashout gas transfer and compliance, acceptable edema, and blinded transplantability assessment, with donor-level effect estimates and confidence intervals.

A final large-animal transplant stage should isolate **ex vivo-only** treatment and measure recipient graft function after washout. Transplant survival or sustained organ function would be materially more informative than another set of cytokines. Tissue-bound material and recipient carryover must be measured so “washout” is an observation rather than an assumption.

**Advance:** the functional advantage survives composition matching, persists after washout, and appears at a reproducible potency/exposure without a recipient liability. **Stop:** physical matching explains the effect; benefit exists only while the circuit contains drug; or markers improve without useful graft function.

### Current public development facts

The CSL organ-perfusion family remains a real public rights consideration; Google’s US application page displays pending status, not a legal clearance determination. The Canadian portal lists UHN’s randomized Zemaira lung-transplant prevention trial, protocol 23-5745, as not yet recruiting, last updated 18 July 2026. That listing does not establish an ex vivo-only regimen. A new venture needs a distinguishable product/proof advantage, not merely a new name for the known use. [A3, A8]

## 2. FXT-001/UAMC-3203: the strongest addition, with an explicit methods boundary

### Evidence and material identity

The 2026 Cell paper reports functional protection in porcine liver/lung perfusion and preservation of declined human lungs in a split-organ design. FXT-002/003 are separate optimized analogues with improved mouse PK/safety evidence; they do not inherit FXT-001’s human-organ result. The human-liver lipid-peroxidation observations are target evidence, not an interventional human liver trial. [F1]

The primary full paper is publicly deposited at Liège and Leuven, but the available 15.6 MB postprint exceeded the research tool’s size limit; the normal managed download also failed. Exact dose, human donor count, effect magnitude, vehicle matching, and any transplant/washout details therefore remain **not primary-verified** in this review. Secondary descriptions say five human pairs, but that number is not promoted into the verified evidence field. [F2, F3]

This is a bounded retrieval limitation, not evidence that the material is unavailable or that an email is needed to decide whether the lead is scientifically interesting. It prevents precise quantitative ranking against the AAT study.

FXT-001 is identified publicly as UAMC-3203. The original 2018 chemistry and the granted patent disclose compound 39 and a synthetic route. Cayman lists the **free base**, CAS 2271358-64-4, molecular weight 471.7; MCE separately lists the **hydrochloride**, CAS 2271358-65-5, molecular weight 508.12. These are not interchangeable by mass. The exact form used in the organ paper still needs reconciliation. Catalog availability is a research-material route, not a received, authenticated lot or a clinical supply chain. [F4–F7]

### Counterevidence and ownership

The earlier primary multi-organ paper gives orthogonal ferroptosis support but is not fully independent of the originating team. UAMC-3203 showed substantial tissue distribution, little brain penetration, and context dependence: it did not improve the measured injury markers in TNF or polymicrobial-sepsis models. Some renal experiments were pretreatment. This argues for a defined ischemia/reperfusion indication and measured exposure, not broad anti-inflammatory “repair.” [F8]

The granted US11339124B2 page identifies Antwerp/Ghent/VIB assignees and displays active status. The university science park describes FiriX Therapeutics as developing FXT-001 for ischemia/reperfusion injury. Thus this is an active, claimed asset with public chemistry, not an abandoned free asset. No claim-by-claim freedom-to-operate opinion is made. [F5, F9]

### Narrow investable thesis

The plausible first product is an **exact FXT-001 short-exposure graft-conditioning regimen**, starting from an authenticated chemical form, with a measured free/tissue exposure specification. The intended window is after procurement injury but before or during reperfusion. It should be called preservation/reperfusion protection unless an experiment explicitly establishes an existing deficit and then demonstrates reversal.

The decisive development contrast is not another ferroptosis marker. It is whether this small molecule delivers more useful, sustained organ function than an optimized perfusate and a credible competing repair additive such as AAT. A separate dose/exposure qualification stage should establish solubility, albumin binding, circuit loss and tissue retention before assigning scarce donor lungs.

**Advance:** after the full methods are audited, donor-level benefit is large enough to change acceptance or useful graft function; the result persists through a drug-free reperfusion challenge; and recipient exposure has an interpretable safety margin. **Stop:** only lipid markers or edema shift, control mismatch accounts for the signal, the result does not persist, or effective tissue accumulation produces a graft/recipient liability. The existing spinout is a commercial-context fact; a distinctive regimen or demonstrable superiority is required for a separate value proposition.

## 3. CYM5442 and SAR247799: use a mechanistic weakness to generate a precise successor

### The inspected human-organ experiment

The accepted CYM5442 paper is openly readable. Seven donor pairs underwent six-hour EVLP with **0.05 μM CYM5442 hydrochloride** (Tocris 3601, water vehicle), in DMEM with 5% bovine albumin. Leak/edema and pressure measurements favored treatment, but **dynamic compliance, P/F and PVR were not significantly improved**. Endothelial S1PR1 protein declined by six hours; higher exposure disrupted barrier function in cell experiments. The endothelial-cell experiments used two biological donors, with technical replicates. No washout/transplant durability was demonstrated. [C1]

This has better inspectability than many candidates but a weaker functional result than a headline suggesting human lung repair. CYM5442 is useful as a well-specified mechanistic comparator. Its potential to protect a barrier and then downregulate the same receptor makes recovery after withdrawal central to the product question. Differences between DMEM/bovine albumin and clinical perfusion media also require an exposure bridge.

### Why SAR247799 is a specific, testable successor

SAR247799 is an exact biased S1PR1 agonist, CAS 1315311-14-8, with public identity and research catalog routes. Primary experiments show an activation/desensitization separation and renal/coronary endothelial protection; these findings do not establish postinjury human lung repair. No direct SAR-versus-CYM whole-organ experiment was found. [S1–S3]

In a 54-patient randomized T2D trial, the 5-mg group had a maximum placebo-adjusted brachial FMD improvement of 1.07 percentage points (95% CI 0.13–2.01). This is a macrovascular surrogate. It does not establish superiority to sildenafil merely because one comparison reached statistical significance and another did not. The sponsor’s original report states that Sanofi terminated development and reports no interpretable microvascular laser-Doppler treatment effect. No reason for the termination is established here. [S4, S5]

Human dose-escalation work also shows that higher exposures can produce heart-rate and lymphocyte effects; biased signaling does not remove all class liabilities. The Sanofi AKI-use patent US9782411B2 displays active status. Discontinuation does not mean public-domain chemistry or available commercial rights. [S6, S7]

### New proposal and stop rule

Use a low-cost human pulmonary endothelial functional comparison to decide whether **SAR247799 preserves barrier function after an established injury and through withdrawal**, with less receptor loss than CYM5442. Compare at measured free exposure and comparable target engagement; equal nominal concentrations would not be a meaningful comparison. Include the relevant perfusate protein context and assess recovery after washout.

Only a favorable result should move SAR into paired human EVLP. The desired advantage is sustained oxygenation/compliance and fluid handling, not receptor staining alone. **Stop** if its bias fails to translate in pulmonary endothelium, if withdrawal unmasks barrier failure, or if useful exposure overlaps class effects. This is a new-to-repository successor proposal within the same S1PR1 family, not a second independent human-lung discovery.

## 5. What this changes in the search process

**Use a claim-level progression rather than a paper-level score.** A new paper can supply one part of the case—identity, target engagement, organ physiology or tolerability—without supplying all four. The strongest new example is FXT-001: the material route can be resolved publicly while exact human magnitude remains unverified. The strongest cautionary example is SAR247799: a positive publication sits beside a negative secondary endpoint and an explicit sponsor termination report.

**Make the comparator carry the rival explanation.** In AAT, the rival explanation is physical perfusate composition. In CYM5442, it is transient receptor activation with later desensitization. In MitoQ, an apparent flow advantage may coexist with mitochondrial injury. In PTL060, thrombus histology can improve without survival. These are targeted reasons to stage an experiment, not generic requests for more research.

**Separate preservation from repair in the register.** Treatment before ischemia is prevention. Treatment after procurement injury but before reperfusion can be useful conditioning without proving reversal of established organ dysfunction. A treatment must start after a documented functional deficit and restore that deficit before the stronger repair claim is earned. Postwashout and post-transplant outcomes further test whether the effect is durable.

**Do not count publicly listed material, exact authenticated material, institutional model access, and rights as one availability field.** A supplier listing can remove an entity-definition bottleneck. It cannot demonstrate a matching salt form, reproducible potency, legitimate clinical manufacture, or actual organ access. None of these distinctions requires an owner email as a scientific-selection gate.

### Recommended allocation of the next proof effort

1. Complete the public methods extraction for **FXT-001** and reconcile free-base/HCl identity; its human organ claim warrants this focused effort.
2. Prepare the **AAT matched-formulation/potency experiment**, which addresses a specific alternative explanation and produces a useful product specification.
3. Run the **SAR-versus-CYM pulmonary barrier/washout decision** before committing whole organs to a successor hypothesis.
4. Keep **PTL060 and APT102** as narrower functional reserves; hold MitoQ behind a targeted toxicity/exposure question; use M101, mirococept and STS as occupied comparator/development lanes.

The fundable unit here is a bounded proof that can remove a decisive uncertainty and change selection. It is not yet a claim that one of these molecules is obviously financeable as an independent company. Scientific selection can continue now from public evidence; commercial exclusivity remains a separate, explicitly unresolved assessment.
