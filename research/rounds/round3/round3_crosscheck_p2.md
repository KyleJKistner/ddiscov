# Independent challenge: exact 8015-P2 for MFN2-CMT2A

> **Historical review snapshot.** Later findings and rankings are reconciled in the [current shortlist](../../../decisions/current-shortlist.md) and [round 4 review](../round4/README.md). Statements about activity or access describe this review period.

**4 November2026. Decision: retain as a high-priority functional-proof opportunity; do not call the human bridge established or the result S-tier.** The parent team's proposed exact-P2, post-onset human neuromuscular experiment addresses a real gap. It should be strengthened by exposure measurement, human donor/genotype replication and a design separating neuronal from direct muscle effects.

## Source facts that must remain attached to the claim

### 2024 mouse and human-cell paper

The human fibroblast and reprogrammed-motor-neuron figures used **8015**, before stereoisomer separation, from **one T105M patient**. Their endpoints were mitochondrial morphology/polarization and motility. Pure P2 was the slower-eluting chiral-SFC fraction; it was more potent than P1. Exact-P2 mouse sensory-neuron work used100nM for48h; the late-disease knock-in mouse experiment began around50weeks and used50mg/kg daily for6weeks. PK experiments had small n and identified enterohepatic recirculation. The same research organization supplied compounds under an MTA. These are not patient neuromuscular-force results and are not multiple independent human donors. [2024 primary paper, PMID39284622](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11493442/); refs , .

### 2026 rat paper

The rats were treated after baseline phenotyping at50weeks; randomization and blinded functional/pathology assessment are reported. The regimen was **oral treatment Monday–Friday**, not seven days weekly. Dose-response arms used n4/group and WT n7. A100mg/kg acute experiment measured nerve-mitochondrial motility, whereas chronic efficacy used1–10mg/kg. The paper defined some “normalization” by nonsignificance versus WT; this is not equivalence testing. Improved CMAP, strength, sensation and MRI muscle volume support useful function. The paper also argues that direct muscle repair contributes to the rapid response. A10µM off-target panel found59% MAO-A inhibition; the panel is not comprehensive systemic safety. Same-group authorship and company interest limit independent replication. [2026 primary paper, PMID42653381](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13513834/); refs , , , .

### Exact identity and public rights record

The later primary description identifies P2 as the trans-(1R,2R)-cyclopropyl enantiomer. Its full chemical name is independently printed in a2026 worm study: (1r,4r)-4-hydroxy-N-[(1R,2R)-2-(4-phenylbutyl)cyclopropyl]cyclohexane-1-carboxamide. The2024 abbreviation for undifferentiated8015 must not be copied blindly as a P2 identity. The worm study adds an exact-compound source, not human-CMT validation. [Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13664537/).

The cited patent family WO2023196340A1 has an issued US family member, **US12396968B2**, granted26August2025, plus a continuation. A “ceased” PCT page is not proof that the compound is unprotected. Claim mapping was not completed, and no freedom-to-operate conclusion is offered. [Issued US patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12396968B2/en); [family record](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240050386A1/en).

## What changes in the proposed proof

| Potential overstatement | Required correction | Experimental implication |
|---|---|---|
| P2 already rescues several human patients | The relevant2024 human data use mixture8015 from one patient | Authenticate pure P2 and test independent donor backgrounds |
| More mitochondrial motility means recovered neuromuscular function | Motility is a mechanistic readout, not force/transmission | Include evoked contraction or qualified neuromuscular transmission |
| Rat outcomes are statistically identical to normal | Nonsignificance at n4 does not establish equivalence | Estimate effect sizes/confidence intervals and prespecify a meaningful restoration margin |
| Strong acute target engagement establishes coverage at low chronic dose | Acute and chronic doses differ markedly | Measure free exposure and target engagement during the effective regimen |
| Two species provide independent replication | Mouse and rat studies share a research group | An independent laboratory/model execution is valuable |
| Rapid force recovery proves axonal regeneration | Direct muscle response can improve integrated output | Separate neuronal, muscle and coculture contributions |
| High selectivity ratio establishes a safety margin | Limited panel and potency ratio cannot establish chronic organ safety | Tie functional exposure to cytotoxicity and relevant off-target/organ assessments |

These qualifications narrow the interpretation without erasing the encouraging late-disease animal results. Unlike a prevention-only hit, P2 has a legitimate established-disease rationale. The most valuable next experiment therefore asks whether its specific chemical identity carries that result into human neuromuscular function.

## Recommended bounded experiment

First qualify a reproducible functional deficit in mature human MFN2-T105M neurons coupled to a competent muscle system. Use independent donor backgrounds and isogenic correction where possible. Replicates within a clone, imaging fields and mitochondria should remain nested observations. If a mature deficit cannot be produced, the model has failed qualification; improved morphology in immature cells should not be relabeled as therapeutic rescue.

Treat after the functional deficit is established. Compare authenticated P2 with vehicle, P1 and the8015 mixture; an earlier chemically distinct mitofusin activator can serve as a pathway comparator. The P1 control is valuable for stereospecificity but cannot substitute for a practical efficacy comparator. Keep concentrations on a measured, unbound exposure scale where feasible and verify that purity/stability persists in the medium.

The primary decision should be restoration of an evoked neuromuscular function with a predefined biologically meaningful margin. Mitochondrial transport, membrane potential and axonal morphology should explain the response rather than determine the outcome alone. Measure persistence and rebound after washout. A response confined to continuous high exposure can still be useful, but it creates a different dosing and safety question than durable recovery.

Use compartment-restricted treatment or separately qualified neuronal and muscle assays to identify the source of recovery. This protects against both errors: crediting a direct muscle effect as axonal regrowth, and dismissing a useful muscle contribution because the original narrative was neuron-centered. Extend to another mechanistically distinct pathogenic MFN2 genotype only after the anchor genotype reproduces; broad CMT2A positioning should not precede that evidence.

**Advance** if pure P2 independently restores the chosen human function after onset, at a plausible exposure with a separable safety window, and with reproducibility across donor backgrounds. The resulting donor-response, exposure and mechanism dataset would be a transferable development milestone.

**Stop or narrow** if only the mixture works; only mitochondrial shape/motility improves; the necessary exposure is implausible; force reflects nonspecific excitation or muscle toxicity; the result depends on one clone; or healthy systems are impaired near the active concentration. If T105M responds but other alleles do not, preserve a genotype-specific opportunity rather than stretching the label.

## Ranking implication

P2 may outrank NDC-9009 on breadth of established-animal functional rescue, while NDC-9009 has a more local delivery concept and direct patient-skin mechanics. Their uncertainties differ and should not be collapsed into one numeric score. P2's next proof is human neuromuscular function with exact identity and exposure; NDC-9009's is delayed mature-skin repair and a local exposure window. Neither presently establishes an obviously fundable development package, but both support concrete experiments without waiting for owner correspondence.
