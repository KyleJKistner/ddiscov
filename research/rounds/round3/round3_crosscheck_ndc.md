# Independent challenge: topical NDC-9009 for Darier disease

> **Historical review snapshot.** Later findings and rankings are reconciled in the [current shortlist](../../../decisions/current-shortlist.md) and [round 4 review](../round4/README.md). Statements about activity or access describe this review period.

4 November 2026. Scope: challenge the proposed topical proof, not repeat the originating team’s broad screen. No outreach or experiments performed.

## Decision

**Retain a bounded NDC-9009 proof, but add a formulation/exposure gate before interpreting a topical 3D result.** The most concrete new weakness is unusually low measured aqueous solubility relative to the concentration used in the Darier experiment. A second, biologically specific risk is that an activator of residual SERCA may prevent a flare more readily than reverse a lesion in which that residual protein has already been inactivated.

## 1. The nominal 5 μM concentration is not yet a topical exposure specification

The original NDC paper’s Table 1 reports NDC-9009 molecular weight 305, **PBS pH 7.4 solubility of 0.23 μM**, and 17.1 ± 1.0% SERCA activation at 2 μM. The Darier adhesion experiment uses nominal 5 μM. Thus its nominal concentration is approximately **22-fold the measured PBS solubility**. The 2023 paper also found effects on normal neuronal spine morphology at 0.1 μM, which supplies no skin safety margin but does show that the molecule is not inactive in healthy cells. [1,2]

**Inference:** this does not invalidate the Darier experiment. Protein, cosolvent, formulation, supersaturation and membrane partitioning can make culture exposure differ substantially from PBS solubility. Nor is aqueous solubility a ceiling on achievable epidermal exposure. It means that “5 μM worked in a dish” cannot be used as the delivered topical dose, unbound concentration, or penetration target without measurement. Insoluble drug can also create misleading concentration-response curves or make apparent batch effects depend on formulation history.

The first useful development step is therefore a small formulation-to-exposure bridge: authenticate the exact article, determine what fraction is dissolved under the assay conditions, and measure exposure in viable epidermis after topical application. Vehicle controls must follow the same formulation. Surface residue or total whole-skin concentration cannot substitute for delivery to the relevant living cell layer. A topical vehicle that itself changes adhesion or irritation would confound the intended mechanism.

## 2. Prevention of junction failure is not reversal of an established lesion

The Darier paper’s functional result is a short monolayer fragmentation assay during junction assembly. Its four isolates come from three donors with two ATP2A2 variants. The same paper proposes that stress can push residual SERCA toward irreversible oxidation/inactivation and reports functional protection with **5 mM NAC**; glutathione monoethyl ester also protected junctional structure. Baseline desmoplakin localization catches up by four hours without treatment. [1]

**Inference:** a positive allosteric modulator requires sufficient activatable protein. In a mature lesion with inactivated SERCA and reorganized junctional/cytoskeletal proteins, activating the remaining pool may be inadequate even if it improves fresh junction assembly. This is a specific, testable failure mode. It does not require guessing that the target is wrong.

The proposed 3D model should establish a reproducible functional barrier/adhesion deficit **before** treatment, then distinguish reversal from spontaneous recovery and from prophylaxis. An exposure-matched preventive arm can determine whether a failed reversal experiment still supports a separate flare-prevention thesis. Those are different product claims and development paths.

## 3. Comparator implications

A topical NDC program should earn an advantage over more than vehicle. **NAC is a particularly relevant mechanistic benchmark because the same disease model shows functional protection.** Its millimolar nominal dose is not evidence of inferior topical feasibility, and NDC’s micromolar nominal dose is not by itself an advantage. Compare each at an exposure that can actually be delivered, with tolerability and formulation differences measured.

CDN1163 can serve as a same-target benchmark; the label “next generation” does not establish NDC-9009 superiority in Darier skin. A clinically relevant topical retinoid is also useful when the model captures the disease features that treatment addresses. The 2026 expert consensus describes the present treatment landscape; a short adhesion assay alone should not be used to claim clinical superiority over it. [1,3]

## 4. Healthy-skin margin must be functional

No convincing topical healthy-skin exposure margin was established in the retrieved NDC sources. This is an absence of qualification, not an observed dermatotoxicity signal. A same-donor or matched healthy-skin arm should measure intact barrier, normal differentiation and tissue integrity during repeat exposure and after withdrawal. Viability alone can miss disrupted epidermal organization. The therapeutic window must cover the exposure needed to rescue established diseased tissue; a nominal concentration ratio across unrelated assays is inadequate.

## Revised advance and stop conditions

**Advance:** a reproducible topical formulation delivers authenticated NDC-9009 into viable epidermis; measured exposure restores an established functional deficit across independent donors; benefit has a useful advantage over NAC and the selected clinical benchmark; healthy skin retains normal function; and benefit persists through a relevant washout/stress challenge.

**Stop or narrow the indication:** rescue exists only during junction assembly, requires an irritating or otherwise confounding vehicle, cannot be reproduced at measured dissolved exposure, or fails against the simpler comparator. If only prophylaxis works, retain a distinct prevention hypothesis rather than calling the result lesion repair. No owner reply is needed to make this scientific decision.
