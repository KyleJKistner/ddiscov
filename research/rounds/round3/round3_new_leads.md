## Decision

**NDC-9009 for Darier disease is the strongest new proof opportunity from this domain screen. Org43553 for selected LHCGR dysfunction and recombinant MG53 corneal gel merit reserves with concrete, different proof requirements. None qualifies as S tier on the public record.** NDC-9009 has a particularly useful combination of a disclosed chemical article, patient disease biology and a direct mechanical phenotype; the missing evidence is sufficiently specific to design a decisive experiment. Its advantage is the tractability of that question, not proof of clinical readiness.

The screen adds **15 article/indication records, representing 13 families** after grouping three approaches to Darier disease. Fourteen records are new to the reviewed inventory; trametinib is a refinement of an already recorded drug in a different indication. The count includes existing clinical benchmarks and rejected hypotheses. It is not a count of investable products. ## 1. NDC-9009: a meaningful Darier proof, with a narrower claim than the headline

The 2026 study's four keratinocyte isolates come from **three patients and two ATP2A2 variants**, not four independent patients. NDC-9009 at 5 µM improved mechanical adhesion during a four-hour calcium switch. That result directly concerns tissue cohesion, but treatment accompanied assembly rather than reversing an established lesion. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13440392/);.

An independently makeable article is plausible: the earlier work publishes synthesis and analytical characterization of NDC-9009. The name **4-(dimethylamino)-N-(2-methylquinolin-8-yl)benzamide** is inferred from those disclosed reagents/product data; the reported ion is [M+H]+306. CDN1163 is a different compound and must not inherit NDC-9009's mechanical result. [Chemistry](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10530588/); [later NDC-9009 study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12311773/).

### Why the next proof can create transferable value

The strongest project is a **quantified local repair window in mature Darier tissue**. A disease-cell result alone does not tell a future developer whether a formulation can expose the living epidermis, whether residual SERCA activity can support repair after acantholysis, or whether benefit separates from nonspecific tissue effects. A coherent dataset answering those questions would travel with the compound, formulation and assay, even if a particular partner or licensing route changed.

The experiment should begin with tissue that has a stable, measured adhesion/barrier deficit. Treatment starts after that qualification. A prevention arm can explain mechanism, but it cannot substitute for the delayed arm. Use donor-stratified analysis, with multiple isolates from the same donor nested, and prespecify which endpoints determine success. The most useful pair is mechanical cohesion plus barrier permeability or an equally direct epithelial function; junction staining is explanatory support.

Nominal media concentration is not a dose-selection justification for topical development. Measure unbound or otherwise pharmacologically relevant drug in viable epidermis alongside tissue function. Include healthy tissue and a concentration series sufficient to establish a margin. If the effective tissue exposure also damages healthy epidermis or impairs repair, the lead should fall regardless of a visually impressive marker result.

A useful comparison set includes vehicle, the chemically distinct SERCA activator CDN1163, and a mechanism-distinct calcium-restoring comparator. Dantrolene and trametinib are relevant candidates for that role, with exact exposure and timing kept separate. The objective is to discover whether NDC-9009 offers a better durable repair window, not merely to show that several interventions change calcium-associated markers.

### Development and competition constraints

The public Neurodon quinoline/SERCA patent family exists. I have not mapped NDC-9009 against each issued claim or jurisdiction; neither freedom to operate nor a universal block on Darier formulations is established. This is a concrete rights-mapping task, not a reason to wait for an email before scientific selection. [Patent family example](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20190151303A1/en).

The alternative Darier evidence should prevent anchoring. Dantrolene's trial EUCT2024-519201-36-00 is complete, and a public mirror lists a September2026 results document, but the document's outcomes were not recovered. Completion cannot be called success or failure. The October2026 mitochondrial/adhesion paper also deserves incorporation once its full denominators are extracted. [Official trial](https://euclinicaltrials.eu/ctis-public/view/2024-519201-36-00); [results-document listing, secondary mirror](https://ctis.eu/trial/2024-519201-36-00); [2026 paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022202X26037681).

**Advance:** reproducible donor-level delayed cohesion and barrier recovery at achievable local exposure, persistent after washout, with an adequate healthy-tissue margin and comparative value. **Stop/redesign:** only junction assembly is accelerated; only staining changes; required exposure cannot be achieved; or the apparent effect tracks general injury/proliferation. Numerical thresholds should be selected from disease deficit, reproducibility and comparator performance, not invented from a figure lacking individual donor estimates.

## 2. Org43553: rare-disease selection is more credible than generic oral ovulation marketing

The 2026 adult LhcgrT465I mouse experiment links the human T461I variant to cycling/corpora-lutea rescue with LHR-Chap when hCG did not rescue. The calcium dataset contains **391 cells from three treated mice**. It does not demonstrate treated-mutant live births. [Primary study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1038/s44321-025-00369-2), refs .

There is a useful human exposure precedent, but it must be counted correctly. The 2013 study's **159 healthy women span two compounds**, Org43553 and Org43902, and multiple study parts. It is neither 159 mutant patients nor 159 Org43553-treated women. [Human trial](https://academic.oup.com/jcem/article-abstract/98/4/1558/2536901). Earlier normal-animal work reported oral activity with successful fertilization and implantation; that supports reproductive pharmacology without filling the mutant-fertility gap. [Primary animal pharmacology](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19088107/).

### The differentiated proposition

A plausible product definition is **a pharmacochaperone for a prespecified set of LHCGR defects whose endogenous/hCG response can be restored**. A generic agonist for everyone is a weaker and much more competitive claim. Published mutant-receptor work already shows variant-dependent rescue; the same small molecule can help one receptor defect and fail another. [Mutant receptor primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32316022/).

The asset-creating deliverable would combine a functional variant-selection rule, authenticated compound/salt, human-exposure-matched potency and a relevant reproductive endpoint. Engineered human receptor cells can establish causality and map variants, but they do not by themselves establish ovarian function. A disease-relevant mature cell/tissue model must show restoration of a hormone-linked function, such as qualified steroidogenesis or ovulatory signaling, with hCG and wild-type controls.

The next animal step should be contingent on that result. Cycling is useful, but the strongest endpoint is reproductive competence with appropriate developmental assessment. An unchanged number of corpora lutea, abnormal oocyte quality or poor embryo outcomes despite restored cycling would materially weaken the project. It should not be presented as a fertility therapy merely because a hormone response returns.

### Identity and exposure are tractable but incomplete

Org43553/Org42599/LHR-Chap naming spans salt forms. The public parent structure and current research listings provide a route to material; they do not establish that a random catalog vial reproduces the article. Normalize to parent-molecule molarity after confirming counterion, purity and activity. [Public entity](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/9914457); [supplier listing](https://www.medkoo.com/products/35532).

A historical oral dose is not a substitute for unbound ovarian exposure at the mutant rescue concentration. Recover the original PK data, account for species and repeated-dose differences, and measure exposure in the proposed model. If the pharmacochaperone effect needs sustained concentrations far above the human bridge, the appealing repurposing story may collapse.

**Advance:** variant-specific function at a realistic exposure, followed by normal reproductive competence in the established disease model. **Stop:** broad all-LHCGR positioning; trafficking without useful function; cycling without reproductive competence; or an exposure requirement that cannot be reconciled with a reasonable product. Existing literature and a supplier do not prove that the compound is abandoned or commercially unclaimed.

## 3. MG53: test delayed optical recovery, not another acute closure result

The 2025 thermogel study's main useful distinction is recovery quality. Both mouse arms re-epithelialized by day5. The methods report seven animals per arm while the healing figure reports nine; terminal staining uses seven. Those denominators should remain visible. [Primary gel study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12030682/), refs .

The protein route is more concrete than a generic regenerative factor: recombinant human MG53 production and purification are described, and the gel components are public. This establishes a reproducible research plan, not a qualified batch. Tags, aggregation/redox state, endotoxin and released-protein potency must be controlled. [Production paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3777623/); [earlier corneal study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-019-0316-7).

### The experiment worth doing

A useful comparison starts after a persistent or well-established injury deficit and asks whether MG53 improves **optical clarity, epithelial barrier and functional innervation**. Nerve-density staining is supportive but does not measure corneal sensation. A clear-looking photograph is not a quantitative optical endpoint. The model should make a meaningful functional question possible and be chosen before a formulation is optimized around an easy acute endpoint.

Use blank gel, matched soluble protein and a strong practical corneal-repair comparator. This portfolio already contains insulin and mIGF concepts, which makes them relevant comparison choices rather than separate siloed programs. If the gel simply increases residence time of any repair agent, that might be a formulation result, but it is not proof of MG53-specific differentiation. A factorial or otherwise carefully matched comparison can separate protein and vehicle contributions without multiplying uncontrolled treatment combinations.

The proposed asset is a local product that delivers durable repair quality with fewer applications or better outcomes than practical alternatives. Evidence that only shortens early closure in a wound that heals completely anyway would not clear that bar. Antiangiogenic readouts also require a viability window; suppressing endothelial activity through toxicity would not establish selective antifibrotic action.

The 2026 modified-RNA MG53 study is a separate article and route. Its outcomes must not be credited automatically to recombinant-protein gel. Existing MG53 development interests are disclosed; an exact copy of the published formulation is not automatically a differentiated venture.

**Advance:** delayed, durable optical/barrier or sensory benefit beyond the strongest active comparator at measured local exposure and acceptable tolerability. **Stop:** transient early closure only; blank-gel equivalence; unqualified protein; toxicity-linked antiangiogenesis; or failure to show a meaningful patient-facing function.

## 4. BRD4780: an example of how the revised process should change a decision

The September2026 independent preprint used **four positive clones from three affected donors**, with negative controls, and 10µM BRD4780 for24h. It reproduced mutant-protein lowering but also found normal MUC1 suppression and broad pathway effects. It is a preprint, and these observations do not invalidate every TMED9 strategy. [Independent report](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.16.26363125v1.full), refs .

This finding should lower confidence in a simple “clear the toxic protein, therefore rescue the kidney” narrative. A compound may reduce the measured cargo because it interrupts secretion or transcription generally. That can improve an image while harming the physiological function the program is intended to preserve.

The decisive experiment is not another mutant-staining replication. It must pair clearance with normal cargo handling, epithelial polarity and a qualified tubular transport/function endpoint across independent patient models. Compare exposure-response relationships for benefit and normal-cell impairment. If the same concentration produces both, the parent compound should stop even if it is easy to buy. A more selective exact analog can be considered only after demonstrating its improvement, not by transferring BRD4780's accumulated citations to a new name.

The earlier Cell study and UMOD extension remain valuable mechanistic evidence, but those are separate models and endpoints. [2019 primary paper](https://heiman.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/dvela-levitt-et-al.-2019.pdf); [2024 UMOD study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11645142/); [public chemical/patent route](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11840495B2/en).

## Other screened leads and what their evidence changes

### Real human benchmarks that are already active programs

Ripasudil after Descemet stripping has an especially useful direct functional comparator: the 2026 phase2 trial randomized65 participants and reported corneal edema clearance in17/21 QID recipients versus2/22 placebo at12weeks. That materially raises the standard for new endothelial-repair ideas. It is already in a phase3 program. [Primary phase2 report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42303065/); [phase3 registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05795699).

αCT1 has chronic human-wound randomized evidence. Its phase3 termination record explicitly attributes termination to sponsor decision and says no safety or efficacy concerns. The current issuer lists ophthalmology rights as globally outlicensed. Neither an unsupported “failed phase3” dismissal nor a “neglected peptide ready to acquire” claim is justified. [Human wound paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4472499/); [official trial history](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02667327?a=13&tab=history); [current pipeline](https://xequel.com/pipeline/).

KUS121 has nine-person uncontrolled acute CRAO data and a later randomized phase2 presentation announcement. The announcement is not a numerical results report. Its current development status makes duplication a scientific/competitive differentiation problem, not an unknown-material problem. [First-human study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7018138/); [sponsor's phase2 announcement](https://www.kyoto-drug.com/en/August062025.pdf).

Tonabersat illustrates why current negative results belong in every target-family review. The 129-person randomized DME study missed its primary endpoint and showed no visual-acuity advantage. An exploratory subgroup does not rescue the broad indication. This negative does not automatically invalidate αCT1: different article, route and tissue function require separate analysis. [Primary randomized report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/pmid/42064012/).

### Earlier reserves with precise reasons for remaining below the shortlist

MANF has intriguing UMOD causal biology, but the human organoid experiment uses inducible intracellular expression. It does not prove that an administered protein can enter the required compartment. The first question for a protein product is delivery-linked function, not another gene-expression replication. [Mouse study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-42154-0); [human organoid letter](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/205631).

ML141 reversibly shrinks patient-derived ADPKD cysts, with an exposure probe reaching30µM. That is worth retaining as physiology and chemistry evidence, but cyst volume is not kidney function. A therapeutic window must include normal transport and an achievable renal exposure. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/pmid/41946363/).

Amlexanox is a more appropriate lead from the PAX6 paper than selecting DAP solely because it raises protein. The work supports tissue-dependent readthrough and developmental phenotype changes, leaving adult corneal barrier rescue unresolved. Prior ataluren trial results require explicit comparison rather than assuming all readthrough compounds are interchangeable. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10362734/); [EMA trial assessment](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/variation-report/translarna-h-c-2720-p46-028-epar-assessment-report_en.pdf).

TFF3 is a defined topical-protein reserve with small acute-injury studies. A chronic-defect experiment against an active comparator is needed to make its clinical value credible. It does not outrank MG53 merely because procurement appears straightforward. [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2442345/), refs .

VP1-001 has mouse optical readouts and stereoselective mechanistic support, but independent human-lens work with the corresponding oxysterol was negative under different assay conditions. This is a replication question, not a platform already ready for clinical positioning. The discriminating study uses matched authenticated material and objective optical function in established human cataract tissue. [2022 mouse optics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9123516/); [enantiomer-control study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6676924/); [independent negative study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-44676-4).

## What this screen says about the search process

The most consequential errors here were **claim transfer**, not lack of compounds. Four isolates became a tempting four-patient denominator. A compound that improved junction staining could be confused with the one that improved mechanical adhesion. Intracellular MANF expression could be described as ready protein replacement. MG53 modified RNA could add apparent validation to a distinct recombinant protein product. A completed clinical trial could look positive without its results. A public supplier could conceal the fact that a candidate is already an advanced company program.

The revised record should therefore keep six things together: exact article; disease/genotype; timing; biological denominator; direct function; exposure. The claim should be downgraded whenever one of those moves away from the proposed product. A separate source should record the strongest current comparator or contrary result. This makes the shortlist robust to new publications and avoids re-promoting old leads under aliases.

A practical research sequence is: authenticate and qualify the article/model; demonstrate a stable disease deficit; test delayed recovery and the active comparator at measured exposure; then ask whether the result creates a distinct development milestone. Rights analysis belongs alongside that plan, with known patent and pipeline facts stated accurately. Unresolved commercial terms do not need to become an outreach gate for the scientific decision.
