## 1. FD-2 for ALS: first finite information gate

The paper describes two extracellular Pgk1-derived 16-amino-acid peptides, FD-1 and its single-residue variant FD-2. FD-2 is the selected lead because its in-vivo results appear stronger, although FD-1 performs better in some cell comparisons. The proposed stability/conformational explanation is a hypothesis; measured pharmacokinetic superiority was not demonstrated. Neither intracellular Pgk1 enzyme activation nor the full-length extracellular protein is the same therapeutic article. [R26-F02](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neurot.2025.e00806)

### What the evidence supports

The publication connects engineered neuronal assays, developmental fish models and SOD1-G93A mouse outcomes within **one investigator lineage**. The mouse motor assessments use **five animals per group**; survival uses **twelve per group**. Repeated grip readings do not increase the animal denominator. Treatment begins at postnatal day 60, before overt deterioration. The survival endpoint is a predefined humane endpoint that includes inability to feed with forelimb paralysis, not natural lifespan. Random allocation and blinded endpoint assessment are reported. Grip, locomotion, NMJ innervation and motor-neuron markers provide more useful evidence than a target-only claim. [R26-F02](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neurot.2025.e00806)

The evidence most directly supports **early preservation or delay**. It does not establish post-symptomatic rescue in people, patient-derived mature motor-unit function, efficacy across ALS genotypes, relevant tissue exposure, immunogenicity or a chronic clinical formulation. Developmental C9orf72-knockdown and TDP43 fish observations do not fill those gaps. A viable early-treatment population could still be valuable; prevention-only activity calls for a population/window and economic re-scope rather than automatic rejection. A particular delivery compartment, including brain penetration, should not become a universal requirement if useful peripheral neuromuscular benefit can be demonstrated. [R26-F02](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neurot.2025.e00806)

### What changed the capture assessment

The newly retrieved **US20240327462A1** names FD peptides and lists **Mackay Medical College and Fu Jen Catholic University**. It contains short-peptide composition and neurological-use claims. This is a plausible institutional asset relationship, not proof of an available license. The Google Patents transcription's pending-status and assignee fields are not independent verification of current official prosecution or title. Exact article/claim correspondence, including final chemical form, remains to be resolved. The paper's no-conflict declaration cannot substitute for a patent search. [R26-F04](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240327462A1/en)

Patent and article experiments must remain in the same lineage unless proven otherwise. Their survival counts differ: the patent describes seven controls and five per treated group, while the later article reports twelve per group. They cannot be added or treated as independent confirmation. The online paper date is November 2025 and its issue date is January 2026; that is one publication, not two studies. [R26-F02](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neurot.2025.e00806) · [R26-F04](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240327462A1/en)

**Next information gate:** reconcile the final FD-2 article and claimed peptide form, current public national records and any existing product/exposure packet. Then specify the feasible early or symptomatic claim and whether an obtainable, bounded evidence route can answer it. This gate can cheaply remove an attractive but unbuildable thesis; it does not buy useful human function.

**Stop or re-scope:** close the public-record pass once the named records are resolved or accessible routes are exhausted. If the next informative step requires broad peptide-delivery discovery plus a new model platform, compare that larger program afresh. No private owner response is a scientific-selection prerequisite, and no available rights or low-cost peptide development is presumed.

## 2. PSB-KD107 for DMD: meaningful force, incomplete product thesis

The primary study uses the defined GPR18 agonist **PSB-KD107**, with **absolute and specific EDL force** improving in mdx mice. Treatment starts at ten weeks and runs for three weeks. The force caption reports **five animals per group, except O-1602 with three**. RvD2 is an active comparator with a favorable force result; the smaller O-1602 group does not show significant functional benefit. This is post-early-onset treatment, but there is no within-animal pretreatment force record or demonstrated advanced-disease reversal. [R26-F01](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2023.1187253)

Human evidence is narrower: **two DMD donor lines and one Becker line** show improved fusion/myotube formation. These are not three DMD donors and not mature human-force rescue. Technical duplicate receptor measurements are not new donors. Diaphragm fibrosis and inflammatory changes add biological support without establishing respiratory reserve, cardiac function, survival or clinically useful add-on benefit to relevant DMD care. No appropriate current-care head-to-head/add-on result was established in the paper. [R26-F01](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2023.1187253)

The paper names Cayman as supplier, and Firecrawl found the exact item 31393. The live primary catalog page returned **HTTP 403**, so the screen does not claim current stock, a price, a qualified lot or permission for commercial use. No exact obtainable commercial interest was verified in the bounded public search. A historically supplied small molecule may reduce article reconstruction effort, but public availability and a prior publication can also weaken a generic standalone product thesis. Missing search results are not freedom to operate. [R26-F01](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2023.1187253) · [R26-F11](https://www.caymanchem.com/product/31393/psb-kd107)

**Next information gate:** decide from the exact article, public claim landscape and existing exposure record whether there is a practical chronic product and a retainable contribution. A surviving thesis needs a mature human-muscle function question with an appropriate incremental-benefit comparison. Fusion alone cannot settle it.

**Comparison with BLU and APR:** PSB has a concrete historically supplied article and direct published mouse-force evidence. BLU has a more explicitly specified post-deficit human-force qualification route, despite its own identity, exposure and rights questions. APR starts from uncontrolled same-disease human observations and a local historical article. These advantages concern the information bought by the next commitment. They are not exemptions from an actual price, access, output-rights or stop-rule assessment. [R26-F13](../../../decisions/current-shortlist.md)

**Stop or re-scope:** decline an independent commercial thesis whose only contribution is repeating the known research effect without retained value. If meaningful function requires an open-ended chemistry or model-development program, end the small-option assumption and compare that new scope. A useful prevention window remains eligible if it is clinically feasible.

## 3. Copper nanoclusters for Menkes: survivor bias and a changed comparator

The selected family is the paper's defined **CuNC@HisAc / CuNC@HisAcAsc**, not generic copper, copper histidinate or a copper ionophore. In Mo-blo ATP7A-deficient mice, treatment begins at postnatal day five and follow-up ends at day 50. Survival groups are **CuNC 17, untreated 14, CuHis 13 and wild-type 20**. The model's early mortality includes vascular/aortic disease, so improved survival cannot be assigned wholly to brain rescue. [R26-F03](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aeg6972)

The paper reports a 50-day survival value for CuNC and wild-type that coincides with the observation boundary. It must not be presented as an independently established **median lifespan**. Later behavior largely compares CuNC survivors with wild-type because disease-control survival is poor. That selection makes neurological normalization a stronger claim than the data support. Treated animals still stumble more than wild-type, while nonsignificant differences on other tests do not establish equivalence. Brain cuproenzyme activity is supportive, but it does not repair the missing later functional disease-control comparison. [R26-F03](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aeg6972)

Safety interpretation also needs restraint: ALT and beta-2-microglobulin abnormalities are reported, groups differ in age and survival, and some late CuHis measurements have only two or three animals. The authors' adaptive/reversible explanation is not verified longitudinal reversibility. Normal histology or negative staining is not complete clearance of chronic copper toxicity. [R26-F03](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aeg6972)

### The comparator has changed

The **FDA approved Zycubo/copper histidinate for pediatric Menkes in January 2026**. The FDA page is dated **January 12**, distinct from the January 13 sponsor release. It describes two open-label single-arm studies with **66 treated participants and 17 untreated contemporary external controls** overall. That is not randomized proof, but it establishes a current survival-benefit treatment baseline. CuNC must supply a consequential neurological, practical delivery or safety increment rather than rely on untreated disease alone. [R26-F06](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-treatment-children-menkes-disease)

Menkes is also **not a newly encountered disease concept** for ddiscov. Baseline P010 already concerns a copper-ionophore formulation. Its two primary links identify **elesclomol–copper**, including the JCI two-child report and a separate preclinical copper-deficiency paper with a Texas A&M–Engrail licensing disclosure. These are different articles from CuNC, not an exact-formulation duplicate; their human observations are not new CuNC patients or newly screened cohorts. [R26-F14](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12483558/) · [R26-F15](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40023603/)

**WO2022238656A1** lists University of Lorraine and CHU Nancy in the indexed record. Its WO ceased status cannot establish abandonment of national rights. The paper's orphan-designation statements do not constitute independently verified available rights, and the cited FDA regulation is not a unique designation identifier. This is a possible institutional translation/partner program with unresolved exact formulation, manufacturing control and terms. [R26-F05](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2022238656A1/en)

**Next information gate:** reconcile censoring, survivor/age strata and formulation-to-patent identity, then define an incremental claim against current copper histidinate. A finite existing-data audit is different from financing a chronic pediatric nanomedicine program. Useful neonatal prevention can be a valid product claim; late reversal is not mandatory. Hold broad brain-restoration claims unless comparable functional evidence supports them.
