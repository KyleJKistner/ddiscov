## Decision

**Ten exact intervention/use records were added to the screen. All three agents worked on new candidates using Firecrawl, with Unpaywall for lawful research-access discovery.** The leading questions are **topical erlotinib for KRT16 keratoderma**, **FD-2 for ALS**, and **levodopa/carbidopa for MCT8 deficiency**. They lead different information purchases; they are not three interchangeable drug assets or a quantified financial ranking.

Erlotinib is the strongest new local clinical-development question: a defined existing drug, a narrow genotype and small blinded opposite-foot comparisons make the intended symptomatic benefit unusually concrete. FD-2 is the first functional-preclinical asset question because useful mouse outcomes now connect to a named institutional patent family. MCT8 has the leading new human-evidence question, but a clinically worthwhile generic indication does not by itself create a proprietary investment.

**Prioritize these newly opened, finite questions over another unchanged broad search of APR or BLU.** The inherited development queue remains provenance under its original scope conditions; this round did not freshly revalue every incumbent. Both incumbents and new candidates lack actual offered, priced commitments. That prevents a verified financial ordering, not a conditional comparison of information quality, tractability and retained value. No paid-development or S-tier promotion is made, and capital remains uncommitted. [Integration review](integration-review.md).

## What the primary reads changed

### Topical erlotinib: meaningful local observations, a very small evidence base

The recovered two-page primary report concerns a mother and son with **KRT16 Arg127Cys** hereditary palmoplantar keratoderma. It includes blinded randomized opposite-foot phases and later uncontrolled extensions. The mother's pain-free ambulation occurred during the extension; the son's controlled-phase improvement was more modest. Keratolytic use and adherence matter. This is not a general pachyonychia cohort or a transfer of oral erlotinib/Olmsted efficacy. [Primary author copy](https://www.pachyonychia.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/LeeG2022Topical-Erlotinib.-pdf.pdf), R26-I09.

The local screen also retains contrary topical experience, prior Darier findings and product/rights questions in their exact scope. Sol-Gel's August 2026 issuer update says SGT-210, a topical EGFR-inhibitor program, continues; it does not specify this genotype/formulation or an available license. The field is not assumed vacant. [Issuer update](https://ir.sol-gel.com/news-releases/news-release-details/sol-gel-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results-and), R26-I15. The smallest valuable next step is a defined local clinical/article/evidence-rights question; it need not automatically restart with a new animal model. An adequate generic article may support useful research, while commercial capture still requires a concrete contribution.

### FD-2 and PSB-KD107: useful force or motor evidence is not human functional rescue

FD-2 improves motor outcomes and time to a humane endpoint in a SOD1 mouse model, with **five animals per group for grip/locomotion and twelve per group for survival**. Treatment begins before overt decline. The peptide's proposed stability advantage is not measured pharmacokinetic superiority. A named institutional publication, **US20240327462A1**, explicitly covers the peptide family; the paper's no-conflict sentence cannot be read as absence of patents. Current national claims, title, exact final product and obtainable terms remain unresolved. [Primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neurot.2025.e00806), [patent publication](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240327462A1/en), R26-I13–14.

PSB-KD107 has a direct mouse muscle-force result after in-vivo treatment, measured ex vivo. Human-cell findings concern fusion in **two DMD and one Becker donor background**, not force in treated patients. Its named research-article route is useful, but a live stock/price, mature human-force bridge and retainable commercial contribution were not established. It remains the next functional-lane reserve after FD-2's finite document/product question. [Primary paper](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2023.1187253), R26-I05.

### MCT8 and PURA: keep patients, endpoints and maturation separate

The later MCT8 report describes ten phenotyped patients but **nine evaluable treatment courses**. Broad benefit in seven of nine, GMFM improvement in five of eight and CGI improvement in six of nine are different observations. Functional-category scales and dystonia findings do not uniformly improve. An earlier report supplies another clinical lineage with its own denominators; no pooled unique-patient count or response probability is asserted. Current tiratricol labeling addresses peripheral thyrotoxicosis, leaving the symptomatic neurological adjunct question distinct. [Later paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.70093), [earlier paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12089910/), [human screen](human-evidence-screen.md).

The PURA paper contributes two new salbutamol-treated patients and discusses one earlier treated recipient. Respiratory maturation, treatment sequence, persistent support needs and adverse effects prevent a three-of-three causal efficacy claim. This remains a smaller clinical-information reserve. [Primary report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13375090/).
