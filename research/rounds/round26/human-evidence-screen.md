## Decision

**Levodopa/carbidopa for the movement disorder of MCT8 deficiency is this lane's first clinical-information priority.** Salbutamol for the respiratory and motor component of PURA syndrome is a smaller conditional reserve. TTI-0102 for MELAS is an active external program on hold for a precise endpoint, masking and attrition reconciliation. The comparison concerns the next uncertainty that could change a decision. Missing prices do not establish negative program value, and the same pricing standard applies to inherited programs. That conditional scientific-information preference does **not** establish superior retained financial value to APR or an executable clinical commitment. Generic capture, article suitability, output rights and actual terms remain unresolved. No paid-development or S-tier promotion is made. ## 1. Levodopa/carbidopa in MCT8 deficiency — R26-HUM-01

### Exact claim and evidence lineage

The proposed use is **useful ongoing improvement of hypokinesia, movement initiation or another patient-relevant motor function in genetically confirmed SLC16A2/MCT8 deficiency**, also called Allan–Herndon–Dudley syndrome. It is a symptomatic adjunct claim. Correction of thyroid transport, global developmental restoration and permanent off-treatment repair are not established or required for this narrower benefit.

Wilpert and colleagues' March 2025 primary paper describes **11 phenotyped males**. Six received systematic treatment assessment at one center, with paired six-month observations; the report separately describes another longer exposure with caregiver-perceived benefit and a one-week exposure without response. Those extra cases do not enlarge the six-person paired analysis. The investigators used an oral or gastric-tube levodopa/carbidopa suspension; the report does not establish a commercially qualified transferred pediatric lot. [R26-H02; [primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12089910/)]

The six-person analysis reported improvements in dystonia movement and disability scales and the Infantile Parkinsonism-Dystonia Rating Scale. Fine-motor developmental age increased modestly. **Gross-motor, language, cognition and CPCHILD quality-of-life measures did not show significant improvement**, despite favorable caregiver impressions; myoclonus did not improve. The comparator was baseline, with historical natural-history context, not a concurrent randomized control. A positive paired result is a signal for a controlled question, not a causal effect estimate. [R26-H02]

Bruschi and colleagues' later report was **published online on 27 October 2025**, with a February 2026 issue date. It describes ten males, three retrospectively and seven prospectively assessed. Its abstract's seven-of-ten wording should not become a response probability: the main text documents **nine evaluable treatment courses**, while one patient contributed biochemical data without an assessable response after death. The report does not establish that death as drug-related, and this screen does not infer the unassessed patient's exposure status. [R26-H01; [primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13001712/)]

The endpoint-specific accounting is more useful:

- Broad motor/nonmotor benefit was described in **7/9** and reduced hypokinesia/bradykinesia in **8/9**.
- GMFM-88 improved in **5/8** assessable patients; two declined slightly and one did not improve.
- CGI-I classified **four as much improved, two minimally improved and three unchanged**.
- Functional-category scales did not improve consistently. Mean dystonia movement scores increased, even as spontaneous movement increased.

The paper's mean relative GMFM increase is not a cohort-wide percentage-point recovery or restoration of normal function. The different dystonia directions across reports are a consequential endpoint tension, not a basis for combining scores. The two publications share a coauthor and clinical field; they describe different recruited settings, but no participant-level crosswalk was obtained. **No pooled unique-patient total or responder rate is asserted.** Videos and supplementary patient-level data were not independently audited. [R26-H01–02]

### Comparator, contrary interpretation and product contribution

The September 2026 FDA prescribing information for **Emcitate/tiratricol** specifies peripheral thyrotoxicosis in adults and children with MCT8 deficiency. That labeled endocrine benefit is a contemporary comparator-care fact, not a demonstration that the neurological adjunct question is already answered. A useful levodopa comparison must retain appropriate thyroid treatment, symptomatic care and rehabilitation; it should not assume a care-free control or that the two drugs are substitutes. Eighteen of the label's 23 pages were retrieved, including indication and clinical-study sections; the remaining instructions-for-use pages were not needed for this screen. [R26-H03; [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220963s000lbl.pdf)]

An April 2026 expert monitoring article takes a more cautious position on levodopa, declining to treat it as standard or disease-modifying care and regarding efficacy as unproven. Its methods describe an advisory meeting in November 2024 and literature search in June 2025. The cited support for that paragraph is the same Wilpert report; it is **interpretation of existing evidence, not a new negative controlled dataset**, and it predates the later report's online publication in its stated search window. Egetis supported the meeting and editorial work; the article states that the funder did not direct interpretation. The disagreement strengthens the case for a controlled symptomatic question without establishing a biological veto or a mechanistic certainty. [R26-H04; [monitoring article](https://doi.org/10.1159/000551857)]

A marketed active ingredient and a described suspension reduce some starting-article uncertainty. They do not establish an exclusive asset, supply terms, regulatory data-reference rights or a new-indication business. No exact-use commercial license or differentiated product contribution was verified. A first controlled indication package could still create value through an adequate existing article and contracted data/regulatory rights; a new formulation is not a universal requirement. A formulation project should be justified by an actual administration, exposure or tolerability need, not imposed to manufacture a moat. [R26-H01–04, H13]

### Smallest useful information package

The next package should reconcile the two reports at person and endpoint level, establish whether existing records support reliable assessment of the intended functional benefit, and identify background-care and article differences that could change interpretation. It should specify who can supply and reuse the necessary data, the concrete product contribution, and a separately priced route to a controlled clinical decision. This is a specification for a possible information purchase, not recruitment, trial execution or permission to obtain private records.

**Advance** to comparing an actual controlled-proof proposal if the functional claim remains meaningful, assessment is feasible, and access/output rights make the information usable. **Rescope or hold** if the record resolves only caregiver impressions without a useful interpretable outcome, adverse dystonia offsets the proposed benefit, or a generic implementation offers no defensible retained economics. An uncertain mechanism alone is not a stop for a demonstrated useful symptomatic claim. A return to cell or animal work is optional only when it addresses a specific consequential uncertainty.

## 2. Salbutamol for PURA-related respiratory and motor dysfunction — R26-HUM-02

### What was actually observed

The July 2026 primary report contributes **two unrelated new patients** and summarizes **three previously published patients**. Only **three of the five described people received salbutamol**: two new recipients and one earlier literature-derived recipient. The older salbutamol case's full original report remained inaccessible in this pass; its detailed response is supported by the 2026 authors' literature table, not an independent reread of that patient record. [R26-H05–06; [primary report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13375090/)]

The new cases include changes in ventilation requirement, antigravity movement and motor milestones. One infant had partial respiratory benefit with pyridostigmine before salbutamol was added. The second had measurable endurance and motor changes, subsequently achieved unsupported sitting, but remained dependent on tube feeding and still used BiPAP; confirmatory sleep assessment was pending. These are useful functional observations, with no concurrent control. The first child's AIMS score increased while remaining at a low percentile, and later epileptic spasms were followed by neuroregression. Global developmental rescue is not demonstrated. [R26-H05]

The published CHOP-INTEND values are reported as 50/65 to 54/65; this screen preserves the source's denominator rather than silently normalizing it. A change in a raw developmental score during infancy does not by itself isolate treatment from maturation.

### Why the apparent three-of-three signal is insufficient

The authors explicitly note that respiratory insufficiency often improves with age in PURA syndrome. Treatment began at different ages and followed different care sequences; two salbutamol recipients had prior or overlapping pyridostigmine exposure. Successful case publication can select responders. Consequently, three favorable reports do not estimate a population success probability or show that earlier hospital discharge would occur under a controlled comparison.

Safety and the active-treatment context are consequential. One new salbutamol recipient developed tachycardia, sleep disturbance and increased startle that resolved after dose reduction. Among four pyridostigmine recipients in the combined report, the table classifies one clear improvement, one partial improvement, one nonresponse and one deterioration with profound apnea and bradycardia. That is a heterogeneous comparator history, not a head-to-head drug ranking or a universal treatment sequence. [R26-H05]

The two new cases did not demonstrate a clean repetitive-stimulation decrement; their studies were limited by age or setting. A neuromuscular component is plausible, but the paper does not establish that all PURA disability is peripheral or that a biomarker selects salbutamol responders. No need to demand permanent CNS repair should obscure a potentially useful reduction in respiratory burden.

### Information and capture decision

The finite next question is whether the existing respiratory and motor chronology can distinguish an added salbutamol contribution from age-related improvement, preceding treatment and supportive care. A reviewable package should define a practical patient-benefit outcome, handle persistent ventilation/feeding support and adverse effects, and state the evidence that would support or defeat the claim of clinically useful acceleration. It need not begin with a new disease model.

This is behind MCT8 for clinical-information priority because the directly recovered treated series is smaller and maturation is a particularly strong alternative explanation. It may nevertheless outrank a broad preclinical screen under equal information cost if a usable existing clinical record and a narrow question are available.

Salbutamol is an existing drug, and the new indication does not itself supply exclusivity. The bounded search did not establish an exact-use license, sponsor transaction or regulatory-data package; irrelevant patent gene lists do not answer that question. Potential value lies in a useful first indication package or documented product improvement with contractual output rights. **If only nonexclusive care guidance is obtainable, label it as valuable clinical research rather than an investable proprietary drug program.** No current offered terms were found. [R26-H05–06, H13]

## 3. TTI-0102 for MELAS — R26-HUM-03

### Defined article, existing sponsor and bounded human result

TTI-0102 is described as the acetate salt of a cysteamine–pantetheine asymmetric disulfide, a prodrug developed by **Thiogenesis Therapeutics**. Parent cysteamine, the exact prodrug and the company's other indication programs cannot exchange evidence. The March 2026 medRxiv report is a **preprint authored by the sponsor's CEO**, with sponsor funding. The full HTML and the Unpaywall-returned 15-page PDF were read; they are one version of one study, not replication. [R26-H07; [preprint](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.26.26347968v1)]

The paper reports **nine randomized participants: six active and three placebo**. Recruitment stopped before the planned twelve after pharmacokinetic review, with gastrointestinal intolerance and dropouts at the fixed dose. The retrieved text does not provide a complete usable participant-flow table or the exact fatigue-analysis population. Repeated measurements and exposure observations are not independent participants.

The official registry independently confirms **nine actual participants**, completion on **12 January 2026**, last update **24 July 2026**, and **no posted results** at this retrieval. The remaining twelve-person language is the planned design, not twelve treated people. [R26-H08; [registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06644534)]

### The discrepancy that controls the decision

| Field | Registry | Preprint | Required interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Functional efficacy primary | Change in 12-minute walking capacity | No change reported | Preserve the negative functional observation; precision is insufficient to claim universal inefficacy. |
| Fatigue secondary | Fatigue Severity Scale (FSS) | Modified Fatigue Impact Scale (MFIS), reported p = 0.04 | Different instruments; obtain protocol/amendment and analysis provenance before calling a prespecified positive. |
| Masking | Double-blind prose; quadruple categories | Single-blind description | Unresolved source discrepancy, not a misconduct finding. |
| Participant flow | Nine actual enrolled; no posted results | Six active/three placebo, early stop and GI dropouts | Fatigue evaluability, missingness and treatment discontinuations need reconciliation. |

The paper also reports no treatment-related change in GDF-15 or FGF-21 and sample-stability problems for glutathione measurements. Its Safety section emphasizes intolerance in people below 50 kg, while Discussion uses a below-80-kg description. That internal discrepancy should be reconciled, not turned into an accepted therapeutic window. Exposure comparisons with another cysteamine product are not a randomized clinical tolerability or efficacy comparison. A patient-reported fatigue benefit could be valuable even without walking improvement, but its credibility and usefulness require the actual analysis and attrition context. [R26-H07–08]

### Rights, current context and next decision

The sponsor's current pipeline page describes the MELAS study as completed and exploratory, with findings informing other programs. A 2024 sponsor-origin release describes European patent allowance and prior US/Japanese grants. These are evidence of an active commercial program and a stated IP strategy; this screen did **not** inspect the current assignment chain, granted claims or any offered license. Sponsor claims about expedited pathways, markets or future vouchers are not adopted as regulatory or financial conclusions. [R26-H09–10; [pipeline](https://www.thiogenesis.com/pipeline)]

The relevant investment route is evaluation of an existing sponsor-controlled asset or a specifically offered contribution. An independent generic-repurposing program is not the asset described here. No valuation, licensing terms, available financing allocation or investable return was established.

The cheapest consequential gate is a **document reconciliation**: current protocol/amendments, statistical analysis plan, the origin of the MFIS analysis, actual masking and participant-level disposition. The registry states no planned individual-participant-data sharing; the preprint offers data on reasonable request. Neither statement establishes access, and no request was sent. If a valid, interpretable fatigue signal survives, compare a genuinely offered confirmatory or partnering opportunity with alternatives. Otherwise hold the efficacy promotion; do not transfer MELAS findings to Leigh syndrome or cystinosis.
