## What the source review changed

The [human/function audit](aat-human-function.md), [formulation/exposure audit](aat-formulation-exposure.md) and [product/capture audit](aat-product-capture.md) preserve each source's article, route, biological unit and version. ### Human EVLP: more methods, with version and missingness limits

The 2026 article abstract and 2020 conference report describe eight donor blocks split between treated and control circuits. These are eight independent donor pairs, not sixteen independent donors. The newly recovered 2019 author thesis supplies substantially more detail for an eight-pair study: nominal AAT concentration, replenishment of treated perfusate, blinded/randomized assignment language, repeated observations and a stated stopping rule of twelve hours or organ failure. It records 184 of 192 physiological observations and 78 of 80 cytokine observations. These counts concern repeated observations, not additional donors or a verified complete twelve-hour cohort. Individual failed-lung counts and the reasons for each missing observation were not recovered.

The thesis is a primary author document accessed through a public author-copy platform. Its detailed study can narrow feasibility uncertainty, but its methods and estimates are not silently assigned to the inaccessible full 2026 article. The thesis, 2020 abstract and 2026 report have likely overlapping lineage and are not counted as independent replications. The patent's five-donor pilot is also not added to the eight without participant provenance. The thesis's replenished exposure does not establish a single-pulse regimen, and improved circuit physiology does not establish additional transplantable grafts or recipient benefit. [2026 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42790593/); [2020 abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053249820312997); [2019 author thesis](https://www.academia.edu/88941544/Alpha_1_Antitrypsin_Treatment_of_Donor_Lungs_A_Translational_Pathway_to_Clinical_Application).

### Functional graft protection and systemic recipient treatment are different evidence

The independent 2018 mouse study retains causal value: active recombinant AAT, a non-inhibitory mutant, an albumin comparator and PR3/NE-deficient recipients converge on a protease-related explanation for early graft protection. The accepted manuscript explicitly reports a plain-Perfadex rinse before implantation and detectable human AAT in graft tissue at four hours. That supports a benefit after removal of the storage solution; it does not establish drug-free tissue, long-term repair or warm-perfusion equivalence of plasma-derived Zemaira. Mutant structural effects and a double knockout also do not isolate a universal requirement for simultaneous inhibition of both proteases. [Accepted primary manuscript](https://push-zb.helmholtz-munich.de/deliver.php?id=21907).

The recovered 2023 pig letter supports a useful, narrower observation: systemic recipient Zemaira was associated with faster early oxygen independence in a randomized, blinded six-per-arm study. Its three-day survival-model setting does not establish a survival advantage, and daily recipient treatment does not validate an EVLP-only product. The letter's published estimate and earlier thesis wording remain version-specific. The separate 2025 AAT-deficient-recipient cohort is observational systemic augmentation; its 447 eligible recipients, 32 continuers, 13 new post-transplant users and 45-versus-77 analytic comparison must not be conflated. Its survivor-selection handling and adjusted associations remain explicit. [2023 primary letter](https://www.amjtransplant.org/article/S1600-6135(23)00370-2/pdf); [2025 recipient study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11935422/).

### Exact activity and physical formulation remain the affordable uncertainty

The patent still reports pig nominal AAT at 3 mg/mL and measured human-AAT antigen around 0.5 mg/mL. The inspected record does not establish a mass-balance explanation. It would be unjustified to label the difference as a measured fraction of degraded, adsorbed or inactive protein. Antigen concentration, nominal addition and functional protease inhibition answer different questions. Some patent NE readouts are antigen ELISA, and control-side perfusate replacement complicates uncorrected cytokine concentrations.

The starting product's labeled amount is defined by elastase-neutralizing potency, while label reconstitution and IV handling do not qualify a prolonged warm recirculating admixture. Protein load, colloid osmotic pressure, electrolytes, excipients, dilution and handling remain plausible contributors to organ physiology. They are competing explanations to assess, not proof that the observed functional benefit is false. The published warm-EVLP article, cold recombinant-AAT preservation and systemic augmentation cannot exchange an exposure or stability specification. [Patent disclosure](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20190059362A1/en); [manufacturer prescribing information](https://labeling.cslbehring.com/pi/us/zemaira/en/zemaira-prescribing-information.pdf).

The financial objective is the value of a better continuation decision after learning, net of the bounded action and remaining development costs, rights payments, commercial costs and sharing. Patient benefit can support adoption; it does not identify the share retained by ddiscov. AAT can justify considering informative qualification before transplant efficacy or a complete license is already proven. Requiring the final positive result before the step that could produce it would be circular. Equally, qualification success alone does not authorize scarce-organ work or justify ignoring an uncompetitive continuation. Commercial diligence should be proportionate to the commitment and prospect of retaining value; all such execution remains outside the present review.

The prior regadenoson EVLP trial is a relevant translational benchmark already in the repository, not a newly screened opportunity. It reported 48 randomized donor pairs, 34 evaluable pairs and no statistically significant advantage in graft use or severe PGD. Baseline imbalance, exclusions and wide uncertainty limit a strong null interpretation. The lesson is to connect circuit physiology to useful recipient or graft-use outcomes, not to infer that AAT fails because another intervention did. [Primary trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12745136/).

## How later evidence would change the decision

These are decision branches, not an experimental protocol, sample-size plan, procurement request or work authorization.

1. **Active exposure or process compatibility remains unresolved.** Compare a finite qualification deliverable with its cost and ability to enable the next question. Antigen-only work cannot answer an activity question. A failed formulation or uninterpretable recovery assay does not establish biological target failure; a different formulation requires its own cost and value comparison.
2. **Qualification succeeds at a feasible cost.** Consider the separately priced donor-organ or recipient-relevant stage. Preserve the exact product and workflow. There is no automatic release of a larger budget, and completed qualification is not clinical efficacy.
3. **Physical matching removes the apparent AAT-specific increment.** Reduce confidence in a protein-specific product advantage. A useful physical formulation could still have practical value if it improves an actual outcome and supports a retained contribution; merely reproducing an incumbent perfusate does not establish that contribution.
4. **A simpler comparator provides similar useful outcomes.** Compare real total cost, handling, exposure, safety and workflow. AAT need not prove a unique dual-NE/PR3 mechanism to have value, but it needs a meaningful practical or clinical reason to bear its additional costs. Statistical nonsignificance is not equivalence.
5. **An organ-processing regimen improves meaningful graft or recipient outcomes.** Consider confirmation and the intended clinical claim. Tissue-retained AAT or temporary protection can be the mechanism of a valuable finite processing intervention. Claims of free-drug-independent repair or durable structural regeneration require additional evidence; complete drug-free tissue is not a universal entry gate.
6. **Only systemic recipient treatment or a different article is supported.** Keep that result in its own route and population. It does not complete the intended EVLP-only product proof or silently convert this review into a new development program.
7. **Interpretable benefit is absent, exposure creates an unacceptable liability, or usable rights and economics are unattractive.** Stop or narrow the affected product/financial claim in proportion to the evidence. A clinically helpful but unretained contribution may suit a different objective; it is not automatically an attractive ddiscov investment. Waiting remains available.
