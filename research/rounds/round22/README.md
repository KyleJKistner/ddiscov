## What the package adds

- **Human study provenance:** a primary 2019 author thesis supplies eight-pair exposure/replenishment, repeated-observation and missingness detail. It is not automatically the full 2026 article's methods or independent replication. The five-donor patent pilot is not additive without provenance.
- **Functional route clarity:** the 2018 cold recombinant study includes a storage-solution rinse and retained graft AAT at four hours. A published 2023 pig result concerns repeated systemic recipient treatment and early oxygen recovery. The 2025 AAT-deficient recipient study has different enrollment/comparison sets and remains observational.
- **An exact qualification question:** nominal protein, immunoreactive antigen and functional inhibition are distinct; the pig 3 mg/mL versus 0.5 mg/mL discrepancy remains unexplained. Product labels, physical perfusate effects and replacement policy constrain interpretation without proving that the functional signal is false.
- **Specific rights and product boundaries:** AU/KR grants were already recorded; original granted-claim inspection is added here. US status remains unresolved. IV biological-product precedent, platform labeling and Canadian trial metadata do not establish an approved or commercially controlled EVLP product.
- **Claim-proportionate value:** temporary or tissue-retained protection can support a valuable finite processing intervention. Unique dual-NE/PR3 mechanism, completely drug-free tissue and permanent repair are not universal value gates. Practical/clinical advantage and a retained contribution still matter.

These are refinements of published evidence. No new biological observations, numerical financial EV estimate, allocation or S-tier promotion is claimed.
