**Evidence date: 4 November 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Retrieval: 5 November 2026 UTC.** Existing program `AAT_EVLP` / `BE-N01`. Baseline commit `2db430cd752292d82a6e6a94bd7b08cf2ebb3614`; tree `7ba52d7ed428635bcf3a7f338c0ca4cc5ce75766`.

## Decision

**Retain the conditional formulation/potency/process qualification option and close this bounded functional-evidence pass.** The independent review's inherited rank seven prices that initial qualification itself: **US$30–100k / two-to-four months**, excluding scarce organs and transplant validation. Qualification has not become an inferior substitute for an originally priced human-organ efficacy study. Its value depends on whether the result enables a useful and affordable subsequent graft decision and a contribution whose value can be retained. The source review supplies neither a quote nor permission to execute that work.

The literature supports continued interest in AAT's organ-protective biology. It does not yet establish the commercial proposition that a specified warm-EVLP product increases usable grafts or improves transplant outcomes. The most useful new retrievals are a version-specific author thesis, the published 2023 recipient-treatment letter, and the full 2025 recipient-cohort methods. Those documents sharpen attribution and denominators; they do not constitute new experiments or independent human-organ replication.

## Human-organ evidence and report lineage

The **2026 article abstract** describes eight rejected donor lung blocks split into randomized treated/control lungs, assessed during 12-hour EVLP. It reports better oxygenation/compliance and less perfusate loss/edema. The inspected abstract does not supply exact preparation, dose, numerical effect sizes, completion accounting, or transplant outcomes. Its claim concerns ex vivo physiology, not a demonstrated increase in transplanted grafts. [H22-S01]

The **2020 conference abstract** likewise reports eight split donor blocks and 12-hour EVLP, with overlapping investigators and endpoints. It supplies estimated PAP and PVR differences of −0.4 mmHg (p=.04) and −30.4 dyn·s·cm⁻⁵ (p=.01). It is a likely antecedent report, not a separately verified eight-donor replication. [H22-S02]

The newly read **2019 Mariscal de Alba thesis** is a primary authored document hosted on Academia. Chapter 5 specifies Zemaira, nominal 10 mg/mL in 1.5 L Steen, with replacement perfusate containing or lacking AAT. It reports eight pairs, incomplete longitudinal observations and mixed-model analysis. The companion JSON preserves the recovered model estimates and source conflicts. An official university facsimile and final-article correspondence were not established. These details belong to the thesis version; they cannot silently fill the 2026 article's missing methods. [H22-S03]

The **patent's five-donor pilot** is another report requiring participant provenance. It describes split paired lungs and case-specific outcomes with nominal 10 mg/mL AAT. Independence from the later eight-pair work is unverified. It must not be added to the eight to produce thirteen unique donors. Its placebo and dilution descriptions do not establish physical matching or isolated antiprotease causation. Future-tense clinical examples are proposals, not completed treatment results. [H22-S04]

**Counting rule:** a donor pair is the biological block. Two lungs, serial physiological readings, multiple tissue sections and cytokines do not create additional independent donors. Treat the thesis/2020/2026 material conservatively as a probable common research lineage unless a participant-level provenance record establishes otherwise. That is an inference from the matching designs and investigators, not a verified mapping of every specimen.

### What the recovered methods change

Missing longitudinal observations matter because stopping a run after organ failure can make later observed averages describe surviving lungs. A repeated-measures model handles correlation; it does not by itself establish that failure-related missingness is ignorable. This review did not refit any model, recover all donor-level data, or infer which arm supplied each missing observation. Complete 12-hour follow-up for every lung remains unverified.

The recovered preparation and replenishment information strengthens the reason to qualify delivered activity and mass balance. It does not establish that the commercial preparation was reconstituted identically in every experiment. In particular, the thesis's explicit manufacturer-instruction sentence is in its **cell-culture** subsection. A circuit specification must be supported by the whole-organ methods rather than borrowed from adjacent culture work.

Positive oxygenation and compliance are relevant functional observations. They are not proof of reversal from each treated lung's own baseline, equivalence to a transplantable reference, or prevention of recipient graft dysfunction. A treatment can nevertheless have useful preservation or conditioning value without repairing an established recipient failure.

### Published survival-model result

The 2023 letter's outcome was oxygen independence after extubation, defined using room-air saturation above 85%: **21 ± 9 versus 125 ± 25 minutes, p=.022**. Its three-day study used recipient IV Zemaira before reperfusion and repeated dosing, with saline-volume controls. All animals showed normal activity and appetite after the initial 12 hours. This is a useful early recovery signal within that design; neither the title nor short safety observations establish a mortality advantage or a human safety margin. [H22-S07]

The thesis has additional day-three functional and model-severity limits, and internally differing summary wording. The published letter controls the reported published recovery estimate. The thesis does not become an independent replication of the later publication. [H22-S03, H22-S07]

### Rinse, retained drug and useful benefit

The cold-preservation experiment prevents an overly strong negative claim that no organ-only AAT treatment has ever shown post-transplant benefit. It also prevents calling a rinse complete drug clearance: graft-associated AAT persisted. [H22-S08]

For this program, the relevant product question is a useful graft advantage after a finite, practical processing step with interpretable recipient exposure. Continued action of retained AAT can contribute to that advantage. Complete molecular washout and permanent tissue repair are not universal prerequisites. Conversely, a benefit obtained by repeatedly treating the recipient cannot be attributed to an isolated donor-organ procedure without a separating comparison.

## Human recipient evidence is contextual

The 2025 national AAT-deficiency study's eligible cohort was **447 recipients**. Its main post-transplant comparison was **45 users**—32 continuers plus 13 new users—versus **77 discontinuers**. Early deaths were excluded and entry was delayed to address survivor bias. Unadjusted survival and graft-failure comparisons were nonsignificant; age-adjusted estimates favored use. The estimates remain observational, with incomplete prescription capture and exposure-classification assumptions. This is systemic augmentation in AAT-deficient recipients, not a test of treating generally injured donor lungs on EVLP. [H22-S11]

It would be incorrect either to pool these patients with the eight donor pairs or to claim that the authors ignored survivor bias entirely. The source's effort to address one bias does not establish exchangeability of treated and untreated recipients, or convert an estimated treatment effect into randomized causal evidence.

## What could change the next commitment

An informative initial qualification can answer whether a specified preparation delivers reproducible inhibitory activity in the intended process, whether its physical contribution is understood, and whether the remaining functional question is feasible. It does not need to prove all later clinical outcomes before being worth considering. At the same time, the initial budget does not include obtaining scarce donor organs, transplant validation or demonstrating a commercially useful graft effect.

A subsequent organ comparison should earn its own cost-and-information judgment. A useful difference over an adequate contemporary process can matter even when its mechanism combines protein, formulation and antiprotease effects. If a simpler process delivers the same graft benefit, or the remaining study and product economics become unattractive, the next commitment needs reassessment. Those are decision branches, not operational instructions or newly specified experiments.
