## Decision

**Keep APR-246/AEC as the first conditional small development option, retain GA002 as the leading clinical information priority, and make no new paid-development promotion.** The attached claudin-5 concept does not displace the defined existing articles on the evidence reviewed. This is an ordinal, conditional judgment. No offered transaction or executable development tranche has been shown to beat cash at actual terms.

The seven-program order—APR-246, RM4404, BLU-3797, NDC-9009, mIGF-Gel, exact 8015-P2 and AAT—was inherited through candidate-specific reviews. It is useful decision history, not a freshly priced comparison of interchangeable milestones. Those milestones buy different information. The review below makes those differences explicit rather than granting the old cost bands new authority.

Twelve existing programs are covered: five receive a focused comparative challenge; P2, AAT, enasidenib and ruxolitinib retain bounded scope checks; GA002, vaultAAV and PSAM pain receive focused evidence/access reassessment. No new candidate, patient, experiment or independent replication is counted. The prior source closures remain in force except for the named documentary checks recorded here.

The main text, protocol and appendix remain unread after the bounded access check. The publisher abstract does not replace a full clinical audit. [E01]

The newly recovered author data-sharing statement promises de-identified trial data on ResMan within six months **after trial completion**. It does not promise availability six months after publication, identify a presently downloadable dataset or establish that the trial has completed. This gives the waiting alternative a specific documentary basis. It adds no people or replication. [E02]

**BLU's external comparator has a current sponsor snapshot.** Astellas's 4 November 2026 presentation still places ASP2957 in its pre-proof-of-concept neuromuscular pipeline as of October. The current sponsor study page describes an open-label Phase 1/2 study and recruiting sites. No ASP2957 clinical effect estimate or safety result was recovered. This is new-to-repository status evidence, not a new clinical observation and not evidence that the comparator will fail. [E03–E04]

Aprea's current filing again reports no ongoing company APR-246 trials while describing patent-maintenance, regulatory and closeout costs. The vaultAAV inventor's current account again describes the 2025 acquisition and an existing commercial entity. These are revalidations of the prior capture constraints. Inactivity does not establish abandoned rights; an academic paper does not establish an unclaimed platform. [E05, E07]

## Focused challenge of the existing front group

### APR-246 remains the most defensible first conditional local option

APR has a defined historical article and a small same-disease human lesion signal. Those properties reduce the number of entirely new propositions required before the next question is meaningful. The source denominator remains two children, with repeated sites and later abstracts from the same program. It supplies no causal response probability, complete barrier-restoration proof or validated long-term exposure window.

The useful claim is sustained control of persistent erosions and treatment burden with acceptable tissue integrity and exposure. Permanent off-treatment regeneration is a different claim. A mandatory new preclinical efficacy program would be an unnecessary reset if a suitable clinical-development route could answer the consequential uncertainty more directly.

The principal financial challenge is that commercial continuation must connect to an attainable, capturable contribution. Inexpensive disconfirming information can still create value by preventing larger commitments. Aprea-related interests, the academic use family, transferable product information and clinical data are separate. The fresh filing strengthens the reason not to equate an inactive company trial program with freely available rights. [E05; Round 16]

**Reopening event:** substantive exact-article stability/exposure information, an interpretable clinical correction or new outcome, an accessible model-fit record, or concrete product/rights facts that change the contemplated commitment. If substantial invention is required before useful information can be obtained, APR loses the premise supporting first place.

### RM4404 remains a credible second option with a particularly important endpoint risk

The prior primary-source audit preserves functional and structural mouse findings, while separating stronger direct chemical-model comparisons from weaker surgical-model inferences. The current primary abstract revalidates that this is an existing formulation with animal functional evidence; it does not resolve the earlier denominators, clustering or comparator limitations. [E11; Round 17]

The key uncertainty is whether improved evoked sensitivity represents worthwhile protective corneal function at an acceptable product burden. Blink sensitivity is informative, but cannot alone establish good sensory quality, optical performance or absence of pain. The carrier and exact article must be sufficiently understood for that interpretation. This is a different risk from the claudin proposal's uncertainty over whether its intended molecule exists and has the proposed functional direction.

**Reopening event:** a substantive animal-level correction, useful independent exact-product function, informative product/exposure documentation, or meaningful current comparator results. A full sterile-product development campaign cannot be hidden inside the inherited short qualification range.

### BLU retains the strongest rationale for a systemic human functional bridge

BLU's mouse efficacy remains valuable evidence for an exact compound and a mechanistically coherent target. The fresh publisher check adds no human result. An official Kyoto accepted-manuscript link for the existing Kora model paper was checked once and remained unread; no new force, timing, model-access or cost inference is made from it. [E10, E12]

The highest-value bridge remains useful human muscle function at an interpretable exposure, with systemic risks assessed at the appropriate later commitment. The important financial correction is to preserve the distinction between initial qualification and the subsequent function tranche. A plausible model route does not establish that the full functional answer can be bought at the initial price.

The current ASP2957 program means waiting can generate competitive information without ddiscov financing that trial. Its early clinical status neither invalidates BLU nor establishes a permanently protected population of gene-therapy-ineligible patients. A differentiated oral or repeatable-control product remains a hypothesis requiring its own benefit and exposure case. [E03–E04]

**Reopening event:** substantive usable human-model methods, exact-BLU function/exposure evidence, relevant competitor outcomes, or actual scoped cost/access/rights facts. No new generic model or patent sweep is justified by this update.

### NDC remains an article-specific opportunity with substantial capture dependence

The previous source audit ties the exact article to a disease-relevant cellular program and a sponsor-associated chemistry trail. That is materially more concrete than a new target-binding concept. It still does not establish human clinical benefit or repair of an established lesion. The current review adds no fresh efficacy.

The controlled non-differentiation of the separate SGT-210 Darier program remains important contrary context. It increases the value of distinguishing an article contribution from vehicle and background care; it does not constitute NDC failure. A clinically useful prevention or ongoing-control claim can have value even if a stronger repair claim fails, but it requires its own evidence and economics. [Round 19]

**Reopening event:** exact-product topical exposure/function, an interpretable controlled clinical result, a substantive claim record or commercial contribution fact. A marker- or assembly-only result cannot inherit the original useful-function tranche's financial rationale.

### mIGF-Gel remains a product-comparison option, not a convenience claim established by dosing frequency

The prior preclinical repair and small sensory observations remain. The practical benchmark is a credible topical-insulin product, including relevant human persistent-defect evidence and its confounding limits. Fewer administrations can matter, but only after accounting for repair, handling, tolerability, vision-related burden and product reliability. Similar small-group animal means are not clinical noninferiority.

This opportunity can improve its position through a demonstrable useful product advantage or a much cheaper informative comparison. It can lose its position if the real deliverable is only gel formation, tracer retention or an isolated exposure measurement with costly additional work before a decision changes. [Round 20]

**Reopening event:** exact-product function/exposure, substantive active-comparator data, or rights/product facts that change the practical contribution. No superiority or inferiority to insulin is newly established here.

## Existing reserves and clinical development routes

**Exact P2** retains its exact-stereoisomer human-function question. Mixture, related-compound and animal results remain separated. An available mitochondrial-transport assay can reduce some uncertainty without becoming a demonstrated motor-unit functional bridge. The new stereoisomer patent application was already incorporated in Round 21; no new rights event is claimed.

**AAT** retains a legitimate formulation/potency/process qualification option. The existing donor-pair and transplant evidence must remain attached to product, route and source version. Circuit physiology, recipient systemic treatment and an EVLP-only commercial product are different claims. The value-capture question is whether a new participant retains a useful share beyond incumbent protein and perfusion suppliers. [Round 22]

**Enasidenib/D2HGA2** retains the existing-article indication-development route, with an automatic new-formulation project on hold. No new article is justified merely by absent public infant-use information; it must solve a meaningful documented problem. **Topical ruxolitinib/HHD** retains a controlled clinical-benefit question, with maintenance, rescue burden and meaningful symptoms distinct from lesion endpoints. The external HS trial is not HHD efficacy. Neither closed review was reopened by this pass. [Rounds 14–15]

## The newer information queue

**GA002 stays first for additional consequential clinical information.** Human observations make it a more mature therapeutic lead than vaultAAV's cell-level protection or an unbuilt claudin binder. The evidence remains a three-person uncontrolled signal, and stopping the controller drug does not remove the introduced gene. The now-identified data-release promise supports waiting for usable primary material; it does not justify an automatic company investment at unknown terms. [E01–E02; Round 23]

**VaultAAV stays the leading delivery reserve.** The publication supports cell transduction despite neutralizing serum; clinical eligibility expansion and repeat dosing remain hypotheses. The current inventor account retains the 2025 acquisition and existing commercial entity, with acquirer and terms undisclosed. A new participant needs an attainable field, interest or contribution; repeating the cell result does not establish control of the platform or its economics. [E06–E07]

**PSAM pain stays a distinct preclinical reserve.** The 2023 primary work supports mouse pain-related findings and human sensory-neuron physiology. That is not human treatment efficacy, and acceptable protective/ordinary sensation remains part of the value case. The October 2026 cell-state targeting preprint remains a bibliographic lead: its primary full text was not recovered. Third-party copies of its abstract are not promoted to an audited result or a reason to move its rank. [E08–E09]

## Fit of the attached claudin concept

The attachment proposes a discovery program; it supplies no demonstrated sealing construct, exact-product efficacy or verified commercial package. Its potential advantage therefore depends on several unresolved scientific and product steps preceding the questions already reachable with APR, RM, BLU and other defined articles. The current review finds no basis for allocating development money to it ahead of those conditional options.

That judgment does not assert that an early invention can never have higher EV. It says that an attractive mechanism and a large disease market do not establish the value of the next purchasable decision. A concrete, informative and inexpensive opportunity could change the comparison later. The parent lead audit governs the exact biological, competitive and rights corrections; no new operational design or simulation is supplied here.

## Decision boundary

Keep the inherited queue as a conditional prior, use the new source facts to improve the dependency record, and retain cash when no sufficiently informative, affordable and capturable commitment is defined. This is not proof of negative EV for the programs. It is a refusal to convert unknown transaction terms, broad scientific promise or old planning ranges into an invented return.

No outreach, purchases, recruitment, experiments, simulations, material receipt or applications occurred. No new automatic monitoring task, development commitment or funding approval was created.
