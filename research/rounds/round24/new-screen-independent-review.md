## Portfolio and cost interpretation

Pyridoxine availability may make some knowledge acquisition inexpensive, but availability is neither a controlled efficacy result nor an investable exclusive product. The proposed precision subgroup should remain R222Q unless another genotype has its own evidence. A future commercialization thesis needs a concrete capturable contribution and terms; lack of those terms does not by itself prove negative EV.

ATP1A3 editing and VS-X4 cannot inherit the old small-molecule/topical qualification budget bands. Those old bands are unquoted analyst assumptions with different article, rights, manufacturing, delivery, and clinical scope. A new lead may be a stronger scientific story while still needing more expensive or less decisive next commitments. Conversely, uncertainty does not prohibit a cheap desk check that could resolve a decisive question. Keep information priority, scientific promise, and capital allocation distinct.
