The parent replaced it with a defined starting article and scoped qualification question. The current wording preserves the real advantage—specificity and existing evidence—without implying received material, qualified exposure, transferred models or execution readiness.

2. **Information can have value by preventing investment.** The earlier existing-candidate paragraph made a biological proof's value depend too categorically on eventual capturable commercialization. The parent now distinguishes a capturable commercial continuation from inexpensive disconfirming information that prevents larger commitments. These statements are supported by C04. The report therefore uses BRUNELLO to establish an occupied clinical-development field, while retaining the limitations of noninferiority, sponsor attribution and safety imbalances.

These revisions were reread in the integrated files.
