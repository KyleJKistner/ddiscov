# Claudin 5 sealing antibody concept for diabetic macular edema

Evidence cutoff: 8 November 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Candidate `R24-L01`.

## Decision

**Retain as an early discovery concept on hold. It does not displace the existing conditional development queue or justify a paid discovery commitment on the evidence recovered.** The disease rationale is credible, but the attachment does not identify a demonstrated sealing molecule. It proposes a biparatopic single-domain antibody format whose required activity, access, durability and useful clinical differentiation remain unestablished. This is a different decision from independently qualifying an existing molecule with a published functional result.

The most consequential changes are a much narrower novelty claim, an active barrier-restoration competitive field, and removal of the assumption that linker length alone makes the proposal a cheap, decisive project. The original document is a hypothesis source, not experimental evidence. Its raw contents are not republished here. [C01]

This review is investment and evidence assessment only. No outreach, purchases, recruitment, experiments, simulations, material receipt or applications were performed. No operational biological instructions are introduced.

## What supports the idea

Arima and colleagues' 2020 study links inflammatory claudin-5 redistribution with retinal leakage that was not restored by VEGF blockade alone in its mouse model. The abstract also reports ROCK-directed stabilization and limited human combination-treatment observations. This is relevant target/pathway support; it does not test a direct claudin-5 sealing antibody. The study's patient observations and experimental models must not be collapsed into clinical validation of the proposed VHH. [C02]

The commercially coherent intended population is persistent, vision-relevant DME despite appropriate current care. However, persistent edema does not establish one uniform claudin-5 defect, complete VEGF suppression, or reversible vision loss. A future product would need a defined clinical contribution and treatment context. A mechanism different from VEGF inhibition can support a hypothesis of complementarity, but actual incremental benefit remains a separate question.

Merck's report is sponsor-presented trial evidence, not approval or a head-to-head result against this proposed VHH. It reports no secondary-endpoint superiority to ranibizumab; all arms received monthly treatment in year one, so this report does not establish a treatment-burden advantage. [C04] Surrozen's subsequent announcement reports $95.1 million in gross financing proceeds supporting multiple programs and operations. That shows a financed competitor; it is neither the price of this lead nor a minimum development budget. [C06]

### Leakage and vision are different endpoints

DRCR Protocol U randomized 129 eyes from 116 adults with persistent DME. Adding dexamethasone to ranibizumab improved retinal thickness but did not improve mean visual acuity over continued ranibizumab at 24 weeks; pressure-related effects also mattered. This does not predict failure for claudin-5. It establishes why an anatomic or barrier readout cannot be treated as the clinical value proposition. [C12]

A future useful product might improve vision, preserve vision, reduce consequential treatment burden, or help a clearly defined inadequately served subgroup. It need not be a permanent cure. The intended claim determines the necessary evidence.

## Product and financing issues that remain real

**No exact asset is established.** The document supplies no identified functional binder pair, experimentally supported sealing activity or qualified product. General extracellular claudin binding and a proposed multivalent format are insufficient to transfer another molecule's activity to this one. Published structures can constrain a hypothesis, but a favorable drawing or model would not establish useful function.

**Residence time is an unresolved liability, not a measured failure.** Published ocular pharmacokinetic work supports a relationship between macromolecular size and ocular residence. It does not provide this hypothetical product's half-life or clinical dosing interval. Enlarging or modifying the format would create a product whose access and performance need separate support. No residence-time prediction or simulation was run here. [C13]

**Retinal access is unproven.** Gut or renal disease-associated access observations do not establish delivery from the vitreous to the relevant retinal endothelial binding sites. The proposed disease-dependent access and self-limiting exposure are hypotheses. They cannot currently be credited as targeting, safety or durability advantages.

**Rights remain a separate gate.** The independent audit identifies direct claudin antibody patent context. A novel application, an apparently different format and a public academic paper do not establish claim coverage, freedom to operate, available rights or ownership. A new asset could create a valuable proprietary position, but none has been established for this proposal. The search is a landscape check, not a legal freedom-to-operate opinion. The parent also inspected granted US10882906B2: claim 1 defines specified antibody classes through their binding-region sequences, so its title cannot be read as a blanket claim to all claudin-5 binders. The anti-claudin-2 grant US11078271B2 includes barrier-enhancing examples and separately defined antibody claims. Its examples are not counted as independent replication of the associated paper. These observations establish relevant prior art without resolving a future VHH’s legal position. [T05, T06]

**The next dollar must buy a defined decision.** A new binder-discovery program cannot inherit APR, RM or BLU's historical small-tranche assumptions. The relevant comparison includes the probability of obtaining an interpretable result, the remaining money and time after that result, the product's likely clinical contribution, and the economic share ddiscov could retain. There is no defensible numerical EV estimate without an actual scope, price, evidence trajectory and rights position.

No annual treatment-cost estimate is assigned to the hypothetical product: dose, duration, price, reimbursement and treatment combinations are unknown. No vendor quote, investment terms, asset purchase or license opportunity was verified. These missing inputs prevent a priced superiority claim over cash; they do not prove that the target has negative scientific or financial value.

## Fit with current candidates

The concept belongs in an early discovery reserve, below the existing leaders for immediate paid development consideration. APR-246, RM4404, BLU-3797, NDC-9009, mIGF-Gel, exact 8015-P2 and AAT each already have a more specific intervention and a previously articulated functional or qualification question. Their own rights and execution problems remain open; those problems do not make an undefined new molecule equivalent to a defined starting article with a scoped qualification question.

GA002 is a higher priority for clinical evidence interpretation, while VaultAAV is a distinct delivery-evidence and rights question. Neither is automatically a better investment at any price, and neither validates this claudin concept by analogy. The [existing-candidate reassessment](existing-candidate-reassessment.md) and [new-candidate screen](new-candidate-screen.md) compare the work now available without treating old allocations as binding.

### Evidence that could change this decision

- A documented exact claudin-5 asset with reproducible barrier-improving activity, rather than only binding or effects borrowed from another claudin.
- Credible target-access and exposure evidence appropriate to the intended ocular product, with a plausible practical advantage or incremental patient benefit.
- A materially differentiated position relative to vascular-stability competitors and an obtainable economic interest.
- A bounded, priced information opportunity that could change a specific commitment at lower risk-adjusted cost than the alternatives and waiting.

These are decision conditions, not an authorized development plan. No additional generic literature loop or speculative simulation is required to preserve the concept. Reopen the bounded review when a substantive new asset, result, structural constraint, rights fact or scoped cost changes the decision.
