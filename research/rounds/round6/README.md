# Round 6 — a fundable next proof and stronger challengers

**Evidence cut: 4 November 2026.** This sprint starts from main `44fe86f9b5d22914ce366bcef9f6c3667b1e14b2`. It adds **six new exact intervention/use screens**, updates **three existing priority candidates**, and separately clarifies **two clinical comparators**. Two earlier continuation packages are integrated into the current decisions without being counted as new research here.

## Decisions

**Recommend an initial BLU3797 material/model qualification project, with a conditional human-function comparison.** The scientific case is strong enough to support this finite early milestone. It does not need a prior human efficacy result to justify asking the human-function question. A larger development investment requires the result of that work and a relevant exposure window. [Funding milestone](round6_blu_milestone.md)

**Upgrade enasidenib's formulation assessment.** The original public supplement documents actual sprinkle use in a child with the exact IDH2-R140Q disease. It also provides a clearer trajectory of improvement in established severe cardiomyopathy in the other child. The blanket pediatric-formulation hold is no longer justified. Exact infant formulation/exposure, chronic tolerability and a distinct contribution alongside an existing program remain open. [Reserve challenge](round6_reserve_challenge.md)

**Add topical ruxolitinib/HHD as a new independent-validation priority.** A defined marketed 1.5% cream and established-lesion human responses make a controlled clinical proof concrete. The nine-patient preprint remains abstract-only; its response rate and independence cannot be reconstructed from that abstract. Useful ongoing lesion control can justify development without permanent off-treatment cure. [New-use screen](round6_new_leads.md)

No candidate is promoted to S-tier. That is an asset-level assessment, not a negative decision about funding every early experiment. The [current shortlist](../../../decisions/current-shortlist.md) and [selection policy](../../../decisions/selection-policy.md) govern priorities.

## What changed

| Question | New evidence | Development consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Does PIK3C2B inhibition have a relevant mitochondrial liability? | The October 2026 mitochondrial-fusion paper reports cardiac harm after combined C2α/C2β deletion. Its underlying thesis also reports fragmentation after individual β depletion in transformed cells. These belong to one project. | Add a focused mitochondrial-function comparison and measure cellular C2α/VPS34 sparing. Neither dual knockout nor morphology establishes exact-BLU toxicity. |
| Is there counterevidence supporting a window? | Separate vascular data retain function after individual α or β perturbation; kinase-dead β mice had reassuring long-term reported histology, but the recovered supplement does not supply its animal denominator. | Keep tissue/endpoint-specific redundancy and catalytic versus protein-loss distinctions. A functional window remains plausible and measurable. |
| Can the juvenile-to-human bridge be made more specific? | An adult local Mtm1-deletion model exists. An orthogonal DNM2 rescue can preserve resting breathing despite reduced diaphragm force. A 2026 adult clinical baseline report separates age, sex, function and tissue biomarkers. | Adult maintenance, respiratory capacity and human endpoint selection require explicit claims. No new exact-BLU adult/respiratory efficacy is inferred. |
| Is enasidenib's pediatric article merely hypothetical? | A child in the original exact-disease report actually received sprinkle for three months; the supplement gives dose, switch and follow-up. Trial records define a formulation-development route. | Remove the generic formulation-absence hold. Qualify the intended infant article and contribution rather than rediscovering a first pediatric program. |
| Is there a concrete oral downstream CoA precursor? | CoA-Z used 4′-phosphopantetheine in an oral/gastrostomy preparation. Posted PKAN trial results and protocol expose statistical and analysis-concordance limits. | Add a chemically distinct comparator to PPCS work. Do not transfer PKAN results to PPCS cardiac efficacy or equate PPanSH with pantethine. |
| Which new lead has the clearest local clinical proof? | Ruxolitinib/HHD has a verified monotherapy case, an abstract-level nine-patient signal and a manufactured cream. KB111 has five enrolled patients and ongoing dosing as of 2 November. | Prioritize controlled lesion response, recurrence, exposure and a meaningful new-product contribution. Active competition is relevant without invalidating the scientific proof. |

Each claim is tied to the primary sources, exact article and access limits in the reports below. Different targets, constructs, disease populations and formulations do not inherit each other's efficacy or toxicity.

The clinical audit and BLU milestone received independent review. Primary supplements, registry records and selected figures were inspected; access failures and unresolved inconsistencies remain explicit. A source's abstract, full text, supplement and registry are distinguished.

The 622 original coverage records, generated concepts, prior rounds and two continuation packages are retained. No new experiments, received materials, outreach or spending are represented. Public source files were analyzed as intermediates; full copyrighted papers are not redistributed with this sprint. Git object and branch verification accompanies publication.
