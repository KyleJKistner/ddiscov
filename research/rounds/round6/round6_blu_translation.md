# Round 6 — BLU3797: mitochondrial liabilities, adult disease and respiratory proof

**Evidence reviewed 4 November 2026.** Base: verified `ddiscov` main `44fe86f9b5d22914ce366bcef9f6c3667b1e14b2`. This extends round 5 and both subsequent continuation folders. It reports public-source research, not experimental results, obtained materials or commercial clearance. The structured companion is `round6_blu_translation.json`.

## Decision

**Keep BLU3797 as a leading staged proof; do not promote it to S-tier.** A newly published mitochondrial-fusion mechanism changes the appropriate selectivity and functional checks. It does not establish BLU3797 cardiotoxicity, and there is counterevidence against assuming that selective PIK3C2B inhibition must be systemically intolerable. The mature-muscle and respiratory gaps can now be stated more concretely.

The most useful portfolio change is to make **cellular C2α sparing and preservation of mitochondrial function at the muscle-effective exposure** explicit in the existing proof. This should be a focused companion to the force experiment, not an expanding collection of disconnected safety assays. A morphology change alone is a signal to resolve, not an automatic fatal contradiction.

## 1. A newly relevant mitochondrial mechanism, with the isoform question preserved

The October 8, 2026 *Science* paper reports PI(3,4)P2-containing Golgi vesicles at mitochondrial fusion sites, fragmentation following loss of C2α/C2β, and heart failure after **cardiomyocyte-specific combined C2α/C2β deletion**. The accessible abstract does not provide animal counts or settle the phenotype of either cardiac single knockout. The publisher full text/supplement was inaccessible during this review. [S01]

An official Cambridge repository supplied coauthor Mayuko Segawa's January 2025 thesis. Relevant figures were inspected visually as well as through extracted text. This is the **same research lineage** as the 2026 paper, not a second replication or a substitute for auditing its final figures. [S02]

### What the underlying experiments actually distinguish

| Thesis evidence | Perturbation and observation | Denominator and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Figures 4-10, 4-15 and 4-16 | HeLa, COS7 and A549: individual C2α, individual C2β and combined siRNA depletion each produce fragmentation. | Each figure reports at least 30 cells/condition/experiment and three independent experiments. Cells are nested measurements, not independent donors. |
| Figure 4-14 | The add-back series is under **combined α/β depletion**. Combined WT α+β restores morphology; kinase-dead combinations fail. The clearly restored group is the paired WT add-back. | At least 15 cells/condition/experiment; three independent experiments. Mutants are C2α-D1268A and C2β-D1213A. This is not isolated β depletion followed by a β-only catalytic rescue. |

These are transformed-cell morphology experiments, not MTM1-patient muscle force or a selective-inhibitor exposure curve. The thesis acknowledges restricted image-analysis dynamic range and that quantified dual depletion was not worse than each single depletion. Cardiac single-isoform outcome and animal denominator remain unverified. [S02, printed pp.151–161, 187–191]

**Inference for selection.** C2α sparing is worth measuring, but cannot be presumed to eliminate a possible C2β-mediated mitochondrial effect. Conversely, deep protein depletion and combined isoform deletion cannot be translated directly into the consequence of reversible, partial catalytic inhibition. The figure supports a class-II catalytic contribution in its tested context; it does not identify an adverse C2β occupancy threshold.

The mechanistic trade-off is testable: correcting a harmful phosphoinositide compartment in MTM1-deficient muscle may coexist with disturbing another compartment needed for organelle maintenance. A favorable bulk PI3P or p62 measurement cannot answer both questions. This reasoning extends the existing compartment-specific interpretation; the newly added evidence is the mitochondrial-fusion work, not the previously reviewed p62-withdrawal observation.

**Concrete amendment to the proposed proof:** at measured free exposures that restore contractile function, assess mitochondrial organization together with an energetic or fatigue readout in the same qualified muscle system. If a signal appears, distinguish exact-BLU off-target effects from C2β catalytic effects using an appropriately qualified orthogonal intervention and isoform controls. A cellular C2α assay is particularly relevant because the prior exact-compound audit did not recover a cellular C2α measurement. Do not substitute an isolated biochemical selectivity ratio for the intact-cell answer.

## 2. There is counterevidence to a blanket systemic-toxicity conclusion

### Vascular redundancy is directly tested, but is tissue-specific

Islam et al. examined constitutive smooth-muscle C2α deletion, global C2β deletion and combined loss. In 10–12-week male mice, no blood-pressure difference was detected after either individual knockout; combined loss lowered it. Figure 3 counts were α control/KO **10/10**, β WT/KO **10/9**, and β-only control/double KO **13/10**. Double-knockout aortic-ring contraction was impaired, with **n=3/group**. Human aortic smooth-muscle cells retained ionomycin-induced contraction after either individual siRNA; combined depletion impaired it. Figure 6 does not resolve independent donor counts. [S03]

This is a different experimental dataset, but Aki/Takuwa investigators overlap the newer fusion study: it should not be called independent-laboratory replication. It also does not establish selective β-only cardiac safety. Its main contribution is that functional redundancy depends on the tissue and endpoint. Normal vascular contraction and abnormal mitochondrial shape are not logically inconsistent. The article's data statement offers underlying data on request; public figures are available, but a deposited individual-level dataset was not recovered. No request was made. [S03]

**Inference:** spare C2α to avoid unnecessarily testing a dual-isoform mechanism. Do not use the vascular result to waive a β-specific mitochondrial check, or use the double-knockout result to reject all selective β inhibitors.

### The chronic catalytic-loss reassurance is useful but less quantified than its headline

The 2015 protein-retaining C2β-D1212A knock-in study reports normal viability, growth and fertility and no abnormalities in histological examination of 38 tissues through 20 months. The supplement was retrieved from Europe PMC and Table S1 visually inspected. It lists organs, including heart, brain and skeletal muscle; it does **not** provide an animal count, age-stratified lesion table or functional cardiac/respiratory measurements. The histology denominator was not recovered from the main text or Document S1. [S04]

**Inference:** this is real counterweight against assuming catastrophic consequences of all systemic catalytic suppression. It is not a quantified chronic safety margin. A lifelong kinase-dead allele allows adaptation; healthy animals do not settle diseased-tissue sensitivity; normal histology does not establish energetic reserve, electrophysiological tolerance or ventilation capacity. The existing MTM1 genetic catalytic-rescue evidence also remains favorable and more disease-proximate than a transformed-cell hazard observation. Its round-5 genotype and force distinctions continue to apply.

## 3. Adult muscle offers a more direct test of the developmental alternative

Joubert et al. used a desmin-promoter AAV-Cre vector to delete Mtm1 locally in muscles of **three-month-old conditional mice**. The primary abstract reports muscle weakness, atrophy, organelle displacement and T-tubule abnormalities. This establishes an adult maintenance requirement and provides a model route for interrogating mature muscle. It does not report PIK3C2B inhibition or BLU3797 rescue. [S05]

**Access and denominator limit:** the primary abstract and bibliographic record were verified. The full publisher PDF could not be retrieved. An indexed article-text copy exposed further force values, but these are deliberately not used here because complete figure/denominator verification was not achieved. No animal count is assigned to the abstract-level result.

**Inference for the next milestone.** Adult-local onset would reduce the confounding between treatment benefit and normal juvenile maturation. It would not recreate years of congenital disease, respiratory dependence, systemic MTM1 deficiency or liver involvement. Before using this model to falsify the target, it must show a quantified deficit and a working delayed positive intervention. A negative result in irreversible or poorly qualified tissue would be an assay/model result rather than decisive target evidence.

This model route should remain an optional orthogonal bridge after the more immediately decision-relevant human functional qualification. It is not a newly acquired line, an established off-the-shelf assay, or a reason to make an investigator reply the selection gate.

## 4. A respiratory claim needs respiratory capacity, even when motor rescue looks strong

Cowling et al.'s DNM2-reduction study provides a useful endpoint benchmark. At six months, Mtm1-null/Dnm2-heterozygous mice showed resting breathing measurements similar to controls. Yet the same results section reports reduced **diaphragm specific maximal force**, despite no significant difference in the force-frequency relationship. The Figure 7 legend says at least five mice; methods say three to five/group for plethysmography and diaphragm-strip testing. Retain this inconsistency instead of assigning a fabricated exact denominator. These were germline double mutants; untreated Mtm1-null animals did not provide a six-month disease control. [S06]

**Inference:** an apparently normal resting breathing pattern does not demonstrate normal respiratory reserve. Survival, hanging, limb strength and diaphragm force are related but noninterchangeable endpoints. This paper cannot establish exact-BLU respiratory efficacy, nor can its six-month result be relabelled as delayed treatment.

For a later animal bridge supporting a respiratory product claim, require direct respiratory-muscle output or a validated breathing-capacity endpoint alongside relevant exposure and tolerability. Whole-body motor improvement alone remains insufficient. The human proof can still earn advancement on a meaningful muscle-function result; respiratory differentiation should be earned by its own subsequent evidence.

## 5. Revised advance, hold and redirect decisions

These are proposed decision criteria, not authorized or completed experiments. No universal numerical margin is invented from genetic allele dosage or cell counts.

| Decision | Evidence needed | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Advance the bounded proof** | Reproducible established-deficit force benefit at authenticated, measured free exposure; cellular isoform profile interpretable; no corresponding loss of useful mitochondrial/contractile function in the tested window. | Continue exact-BLU development diligence and qualify an orthogonal exposure/article comparison. This alone does not clear systemic safety or confer S-tier status. |
| **Resolve an ambiguous mitochondrial signal** | Fragmentation without functional loss, uncertain target coverage, or an effect only at substantially higher exposure. | Establish its exposure, duration, reversibility and functional relevance before changing the portfolio rank. |
| **Redirect the molecule** | BLU impairs energetic or contractile function at beneficial exposures while a more selective, orthogonal C2β intervention retains benefit. | Treat the problem as an article/selectivity issue; pursue a better defined selective comparator or chemistry project. |
| **Reconsider the target/product window** | Orthogonal catalytic C2β interventions reproducibly cause useful-function loss at the same exposures/engagement needed for rescue, in a qualified disease system. | Narrow the required tissue distribution, inhibition depth or product population; stop the present systemic thesis if no useful separation remains. |
| **Withhold the respiratory claim** | Motor rescue without respiratory-muscle capacity evidence. | Continue only with a muscle-function claim while the respiratory bridge remains open. |

The original human force/model qualification, material reconstruction, brain/liver exposure and commercial-differentiation gates remain. This review adds a focused challenge; it does not convert every mechanistic observation into a mandatory pre-proof development program.

## 6. Bounded negative searches and items not counted as new

Searches combined exact BLU3797 with respiratory, diaphragm, cardiac, mitochondrial, brain and muscle pharmacokinetic terms. They recovered the already reviewed 2026 JCI Insight article and derivative records, not an additional exact-BLU functional respiratory dataset or timed free-muscle/free-brain dataset. Searches for the new Science DOI and supplementary/raw-data records did not recover an accessible final supplement. This is an access/search limit, not proof that no such information exists.

The following are explicitly carried forward, **not rediscovered findings**: juvenile delayed PIK3C2B genetics; the catalytic-knock-in MTM1 rescue; BLU withdrawal despite p62 improvement; VPS34 genetic antitarget evidence; the ten-boy ASPIRO biopsy mismatch; ASP2957 eligibility; the previously reviewed CNS susceptibility; AZD8055; and TAM4MTM. Clinical natural-history and liver updates belong to the parallel round-6 workstream.
