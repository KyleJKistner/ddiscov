# Round 6: a bounded BLU3797 funding decision

**4 November 2026. Scientific planning recommendation; no spending or experimental execution.** The purpose is to identify the next valuable development result with a finite scope. The existing human-proof document supplies the eventual evidence standard; this memo stages the work required to reach it.

## Recommendation

**BLU3797 warrants an initial material-and-model qualification project, with a conditional human-function comparison.** This is an affirmative early research recommendation. The missing human efficacy result is the question the project should answer, not a prerequisite to funding every preliminary step. A larger development investment would require stronger function, exposure and product evidence.

The scientific rationale is the conjunction of catalytic target genetics, exact-compound animal activity, a defined chemical article and concrete human model precedents. The limiting uncertainty is whether useful muscle benefit can be separated from unwanted effects of inhibiting lipid-kinase functions in the relevant biological context. Newly recovered mitochondrial evidence makes that last question more specific; it does not by itself establish a fatal contradiction. See the [translation review](round6_blu_translation.md), [clinical anchor](round6_clinical_translation.md) and [prior human proof](../round5/blu3797-human-proof.md).

## 1. Separate the decisions being funded

| Decision | Evidence appropriate to that decision | Present BLU3797 position |
|---|---|---|
| Is another literature search worth doing? | A primary source could change target, article, product or model selection | Yes for specific unresolved evidence; low value for repeated restatements of known mouse markers |
| Is material/model qualification worth funding? | Defined article, plausible reconstructible/model route, strong enough rationale, bounded outputs and failure conditions | Scientifically justified; no received material, operator quote or actual price established |
| Is a decisive human-function comparison ready to execute? | Qualified material and disease assay, interpretable delayed-treatment question, appropriate controls and analysis | Conditional on qualification; this work has not executed it |
| Is a larger translational program justified? | Useful reproducible human function, exposure linkage, relevant tissue window and product differentiation | Not yet established |
| Is there a financeable standalone asset/company? | The preceding evidence plus a concrete development/product contribution, execution resources and value-capture route | Not established by the literature review alone |

These statuses can coexist. A favorable early research decision does not assert that a completed clinical asset already exists. An existing sponsor, generic molecule or uncertain commercial term does not erase scientific value; each changes what an independent project could contribute.

## 2. Stage the work so uncertainty does not create an unlimited checklist

### A. Article and assay qualification

Start with one clearly specified disease/control system and independently authenticated exact BLU3797. Establish dissolved exposure, a usable target-engagement measurement, a functional disease deficit and the response of an appropriate control. The existing p.L463S, p.E48* and independently constructed null routes are alternatives or later complements, not interchangeable versions of one model.

A disease/isogenic pair is the preferred causal starting point where it can be qualified. An available nonisogenic patient line can serve an exploratory purpose with an explicitly narrower inference. Multiple wells or clones do not create independent donors. An initial result in one background should determine whether investing in additional backgrounds is worthwhile; it cannot establish broad genotype efficacy.

Specify the target-engagement and relevant selectivity problem at this stage. PIK3C2A and VPS34 sparing require appropriate measurements rather than an inherited kinome score. The new mitochondrial findings also justify testing the consequences of selective C2β perturbation itself; alpha sparing cannot logically remove every possible on-target effect. Measurements must distinguish depletion, catalytic inhibition and compound-specific effects.

**Advance:** exact article and assay are interpretable, the relevant deficit is reproducible, and the planned comparison can discriminate outcomes. **Redirect:** the material fails authentication or the assay cannot address the intended functional claim. Failure here does not establish that PIK3C2B is a bad target.

### B. An exploratory post-deficit function comparison

Ask whether BLU improves an established deficit at measured exposure, with the baseline trajectory and biological units retained. Track useful active force and fatigue alongside target engagement; preserve healthy/corrected-tissue effects. A molecular or morphological change alone does not establish the proposed functional benefit.

A delayed functional positive control is particularly important before interpreting a negative result as failure of a reversal hypothesis. If a model never responds to any delayed corrective intervention, use the result to question assay modifiability before rejecting the target. Distinguish recovery, ongoing preservation and benefit during maturation.

**Advance:** a credible functional signal supports replication and a more complete exposure comparison. **Hold or redirect:** the estimate is inconclusive, the effect is limited to a surrogate, the model is unqualified, or adequate target engagement cannot be achieved. A positive exploratory result is a reason to perform confirmation, not a confirmed treatment effect.

### C. Confirm function and resolve the relevant window

Confirm the finding in an independent disease background with a predeclared useful-effect threshold, donor/batch-aware uncertainty, orthogonal target support and practical active comparisons. Establish the dependence on continuing exposure and distinguish an on-treatment benefit from a lasting post-withdrawal effect. Ongoing treatment can be valuable if its repeat-use window is adequate.

Connect the muscle-effective exposure to relevant cardiac, neuronal and disease-liver effects. Begin with focused functional assays responsive to the identified mechanisms. Equal free concentrations characterize intrinsic sensitivity; tissue distribution and active metabolites are required for systemic interpretation. A limb-muscle or cultured-muscle result alone does not support a respiratory claim. The adult/diaphragm evidence described in this round should guide a later, explicit translational bridge.

**Advance:** confirmation supports a usable benefit–exposure relationship and a defined population/product proposition. **Change article:** target-specific benefit survives but BLU has inadequate exposure, or relevant tissue exposure/distribution supports overlap with a harmful effect. A culture overlap with unknown distribution remains unresolved. **Downgrade the target hypothesis:** appropriately engaged, target-specific perturbation fails in independently qualified, modifiable disease systems. None of these branches is decided by a missing owner email.

## 3. What can and cannot be made concrete without outreach

The public package identifies the exact compound, the joined chemical route, candidate model sources and controls, decision-relevant outputs, interpretation rules and alternatives. It is sufficient to specify what should be priced and qualified. It does not establish current supplier stock, received-lot behavior, an operator's capacity, permission for a particular commercial use or a dependable budget. Those are distinct execution facts.

Do not label an article “unavailable” when the unresolved fact is stock, reconstruction, model qualification or commercial scope. Equally, a catalog entry should not be described as an execution-ready program. A quote or commercial agreement may later matter for execution; it is not a substitute for the scientific selection made here.

## 4. Stop the literature loop when it stops changing this decision

Further desktop work should resolve a named gate: a new functional result, a corrected exposure or selectivity measurement, a model capable of answering the question, an exact adverse finding, or a material competitor/product fact. Repeating known caveats or accumulating more markers should not postpone the finite qualification decision.

The funding judgment should be reconsidered when a better challenger offers a more informative and tractable proof, when a concrete article-level contradiction appears, or when qualified functional evidence changes the expected value of the target. The project should be able to terminate cleanly, redirect chemistry or narrow its intended use without treating any of those outcomes as a reason to hide the result.
