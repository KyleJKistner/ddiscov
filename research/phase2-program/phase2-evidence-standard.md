# Evidence standard for a Phase II candidate

Decision framework, 8 November 2026. This is a proposed selection discipline; it does not certify any candidate's probability. Read the [selection policy](../../decisions/selection-policy.md) for the financial decision.

## Define the event before claiming better odds

Evaluate “greater than chance” against two separate thresholds: an evidence-supported improvement over a relevant historical reference class, and an absolute probability above 50%. Report which, if either, the evidence supports. Neither is the statistical significance threshold in one trial; beating a low historical base rate does not establish a more-likely-than-not outcome.

Record separately:

| Event | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Clinical proof of concept | Prespecified clinically meaningful efficacy, acceptable safety and interpretable uncertainty in the intended population at a feasible dose. A biomarker change alone qualifies only for a justified biomarker claim. |
| Phase II progression | Program progresses to Phase III or the explicitly specified next registrational stage. Financing and sponsor decisions can affect this outcome even after useful clinical evidence. |
| Success through Phase II from today | Reaches the intended Phase II evaluation and meets the defined success event; includes earlier translation, product, safety and execution attrition. |
| Investor recovery | Actual retained cash returned by a stated horizon exceeds cumulative investor cash contributions, net of relevant costs and sharing. A marked-up financing valuation is a separate outcome. |

Use conditional probabilities only when the conditioning is explicit. For example, probability of reaching Phase II multiplied by probability of success conditional on reaching it equals success-through-Phase-II probability when the definitions and conditioning match. Do not multiply unrelated stage averages or genetic/biomarker associations. Financial recovery also requires rights, financing and a monetization path; clinical success is insufficient.

## Historical anchors, not a candidate calculator

The [BIO/Informa/QLS report](https://go.bio.org/rs/490-EHZ-999/images/ClinicalDevelopmentSuccessRates2011_2020.pdf) (February 2021; 2011–2020 observations; checked 8 November 2026) reports a **28.9% Phase II transition rate across 4,933 transitions**. Programs with patient-preselection biomarkers had **46.3%** (149 transitions), versus **28.3%** without. This is sponsor progression, not the probability that a randomly chosen trial meets its primary endpoint. Company-sponsored registration programs and the report's inclusion rules differ from small investigator-led repurposing studies. Neither the aggregate rate nor the selected subset establishes a 2026 asset probability. These observational strata overlap other favorable features and cannot be stacked as independent advantages.

[Minikel et al., Nature 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07316-0) report an association between genetic support and clinical-development success, with heterogeneity by disease area and stage. That supports inspecting exact target–indication causality and the direction of modulation. It does not validate the specific molecule, exposure, reversibility of disease, or a numerical uplift for our candidate.

The primary article's indexed abstract was checked on 8 November 2026; publisher and PMC full-text retrieval did not succeed in this pass. The association stated here is abstract-level evidence, not a fresh audit of its underlying dataset.

[Wong, Siah and Lo](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6409418/) (2018 online / 2019 issue) use different data and estimation approaches and describe missing-data limitations. Comparator selection must record era, indication, modality, stage, endpoint, inclusion rules and ascertainment. Show how conclusions change under a less favorable plausible comparator. Preserve denominator and uncertainty; an attractive selected subgroup is not an unbiased baseline.

## Candidate evidence gates

These gates direct information purchases, not a weighted score whose strengths can cancel a fatal weakness. Each item is **supported**, **uncertain**, or **contradicted**, with its source and decision consequence. An early lead can advance to a bounded information task while remaining uncertain. A nomination for material development requires resolution of the uncertainties that can make the commitment infeasible.

| Gate | Evidence to seek | What changes the decision |
|---|---|---|
| Exact biological match | Human causal evidence, target direction, genotype/pathology and reversible treatment window. Alternatively, replicated clinically relevant human efficacy for a symptomatic claim. | A genotype label or animal rescue alone does not establish a treatment effect; inspect relevant human tissue and mechanism. |
| Exact article and exposure | Identity, formulation, intended route, active species, relevant tissue exposure, feasible dose and duration. | Class-level success must survive the molecule-specific bridge; a local formulation or pediatric population can reset safety/exposure assumptions. |
| Human benefit and replication | Controlled data where feasible; full denominators, effect and uncertainty, independent cohorts, clinically useful endpoints. | Two papers from the same cohort or two relatives do not count as independent replication. Account for natural history, regression to the mean, background care and missingness. |
| Safety margin and product | All-treated safety, dose limitations, interactions, chronic-use needs, manufacturing and supply appropriate to next stage. | Approved-drug status supplies relevant history, not automatic safety for a new route, dose or population. |
| Learnable Phase II design | Recruitable population; justified meaningful effect; endpoint reliability and timing; comparator; blinding/randomization where feasible; exposure/target engagement; missing-data and multiplicity plans. | A positive biomarker without meaningful benefit, a noisy endpoint, or an unreachable cohort can defeat a sound mechanism. |
| Reproducible challenge | Strongest negative study and alternative explanation, independent source extraction and prospective decision rules. | A challenger names the observation that would falsify the leading thesis. Unresolved disagreement is recorded, not voted away. |
| Attainable economics | Supply and data rights, regulatory reliance, commercial interest, all-in next tranche, stopping obligations, prospective payer/partner and follow-on funding. | Patient benefit and investor return are evaluated independently. No price, quoted scope or partner terms means no substantiated return estimate. |

[FDA's March 2019 enrichment guidance](https://www.fda.gov/media/121320/download) supports prospectively justified patient selection and addresses interpretation, generalizability and error control. An enriched design must specify the marker and selection rule before confirmatory testing. Post-hoc responders generate a new hypothesis; independent confirmation is needed. The proposed population, endpoint and design require qualified clinical, statistical and regulatory review before execution.

## How the program learns without manufacturing confidence

Each coordinator proposes one lead and one alternative. The evidence worker extracts sources; the challenge worker independently audits the translation and economics. The coordinator reconciles them and preserves disputes. Root deduplicates shared studies and assets across teams, compares the exact proposed next actions, and chooses the smallest set of information tasks that can change an affordable decision.

For any later numerical forecast, freeze the event, horizon, reference class, evidence cutoff, range and rationale before outcomes are known. Retain stopped and failed leads in the denominator. Track calibration on genuinely subsequent outcomes; do not train and evaluate on the same selected success stories. Ten teams finding the same study is one study. Shared target, indication, assay, sponsor, supply and financing risks remain correlated.

## Nomination rule

A **research lead** merits a specified information task. A **development candidate** additionally needs a defensible clinical program, attainable rights, a priced milestone and financeable continuation under the capital constraints. An **investment recommendation** further requires a comparison of retained cash outcomes against preserving capital.

For every milestone record advance, stop and inconclusive branches before spending. A negative result can justify stopping; an uninterpretable result cannot be relabeled as evidence for efficacy. No current team consensus establishes a candidate as more likely than not to succeed in Phase II or to return capital. Such a claim would require substantially stronger, asset-specific evidence than a first-pass desk review.
