# Proposed target profile and decision gates

Research target, 8 November 2026 America/Los_Angeles. This is a development hypothesis, not an established product profile, treatment recommendation, approved protocol or claim of clinical efficacy. Read [human evidence](human-evidence.md), [product feasibility](product-cmc.md) and [clinical design](clinical-design.md) for the supporting observations and uncertainties.

## Product hypothesis

A reproducible topical erlotinib presentation, used with defined background keratolytic and supportive care, could reduce painful plantar keratoderma sufficiently to improve daily life while avoiding unacceptable local fragility and systemic exposure. Useful benefit during continued treatment is the intended claim; genetic correction, permanent repair and off-treatment cure are not prerequisites.

| Attribute | Initial development target | What cannot be assumed |
|---|---|---|
| Population | Adults with genetically documented KRT16 p.Arg127Cys-associated painful plantar keratoderma and enough persistent burden for an interpretable comparison | All KRT16 variants, all pachyonychia congenita or all inherited PPK have the same response. Broader enrollment is a separately justified expansion, not free extrapolation. |
| Clinical benefit | Meaningful reduction in local pain and treatment burden, with patient function assessed appropriately | Reduction in skin thickness alone establishes patient benefit; improved global mobility is a foot-level randomized outcome. |
| Background care | Stable, prospectively documented keratolytic, debridement, footwear and rescue-care rules appropriate to the comparison | Erlotinib alone reproduces the combination signal, or a change in background care can be ignored. |
| Product identity | Exact, characterized presentation with a valid bridge to the clinical hypothesis and reproducible clinical supply | A stable low-concentration cream, the historical positive ointment and a high-concentration sponsor product are interchangeable. |
| Local therapeutic window | Useful pain/control without worse fissures, erosions, infection or clinically important tissue fragility | Lower callus burden always means healthier or stronger skin. |
| Systemic exposure | A sensitive, fit-for-purpose assessment under relevant skin/use conditions, interpreted with systemic safety history | Below a historical assay's quantitation limit means zero exposure, or healthy-volunteer exposure qualifies fissured disease skin. |
| Administration and burden | A usable presentation whose application burden and care requirements are acceptable to patients | More frequent application or intensive co-treatment will be adhered to because it worked in a case report. |
| Development value | A technically consequential readout tied to a plausible retained product/data interest | A positive off-label treatment study by itself creates an asset that repays its sponsor. |

The exact variant is a sensible replication starting point, **not a validated predictive biomarker**: one family cannot separate allele response from shared modifiers or care. A broader, separately analyzed exploratory cohort may be appropriate after product qualification and a defined rationale. Budget flexibility does not remove the need for an interpretable population.

The precise meaningful-effect threshold needs patient and clinical justification. The numerical thresholds in the design model are assumptions for sensitivity analysis. They are not validated PPK thresholds, sample-size approval or a promise of a positive trial.

## Gates before a material clinical commitment

1. **Product and exposure:** identify the article, trace its evidence, and establish which analytical, stability, release/permeation, clinical-supply and relevant-skin safety work is actually missing. Reuse adequate evidence where possible; do not require a new formulation by default. Stop or reprice the route if major redevelopment is necessary.
2. **Population and interpretability:** establish a plausible independent-patient/pedigree recruitment pool, interpretable baseline burden, comparator and patient-level analysis. Published registry counts are not consenting eligible participants. A broad genotype pool requires a plan for heterogeneity and a claim narrower than the enrollment label where appropriate.
3. **Benefit–risk:** connect an achievable local exposure to meaningful benefit while monitoring local integrity and relevant systemic risks. A robust negative benefit–risk result stops the tested product/use. An uninformative delivery or measurement result is inconclusive, not evidence of efficacy.
4. **Retained interest and complete cost:** distinguish research use, clinical supply, source-data reference, ownership of new outputs and commercial rights. Model cost through the same readout, including prerequisites, contingencies and termination obligations. The present estimates do not establish available rights or financing.

These are commitments to answer questions, not a requirement to run every possible assay. The next work is the least costly credible way to resolve a binding uncertainty. An unnecessary animal experiment or redundant formulation program can consume the capital without improving the decision.

## Three possible outcomes of the diligence

| Outcome | Evidence needed | Development implication |
|---|---|---|
| Existing-product route remains credible | Sufficient product/safety/reference package, a feasible independent comparison and a retainable contribution at tolerable cost | Consider a bounded clinical-development package after the remaining access and execution facts are established. |
| Useful therapeutic hypothesis, weak private investment route | Clinical rationale survives but the market, rights, contribution or complete cost does not support retained value | Preserve the clinical finding; prefer another investment candidate or a different contribution structure. |
| Exact product/use loses support | Adequate evidence excludes worthwhile benefit, local/systemic risk overwhelms benefit, or no interpretable feasible exposure can be obtained | Stop that claim or product; do not relabel an adjacent molecule, genotype or formulation as confirmation. |

The user's instruction to investigate deeply does not prejudge which outcome is correct. A defensible decision to narrow or stop is a successful research result.
