## Evidence discipline

Use primary papers, public trial records, regulator sources, actual patent claims and clearly labeled issuer statements. Record observation, publication and retrieval dates separately when they matter. Preserve denominator, randomization/blinding scope, co-treatment, genotype, article identity, attrition and overlapping cohorts. Failure to retrieve a source is not a negative result.

Computations use explicitly labeled hypothetical assumptions where patient-level data are unavailable. They can show design sensitivity and break-even requirements; they cannot create a validated probability of Phase II success or profitable exit. Cost estimates must name the entire decision boundary and excluded costs. No hypothetical transaction is treated as an existing offer.

Keep summaries concise and supporting evidence in focused files. Read the relevant workstream rather than loading every document. Apply the repository checks plus computation-specific verification and independent challenge before publication.
