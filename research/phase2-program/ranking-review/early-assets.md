## Comparative judgment

**8015-P2 and BLU-3797 form the stronger scientific pair; HJ-02 and FD-2 form a more speculative pair.** P2 has the more persuasive established-deficit functional animal package; BLU has particularly coherent genetic/pharmacological target support and a defined human-muscle qualification route. HJ has a concrete institutional licensing invitation, but that cannot compensate for uncertain durable cardiac benefit. FD has useful early-treatment animal results but a substantial exposure, population/window and human-model bridge.

All four lack exact-article human clinical efficacy in the reviewed evidence. They should compete as preclinical development opportunities, not receive human-efficacy credit from cultured human cells or from a related compound. A positive affordable experiment would still leave substantial clinical-product, safety and financing work.

H = exact-use human-benefit evidence, T = weakest major translation/product/learnability bridge, K = capital fit of a consequential milestone and continuation, V = differentiated value capture. ## Exact 8015-P2: strongest animal-function package, unresolved human bridge

**Package:** a conditional evaluation/development interest in exact P2 for an initially defined MFN2 population, tied to exposure-linked functional translation. [Weigele et al., 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11493442/) reports treatment of approximately 50-week-old T105M knock-in mice after baseline deficits, with motor, sensory and electrophysiological improvement. The paper's human fibroblast and reprogrammed-neuron experiments used the **8015 mixture from one patient**, not P2. Mouse panel counts vary and are not pooled here. The [2026 rat paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42653381/) adds functional and electrophysiological observations in the same allele; the [earlier detailed extraction](../../rounds/round21/p2-article-exposure.md) records four male rats per treatment-dose group and seven WT references. Different species strengthen the animal package but do not supply independent human or allele replication.

The meaningful claim is improved motor/sensory function, not normalized mitochondrial morphology alone. The principal alternative explanation is a model/allele-specific response with inadequate tolerable human exposure. The [off-target audit](../../rounds/round5/p2-off-target-audit.md) distinguishes binding screens from functional MAO-A/hERG liabilities; neither absence nor presence of a clinical safety margin is established. The 2024 main text was freshly read; rat details and the supplement liability extraction retain their stated prior audit dates after full-text retrieval failed in this pass.

**Next development milestone:** authenticated P2 produces a reproducible, exposure-linked functional improvement in a qualified human disease system, with suitable controls and an initial liability margin. A morphology-only assay would be a smaller qualification output, not completion of this milestone. A negotiated option or funded data contribution is plausible, but publication/patent linkage does not establish attainable rights. Rank falls if human function cannot be measured without open-ended platform development; it rises if existing data show exact-P2 function at a credible safety margin. [Team 07](../teams/07-neuromuscular-translation.md) preserves the article, patent and model distinctions.

## BLU-3797: target coherence and a testable function question

**Package:** an evaluation/development interest or differentiated translational contribution for an on-treatment muscle-function claim in MTM1-XLMTM. The freshly read [Shearer et al., 2026 paper](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/198568) connects PIK3C2B genetic inactivation and inhibitor treatment. Pharmacological cohorts contain **five to seven male mice per group**; vehicle animals reached humane endpoints three days after the day-22 start. A separate day-22 versus day-25 comparison tests later intervention. Survival and wire-hanging improve, but wire-hanging is not isolated muscle force. The study contains no human participants.

The strongest limitation is translation from a rapidly lethal developing mouse to heterogeneous congenital human muscle disease. Function declined after withdrawal while p62 remained reduced: a biomarker cannot substitute for benefit. Continued treatment remains a valid claim. A future systemic program also needs exposure and safety appropriate to vulnerable children; historical liver concerns in XLMTM care are context, not proof of BLU toxicity.

**Next development milestone:** qualify the exact article and human-muscle model, then demonstrate contraction/force benefit at interpretable exposure across independent relevant biological backgrounds. The existing [qualification package](../../rounds/round8/blu3797-qualification-package.md) defines a starting scope; it does not mean that the model or response has already been achieved. A large existing compound program may value such evidence, but no commercial interest is presumed. BLU can outrank P2 if its human system yields a more decisive affordable experiment; it falls if model qualification alone absorbs the useful tranche or the treatment window excludes the proposed population.

## HJ-02: credible licensing lead, demanding efficacy replication

**Package:** a cardiac-field option with value tied to independent, exposure-linked post-MI functional replication. The [2022 cardiac paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9617125/) reports early reinnervation, reduced provoked arrhythmia and smaller infarcts. The [prior full-text audit](../../rounds/round1/hj02_diligence.md) records day-14 studies with five mice/group and day-15 pump-function groups of five, sham four. Crucially, pump-function panels used sham as their statistical reference. Not differing from sham does not establish improvement versus injured vehicle. Fresh full-text access failed; these details retain the earlier audit provenance.

The [chemistry study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9112862/) supports a phenotypic neurite-growth effect, with unresolved direct binding. Reactive acrylamide chemistry warrants selectivity/safety work; incomplete target identification alone is not a veto. [Related ISP work](https://physoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.14814/phy2.15702) found no durable day-40 advantage over vehicle; ISP is not HJ-02, so this challenges the reinnervation-to-lasting-repair inference without establishing exact-compound failure.

**Next development milestone:** authenticated article plus basic exposure work, followed by independent injured-control replication of durable pump/rhythm benefit with baseline injury balance. The freshly opened [OHSU #2944 listing](https://apps.ohsu.edu/research/tech-portal/technology/view/5799762) invites licensing/co-development and lists US12,729,177. This is a specific entry point, not proof of unencumbered cardiac rights or affordability. Rank rises with direct durable benefit and a tolerable exposure window; it falls if reinnervation changes without meaningful function or worsens rhythm outcomes.

## FD-2: useful preservation evidence, largest scope uncertainty

**Package:** a conditional institutional peptide interest for a specified early ALS window, supported by reproducible function and exposure rather than generic ALS positioning. [Lin et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41298223/) was published online in November 2025 and in the January 2026 issue; these are one study. The refreshed abstract confirms peptide, fish and SOD1-model findings. The [Round 26 full-text audit](../../rounds/round26/functional-rescue-screen.md) records day-60 treatment, **five mice/group for motor outcomes and twelve/group for humane-endpoint survival**, with random allocation and blinded assessment. Fresh full-text retrieval failed. Patent examples and later article cohorts cannot be pooled as independent replication.

The supported hypothesis is early preservation/delay. It does not establish reversal in symptomatic patients, broad ALS coverage, measured peptide stability superiority or a chronic clinical product. The binding question is whether exposure, timing and the proposed recruitable population align; a short peptide is not automatically cheap to develop. CNS penetration should not be imposed if an effective peripheral mechanism suffices, but relevant-site exposure still needs evidence.

**Next development milestone:** exact-article/exposure qualification followed by an independent functional test in a clinically justified treatment window, with a human-relevant functional system where feasible. [US20240327462A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240327462A1/en) supplies a dated institutional claim lead; current title, prosecution and usable terms are unverified. Rank rises if existing product/exposure data support a bounded relevant experiment; it falls sharply if delivery discovery plus new model development is needed before testing the thesis.
