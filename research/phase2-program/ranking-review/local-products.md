# Local products: comparable screening packages

Reviewed 9 November 2026 UTC by `/root/rank_local_products`, against `cc34bf2`. Scope: six existing local candidates. This is a provisional screen under uncertainty; no inquiries, procurement or development work occurred. It does not inherit earlier candidate ranks. Current observations and source-access limits are separated from analyst estimates below.

## Decision

**Topical erlotinib, topical insulin and APR-246 form the leading human-evidence group.** Erlotinib has the strongest exact-use experimental design but only two related participants. Insulin has broader clinical experience but heterogeneous products and populations, weaker causal evidence and a difficult generic-product value-capture thesis. APR has unusually relevant patient observations for a serious, poorly served condition, with less controlled evidence and a larger product bridge. These differences justify a tentative screening order, not a confident superiority claim.

RM4404 leads the preclinical group because it connects an exact local article to animal sensory function. NDC-9009 has patient-cell relevance but a large dissolved-exposure and useful-function gap. mIGF-Gel must establish an advantage over a practical insulin product while solving a more complex sterile-product problem. NDC versus mIGF is especially sensitive to the intended claim and obtainable product contribution.

## Scores and their meaning

Each triple is **[low, central, high] judgment**, not an outcome distribution. H = relevant benefit evidence; T = product/exposure and clinical translation; K = capital fit of a consequential next milestone; V = plausible retained value. All use the program's 0–4 rubric. Unknown commercial terms widen uncertainty; they are neither assumed free nor automatically scored zero. None of these packages has a demonstrated absolute Phase II success probability.

## Defined next milestones and planning estimates

These are **new analyst planning allowances in 2026 USD**, not quotes, literature-derived costs or budgets already obtained. Estimates include project coordination and analysis, assume existing capable operators and facilities, and do not assume donated clinical execution. They **exclude unpriced background-IP acquisition, sponsor data/reference rights, taxes and financing costs**. ## Candidate cases

### Topical erlotinib / KRT16 p.Arg127Cys keratoderma

The [Lee and Lowe 2022 letter](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamadermatol.2021.5302) describes two people from one family, not independent genotype replication. Randomized double-masked opposite-foot phases are a genuine causal-design advantage. The mother's improvement was stronger; the son's controlled effect was modest. Keratolytic co-treatment, initial low-strength failure and uncontrolled extensions constrain interpretation. The intended claim is sustained pain/function improvement during treatment.

The complete letter was read in the immediately preceding [ranking audit](../apr-ranking-audit.md); this lane's direct PDF retry failed, so its detailed extraction is retained rather than presented as a fresh successful download. [Greco 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9028469/) supplies related different-genotype/preparation topical failures. [Sol-Gel's December 2025 disclosure](https://ir.sol-gel.com/node/9126), refreshed here, reports no separation from vehicle in Darier; that is not failure in the exact KRT16 population. Its [current pipeline](https://www.sol-gel.com/pipeline/) supports a possible product contribution, not identical formulation or available rights.

**Capture thesis:** indication-specific controlled data and a dependable topical product, potentially linked to an existing developer. **Reversal:** poor independent response, an expensive product bridge, or no differentiated contribution lowers it below APR. A sponsor's name alone supplies no priority.

### Exact topical insulin / refractory noninfectious PED

The starting package remains the Actrapid-derived regular-insulin product in [Diaz-Valle 2022](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/aos.14997), freshly inspected. Historical cohorts comprised 61 insulin patients and 23 serum controls; closure occurred in 51 and 11. **Twenty insulin patients had infectious etiology and only 21 were classified neurotrophic.** The whole-cohort rate must not become an expected response rate for our noninfectious indication. Calendar differences, background care, exclusions and unequal follow-up weaken causal inference.

[Lixi 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40123-026-01446-6), also refreshed, reports 44 closures in 45 eyes/43 patients using a different Humalog-derived presentation. Sensitivity categories did not improve; recurrence included a melting event requiring surgery. This supports the therapeutic direction, not exact-product replication or sensory restoration. Official trial pages failed substantive retrieval in this lane; active competition retains the explicit 8 November verification in [Team 06](../teams/06-ocular-local-delivery.md).

**Capture thesis:** differentiated sterile shelf life, reliable delivery or product/data contribution, not generic insulin ownership. **Reversal:** a defensible practical advantage raises it; an undifferentiated formulation or existing trial already answering the question drops it despite patient benefit. Clinical plausibility exceeds commercial clarity.

### APR-246 / persistent AEC erosions

The [original 2020 paper](https://www.sindrome-eec.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/s41419-020-2223-8.pdf) was refreshed. Two children improved during continued treatment: one treated palm had contralateral vehicle, while the other patient's scalp lacked simultaneous control. Patient-derived cellular observations complement the clinical signal. They do not establish a randomized population effect, complete barrier restoration or chronic dermal safety. Later reports remain conservatively overlapping.

The meaningful claim is reduced erosions, pain and care burden during use. Cure, complete cornification and off-treatment permanence are not prerequisites. A new animal efficacy campaign is not automatically the best next step. Transfer and exposure must establish whether the historical presentation can support useful independent clinical confirmation.

**Capture thesis:** a serious rare-disease product/indication contribution with clinically relevant existing observations. Patent and article-access questions retain the [Round 25 audit](../../rounds/round25/apr246-advancement.md); inactive oncology development is not abandoned rights. **Reversal:** an inexpensive transferable article and feasible patient comparison can put APR first. A new formulation, major safety package or weak local exposure can defeat the small-program premise. The present lower central H reflects control design, not a new adverse APR result.

### RM4404 / injury-related corneal hypoesthesia

[Li 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13266094/) was refreshed. The early chemical-injury arm contains direct vehicle contrasts for structure (five per group) and mechanosensation (six per group), including a later off-dosing observation. Delayed and surgical arms do not inherit that durability. Some analyses use both eyes or many measurements per animal; the [prior audit](../../rounds/round17/rm-function-evidence.md) preserves denominator/statistical ambiguities.

The claim must be comfortable protective function, not blink sensitivity alone. **Capture thesis:** a differentiated sensory-recovery article if licensed or otherwise accessible. **Reversal:** independent animal-level replication, measured exposure and function strengthen it; sensitization without useful tissue protection or an unworkable ocular margin removes the advantage. No human benefit is established, and clinical manufacture/toxicology remains downstream of the proposed milestone.

### NDC-9009 / Darier disease

[Harmon 2026](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aee1599) has patient-derived relevance: four isolates from three donors and two variants, with functional cohesion measured during junction formation. It does not demonstrate repair of established lesions or clinical disease control. The one-hour localization result belongs to CDN1163, not NDC. Fresh full-text access failed; exact extraction retains the [Round 19 source audit](../../rounds/round19/ndc-article-exposure.md).

The [2023 chemistry report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10530588/) reports 0.23 µM PBS solubility, compared with higher nominal cellular test concentrations; this demands dissolved-exposure analysis, not a declaration that the experiment contained no active drug. **Capture thesis:** topical useful-control contribution tied to the exact existing article and rights. **Reversal:** a practical exposure window plus useful disease-model function advances it; prolonged formulation rescue or assembly-only effects keep it below human-evidence alternatives. Sponsor overlap remains unresolved, not presumed fatal.

### mIGF-Gel / corneal epithelial defects

[Shan 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13400331/) was refreshed. Acute rat closure has reported n=5; steroid-suppressed delayed/sensory analyses report n=3. Material controls exist, but there is no direct insulin comparison. Greater residence and reduced animal dosing frequency do not demonstrate improved human adherence or a clinically adequate sterile formulation.

**Capture thesis:** a licensed assembled peptide product with better maintained repair or materially lower treatment burden than practical insulin. Existing disclosures and a [specific product-rights trail](../../rounds/round20/migf-product-capture.md) make that contribution concrete but not owned by ddiscov. **Reversal:** a reproducible active-comparator advantage can move it above NDC or RM; equivalent benefit without practical advantage leaves little reason to pay for the larger product-development bridge. Human sensory restoration remains unestablished.

## Boundaries and next screening work

No documented clinical futility finding eliminates the exact APR, KRT16-erlotinib or insulin packages. All retain product, safety, access and economic uncertainties. Before inquiries, complete public-source competitor and exact-product mapping and test the ranking against plausible product-cost and capture assumptions. The group is narrowed by what could produce useful differentiated evidence with survivable exposure, not by which investigator can be contacted first.
