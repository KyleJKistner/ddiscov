# Additional retained candidates: comparable screening review

Reviewed 9 November 2026 UTC (8 November America/Los_Angeles) by `/root/rank_retained_candidates`. Scope: seven retained Round 26 candidates omitted from the initial 23-candidate comparison. This is public-source screening; no inquiries, study execution or spending occurred. The objective is to narrow the field under uncertainty, not to require negotiated terms before ranking.

H/T/K/V scores below are low/central/high **judgments**, not success probabilities: exact-use human benefit, weakest important translation/execution bridge, capital fit of a consequential milestone, and plausible retained value. Existing rights and potential contracts are hypotheses unless explicitly documented. The same missing-price standard applies to every candidate. A cheap feasibility task is not counted as achieving a valuable development milestone.

## Evidence and exact opportunity

### Dornase alfa — severe tear-deficient dry eye, with an oGVHD-specific variant

**Package:** an indication/product-data contribution around the historical ophthalmic DNase article, or participation in a licensed ocular program. The 2019 [randomized pilot](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6504128/) assigned 47 people and retained 41; it supported improved staining, while the between-arm OSDI symptom comparison was not statistically significant. A material addition is the [2024 randomized oGVHD publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38407974/), reporting 58 participants. Its [official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02702518) was freshly read through the API: 29/29 started, 26/25 completed, and the trial ended in April 2020 after COVID-risk closure. Registered primary staining and secondary OSDI analyses are explicitly within-group. The abstract reports additional between-group global-assessment/mucus findings, but does not establish a successful between-group primary symptom contrast.

These are separate trial records from the same center with overlapping recruitment periods; individual overlap is unresolved, so **do not sum 105 unique patients or label this confirmed independent replication**. The 2024 report is not a new 2026 experiment. The [December 2024 issuer announcement](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/12/23/3001191/0/en/neutrolis-licenses-university-of-illinois-chicago-patent-to-advance-topical-dry-eye-disease-therapy-targeting-nets.html) documents a claimed exclusive ocular license and a distinct L304 product. Clinical evidence cannot be transferred to L304 without a bridge. A prespecified comparative symptom/ocular-surface milestone is plausible, but sterile product, recruitment and licensed-program fit raise cost. **Reversal:** a reproducible between-arm meaningful effect, cohort linkage and a differentiated contribution could move this reserve upward; within-arm significance alone cannot.

### Salbutamol — respiratory and motor burden in PURA syndrome

**Package:** an age-stratified clinical evidence and pediatric-product contribution with rights to usable results. The [July 2026 primary report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13375090/) adds two unrelated infants and reviews earlier reports. Three people received salbutamol in total, not five newly treated patients. Reported respiratory/motor improvements are encouraging, but maturation, prior/overlapping pyridostigmine and selection of responders are major competing explanations. The earlier salbutamol case remains literature-derived; this review did not independently recover its full original report. The fresh indexed primary text confirms that the authors could not estimate the responder proportion.

Reduction in ventilation burden or accelerated useful motor function is the relevant claim; global neurological repair is unnecessary. Existing drug experience helps, but infant exposure, cardiac effects, sleep disturbance and reliable age-adjusted measurements remain consequential. A small prospective controlled comparison with standardized respiratory outcomes could be attainable within the envelope, although rare-infant recruitment may dominate time. Generic availability weakens a standalone proprietary thesis; an improved pediatric presentation or a contractually retained dataset must create actual differentiation. **Reversal:** prospectively controlled benefit beyond maturation plus credible product need raises priority; natural-history-like changes or intolerable burden lower it.

### TTI-0102 — fatigue/function in genetically defined MELAS

**Package:** participation in the sponsor's exact prodrug program, conditional on resolving its existing clinical analysis. The [March 2026 sponsor-authored preprint](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.26.26347968v1) reports nine randomized participants, six active and three placebo, and a fatigue signal. It also reports no walking improvement and gastrointestinal intolerance/dropout. The fresh [official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06644534) remains completed with no posted results; it specifies the 12-minute walk and FSS, whereas the preprint reports MFIS and a different masking description. These are material endpoint/analysis issues, not evidence of misconduct. The full preprint's indexed text was refreshed; its complete underlying patient-flow dataset was unavailable.

Patient-reported fatigue could be meaningful despite unchanged walking, but a nominal p-value from a tiny, incompletely reconciled analysis does not justify confident efficacy ranking. Existing sponsor exposure/manufacturing could shorten a dose/product contribution; this is not a freely accessible existing drug. A small dose/exposure and prespecified fatigue study is conceivable after record reconciliation, with later confirmatory financing still needed. **Hold promotion of a positive fatigue claim** pending protocol, amendments, analysis population and tolerability review. **Reversal:** coherent prespecified results surviving missing-data analysis could raise rank; a post-hoc/exposure-selected signal or unacceptable tolerability could close the thesis.

### Amlexanox — selected COL7A1 nonsense alleles in RDEB

**Package:** a genotype-selected topical product and functional skin-evidence program. The [2017 primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5573171/) reports material from 14 patients spanning 12 premature-stop mutations, with eight mutation contexts responding. Those are neither eight clinical responders nor treated-patient efficacy. Collagen VII expression, secretion and junctional localization support biological plausibility; clinically useful mechanical attachment and wound benefit remain unproved. Allele/background dependence and exposure-related cell-growth effects constrain generalization.

The prior [Round 26 audit](../../rounds/round26/local-product-screen.md) identified CTRI/2021/04/033024, but fresh searching still did not recover an authoritative current trial-result record. That gap is not a negative trial. The next developmental decision is whether an exact topical article produces useful skin function at tolerated exposure, using several responsive and nonresponsive patient backgrounds. Current [Zevaskyn](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/zevaskyn) and [Vyjuvek labeling expansion](https://www.fda.gov/media/188709/download) mean the product needs a practical or incremental-benefit case against available care. A low-burden topical option remains conceptually attractive, but generic ingredient and allele restriction weaken capture. **Reversal:** replicated functional benefit and viable skin exposure could move it above mechanism-only reserves; protein staining alone cannot.

### Topical timolol — chronic diabetic-foot-ulcer healing

**Package:** a differentiated wound product/evidence contribution, not a claim that cheap generic gel already has proven healing superiority. Fresh [official BAART-DFU results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03282981) confirm 48 randomized people and primary 12-week closure counts of 8/21 versus 7/27. The primary [conference report](https://woundheal.org/2025-abstracts/) reports a nonsignificant early comparison and inconsistent timing/percentage descriptions relative to the registry. Later adjusted time-to-event findings do not replace the prespecified primary result. The [20-person barrier paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40159333/) selected healed wounds after randomization and found no significant recurrence difference; it is not another independent healing trial.

Topical exposure and clinical execution are better characterized than for most preclinical candidates. The principal weakness is that a useful healing/recurrence advantage remains uncertain despite controlled evaluation. More mechanistic work would not settle that. After reconciling records, a sufficiently powered comparative healing study—not another selected-healer analysis—would be the consequential milestone, probably beyond the envelope. **Hold a superiority/recurrence thesis**; retain as a low-cost clinical comparator. **Reversal:** a coherent clinically important effect in the complete randomized data or genuinely independent trial could raise priority. Cheap supply alone cannot.

### PSB-KD107 — Duchenne muscular dystrophy

**Package:** a defined GPR18-agonist product plus incremental muscle-function evidence with a retainable commercial interest. The [2023 primary paper](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cell-and-developmental-biology/articles/10.3389/fcell.2023.1187253/full) reports improved absolute and specific muscle force in treated mdx mice, assessed ex vivo. The force comparison used five animals per principal group; the separate O-1602 comparator had three. Human observations concern fusion in two DMD and one Becker donor background, not force rescue in three DMD patients. Current review refreshed the primary functional results; detailed denominator interpretation also uses the prior [full-text extraction](../../rounds/round26/functional-rescue-screen.md).

A stronger next decision would combine fit-for-purpose exposure/safety work with mature human-muscle function and independent confirmation of incremental benefit on a relevant background-care context. Research-article availability does not establish a chronic pediatric product. Respiratory, cardiac and survival benefit remain open, as do translation from mild mdx disease and specific retained rights. **Reversal:** meaningful force improvement at feasible exposure with a distinctive product/rights case could elevate this preclinical reserve; fusion-only replication or open-ended chemistry would weaken it. No current patient benefit is inferred.

### Defined copper nanoclusters — early ATP7A-related Menkes disease

**Package:** the defined CuNC@HisAc/CuNC@HisAcAsc family, not generic copper or elesclomol, seeking incremental neurological/product benefit. The [2026 primary mouse study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13626065/) reports survival groups of 17 CuNC, 14 untreated, 13 copper-histidine and 20 wild-type animals. Day 50 is the follow-up boundary, not an established median lifespan. Late behavioral comparisons are survivor-selected, largely lacking contemporaneous disease controls; treated mice retained some coordination abnormalities. Liver/kidney marker elevations and differing ages/survival complicate safety interpretation.

The [FDA's January 2026 copper-histidinate approval](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-treatment-children-menkes-disease) changes the relevant comparator. A large untreated-mouse survival effect is insufficient for an incremental clinical claim. A consequential next package needs exact-product reproducibility, exposure/toxicology characterization and controlled functional evidence against copper histidinate. The paper's disclosed institutional patent family offers a plausible capture route, not verified title or an available license. Pediatric nanomedicine continuation would likely exceed the envelope. **Reversal:** reproducible neurological benefit beyond the active comparator with a viable safety window can elevate the asset; better copper delivery without useful function or with accumulating toxicity cannot.
