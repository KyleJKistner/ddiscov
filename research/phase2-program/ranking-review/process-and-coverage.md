# Organ-process candidates and coverage sentinels

Reviewed 9 November 2026 UTC by `/root/rank_process_and_coverage`. This is a screening comparison under uncertainty, before inquiries. Evidence is refreshed selectively below; prior detailed extractions are explicitly identified. No inquiry, product order, study or transaction occurred.

## Common estimate and scoring conventions

All costs below are **analyst planning estimates in 2026 USD, not quotes**. Low/base/high cases describe alternative resource requirements, not statistical confidence intervals. They price a consequential evidence package, including project coordination and a contingency, rather than just the cheapest assay. They assume access to an existing academic/clinical infrastructure rather than building a laboratory or transplant centre. Rights fees, an acquired equity stake, commercial manufacturing, pivotal trials and general company-building costs are excluded and remain separate uncertainties. A low cost conditional on that infrastructure is not evidence it is available.

`H/T/K/V` follow the common 0–4 screen: exact-use human-benefit evidence; weakest material product/translation/learnability bridge; capital fit of a consequential milestone and continuation; differentiated value capture. Ranges are low–central–high judgments, not probabilities. An unsuccessful randomized trial does not earn H=3 merely for being randomized. A hypothetical paid contract is not booked revenue.

## AAT / exact Zemaira-containing warm EVLP process

**Disposition: conditional process-development reserve.** Its human-organ evidence makes this more than a generic anti-inflammatory idea, but a cheap qualification assay is not the price of transplant benefit or of a valuable company.

**Evidence.** The [2026 Mariscal primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42790593/) reports improved function and permeability measures in rejected human lungs. The [2020 conference report](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053249820312997) specifies **eight double-lung donor blocks**, split into treated and contralateral control lungs. That is eight paired donor units, not sixteen independent donors. No recipient was treated in this experiment. Both indexed primary reports were refreshed; the final 2026 full methods remain unavailable in this pass. The [Round 22 extraction](../../rounds/round22/aat-human-function.md) identifies likely overlapping 2019 thesis/2020/2026 lineage and unresolved linkage to a five-donor patent pilot; these are not additive replications.

**Why it may work.** The exact organ is accessible, paired allocation reduces donor heterogeneity, and an existing manufactured protein reduces starting-material invention. The proposed useful claim is improved usable-graft performance after a finite processing step, with acceptable recipient carryover and workflow. Continued tissue-retained activity can be useful; complete molecular washout is not required.

**Strongest challenge.** Circuit oxygenation and edema can improve without creating additional usable grafts or improving recipients. Protein load, physical formulation and perfusate replacement can contribute to the signal; antigen concentration does not establish active antiprotease exposure. Prior animal organ-only, cold-preservation and systemic-recipient studies concern different articles or regimens. The [manufacturer label, revised January 2024](https://labeling.cslbehring.com/pi/us/zemaira/en/zemaira-prescribing-information.pdf), was refreshed: its intravenous handling and approved deficiency use do not qualify prolonged warm recirculation. The [Canadian protocol 23-5745 record](https://recherche-essais-cliniques.canada.ca/en/clinical-trial/294199-lung-transplantation), refreshed, still displays “Not yet recruiting,” last updated 18 July 2026. It supplies neither an EVLP-only regimen nor outcomes; actual site activity is unverified.

**Next development package.** First establish active exposure, physical comparators and circuit compatibility; then obtain an independent paired donor-organ result with prespecified useful physiological/acceptance criteria and full donor accounting. A cost model using roughly ten evaluable donor pairs is an illustrative resource unit, **not a powered sample-size proposal**. The package can decide whether a recipient-relevant programme merits continuation; it cannot establish clinical efficacy by itself.

| Cost/time case | Scope assumption | Total through this decision |
|---|---|---:|
| Low | Existing assays/circuit and local donor access; limited method adaptation | $180k / 6 months |
| Base | Qualification and compatibility ~$60k; paired-organ operations/material/logistics ~$210k; coordination, analysis and contingency ~$80k | $350k / 12 months |
| High | Repeated method work, failed/unusable donor runs, substantial logistics and staffing | $700k / 18 months |

The original $30–100k / 2–4 month qualification estimate remains a valid **separate first stage**, not the entire package. A recipient efficacy study would likely exceed the $500k envelope and require outside continuation funding; no narrower reliable budget was established. A licence to a differentiated process/data package or a contracted development contribution could return value earlier. Existing protein and perfusion providers may capture most of that value, and the prior patent audit does not establish available rights or a paying customer.

**Scores:** H **1–1–1**; T **1–2–2**; K **1–2–3**; V **0–1–2**. Raise priority if an independently useful process improvement survives an appropriate comparator and has a credible licensable contribution. Lower it if physical matching removes the increment, active exposure is impractical, or only a costly recipient study can answer the actual value question. A real paid contract would change the transaction screen later; it is not assumed now.

## Regadenoson / marginal-donor EVLP

**Disposition: park expansion of the tested regimen; retain as a comparator and conditional reformulation/selection hypothesis.** More advanced testing makes its negative evidence more informative, not automatically more investable.

The [Fleischmann trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12745136/) was published in 2025 and enrolled in 2021–2024. Indexed primary full-text sections were refreshed; direct reopening returned a challenge page. It randomized **48 donor pairs**, of which **34** were evaluable after EVLP: 26 regadenoson and eight placebo. Fourteen randomized pairs were not evaluable. Graft use was **17/26 versus 6/8**, with a difference CI spanning −35 to +26 percentage points. Grade-3 primary graft dysfunction occurred in **5/17 versus 2/7 recipients**. One accepted placebo pair supplied two single-lung recipients. Baseline impairment was imbalanced. The study therefore failed to establish useful-graft or PGD superiority, without proving equivalence or zero possible benefit. The official [NCT04521569 record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04521569) is completed; the prior review preserves its 47-enrollee/48-randomized-paper discrepancy. The registry's Early Phase 1 label must not become Phase II by inference.

**Next decision.** Review existing participant/exposure data for a prospectively testable explanation before financing another efficacy study. An exploratory subgroup would need independent confirmation and cannot retrospectively rescue the failed primary result. The modest drug procurement cost does not remove organ, centre, recruitment and recipient-follow-up costs. A meaningful new clinical comparison is estimated at **$0.75m / $1.5m / $3m**, over **24 / 36 / 48 months**, conditional on a justified revised claim. The base assumes about $400k centre/setup/monitoring, $800k recruitment/EVLP/recipient assessments and $300k analysis/coordination/contingency; it excludes routine transplant-care charges and acquisition/licence payments. This is an order-of-magnitude resource model, not a trial design, powered denominator or recommended spend. Existing-data reanalysis alone could cost much less but would not establish a new treatment effect.

**Scores:** H **0–1–1**; T **2–3–3**; K **0–1–2**; V **0–1–2**; **adverse controlled-efficacy flag**. Available clinical logistics and exposure observations support T, while the exact-regimen evidence blocks promotion regardless of aggregate score. No specific differentiated proprietary contribution was established. A changed exposure/selection thesis with independent supporting evidence could reopen development. AAT ranks ahead for further screening in this lane; that judgment is not proof AAT is clinically superior.

## Five legacy sentinels: include in the comparison

These are not excluded merely because older reviews called them reserves. Their scientific and financial weaknesses remain distinct. The cost cases are **incremental totals through a small, interpretable clinical evidence decision**, incorporating product/measurement setup, clinical operations, analysis and contingency. They exclude later confirmatory development and rights acquisition. Cheap marketed ingredients do not guarantee cheap recruitment or retained commercial value.

### Ruxolitinib 1.5% cream / Hailey–Hailey disease

**Include as a serious local clinical-proof challenger.** The [Round 7 clinical audit](../../rounds/round7/rux_hhd_clinical.md) resolves five separate peer-reviewed single-patient reports using 1.5% cream, with heterogeneous induction, add-on and maintenance use. [Jobe 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42222696/) and the [nine-patient preprint abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42282850/) were refreshed. The latter remains abstract-level and unpooled with the five cases; it does not make fourteen independently audited patients. Useful ongoing reduction in painful erosions and rescue burden is a legitimate claim.

The strongest alternatives are relapsing natural history, infection/background-care changes, combined treatment and selected reporting. A randomized HS result concerns another disease, not HHD efficacy. The [current manufacturer prescribing-information page](https://www.opzelura.com/opzelura-prescribing-information) establishes an existing topical product and relevant infection/combination restrictions; it does not establish exposure through eroded HHD skin. A differentiated formulation is not automatically needed. A small controlled whole-product or matched-vehicle proof could resolve useful added benefit, with measured treatment burden and relevant exposure. Estimate **$150k / $300k / $600k**, **9 / 15 / 24 months**; base ~$60k product/comparator/measurement setup, ~$180k clinical operations and ~$60k coordination/analysis/contingency. Recruitment, comparator availability or required extra safety work drive the range.

**H/T/K/V:** **1–2–2 / 1–2–3 / 1–2–3 / 0–1–2**. A marketed article makes execution more plausible, but a crowded owned product leaves weak independent capture unless indication data or a specific improvement has value. Independent controlled HHD benefit at feasible exposure would raise priority; strong background-care response or no worthwhile incremental benefit would lower it.

### Topical gentamicin / susceptible LAMA3 or LAMB3 nonsense JEB

**Include as a mechanistically precise local reserve, with potential to challenge the front group.** The [2020 primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7210719/), refreshed in indexed full text, reports three children and nine treated wounds; their keratinocyte data and topical wound observations are linked. Functional laminin-332, adhesion-related laboratory effects and later wound closure provide a coherent readthrough-to-benefit chain. One patient had a contralateral untreated comparison, but this was an open-label series, not controlled randomized efficacy. The [five-child IV study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8892363/) concerns a different exposure route and is not added to the topical denominator.

The key alternatives are antibacterial benefit and optimized wound care; neither is a reason to demand genetic cure. A useful clinical claim may concern safer or more sustained wound control, while a readthrough-specific claim needs stronger attribution. Large-area and repeated-course systemic exposure, auditory/renal risk and resistance burden need explicit assessment. An independent, genotype-matched controlled local proof with useful wound/burden endpoints and exposure monitoring is estimated at **$150k / $300k / $650k**, **9 / 18 / 30 months**. Base ~$70k product/measurement/exposure setup, ~$160k clinical and laboratory assessments, ~$70k coordination/analysis/contingency. Additional recruitment, safety or product work drives the high case. The USC programme identified by the original papers is a relevant potential overlap; live operating status was not independently established here.

**H/T/K/V:** **1–2–2 / 1–2–2 / 1–2–3 / 0–1–2**. The plausible retained asset is a demonstrably improved regimen/product or useful genotype-response data, not ownership of generic gentamicin. Independent benefit and manageable exposure raise priority; no added useful advantage or commercially undifferentiated replication lowers it.

### Lisdexamfetamine / KCNMA1-N999S episodic dyskinesia

**Include as a potentially efficient symptomatic-proof challenger.** The [original six-person series](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8810426/), refreshed in indexed primary text, explicitly selected people already known to respond. Caregiver recall suggested large, temporally related episode reductions; it supplies no all-treated response denominator. Appetite, sleep and irritability effects often constrained dose escalation. The [Round 9 audit](../../rounds/round9/lisdexamfetamine-evidence-update.md) separates genotype-matched mouse dextroamphetamine findings and other human variants from the exact N999S/lisdexamfetamine series.

The next decision is whether masked episode adjudication in an independently ascertained, genotype-defined sample confirms meaningful time/function benefit beyond arousal and expectation effects. A narrow prospective comparison is estimated at **$100k / $200k / $450k**, **9 / 15 / 24 months**: base ~$40k measurement/product setup, ~$110k recruitment/clinical monitoring, ~$50k analysis/coordination/contingency. Feasibility depends on enough eligible patients and an ethical interpretable comparator; this is not a withdrawal recommendation. The [2026 FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/021977s052lbl.pdf) says use is not recommended below age six because of higher exposure/adverse reactions; an all-age rare-disease programme cannot inherit a fully solved pediatric bridge.

**H/T/K/V:** **1–2–2 / 1–2–3 / 1–3–3 / 0–1–2**. This can rank ahead of AAT for an affordable direct clinical answer. Independent benefit or a needed coverage/tolerability improvement raises its value; failure of masked adjudication, poor tolerability or no differentiated contribution lowers it. Generic availability and an attractive clinical signal still leave capture weak.

### Zinc acetate dihydrate / GNAO1-related disorder

**Include as a clinical reserve.** The [2026 ZINCGNAO1 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42542632/) was refreshed; the [Round 11 methods audit](../../rounds/round11/zinc-endpoint-audit.md) supplies the detailed denominator distinctions. Thirteen enrolled, twelve exposed and eleven completing are different sets. The six-month uncontrolled pilot showed exploratory motor/disability signals but no clear blinded movement-severity improvement. Feasibility success is not efficacy success. The relevant next question is a controlled clinically meaningful function result with clear endpoint denominators, not another biochemical response screen.

Estimate **$180k / $350k / $750k**, **12 / 24 / 36 months** for a small coordinated clinical comparison: base ~$60k endpoint/product/site setup, ~$210k monitoring/recruitment/follow-up, ~$80k analysis/coordination/contingency. The [EMA product record](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/wilzin), refreshed, describes copper-lowering activity and gastric effects; chronic use in GNAO1 needs its own benefit/burden assessment. **H/T/K/V: 1–2–2 / 1–2–3 / 1–2–3 / 0–1–2.** An independently replicated functional effect moves it up; maturation/background care, tolerability or a weak capture proposition keeps it below more decisive cases. An existing academic programme can aid execution but may leave no useful new contribution for ddiscov.

### Taurine / residual-transport SLC6A6 deficiency

**Include as an organ- and genotype-specific reserve.** [Ansar's primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31903486/), refreshed, describes two related p.Gly399Val patients. The [Round 10 extraction](../../rounds/round10/new-candidate-screen.md) records mild cardiac dysfunction improving during supplementation; advanced visual loss was not shown to reverse. [Ullah's later primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12679424/), refreshed, reports a treated deletion-case patient's essentially unchanged advanced retinal assessments despite plasma taurine normalization. Seven affected study participants are not seven treated people. The unread 2023 two-brother abstract still cannot expand the treatment ledger or establish replication.

The informative next clinical package is independent cardiac/retained-function evidence in a justified residual-transport subgroup, with background care, tissue reserve and exposure recorded. It must not substitute late-stage visual regeneration for that narrower claim. Estimate **$100k / $250k / $550k**, **12 / 24 / 36 months**: base ~$40k genotype/measurement/product setup, ~$150k international recruitment/follow-up, ~$60k analysis/coordination/contingency. Slow recruitment and genotype heterogeneity drive uncertainty; ingredient cost does not.

**H/T/K/V: 1–2–2 / 0–1–2 / 1–2–3 / 0–1–2.** Intracellular delivery and the feasible subgroup remain weak links. A defined organ-benefit signal beyond the original family could improve its scientific position; widely available supplementation offers little distinctive capture without a necessary product or programme contribution.

## Coverage audit and boundaries

The initial 23-candidate scope covered the active opportunity page and all ten team lead/alternative routes after deduplication. It did **not** cover every retained Round 26 candidate. Seven additional records require explicit comparison: **dornase alfa/ocular disease, salbutamol/PURA, TTI-0102/MELAS, amlexanox/nonsense RDEB, topical timolol/diabetic ulcers, PSB-KD107/DMD and copper nanoclusters/Menkes**. They are assigned to a separate review. A fresh search also identified a [2024 dornase oGVHD randomized trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38407974/) distinct from the 2019 dry-eye pilot; this was forwarded for outcome and lineage review rather than treated as automatic positive replication.

Adding the five sentinels above produces **35 exact-use screening packages**. This is a substantially broader working comparison, not a refreshed ranking of all **622 historical coverage rows**. Those rows include aliases, older screens, comparators and hypotheses. The top-level legacy register is historical and cannot alone decide inclusion or rank. Other reserves such as pantethine/PPCS, miglustat/SCARB2, GSK2193874/TRPV4, vaultAAV and PSAM pain remain outside this bounded pass; no new adverse conclusion is implied. A future expansion should name the candidate and a reason it could change the shortlist, rather than silently assuming the initial agent assignments were exhaustive.

In this lane, a clinical-first or scarce-capital screen generally favors the independently testable local/symptomatic packages over a fresh regadenoson trial. AAT remains viable when the exact process contribution is valuable; it does not earn a preferred capital-recovery position by assuming a customer. The five sentinel candidates all have material uncertainty in attainable value capture, so their comparative scientific promise must not become an unsupported claim of probable cash recovery.
