# Phase II search: integrated first pass

Review date: 8 November 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Base: [dc0cdcc](https://github.com/KyleJKistner/ddiscov/commit/dc0cdccfc6b9b4158094cb11d602ffb9cddf6028). All **10 coordinators and 20 subordinate workers completed** a bounded first pass in five waves. See the [registry](registry.json) for actual task identities and next actions. This record integrates targeted desk reviews; unavailable source data, commercial terms and clinical execution remain explicitly unresolved.

## Decision

Prioritize **exact-use human evidence plus an attainable contribution to a defined development program**. For Phase II confidence, human target engagement must connect to clinically useful benefit at feasible exposure; for investment, that benefit must connect to rights, a bounded commitment and retained cash. None of the first-pass findings supplies both a calibrated above-reference-class Phase II probability and a high-probability capital-return case.

Use the approximately $500,000 planning envelope to constrain the search for an affordable entry and stopping point. It is not a mandate to fund ten assets, and it is not an assumed cost of completing Phase II. Keep maximum acceptable loss, reserve and liquidity horizon unresolved until supplied. All current spending proposals require prices, terms and follow-on funding evidence before allocation.

The preference for greater clinical certainty argues for surveying participation in programs with relevant controlled human proof before committing to de novo chemistry. This is an allocation hypothesis: a later-stage interest may be expensive, unavailable or provide little retained upside. A program that has already passed Phase II is a benchmark or possible transaction target, not evidence that the team successfully predicted a future Phase II outcome. The price and rights determine whether buying that reduced uncertainty helps the investor.

## What improves the selection process

1. Start with the exact drug–population–route combination. A successful target, drug class or adjacent indication does not establish the proposed article's benefit.
2. Favor informative human comparisons, independent replication and causal biology with the correct direction of modulation. Verify that exposure and timing can affect the intended clinical outcome.
3. Choose a population and endpoint that can yield an interpretable answer. Biomarker correction, wound closure, symptom relief and lasting functional recovery are different claims.
4. Examine failed studies and current care before designing a new trial. A prospectively tested subgroup is more informative than a favorable subgroup discovered after failure.
5. Establish what an existing program still needs and what ddiscov can retain. A funded trial can remove the need for our experiment without offering an investment opportunity.
6. Keep the evidence scout and challenger independent, then reconcile sources rather than taking a vote. Shared cohorts, reused data and related patients retain their original denominators.

The [evidence standard](phase2-evidence-standard.md) defines historical benchmarks and separates clinical success, sponsor progression, success from today's stage and investor cash recovery. Its historical associations support these search preferences; they are not causal probability multipliers.

Across teams, several recurring uncertainties are the same underlying dependency: access to a sponsor-controlled article, a usable patient-level dataset, a contract and follow-on financing. Treat them as shared risks. These ten routes are not ten independent investment opportunities.

## Current limit

There is no justified basis here to promise that one of these drugs is more likely than not to pass Phase II, or that spending the planning envelope will probably return it with profit. The practical result is a narrower set of decision-changing diligence packages, explicit ways to disprove them, and a reusable team structure. A research lead becomes a development candidate only after the clinical and execution gates are met; a development candidate becomes an investment recommendation only after the financial comparison is supported.
