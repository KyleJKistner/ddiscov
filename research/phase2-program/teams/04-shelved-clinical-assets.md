# 04 — Clinically characterized assets with a revised indication

**Reviewed 8 November 2026.** Coordinator `/root/c04_shelved_clinical_assets` used evidence worker `/root/c04_shelved_clinical_assets/evidence` and independent challenger `/root/c04_shelved_clinical_assets/challenge`. Both completed primary-source desk research. No outreach, transaction, experiment or development spending occurred.

## Decision

**Lead: oral mavoglurant / AFQ056 / STP7 for adult cocaine use disorder (CUD).** It has actual randomized evidence in the revised indication after failure in fragile X syndrome (FXS). The opportunity is a potentially useful contribution to an existing STALICLA-controlled program; it is not an unowned molecule to acquire cheaply. **Alternative: oral saracatinib / AZD0530 for active adult fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva (FOP).** Its completed randomized study makes recovery of existing results more valuable than commissioning a duplicate study. Neither is an investment recommendation.

## Why this route could improve selection

Mavoglurant offers a prospective placebo comparison in the exact new indication, reducing reliance on animal rescue or a rescued post-hoc subgroup. Saracatinib offers a causal human disease target plus an already completed controlled study. These advantages justify information diligence, not numerical odds. A larger clinically meaningful benefit, reproducibility, appropriate comparator/background care, interpretable missingness, and acceptable chronic safety remain decisive. Secondary outcomes and repeated publications do not multiply the evidence; assess multiplicity in the complete analysis plan.

A forecast start date does not prove Phase III initiation. Current complete development status and any available participation terms remain unresolved.

**First priority:** prepare a request-ready scope for STP7's complete efficacy/safety report, denominator reconciliation, missing-data sensitivity, interaction results, regulatory endpoint plan and a specific unmet program contribution. Price only a contribution that could earn an explicit retained interest or contracted payment. Proposed data/product participation is a hypothesis; no available offer, price or investor-return distribution was found.

**Second priority:** recover STOPFOP's complete randomized analysis and clarify material, data, regulatory-reference and commercial rights. [AstraZeneca's clinical-molecule program](https://openinnovation.astrazeneca.com/clinical-research/clinical-molecules.html) is directed to academic researchers and excludes startups/biotechs; a listing is not a commercial license or secured funding. A separate workable arrangement would be required.

**Advance:** interpretable clinically useful benefit and safety, plus an attainable, priced contribution with financeable continuation. Clinicians must establish the meaningful-effect requirement before a new study. **Stop:** unfavorable benefit–risk, no differentiated contribution, or unavailable rights/economics. **Inconclusive:** incomplete reports or imprecise benefit; obtain the missing analysis before funding more clinical work. Maximum exposure, schedule and retained cash remain unknown. The approximately $500,000 envelope is not assumed sufficient for a new Phase II/III program or likely capital recovery.
