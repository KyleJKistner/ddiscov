# 06 — Ocular local delivery and useful corneal repair

**Reviewed 8 November 2026.** Coordinator `/root/c06_ocular_local_delivery`; completed workers `/root/c06_ocular_local_delivery/evidence` (human insulin evidence) and `/root/c06_ocular_local_delivery/challenge` (independent preclinical, comparator and capture challenge). Completed work is desk research and source verification. Product qualification, commercial diligence and clinical work remain prospective.

**Lead: P2-06-INSULIN-PED**, a specified topical-insulin product for refractory noninfectious persistent corneal epithelial defects (PED), with neurotrophic and other etiologies separated prospectively. The starting presentation is the Actrapid-derived 1 IU/mL glycol-vehicle product in the historical comparison below; another insulin, vehicle or commercial presentation needs its own bridge. This is a new program identifier for an existing comparator opportunity, not an assertion of ownership.

**Alternative: R4-L01, RM4404**, micellar WAY262611 for useful recovery of injury-related corneal hypoesthesia. Its sensory-function hypothesis is more differentiated than another closure-only product, but substantially less clinically de-risked. Neither lead is investment-ready.

## Evidence and limits

The following primary sources were checked on the review date; access limits qualify the claims.

| Exact use/source | Observed evidence | Contrary finding or limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Topical insulin/PED; [Diaz-Valle et al., online August 2021 / 2022 issue](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/aos.14997) | Historical comparison: 61 insulin patients versus 23 autologous-serum patients; closure in 51/61 versus 11/23. The regular-insulin product used the authors' stated glycol vehicle. | Full text inspected. Different calendar cohorts, exclusions for incomplete records and background care can confound benefit. Closure-time comparison concerns healers only. Follow-up differed substantially, weakening recurrence comparisons. |
| Different insulin product/PED; [Lixi et al., 3 July 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40123-026-01446-6) | Retrospective 45 eyes/43 patients; 28 chronic ocular-surface and 17 stage-2 neurotrophic eyes. Humalog-derived 1 IU/mL product; 44 eyes closed, median 29 days. | Full text inspected. Corneal sensitivity categories remained unchanged. Two recurrences included one melting event requiring surgery. Continued supportive care and selection prevent causal attribution. This analog/vehicle is not an independent replication of the lead's exact product. |
| Insulin/acute diabetic surgical defects; [Dasrilsyah et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37267451/) | Randomized double-blind comparison with sodium hyaluronate supports an acute epithelial-healing signal. | Primary indexed abstract refreshed; direct reopening was a shell. The prior [Round 20 audit](../../rounds/round20/migf-comparator-update.md) supplies detailed counts. Surgically induced acute defects are not chronic refractory PED. |
| RM4404/mouse injury; [Li et al., 14 June 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13266094/) | Full text refreshed. Chemical-injury early-treatment arm directly compared structural measures with vehicle, n=5/group, and mechanosensation, n=6/group; an off-treatment sensory observation supported persistence. | Blink threshold alone does not establish comfortable protective sensation, visual function or human repair. Early treatment cannot establish delayed-treatment durability. Product exposure, independent replication and clinical benefit remain unestablished. |

The additional [Padda 2026 report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41453402/) was available at indexed-abstract level: 29 eyes/28 patients received a different, 25 IU/mL product. It is not pooled with the 1 IU/mL records. Likewise, repeated publications and bilateral eyes do not increase independent patient counts.

The challenger also refreshed the [mIGF-Gel paper, July 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13400331/). Its rat acute-closure analyses report n=5 and delayed/sensory analyses n=3; it has material controls but no direct insulin comparator. Keep `MIGF_CORNEA` as a secondary product option pending useful differentiation. Retention and reduced administration frequency in animals do not establish lower human treatment burden.

Its estimated September completion date has passed, which does **not** establish actual completion or results.

These are competitive programs, not efficacy outcomes. Establish a differentiated contribution before duplicating their clinical question.

## Phase II thesis and next commitment

Insulin's actual human epithelial outcomes remove some uncertainty faced by animal-only repair programs. They do not establish a randomized effect in the intended chronic population, an exact-product regulatory bridge or above-reference-class Phase II odds. A relevant comparator class concerns local ophthalmic development for persistent defects, not all approved insulin uses. Closure, durable closure, vision and sensation are distinct outcomes.

**First action:** prepare an exact-product and clinical-contribution proposition: transferable presentation/data, intended etiology, existing-trial overlap, meaningful maintained closure and patient-function endpoints, practical comparator, and a possible paid or licensed contribution. Clinicians and statisticians must establish worthwhile effect and precision before commissioning a study. For RM4404, require an interpretable article/exposure and independent useful-function route before pricing preclinical work.

**Advance** when product/data access, a differentiated clinical question, attainable economic interest, bounded quote and financeable continuation coexist. **Stop or narrow** if capable controlled evidence excludes worthwhile benefit, ocular harm offsets benefit, or product/rights costs defeat the retained-value case. **Inconclusive** exposure, adherence, endpoint or recruitment findings justify only a separately priced finite remedy, not an efficacy conclusion.

Proposed capture is a product/data license, paid development contribution or contractual participation. Current rights, willingness to pay, full development cost, maximum exposure and follow-on funding are unknown. Cheap insulin ingredients do not establish cheap sterile supply or investor returns. The approximately $500,000 envelope is not a verified clinical-program budget. Prioritize insulin contribution feasibility, then RM4404's differentiated sensory option; reverse that order only with materially stronger accessible evidence or terms. Neither has a numerical Phase II or capital-recovery probability assigned.
