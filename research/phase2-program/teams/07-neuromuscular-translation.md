# 07 — Neuromuscular translation and useful human function

**Reviewed 8 November 2026.** Coordinator `/root/c07_neuromuscular_translation` integrated `/root/c07_neuromuscular_translation/evidence` and `/root/c07_neuromuscular_translation/challenge`. Both completed independent source work. This is a desk-research decision; no material, model, license or development work was commissioned.

## Decision

**Lead for a bounded information package: exact 8015-P2 / MFN2-related CMT2A**, existing ID `R3-NM-01`, initially tied to a stated genotype and useful motor/sensory claim. **Alternative: BLU-3797 / MTM1-related XLMTM**, ID `BLU3797_XLMTM`, with a stated on-treatment muscle-function claim.

P2 is the narrow scientific lead here because exact-article treatment after established deficits produced motor, sensory and electrophysiological improvements in knock-in animals. BLU has complementary genetic target support and a more developed repository qualification plan. Neither has demonstrated exact-article human functional efficacy, a qualified clinical exposure/safety window or an attainable priced interest. This information priority does not replace the portfolio's financial comparison or imply either is more likely than not to pass Phase II.

## What could raise Phase II confidence

Both programs connect causal human disease biology to functional animal observations. That supports a focused translation question, not a calibrated uplift over matched neuromuscular Phase II programs. P2 needs exact-isomer benefit at interpretable exposure in a disease-dependent human functional system. BLU needs the same connection from selective inhibition to meaningful contractile benefit, including a credible treatment window. Broad all-genotype efficacy, respiratory rescue and permanent repair remain separate claims.

**Next action:** prepare a comparative article-and-human-function feasibility package. For P2, identify authenticated material, a relevant functional deficit and an operator able to measure benefit and liability at interpretable exposure. Price this separately from building a new model. For BLU, price the existing [qualification plan](../../rounds/round8/blu3797-qualification-package.md) against the same decision standard. Establish meaningful preclinical effect/precision prospectively; clinicians must separately define clinically meaningful benefit before a trial.

- **Advance:** accessible exact article, reproducible relevant deficit, interpretable exposure and a feasible independently confirmable function comparison, with bounded cost and usable rights. A later positive supports the tested article/background only.
- **Stop or narrow:** a capable comparison excludes worthwhile benefit at adequate exposure, unacceptable liability prevents useful exposure, or no affordable retainable contribution exists.
- **Inconclusive:** material, exposure or model failure prevents interpretation. Permit only a separately justified finite remedy; do not call target failure or efficacy.
