## What this changes about Phase II confidence

Azetukalner demonstrates the value of exact-population randomized evidence, a dose response, replication and tolerability assessment. Selecting an asset after Phase II answers a different investment question from predicting which preclinical drug will pass it. Its historical success cannot validate GA002, all Kv7 modulators, or another indication. A clinical benchmark also does not establish achievable returns at today's entry terms.

GA002 still needs independent controlled evidence, complete safety accounting and product/procedure characterization. Its larger uncontrolled percentage reduction cannot be ranked against azetukalner's placebo-controlled effect. Compare clinically useful seizure control, cognition, treatment burden and serious harms on appropriate designs, not raw cross-trial percentages. No new numerical success probability is assigned to either program.
