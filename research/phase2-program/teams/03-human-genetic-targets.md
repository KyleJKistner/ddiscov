# 03 — Human genetics and direction-matched drugs

**Review:** 8 November 2026, America/Los_Angeles. **Status:** completed first-pass desk research; no development or outreach authorized. Coordinator `/root/c03_human_genetic_targets` integrated completed workers `/root/c03_human_genetic_targets/evidence` and `/root/c03_human_genetic_targets/challenge`.

## Decision

**Lead:** oral alpelisib for molecularly confirmed activating **TEK** venous malformations, with a meaningful symptom/function claim. This is distinct from approved PIK3CA-related overgrowth spectrum (PROS), the repository's `historical-topics:T050` comparator. **Alternative:** oral baricitinib for genetically confirmed **CANDLE/PRAAS**, treating inflammatory disease control rather than genetic correction. Neither is an investment-ready package or has a defensible numerical Phase II probability.

The lead's immediate question is whether a differentiated TEK-specific contribution is needed by an existing program. A current Phase II study already covers this pathway; starting another generic proof-of-concept effort has no demonstrated incremental value.

## Phase II thesis and decisive task

Human pathway causality, direction-matched pharmacology and observed patient benefit make both leads more informative than a mechanism-only hypothesis. They do not establish improvement over a matched Phase II reference class: controlled effect, long-term tolerability, endpoint reliability, recruitment and product-specific exposure remain consequential.

**First task:** prepare a TEK-specific contribution brief against VATCH's current scope: reconcile patient-level literature lineage, identify an unfilled symptom/function or exposure question, and define the data/product deliverable a program partner could use. Establish clinically meaningful benefit, precision and acceptable safety with clinicians before designing a study. Radiological shrinkage alone is insufficient for a symptom/function claim.

- **Advance:** a documented unmet contribution, feasible interpretable endpoint, accessible source data/material and an attainable economic interest support pricing a bounded milestone.
- **Stop:** existing funded work fully supplies the contribution, no party values it, required rights are unavailable, or plausible exposure/safety cannot support the intended benefit.
- **Inconclusive:** inaccessible patient-level records, unresolved product/design discrepancy or uncertain endpoint reliability; acquire the named artifact before proposing experiments.

## Retained value and reversal

Proposed economics are a paid data/analysis contribution or negotiated participation with an existing program. Historical compassionate supply and trial collaboration establish neither current access nor commercial terms. Material, data, regulatory-reference and commercial rights require separate confirmation. Price, maximum committed exposure, retained share and follow-on funding are **unknown**; no portion of the approximately $500,000 envelope is allocated.

Rank the TEK contribution brief first and a CANDLE exposure/safety-plus-endpoint feasibility brief second. A usable CANDLE partnership with better terms could reverse this order. This is an information priority, not a financial ranking: no current evidence establishes a high probability of recovering invested capital, and preserving capital remains the comparison.
