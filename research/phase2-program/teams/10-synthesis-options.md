## Phase II implication

FD-2's functional animal outcomes make it more informative than a target-only idea, but early SOD1-model benefit does not establish a recruitable human early-treatment population, relevant exposure, chronic safety or efficacy across ALS mechanisms. HJ-02 offers a distinct repair hypothesis, but needs a direct injured-control functional effect, durability and an acceptable rhythm/safety profile. Neither supports an above-reference-class or greater-than-50% Phase II probability. Probability of reaching such a trial from today is a separate, unresolved question.

## Smallest useful commitment

First prepare a **paired access-and-evidence comparison**, without ordering synthesis: FD-2's exact final article, existing exposure/safety package and available rights versus OHSU's exact HJ-02 article, cardiac-field option, raw functional data and subsequent development work. HJ-02 can outrank FD-2 if its documented invitation yields materially better usable terms. A peptide's short length does not establish a cheap clinical product.

For either surviving package, define one independently interpretable human-relevant functional milestone and obtain an all-in quote that includes article qualification and exposure interpretation. Clinicians and relevant specialists must establish the meaningful-effect requirement before study execution; do not infer it from the original small animal experiment.

**Advance:** exact article and necessary rights are obtainable, the milestone can discriminate useful benefit, and a bounded price plus financeable continuation supports retained value. **Stop or re-scope:** rights are unavailable, adequate exposure lacks useful function, safety defeats the intended window, or the next step becomes open-ended delivery/model discovery. **Inconclusive:** article, exposure, model capability or missing raw data prevents interpretation; consider only a separately priced finite remedy.

Proposed capture is an evaluation option convertible to appropriate development/commercial rights, a field license or a paid co-development contribution. Current prices, maximum committed exposure, retained share, payer/partner interest and follow-on financing remain unknown. The approximately $500,000 planning envelope is not a verified development budget; loss limit, reserve and liquidity horizon remain open. Neither lead currently supports a high-probability capital-recovery claim.
