## Phase II thesis

- Precisely defined clinical-success event and intended next regulatory stage.
- Relevant historical reference class and selection limitations.
- What asset-specific evidence could improve its odds; strongest alternative explanation.
- Separate uncertainty about reaching Phase II, clinical benefit in Phase II, and sponsor progression.
- Numerical estimate only if justified; source, interval/range, conditioning, horizon and unfavorable sensitivity. Otherwise write unknown.
- Proposed endpoint, comparator, population/marker, exposure and safety assessment; feasibility and required clinical/statistical/regulatory review.

## Smallest decisive next task

- Artifact/deliverable, owner, dependencies and completion condition.
- Advance condition; stop condition; inconclusive condition and retest limit.
- Clinically meaningful effect and precision requirements to establish before a study.
- Cost basis: actual quote / estimate / unknown; date, exclusions, full exposure to stopping point, termination obligations and time.

## Retained value and capital

- Required material, research, data, regulatory and commercial rights; holder and evidence of availability.
- Proposed attainable economic interest; actual offered terms separately.
- Payer/buyer/partner, why this deliverable could matter, evidence of willingness to transact.
- Full path to retained cash, funding needed after the next milestone, dilution/royalties, and funding-unavailable branch.
- Success / negative / inconclusive / financing-unavailable retained-cash scenarios; probability of recovering cumulative cash by horizon if defensible, otherwise unknown.
- Cumulative loss, reserve, liquidity and correlated exposure constraints; unresolved user inputs.
- Ranked next action and explicit reversal condition.
