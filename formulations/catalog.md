# Formulation catalog — reviewed 3 November 2026

Historical reference. Use [current opportunities](../decisions/current-shortlist.md) for active decisions.

The screen used reported FY2025 sales to identify 50 high-selling brands or franchises for study and two additional brands near the cutoff. Currency conversion, fiscal year and alliance accounting can affect the order near the boundary. Mounjaro and Zepbound share tirzepatide, and Ozempic and Wegovy share semaglutide. The 50 core entries represent 48 screening families under those two merges. A franchise can contain several active ingredients or presentations.

Five questions warranted closer study. **Xolair** offers a measurable tradeoff between device capacity and antibody concentration. **Xtandi** has a defined usability comparison for people who still struggle with smaller tablets. **Rinvoq** requires an exposure measurement to establish whether rapid transit creates a delivery problem. **Eylea HD** and **Vabysmo** require preserved vision and retinal safety alongside any increase in time between injections. These are proposed comparisons. The recorded work was a public-source screen.

The catalog below is alphabetical. Public sources are linked for each entry. [Download the structured register](comparisons.json).
