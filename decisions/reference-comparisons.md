## Interpreting a study proposal

Four facts can change independently. **Biology** is the observed result for the specified intervention, disease system and endpoint. **Feasibility** covers the materials, operator, comparator and assay needed to obtain an interpretable result. **Contribution** identifies the decision the work would change and who could use it. **Transfer** covers permitted material use, ownership and release of the resulting evidence, and rights to the therapeutic asset where required.

A well-designed experiment can establish feasibility while the commercial role is still open. Conversely, a serious partner may already have the most informative experiment underway. For each case, scope the actual contribution and its study-specific resources before assigning development priority.

For icerguastat, the archived estimate shows how to bound a study cost. A patient line and its corrected control, each tested across three differentiation batches and four conditions, would yield 24 RNA samples from a single donor background. At the public external academic rates reviewed on 3 November 2026, 24 sequencing libraries at $300–350 and six baseline specimens prepared for electron microscopy at $300 each give a two-service subtotal of **$9,000–10,200**. The corresponding industry subtotal was **$11,940–14,580**. Cell access, differentiation, compound qualification, exposure measurements, microscopy time and the conduction experiment itself still require pricing.
