# Functional proof and marginal-dollar selection policy

Effective 4 November 2026. This policy governs the current shortlist. Earlier comparisons and research memos remain a record of what was considered and why a conclusion changed.

## Unit of selection

Specify the exact article, disease and genotype, target compartment, route, treatment timing and useful functional endpoint. Separate the value of a biological target, the quality of a particular molecule, and readiness to execute the next experiment.

An experimental proof may be worth funding before a finished drug asset is ready for licensing. Those are different decisions and must have different labels.

## Evidence requirements

For every serious lead, record:

- Primary source, DOI or study identifier, publication date, verification date and underlying dataset lineage.
- Exact molecule, stereochemistry, sequence, construct, formulation and relevant batch evidence.
- Biological unit and denominator. Keep donors, animals, eyes, clones, tissues, fields and repeated readings separate. Independently assigned tissues can be exposure units; that does not make them independent donors or make repeated twitches independent tissues.
- Baseline deficit, treatment timing, comparator, exposure, useful functional effect and adverse evidence.
- Missing observations, attrition, assay ceilings, exclusions, ambiguous units and source inconsistencies when they affect the decision.
- What would most strongly falsify the proposed product thesis.

Do not count a conference abstract, later paper and patent using the same cohort as independent replications. A related compound, mixture, successor, salt or delivery system does not automatically inherit exact-article efficacy or safety.

## Availability must have a specific meaning

Record these independently:

| Question | Acceptable description |
|---|---|
| Is the article defined? | Public structure/sequence and analytical or construct specification. |
| Can it be reconstructed? | A route with identifiable inputs and qualification work, distinguishing a joined public route from a reproduced synthesis. |
| Is there a sourcing route? | Current catalog or documented manufacturing/distribution route. A quote page, service offer or request-distributed line is labeled as such. |
| Is there a physical lot? | Received material or explicit stock evidence. A literature mention does not answer this. |
| Is the disease model ready? | Demonstrated disease-dependent function and controls appropriate to the claimed stage: one qualified disease/control background can support exploratory work; independent backgrounds are required for confirmatory generality. A catalog entry alone is a starting material. |
| Are commercial rights understood? | Specific public claims, licenses and active programs with their dates and scope. Research supply does not establish commercial clearance. |

Classify the source itself before extracting an availability claim. A recruitment wishlist, future grant aim, archived product, conference plan and current distributed model are different types of evidence. Check corrections and dated sponsor/investigator reports as well as registry labels.

An owner or author reply is not a prerequisite for scientific selection. If public evidence cannot define a transferable article or interpretable experiment, narrow the claim, qualify an independent reconstruction, or move the lead to reserve. State the unresolved fact precisely instead of labeling the whole program 'unavailable'.

## Promotion and stopping

Promote a **bounded proof** when an exact starting article and plausible model or clinical route can be specified, the experiment measures useful function, the active comparison changes a development decision, and advance/stop criteria are explicit. Start with material, formulation or model qualification if those are the most informative uncertainties.

Require independent challenge before inclusion in the current core shortlist. Rank the value and tractability of the next proof. Do not rank by publication recency, mention count or an uncalibrated numerical success probability. New combinations need component controls and added value over the strongest single component.

Use these dispositions consistently:

- **Leading staged proof:** a compelling question with explicit qualification and functional milestones.
- **Defined clinical development proof:** existing human evidence justifies article/exposure or controlled-clinical qualification with a specific product question.
- **New independent-validation priority:** a strong new signal that needs replication before joining the core shortlist.
- **Conditional reserve:** a specific open question and reopening criterion remain.
- **Chemistry or product-development project:** target biology is useful but the present article does not meet the intended product need.
- **Comparator or active external program:** valuable for benchmarking, with a distinct contribution required for a new project.
- **Hold or stop:** state the missing evidence or explicit negative result that controls the decision.

## Fund the next uncertainty at the appropriate stage

Use separate judgments for initial qualification, exploratory function, confirmatory translation and a larger asset/company investment. A defined article, plausible model or clinical route, strong rationale and a finite decision can justify material/model qualification before human efficacy exists. Do not require the result of an experiment as a prerequisite for funding that experiment.

When exact-disease human treatment observations already exist, fund the uncertainty appropriate to that stage. Pediatric article/exposure qualification or a controlled clinical comparison can be the next proof. A new cell or animal rescue experiment is optional when it discriminates a consequential article, tissue, mechanism or safety question; it is not a compulsory reset to an earlier evidence stage.

An initial qualified disease/control background can decide whether a larger replication effort is worthwhile. It cannot establish genotype generality. Independent first-background confirmation can support a narrow claim; additional independent backgrounds are required for broader generality. A prespecified useful effect and relevant exposure belong to confirmation; avoid imposing the complete development package before a first feasibility result. Record the smallest informative deliverable and what happens if it succeeds, fails or is inconclusive.

Scientifically worthwhile clinical work, a differentiated product and a proprietary company are separate opportunities. A generic or an active external program can still support valuable research, but a new developer must identify its contribution. Do not silently replace an intended drug-development opportunity with an academic evidence-generation project; label the different scope and value-capture limits explicitly. Comparable efficacy with a meaningful improvement in tolerability, burden, delivery or another intended benefit can be valuable; superiority on every endpoint is unnecessary.

Useful ongoing control or early developmental preservation can qualify on the intended indication. Permanent off-treatment cure and reversal of every established deficit are not universal requirements. Retain those distinctions in wording and endpoint selection.

Stop expanding a desktop checklist once the next finite funding decision is clear. Reopen it for new evidence likely to change the target, exact article, model, window or product contribution. A negative assay is decisive only for a claim the qualified assay can test; an unpriced execution detail is not evidence against the biology.

## S-tier standard

Require a large useful effect in the intended clinical claim—restoration, preservation or ongoing control—plus independent or orthogonal support, reproducible material, plausible exposure, differentiation from current care, no unresolved fatal safety contradiction at effective exposure, and a compelling next milestone with plausible value capture. A preventive preservation product can qualify on its intended clinical use, but must demonstrate durable useful native-organ, graft or manufacturing benefit and cannot be described as reversal of established disease.

S-tier is not a quota or a guarantee of investor interest. Unanswered commercial terms do not defeat a strong scientific proof, while an interesting experiment does not establish a financeable clinical product.

## Distinguish target, molecule and assay failure

A gene-corrected line made before differentiation can establish disease causality while preventing the deficit. It does not establish that an already-deficient tissue can recover. Before using a negative delayed-treatment result to reject a target, demonstrate a functional positive intervention after the same qualified deficit. A ready transport or imaging assay is not automatically a ready force, conduction or barrier-repair assay.

Require a measurable baseline and a stable or predictable untreated trajectory. Perfectly flat tissue function is unnecessary when contemporaneous controls and baseline adjustment can distinguish a treatment effect from maturation. Keep developmental compensation, preservation and mature reversal as separate claims; useful preservation is not discarded merely because it is not reversal.

Prespecify both an assay-repeatability threshold and a minimum useful effect. Use a qualified corrected-disease or explicitly nonisogenic reference gap, a practical active comparator and the intended product benefit to inform usefulness. A nonisogenic gap is not a genetically established rescuable deficit. Lock any numerical threshold and its calibration before treatment decoding. Preserve donor and batch structure in uncertainty estimates. A precise trivial effect is not enough; an imprecise estimate crossing the useful threshold is inconclusive rather than proven failure.

## Match exposure, chemical species and the selected population

Equal unbound concentrations across cultured tissues estimate intrinsic sensitivity. A systemic window additionally depends on measured or suitably bounded distribution into muscle, brain, liver and other relevant compartments, including active metabolites. Unknown distribution leaves a concerning culture overlap unresolved; it does not establish either in-vivo toxicity or safe tissue exclusion.

The bottle label, nominal added amount, chemical species present in the assay, and active species reaching a patient are distinct. Redox interconversion, salt form, congener composition, metabolites and a combination product must remain attached to the evidence. A biological signal can be real while its transferable chemical article is not yet defined.

Keep all-treated, completer and responder-enriched populations visible. Discontinuation for insufficient response can bias the apparent effect among remaining participants. A positive paired result without a concurrent control does not isolate treatment causality, and subsequent background-treatment changes prevent a simple durability claim. Record submitted-result status, draft labels and internal inconsistencies without treating an uploaded report as peer-reviewed confirmation.

## Numerical provenance and asymmetric evidence

Mark each numerical criterion as a published observation, formula-derived reference, proposed engineering limit or calibration-dependent unknown. A proposed25% gap target, occupancy characterization level or safety review trigger is not a validated clinical threshold. Keep missing inputs null in templates; automated checks must not turn unknown provenance or denominators into a passing gate.

A credible randomized functional positive can justify replication while a mechanistic assay or delayed positive control is unfinished. Negative target inference requires the stronger exposure, perturbation, precision and model-modifiability evidence appropriate to its scope. A bounded target downgrade must not become universal target rejection in a machine-readable flag.

Apply product contribution and public-rights standards equally to BLU and marketed/generic clinical leaders. Data-generation value, a differentiated therapeutic and an exclusive company asset remain separate decisions.

## Round 9: perturbation, stress context and comparator exposure

Separate genetic deletion, catalytic inactivation, protein depletion and pharmacological inhibition. Preserve allele/construct, timing, tissue, residual protein and catalytic readouts. A kinase-defective add-back can reveal scaffold-related effects in its studied system without proving the same mechanism in another tissue. Stable protein abundance alone does not guarantee preserved scaffold interactions.

Baseline tolerability and isolated-cell negatives cannot waive a demonstrated stress-context question. A negative model must be capable of detecting the relevant perturbation before it supports safety reassurance or target rejection. Near-complete persistent genetic inactivation does not define a safe occupancy threshold for a reversible drug. The BLU C2α bridge remains open; the new evidence does not show that BLU itself produces the genetic-study liability. See the [target/safety review](../research/rounds/round9/blu-target-safety-update.md) and [manual qualification amendments](../research/rounds/round9/qualification-amendments.json).

Keep exact drug, formulation, genotype, disease stage and target compartment attached to exposure comparisons. Total tumor exposure, a related human-tested inhibitor, a preclinical probe and a sponsor-renamed program must not exchange efficacy, safety or clinical-readiness claims. Selected known responders cannot estimate an all-treated clinical response probability. Independent investigator replication, different measurement methods and a new biological model are also different forms of evidence.

The [Round 9 screens](../research/rounds/round9/new-candidate-screen.md) apply these rules to miglustat/SCARB2 and the distinct TRPV4 articles. Planning allocations remain judgments about research effort. They are neither purchases nor experimental authorization.

## Round 10: analyte, source conflict and analysis population

Attach each exposure value to its exact parent or metabolite, formulation, species and age, compartment, bound/free basis, sampling time, statistic and denominator. Single-dose radioactivity share, repeated-dose plasma concentration, trough ratio, tumor partition and free normal-tissue exposure are different quantities. Do not add separate peak summaries as a simultaneous active-moiety concentration or transfer activity between R172K and R140Q. The [IDH ledger](../research/rounds/round10/idh-exposure-ledger.md) applies this rule and closes the present CNS-superiority comparison at its identified evidence ceiling.

When a primary source conflicts internally, preserve both passages and their locators. Correct the current interpretation without silently rewriting historical evidence. A source saying “not found” in one section cannot settle absence when another section reports the analyte. Table/prose disagreements are unresolved source conflicts unless a primary correction establishes which is right. Failed access and a bounded search do not prove biological absence.

Keep described people, treated people, completers, analysis populations, selected responders and related family members separate. An enrolled person who never received treatment cannot silently enter the exposed denominator. A comparison against cerebral-palsy trajectories is not a GNAO1 control. An existing inventory entry that already links a result remains an existing-use review even when the result first receives a current disposition. See the [new-use screens](../research/rounds/round10/new-candidate-screen.md) and [ManNAc review](../research/rounds/round10/mannac-controlled-result.md).

Patient function, biomarker correction and causal efficacy remain distinct. A controlled primary result outweighs an uncontrolled mechanistic story for the same defined claim, while an imprecise negative does not prove a zero effect. Historical care sequences do not validate a dose regimen or drug ranking. Comparator care should match the proposed product claim and retain background care, rescue burden and maintenance attrition; it need not expand every early proof into an indiscriminate multiarm program. See the [HHD comparator review](../research/rounds/round10/hhd-active-care-comparator.md).

## Round 15: expected value of the next marginal dollar

For a funding or evidence-sprint decision, compare feasible next tranches by the change they can make to the best continuation, including stopping, deferring or choosing another opportunity. Include remaining costs, time, useful evidence and capturable value. Sunk effort, historical priority and a planning allocation do not determine marginal return. Preserve historical numbers with an explicit historical label; do not invent replacement weights or a financial rank when required inputs are unknown.

Separate patient benefit, scientific information, an improved product and private financial capture. A good proof can deserve scientific support without establishing an exclusive asset. Conversely, a first new-indication development package can create a useful contribution using an adequate existing article; a new formulation is not universally required. Data custody, publication, regulatory use/reference, manufacturing and commercial rights are different facts.

Missing public qualification is not observed incumbent failure. A new presentation must identify a meaningful incremental need and comparator; an existing presentation's adequacy must also be demonstrated for the intended use. Evaluate current regulatory incentives against the exact product and statutory conditions. A designation, potential pathway or published patent is not an approval, award, license or revenue forecast.

End a source pass once its finite spending decision is clear. A subsequent review needs a named new artifact or materially changed fact likely to alter the decision. Unknown commercial terms can block a specific commitment without making an owner reply a scientific-selection prerequisite. The [Round 15 selection](../research/rounds/round15/marginal-dollar-selection.md) and [decision](../research/rounds/round15/funding-decision.md) apply this rule.

## Round 16: claim-specific qualification and scope changes

For APR-246/AEC, the [current decision](../research/rounds/round16/marginal-dollar-decision.md) expressly supersedes the historical universal withdrawal-persistence gate when the intended product is useful ongoing erosion control. Perfect cornification and a particular mechanism are not universal requirements. Meaningful benefit, adequate tissue integrity and acceptable exposure remain necessary. Full barrier restoration and drug-free regeneration are stronger claims requiring their own evidence. Historical package text remains unchanged as provenance.

An independent review's conditional ordinal priority can guide a bounded sprint while quantitative financial inputs remain missing. Record the objective, assumptions, fresh evidence and reversal conditions. An illustrative small article/model tranche is not a verified budget or full-development estimate. Major new formulation, model or rights work changes its scope and must trigger a new marginal comparison, including deferral. Published model existence does not establish access, native fidelity, exact-drug responsiveness or price.
