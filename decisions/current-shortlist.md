## BLU3797: why it stays first

### Substantial target rationale, bounded exact-molecule evidence

Protein-retaining PIK3C2B kinase inactivation rescues mouse muscle force. The heterozygous intervention improved survival/hanging without recovering the same force endpoints; it cannot be treated as equivalent force rescue or converted into an occupancy percentage. Independent inducible genetic work supports a post-onset disease component. These results strengthen the target rationale without supplying exact-BLU human efficacy. [2023 catalytic study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10243799/) · [Integrated biology review](../research/rounds/round5/round5_blu_biology.md)

The 2025 phosphatase-dead MTM1 knock-in and earlier local protein-delivery studies are compatible once context is retained. They caution against making one bulk-lipid measurement the success criterion. A newer mTOR intervention supplies a within-disease force comparator, although its nonrandomized, unblinded design and survivor selection limit inference. Neither cross-study superiority nor chronic pediatric suitability is established. [Catalytic-loss study](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/189286) · [mTOR study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42255-026-01484-1)

The public chemistry now connects a specified S intermediate to the paper's final transformations. No independent batch has been made or authenticated here. Mass/mole/yield entries need reconciliation; route disclosure does not establish commercial clearance. [Primary BLU study](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/198568) · [Pharmacology and route review](../research/rounds/round5/round5_blu_pharmacology.md)

### What the expanded audit changes

An independent parser matched **887 populated cells across eleven additional sheets**. The cellular response concerns fusion/differentiation, not measured human force. Figure 5E–F intentionally concerns VPS34/PIK3C3-knockout cells: the labels are consistent and cannot be reinterpreted as direct human MTM1 lipidomic normalization. Earlier ceiling-limited hanging, regimen-label and animal-linkage limitations remain. [Cell audit](../research/rounds/round5/blu3797-cell-context-audit.json) · [Earlier audit](../research/rounds/round4/blu3797-audit.md)

Three quantitative qualifications matter. A finite published VPS34 NanoBRET IC50 is unreconciled with all-zero released values. The 28 nM biochemical IC50 sits near half the nominal 60 nM enzyme concentration, warranting tight-binding interpretation. Released plasma measurements lack sampling hours and a demonstrated unbound fraction, so they cannot independently establish trough coverage, a human dose or muscle/brain separation. These are different uncertainties; none is an observed clinical toxicity. [Exact assay and exposure audit](../research/rounds/round5/round5_blu_pharmacology.md)

A separate public inhibitor series provides a second-chemistry route. Compound 47 has a more useful functional selectivity profile than compound 91 in that disclosure. It still needs cellular qualification and cannot inherit BLU efficacy. [WO2025003330A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2025003330A1/en)

### The human proof is specified

Curi's p.L463S model is a standalone patient line, not a listed isogenic pair. A separate published study supplies healthy/MTM1-knockout and patient p.E48*/corrected pairs. Coriell fibroblasts provide other starting backgrounds. These make a controlled model route concrete while leaving permissions, received-lot identity, maturation and force qualification separate. [Curi model](https://www.curibio.com/xlmtm-model) · [Human isogenic model](https://academic.oup.com/brain/article/149/1/317/8217423)

The [integrated human proof](../research/rounds/round5/blu3797-human-proof.md) requires a deficit before treatment, a predictable untreated trajectory and a positive intervention demonstrating recovery after the same deficit. Prior genetic correction can prove causality while preventing the defect. Preserve absolute force, fatigue, biological donor/batch structure and a prespecified minimum useful effect. Target markers accompany function; they cannot override it.

Connect effective muscle exposure to exposure in vulnerable tissues. Equal culture concentrations measure intrinsic sensitivity; tissue distribution is needed to interpret a systemic window. Neural genetic susceptibility and MTM1 hepatobiliary disease motivate those measurements without proving BLU toxicity. The [independent proof challenge](../research/rounds/round5/round5_blu_proof_challenge.md) is incorporated.

## Existing leaders and local challengers

**Exact 8015-P2:** a new isogenic R94Q motor-neuron model has transport defects but no overt patch-clamp disease deficit at the tested age. A separate patient/isogenic study has abnormal network bursting. Neither becomes a ready transmission/force assay merely because it is operational. A current CMTA/PsychoGenics R364W rat route supplies a second-allele challenge, with model rights and capacity kept separate. [JCI model](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/200106) · [Patient/isogenic study](https://academic.oup.com/brain/article/144/8/2471/6299386) · [Leader update](../research/rounds/round5/round5_existing_leader_updates.md)

The recovered supplement contains **75 binding and 12 enzyme/cell assay rows**. MAO-A's 59% result is binding displacement; hERG's −14% is binding, not electrophysiology. A screening-dose/fusion-EC50 ratio does not establish universal selectivity or clinical safety. The supplement contains no animal-level efficacy data. Final DMSO matching also needs explicit specification in replication. [Panel audit](../research/rounds/round5/p2-off-target-audit.md)

**NDC-9009:** the official dantrolene results are now read. Sixteen adults started oral Dantrium; ten completed and entered paired analysis. The manuscript flow diagram assigns all six discontinuations to insufficient response. PGA and quality of life improved among completers; EASI and involved body area did not reach significance. There was no control or blinding. Retinoid washout and later restart confound attribution and durability. The final-submitted report remains internally draft-labelled. [Official trial](https://euclinicaltrials.eu/ctis-public/view/2024-519201-36-00) · [Result retrieval and interpretation](../research/rounds/round5/round5_ndc_comparator_update.md)

That supplies a real adult benchmark, not topical exposure or direct barrier recovery. NDC's cell evidence still concerns junction assembly in three donors and two variants. Its proposed local product needs delayed repair at measured skin exposure and comparative value.

**AAT:** the pig patent reports 3 mg/mL nominal addition but 0.5 mg/mL measured antigen, alongside electrolyte differences. The cause is unresolved; no adsorption-loss percentage is inferred. Measure added mass, antigen recovery and retained NE/PR3 activity separately. Further allograft and systemic-recipient studies do not become exact warm-EVLP replication. [Primary disclosure](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20190059362A1/en) · [Leader update](../research/rounds/round5/round5_existing_leader_updates.md)

**RM4404 remains an independent-validation priority.** The study's catalog identifies WAY-262611 hydrochloride, while an alternative catalog lists free base. Reconcile molar basis, loading, release and solvent. A named provisional supports a rights question; its claims were not recovered. Earlier clustering, sensory masking, washout and healthy-reference concerns remain. [Local challenge](../research/rounds/round5/round5_shortlist_challenge.md)

**APR-246/AEC remains a serious local challenger.** Chronic human-lesion observations are meaningful, but pretreatment in cultured cells and incomplete cornification do not establish mature barrier repair. The issued claim's corneal-disease predicate requires precise reading; neither blanket skin blockage nor freedom is asserted. Require independent resilience/barrier benefit and a local window. It competes for research resources and is not a Darier active comparator. [Primary study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41419-020-2223-8) · [Issued claims](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12521392B2/en)

The funding proposition is a bounded milestone with explicit failure conditions. Repeated mentions, catalog hits or an unanswered owner question cannot substitute for it.
