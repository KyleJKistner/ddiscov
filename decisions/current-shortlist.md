## Investment objective

Maximize retained financial value from the marginal dollar across drug-development stages, with a **high probability of recovering invested cash plus a return** and survivable downside. Screen broadly, compare estimates, deepen a handful, then consider inquiries. Missing offers widen uncertainty; they do not prevent screening. Apply the [selection policy](selection-policy.md) and [opportunity template](opportunity-template.md).

Capital is flexible, not a spending target. Historical $500k filters and $750k scenarios are analytical cases, not current eligibility rules or commitments. Keep personal capital and loss thresholds in the private mandate. Required return, recovery deadline, minimum recovery probability, affordable cumulative loss, reserve and liquidity limits remain unresolved; they need not block desk research.

**Current capital decision:** No reviewed package establishes the requested high probability of cash recovery plus return. Further research is supported; deployment is not established by this ranking. Count cash actually collectible by the investor after rights payments, future capital, dilution, costs and taxes. Unspent funds, grants, company valuations and contingent milestones are not investment profit. A self-funded new vehicle starts with 100% founder equity; upstream economics and later dilution are separate.
