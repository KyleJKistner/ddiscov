# Current development shortlist

**Evidence cut: 4 November 2026. Round 4.** This is the controlling portfolio decision. Earlier grades, PDFs and domain memos are historical inputs. The [selection policy](selection-policy.md) explains the standard; the [cumulative register](../research/portfolio-register.json) retains coverage, source links and decision history.

The current result is **four continuing staged proof programs, one new independent-validation priority, and a serious local translational challenger**. No candidate yet satisfies the full S-tier standard. These are judgments about the value of the next experiment, not representations that funding, commercial rights, delivered materials or clinical readiness have been secured.

The ordering of the continuing four programs is a staged work queue, not a calibrated probability-of-success or expected-return ranking. APR-246 treats a different disease from NDC and competes for research resources; it is not a Darier active comparator. Selection must move with the evidence.

## What changed for the continuing leaders

### BLU3797: a better route map and a more demanding efficacy interpretation

Two public patent disclosures describe the exact upstream S-configured intermediate used by the paper's final transformations. This creates a plausible joined route that can be qualified independently. It is not a reproduced synthesis, physical stock or a commercial-rights conclusion. Some mass/mole entries and translated chemistry need reconciliation. [Primary paper](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/198568) · [US disclosure](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240226104A1/en) · [CN disclosure](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN119490502A/en)

The newly inspected supporting workbook has 60 sheets. An independent reader agreed on 815 selected populated cells. Strong dose-dependent wire-hang observations remain, but the assay has a 60-second ceiling. The small late-start comparison also has a caption/header regimen discrepancy, incomplete baseline entries and absent linkage between animals across functional and survival panels. These limit exact regimen transfer and equivalence claims. They do not establish misconduct. The [source-data audit](../research/rounds/round4/blu3797-audit.md) records the cells and limitations.

The TAM4MTM trial team's liver-safety halt adds a concrete disease-context lesson. It concerns tamoxifen, not demonstrated BLU toxicity. A systemic MTM1 program should assess muscle benefit alongside disease-specific bile handling. [Trial-team communication](https://mtmcnmregistry.org/media/images/Tamoxifen-team-study-message.pdf) · [Primary MTM1 liver study](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/166275)

### 8015-P2: distinguish an operating assay from a ready functional program

CMTA's March 2026 account describes a BrainXell/ANANDA R364W motor-neuron assay already used for screening. It measures mitochondrial movement and morphology in a patient line and healthy comparator; it does not demonstrate P2 response, neuromuscular transmission or force. A different apparent source of T105M material was a donor-recruitment wishlist. It is not cell-bank inventory. [Operating assay](https://cmtausa.org/simply-cmt/cmt2a-ipsc-motor-neuron-assay-cmta-star/)

An older R364W-based warning required correction: the authors withdrew specified human-harm extrapolations after identifying the wrong experimental substitution. They did not retract the entire paper. Morphology, fusion and useful nerve-muscle function need separate measurements. Human results with parent 8015 or Chimera C remain support for different articles, not proof for exact P2. [Author correction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7332790/) · [2024 chemistry and efficacy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11493442/)

### NDC-9009: qualify the disease state instead of inflating the efficacy cohort

The treated cultures represent three donors and two actuator-domain variants. Additional biopsy-only specimens do not enlarge the efficacy cohort. Mean SERCA2 protein was approximately 70% of control; protein abundance alone does not establish residual pump function. The maintenance ROCK inhibitor was removed before assays, so a concurrent-treatment confound is not demonstrated. The paper's protein-solubility assay does not measure dissolved drug. [Primary Darier study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13440392/)

These details reinforce the existing gate: treat an established, rescuable tissue deficit and measure exposure. Dantrolene adds a mechanistically distinct comparator rationale, but actual oral-trial outcomes remain unread. Completion and a results-document listing are insufficient to call that trial positive or negative. [2026 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42849607/) · [Official trial](https://euclinicaltrials.eu/ctis-public/view/2024-519201-36-00)

### AAT: the biological premise improved, the product question remains

An independent mouse study compared active recombinant AAT with an inactive reactive-center variant and albumin, and tested PR3/NE-deficient recipients. That substantially weakens a protein-only explanation of the broader premise. It supports measuring both protease activities in the intended perfusate. It is a cold-preservation experiment, not validation of the exact warm human-EVLP formulation or long-term recipient outcome. [Independent study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6078707/) · [2026 human abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42790593/)

Sivelestat belongs in the active-comparator plan. Its simpler NE-selective profile can test which useful effects require AAT's broader activity. Dose, warm-circuit stability, physical matching and residual exposure must be established for the product actually tested. [Donor-flush study](https://academic.oup.com/ejcts/article/32/5/791/373916)

## Why the new local challengers matter

RM4404 is WAY-262611 in a disclosed micellar formulation. Delayed sensory results justify replication. Randomization and blinded secondary imaging review were reported, while sensory-assessor masking remains unresolved. The paired-eye surgical analysis and healthy-reference comparisons limit the stronger durability claim; a separate one-injured-eye model offers cleaner direct comparisons. Day-30 structural medians are nearly identical for drug and vehicle, so differing significance against healthy controls cannot establish structural superiority. Time off treatment is not confirmed pharmacokinetic washout. [Primary study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/jnr.70142)

APR-246 has observations in two chronic human AEC cases, including an opposite-palm vehicle control in one. That is a meaningful advantage over an assembly-only cell result. However, incomplete cornification, withdrawal dependence, barrier quality and the chronic local exposure-to-harm window remain open. Its presumed direct p63-reactivation mechanism was not demonstrated by the clinical observation. The human evidence deserves qualification without assuming either success or superior asset readiness. [Primary study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41419-020-2223-8)

## Secondary work and reserves

DNM2 peptide-conjugated morpholino translation and FXT-001 remain staged secondary programs. FXT's analytical-deposit metadata improves traceability, but the exact human-organ formulation and full methods remain unverified. Existing-program status creates a need for a differentiated contribution, not an automatic negative scientific judgment.

The new reserves with the clearest next questions are CLP290 for useful motor function in chronic SCI, exact TAT-fused P110 for HSP, genotype-selected Bis-T-23 for podocyte function, and alvelestat for functional islet manufacturing yield. They have specific reopening criteria in the [round 4 screen](../research/rounds/round4/README.md). Earlier reserves remain in the register, including mIGF, FeM1269, CPTX and the narrower PLC-zeta proposition.

## What would support funding

A credible first tranche buys a reproducible article, a qualified disease-function assay and an exposure-linked answer to a specific development question. The next tranche depends on that answer. This can create useful data and formulation know-how before commercial terms are settled, but public reconstructibility alone does not establish a defensible clinical asset.

Selection separates **scientific merit**, **execution readiness** and **potential value capture**. An author or owner response is not the scientific gate. A missing exact article, uninterpretable model or lack of comparative function can be. The current opportunities are bounded proofs with explicit failure conditions; none should be presented as an obviously fundable finished drug solely because it survived literature screening.
