## BLU3797: qualification is now concrete

### Article and pharmacology

Original patent drawings verify the S-fluoroisoindolinone precursor junction to the published BLU route. The CN route is the preferred independent reconstruction basis; published quantities and yields remain internally inconsistent. A calculation is not a synthesized lot. Release requires structure, stereochemistry, quantitative content, impurity and stability records.

The JCI v2 PDF names enzyme catalog “PV3574l.” Thermo identifies PV3574 as dilution buffer and PV5374 as C2β kinase; the latter is a GST155–1383 construct. This documents an identification gap, not investigator misuse. Printed 150 nM lipid with 60 nM enzyme also needs reaction/detection qualification. The next assay therefore declares its construct, active enzyme, ATP/lipid conditions, initial-rate window and orthogonal readout. BLU and exact comparator47 are compared side by side; published IC50 values from different constructs cannot establish a selectivity margin. [Article/exposure dossier](../research/rounds/round8/blu_article_exposure.md)

Public native-protein PRM assays provide a route toward muscle C2β measurement. Their SUM159 validation is not muscle target engagement. A CETSA shift is not fractional occupancy, and the published custom NanoBRET tracer remains unspecified. A useful positive function result may justify confirmation while mechanism work continues; unresolved engagement prevents a strong target-negative conclusion.

### First model and quantitative decision

Start with **CB-SKM-XLMTM, MTM1 c.1388T>C/p.L463S**, and a prospectively selected healthy male reference. The specific product is standalone; the proposed CB-SKM-6-WT comparator is nonisogenic and is not proven to be the manufacturer's plotted control. Public 96-well twitch/fatigue data make this a concrete qualification route, while donor/batch counts and raw variance remain unknown.

The proposed first block is **24 untreated longitudinal tissues** across three independently initiated batches and two backgrounds. Conditional exploration uses **96 tissues** across three new batches, two backgrounds, vehicle and three analytically selected BLU exposures. Destructive exposure/engagement samples and optional control modules are additional resources. These are resource grids, not powered sample-size claims.

The primary outcome is baseline-adjusted **absolute evoked twitch force in µN**. Proposed local casting-day20/day27 timing is locked or changed using blinded qualification before treatment outcomes. The useful-effect target is **M = max(25% of the blinded calibration reference gap, the assay-repeatability floor)** in µN. The25% is a prospective program criterion, not a clinical MCID or published BLU effect.

- **Exploratory advance:** point estimate at least M, lower95% confidence bound above zero, qualified article/model/exposure and no confirmed unacceptable functional liability. Independent confirmation remains necessary.
- **Confirmed useful effect:** independent data with lower95% bound above M. A narrow first-background result can justify a second-background investment; clones are not independent donors.
- **Qualified futility:** upper95% bound below M, with adequate exposure/target perturbation, precision and demonstrated post-deficit modifiability. Without those conditions, classify uncertainty or redirect the assay/article. A broader target downgrade additionally needs orthogonal selective failure and another independent background.

The source/article, genotype, formulation, batch/tissue/sample denominator and analysis-lock fields must be complete before the decision utility can emit an advance. No result fields have been filled with fictional observations. [Controlling work order, specification and blank records](../research/rounds/round8/blu3797-qualification-package.md)

### Exposure, mitochondria and other contrary evidence

The published terminal total-plasma values do not define free muscle exposure, trough, AUC or a human dose. Map measured exposure and target response to useful force and energetic function. Proposed80% high-engagement characterization,20% antitarget objectives,3-fold headroom and20% repeated functional-harm review triggers are engineering choices, not validated efficacy or safety boundaries.

Combined cardiac C2α/C2β deletion, single-isoform depletion in transformed cells and paired catalytic add-back are different experiments. They motivate a focused energy/function check without proving BLU cardiotoxicity. The newly integrated kinase-inactive endothelial study reports altered vascular growth/mTORC1, limiting blanket reassurance from older histology. Its unresolved full-text denominator remains explicit. Full cardiac/neuronal/disease-liver distribution work follows a credible muscle signal; a complete systemic battery is not a hidden prerequisite for the initial proof. [Model/safety dossier](../research/rounds/round8/blu_model_function.md)

Continue to preserve earlier contrary evidence: p62 improvement outlasting function does not establish drug-free durability; limb function or resting breathing does not establish respiratory reserve; ASP2957 is a different active product; ASPIRO analyses share lineage. ## Enasidenib: cardiac evidence retained, CNS thesis challenged

The clearest severe-cardiomyopathy observation remains one original child, with concurrent care; the second original child did not start with severe systolic dysfunction. Five outcome records are not a verified complete five-person census. The neonatal report remains metadata-only. The existing source audit, pediatric sprinkle experience, adult accumulation/metabolite/DDI findings and genotype limits remain controlling. [Round7 clinical dossier](../research/rounds/round7/enasidenib_clinical.md) · [Product/exposure dossier](../research/rounds/round7/enasidenib_product.md)

Newly incorporated FDA and primary discovery records show **vorasidenib activity against IDH2-R140Q**. That requires a real comparison for a CNS-directed product. Tumor:plasma distribution is not normal infant-brain exposure; wild-type IDH activity, liver liability and high-exposure animal cardiomyopathy prevent a presumptive safety-superiority claim. Cardiac improvement, established neurological benefit and neonatal prevention remain separate hypotheses.

The next recommendation remains affirmative: qualify a specific pediatric administration/exposure question and a meaningful organ outcome. Existing sprinkle use means a new formulation must solve a demonstrated delivery, exposure or burden problem. Strong biochemical suppression alone cannot establish organ benefit, and a nonexclusive clinical dataset must not be relabeled as an owned drug. [Round8 clinical challenge](../research/rounds/round8/clinical_challenge.md)

## Ruxolitinib: controlled useful HHD care

Five distinguishable 1.5% reports remain selected uncontrolled observations with different background/induction/maintenance contexts. The nine-patient preprint remains unpooled. Marketed cream identity does not authenticate every historical brand/lot; AD PK and label limits do not establish a universal HHD exposure boundary. [Round7 clinical/product dossiers](../research/rounds/round7/README.md)

Onabotulinumtoxin A supplies a concrete sweat-control benchmark. Its tiny randomized study and separate pilot do not establish a definitive standard, and late pilot efficacy excludes relapsers: eleven HHD relapses mean11/26 HHD participants, not11/30 mixed HHD/Darier participants. A ruxolitinib proof should compare useful control and burden against a specified contemporary-care pathway, with rescue, missingness and all-randomized versus responder-maintenance denominators explicit.

A useful on-treatment cream can be valuable without genetic repair. A standalone product still needs differentiated clinical benefit and an executable product/rights route. KB111, guselkumab, existing composition/formulation claims and the branded franchise remain relevant; none is erased by another positive case.
