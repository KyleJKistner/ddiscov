## BLU3797: qualification is now concrete

### Article and pharmacology

Original patent drawings verify the S-fluoroisoindolinone precursor junction to the published BLU route. The CN route is the preferred independent reconstruction basis; published quantities and yields remain internally inconsistent. A calculation is not a synthesized lot. Release requires structure, stereochemistry, quantitative content, impurity and stability records.

The JCI v2 PDF names enzyme catalog “PV3574l.” Thermo identifies PV3574 as dilution buffer and PV5374 as C2β kinase; the latter is a GST155–1383 construct. This documents an identification gap, not investigator misuse. Printed 150 nM lipid with 60 nM enzyme also needs reaction/detection qualification. The next assay therefore declares its construct, active enzyme, ATP/lipid conditions, initial-rate window and orthogonal readout. BLU and exact comparator47 are compared side by side; published IC50 values from different constructs cannot establish a selectivity margin. [Article/exposure dossier](../research/rounds/round8/blu_article_exposure.md)
