## BLU3797: a specific affirmative next step

### Why the initial project is justified

Catalytic PIK3C2B genetics, exact-compound animal activity, a defined chemical article and public human-model precedents support a staged project. They do not already demonstrate exact-BLU human-force recovery. The joined upstream/final chemical route still needs an independently authenticated batch; the p.L463S patient model, p.E48*/corrected pair and independently constructed null routes need functional qualification in their intended use. [Prior human proof](../research/rounds/round5/blu3797-human-proof.md) · [Biology review](../research/rounds/round5/round5_blu_biology.md) · [Pharmacology review](../research/rounds/round5/round5_blu_pharmacology.md)

The next milestone is finite: begin with one specified disease/control system and a qualified molecule, establish a useful deficit and interpretable target engagement, then seek an exploratory function signal. Additional independent backgrounds and a fuller tissue window belong to confirmation. An unsuccessful material or assay qualification does not establish target failure. The [funding memo](../research/rounds/round6/round6_blu_milestone.md) specifies advance, redirect and stop branches.

### The new mitochondrial challenge is relevant but bounded

The October 2026 Science paper reports heart failure after **combined cardiomyocyte C2α/C2β deletion**. The accessible abstract does not settle single-isoform cardiac effects. The underlying Cambridge thesis shows mitochondrial fragmentation after individual β depletion in transformed cells, and its catalytic add-back experiment uses dual depletion. These are one research project, not independent replications, and neither is an exact-BLU toxicity experiment. [Science primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42848893/) · [Cambridge thesis](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/2aa25ead-0909-4db0-bf05-2aa16861a7bd)

Counterevidence matters: single-isoform perturbations retain vascular function in a separate dataset, and protein-retaining kinase-dead β mice have reassuring reported long-term histology. The recovered histology supplement lacks an animal denominator, while vascular redundancy cannot settle myocardial mitochondrial function. The appropriate next check is useful muscle function alongside mitochondrial function at the muscle-effective exposure, with C2α/VPS34 selectivity and genetic/chemical distinctions retained. Morphology alone is not a fatal result; equal-free-concentration culture overlap with unknown tissue distribution remains unresolved. [Integrated challenge and source limits](../research/rounds/round6/round6_blu_translation.md)

### Human and respiratory claims need their own anchors

A published adult local Mtm1-deletion model supplies a maintenance-disease route beyond juvenile development; it does not demonstrate BLU rescue. In an orthogonal DNM2 genetic experiment, near-normal resting breathing coexists with reduced maximal diaphragm force. Limb-muscle performance, resting breathing and respiratory capacity are different claims. [Adult and diaphragm evidence](../research/rounds/round6/round6_blu_translation.md)

The newly reviewed adult Unite-CNM baseline report uses the same 26-person cohort as an earlier hepatic analysis. Its MTM1 subset has substantial age/sex structure, and muscle/plasma DNM2 do not behave as interchangeable biomarkers. A figure-caption mean inconsistency remains unresolved. DYN101 was nonrandomized despite inconsistent wording in a later article. TAM4MTM's investigator transcript reports seven participants versus six in the older registry; no efficacy estimate is invented. Hepatic findings from those different articles inform monitoring and population choice without becoming evidence of BLU toxicity. [Clinical translation](../research/rounds/round6/round6_clinical_translation.md) · [Arithmetic and registry audit](../research/rounds/round6/round6_clinical_translation_audit.json)

## Enasidenib: a stronger positive assessment

The original 2023 public supplement records actual **sprinkle use in an eight-year-old with germline IDH2-R140Q**, followed by what the authors call capsules. The other child had severe established cardiomyopathy: reported LVEF rose from **20–25% to 35–40% at six months and 40–45% at eight months**, with concurrent heart-failure care and later fluctuations. This is meaningful organ-function evidence, not just metabolite suppression. It is an uncontrolled trajectory, not normalization; the second child began with normal systolic function and is not another severe-cardiomyopathy rescue. [Primary supplement](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41591-023-02382-9/MediaObjects/41591_2023_2382_MOESM1_ESM.pdf)

The formulation and program review narrows the remaining work to the intended infant article, exposure, chronic tolerability and an incremental contribution alongside AcSé-ESMART and other existing research. A completed adult formulation study without posted results does not establish bioequivalence. The 2026 five-week-old report remains metadata-only and adds zero verified efficacy observations. A first-ever pediatric formulation pitch is unsupported, but a specific infant exposure/organ-function project can still be worth funding. [Full challenge and evidence lineage](../research/rounds/round6/round6_reserve_challenge.md)

## Local challengers and the new screen

**Ruxolitinib/HHD** deserves active comparison with the existing local-work portfolio. The verified case describes established-lesion improvement, intermittent treatment because of cost, weeks of remission and milder recurrent flares. Three-month photographs do not establish continuous remission. The nine-patient preprint is abstract-level and is not pooled with cases into an independent response rate. A marketed 1.5% article reduces formulation uncertainty; HHD exposure and differentiated clinical value remain questions. KB111's latest primary filing reports five enrolled patients with dosing ongoing and no reported efficacy. [New-use review](../research/rounds/round6/round6_new_leads.md)

RM4404 remains a delayed-corneal-sensation replication priority; APR-246/AEC remains a serious human-lesion/local-barrier challenger. They address different diseases from NDC/Darier and ruxolitinib/HHD. None serves as a same-indication comparator simply because it is topical. Resource allocation can change when a clearer functional benefit, easier execution or better product contribution appears.
