## Nifedipine/lidocaine: territorial or partnered reserve

**Withdraw the unqualified first-place ranking and the undifferentiated $175k/$325k/$625k product-bridge package.** The [focused dossier](../research/rounds/round28/README.md) finds credible nifedipine activity, actual approvals and a mature formulation. It also finds that the extra benefit of lidocaine is unresolved, basic stability and human PK evidence already exist, several territories have incumbents, and Steel's Fizurex is an active US competitor. The small-territory royalty case struggles to repay meaningful development spending even conditional on launch. These are substantive evidence and economic reasons for the change, not a requirement to obtain offers before ranking.

Retain three distinct possibilities for comparison:

| Reserve package | What could create retained value | Reopening condition |
|---|---|---|
| Existing-product territory with an operating partner | Dossier/supply access plus a specific market-entry gap and enough contribution after upstream costs, local operations and launch capital | A plausible territory and channel support positive retained cash under reasonable price, volume and delay estimates; approval and rights assumptions fit that case. |
| Participation in an existing US program | An economically useful interest in a defined milestone, with the rest of development funded separately | The contribution earns adequate retained proceeds and a credible funded path to cash; an equity financing valuation alone is insufficient. |
| Independent differentiated product | A demonstrable delivery, exposure, usability or clinical advantage that existing products do not supply | The advantage survives competitor/patent mapping and can support the additional product and clinical program. Generic stability or healthy-volunteer PK repetition does not qualify. |

The next analytical deliverable is a territory/product/rights and retained-cash comparison. It is **not a default new human study**. Round 28 supplies explicit commercial scenarios and a stage-specific cost ladder, with rights, launch expenses and later capital separated. Full ownership of a dedicated vehicle does not remove payments to licensors or commercializers; those are modeled separately rather than assuming an arbitrary equity haircut.

## Next useful work

1. **Deepen the remaining core comparisons using public evidence and estimates.** Compare exact article, useful endpoint, population, commercial value, remaining development and retained interest. Break the APR/erlotinib tie only when a material assumption or evidence difference supports it.
2. **Apply the Round 28 lesson consistently.** Product familiarity can reduce biological uncertainty while leaving little new contribution to sell. Test what is already available before pricing formulation, PK or qualification work for any candidate.
3. **Stress-test complete cash paths.** Use dated investor contributions and distributions, including rights, carrying costs, launch, working capital, no-deal and funding-unavailable branches. A successful experiment and a company transaction are separate from investor repayment. Preserve historical Round 27 hurdles as calculations, not valuations.
4. **Narrow further, then inquire.** Focus later inquiries on the few assumptions controlling the narrowed choice. The [earlier commercial briefs](../research/commercial/2026-11-08-repayment-screen/counterparty-briefs.md) remain deferred background.

The current evidence does not substantiate a high probability of principal recovery plus a return for any candidate. This is compatible with provisional ranking: compare explicit scenarios and prefer credible retained economics, while avoiding a false conversion of clinical response rates into investment success probabilities.
